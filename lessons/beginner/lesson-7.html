7-Dars: Days, Months, Seasons

Assalomu alaykum! Bugungi darsimizda biz ingliz tilida hafta kunlari, oylar va fasllarni o'rganamiz. Shuningdek, sanani, vaqtni, yilni va oyni qanday so'rash va aytishni bilib olamiz.

Eslatma: Nima uchun kun, oy va fasllarni bilish kerak?

Kundalik hayotimizda biz doimo vaqt, sana va rejalar haqida gaplashamiz. "Qachon uchrashamiz?", "Bugun qaysi kun?", "Tug'ilgan kuning qachon?" — bu savollarga ingliz tilida javob bera olish, chet ellik do'stlar bilan muloqot qilishda va ish rejalarini tuzishda juda muhimdir.

4. Sana aytish qoidasi

Sana aytish uchun ingliz tilida quyidagi qoidalarga e'tibor bering:

  • In + oy/yil (In January, In 2024).
  • On + kun/sana (On Monday, On 5th May).
  • Sanani aytishda tartib sonlardan foydalanamiz (1st, 2nd, 3rd).

Misollar:

  • Today is Wednesday. (Bugun chorshanba.)
  • It is 2002. (Bu 2002-yil.)
  • My birthday is in May. (Mening tug'ilgan kunim may oyida.)
  • We have a lesson on Monday. (Biz dushanba kuni darsimiz bor.)
  • It is the 5th of May. (Bu 5-may.)

5. Dialog: Savol va Javoblar

Quyidagi savol-javoblarni o'rganib chiqing:

Vaqt so'rash: What time is it? (Soat necha?)It is 5 o'clock. (Soat 5.)
Kun so'rash: What day is it today? (Bugun qaysi kun?)Today is Monday. (Bugun dushanba.)
Yil so'rash: What year is it? (Qaysi yil?)It is 2024. (2024-yil.)
Oy so'rash: What month is it? (Qaysi oy?)It is May. (May oyi.)
