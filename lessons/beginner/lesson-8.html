8-Dars: Telling the time & Daily Routine

Assalomu alaykum! Bugungi darsimizda biz ingliz tilida vaqtni qanday aytishni va kundalik ish-harakatlarimizni qanday ifodalashni o'rganamiz. Bu bilimlar sizga kuningizni to'g'ri rejalashtirish va boshqalar bilan vaqtni kelishishda yordam beradi.

Eslatma: Nima uchun vaqt va kundalik ishlarni bilish kerak?

Tasavvur qiling, siz chet ellik do'stingiz bilan uchrashuv belgilashingiz kerak. "Soat nechada uchrashamiz?" deb so'rash va javob berish uchun vaqtni bilish shart. Shuningdek, o'z kuningiz haqida gapirib berish (masalan, "Men ertalab soat 7 da uyg'onaman") ingliz tilidagi oddiy suhbatlarning ajralmas qismidir. Daily Routine (Kundalik ishlar) mavzusi har bir boshlang'ich o'rganuvchi uchun eng muhim mavzulardan biridir.

1. Vaqtni aytish (Telling the time) - 10 xil ko'rinish

Quyida vaqtni ingliz tilida aytishning 10 ta turli ko'rinishi keltirilgan. Har biriga e'tibor bering va dinamika (ovoz) orqali talaffuzni eshitib, takrorlang.

1:00 It is one o'clock. Soat bir.
1:34 It is one thirty-four. Soat bir o'ttiz to'rt.
6:45 It is six forty-five. Soat olti qirq besh.
12:00 It is twelve o'clock. Soat o'n ikki.
23:15 It is eleven fifteen PM. Kechqurun soat o'n bir o'n besh.
07:30 It is seven thirty AM. Ertalab soat yetti o'ttiz.
9:05 It is nine oh five. Soat to'qqizdan besh daqiqa o'tdi.
2:30 It is half past two. Soat ikkidan yarim o'tdi.
4:15 It is quarter past four. Soat to'rtdan chorak o'tdi.
10:45 It is quarter to eleven. Soat o'n birdan o'n besh daqiqa kam.

2. Kundalik ish-harakatlar (Daily Routine)

Endi kundalik ish-harakatlarni ifodalashni o'rganamiz. Quyida 30 ta eng kerakli fe'l keltirilgan. Ularning 15 tasi harakat fe'llari (wake up, go to...), qolgan 15 tasi esa oddiy fe'llar (read, see, write...).

Har bir fe'lning tarjimasi, dinamikasi va beginner darajasidagi misol gapi (vaqt, kun yoki oyga bog'langan holda) berilgan.

Qat'iy buyruq: Yuqoridagi har bir gapni ovoz chiqarib 5 marotabadan o'qing va talaffuzingizni dinamika bilan solishtiring!

3. Double Questions (Qo'sh savollar)

Ingliz tilida ba'zan bitta savolning o'zi yetarli bo'lmaydi. Biz qo'shimcha ma'lumot olish uchun Double Questions (Qo'sh savollar) dan foydalanamiz. Bunda ikkita so'roq so'zi ketma-ket keladi (masalan, "What time...?", "What day...?").

What time (Soat nechada?) Vaqtni so'rash uchun
Misol: What time do you wake up? (Soat nechada uyg'onasiz?)
Misol: What time is it? (Soat necha?)
Misol: What time do you go to school on Monday? (Dushanba kuni soat nechada maktabga borasiz?)
What day (Qaysi kun?) Kunni so'rash uchun
Misol: What day is it today? (Bugun qaysi kun?)
Misol: What day do you play football? (Qaysi kun futbol o'ynaysiz?)
Misol: What day is your birthday? (Tug'ilgan kuningiz qaysi kun?)
What month (Qaysi oy?) Oyni so'rash uchun
Misol: What month is it? (Qaysi oy?)
Misol: What month is your birthday? (Tug'ilgan kuningiz qaysi oyda?)
Misol: What month do you go to school? (Qaysi oyda maktabga borasiz?)
What year (Qaysi yil?) Yilni so'rash uchun
Misol: What year is it? (Qaysi yil?)
Misol: What year did you start school? (Maktabni qaysi yili boshlagansiz?)
Misol: What year is this book? (Bu kitob qaysi yilga tegishli?)

5. Matn va Tarjima

Buyruq: Quyidagi matnni avval daftaringizga yozib oling va tagidan tarjima qilib yozing hamda to'liq rasmga olib istasangiz teacherga Telegram orqali yuboring.

Hello! My name is Aziz. I am a student. I have a daily routine. I wake up at 7 o'clock in the morning. I get up at 7:10. I brush my teeth and wash my face. I have breakfast at 8 o'clock. I go to school at 8:30. I have lunch at 1 PM. I do my homework in the afternoon. On Monday, I play football with my friends. I have dinner at 7 PM. I watch TV in the evening. I go to bed at 10 PM. I sleep at night. This is my daily routine.

Homework (Uyga vazifa)

1-topshiriq: Mustaqil gaplar tuzish

Quyidagi fe'llar ishtirokida o'zingiz gap tuzing (15 ta):

wake up, get up, brush teeth, have breakfast, go to school, have lunch, do homework, play football, have dinner, watch TV, go to bed, sleep, cook, clean, wash.

2-topshiriq: Tarjima va o'qish

Topshiriq: Quyidagi 15 ta gapni ingliz tilida yozib chiqing va ovoz chiqarib hohlaganingizcha takrorlang.
  1. Men ertalab soat 7 da uyg'onaman.
  2. Men ertalab soat 8 da nonushta qilaman.
  3. Men soat 8:30 da maktabga boraman.
  4. Men tushlikni soat 1 da qilaman.
  5. Men uyga vazifani tushdan keyin qilaman.
  6. Men dushanba kuni futbol o'ynayman.
  7. Men kechqurun televizor ko'raman.
  8. Men soat 10 da yotaman.
  9. Men kechasi uxlayman.
  10. Men kitob o'qiyman.
  11. Men xat yozaman.
  12. Men musiqa tinglayman.
  13. Men ingliz tilida gapiraman.
  14. Men ingliz tilini o'rganaman.
  15. Men yakshanba kuni do'stlarim bilan o'ynayman.
