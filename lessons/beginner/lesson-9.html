9-Dars: Present Simple (Hozirgi oddiy zamon)

Assalomu alaykum! Bugungi darsimizda biz ingliz tilining eng ko'p ishlatiladigan zamoni — Present Simple (Hozirgi oddiy zamon) ni o'rganamiz. Bu zamon sizning kundalik hayotingiz, odatlaringiz va doimiy holatlaringiz haqida gapirishga yordam beradi.

1. Present Simple nima?

Present Simple nima?

Present Simple — ingliz tilidagi eng asosiy zamon bo'lib, u doimiy takrorlanadigan ish-harakatlar, kundalik odatlar, umumiy qonuniyatlar va o'zgarmas holatlar haqida gapirishda ishlatiladi.

Qanday hollarda ishlatiladi?

  • Kundalik odatlar (Daily routines): I wake up at 7 o'clock. (Men soat 7 da uyg'onaman.)
  • Doimiy holatlar (Permanent states): I live in Tashkent. (Men Toshkentda yashayman.)
  • Umumiy haqiqatlar (General truths): The sun rises in the east. (Quyosh sharqdan chiqadi.)
  • Jadval va rejalar (Timetables): The train leaves at 5 PM. (Poyezd soat 17:00 da jo'naydi.)

Struktura (Formula):

Tasdiq gap: S + V (Fe'lning oddiy shakli)

Muhim qoida: Agar Ega (Subject) He, She, It bo'lsa yoki birlikdagi ot (masalan, Aziz) bo'lsa, fe'lga -s yoki -es qo'shiladi!

  • I / You / We / They + V (I play, They read)
  • He / She / It + V-s/es (He plays, She reads, It works)

Do / Does haqida:

Do va Does — Present Simple zamonda savol va inkor gaplarni yasashda ishlatiladigan yordamchi fe'llardir.

  • Do — I, You, We, They olmoshlari bilan ishlatiladi.
    Do you like tea? (Siz choyni yaxshi ko'rasizmi?)
  • Does — He, She, It olmoshlari bilan ishlatiladi.
    Does he play football? (U futbol o'ynaydimi?)

Eslatma: "Does" ishlatilganda, asosiy fe'lga "-s" qo'shilmaydi! (Does he play? — Does he plays? EMAS!).

Key Words (Kalit so'zlar):

Present Simple zamonini tanib olish uchun quyidagi kalit so'zlar ko'p ishlatiladi. Har biriga tarjima, talaffuz va misol gap keltirilgan:

Always (doim, har doim) — /ˈɔːl.weɪz/
Misol: I always wake up at 7 o'clock. (Men har doim soat 7 da uyg'onaman.)
Usually (odatda) — /ˈjuː.ʒu.ə.li/
Misol: He usually goes to school at 8 AM. (U odatda soat 8 da maktabga boradi.)
Often (ko'pincha) — /ˈɒf.ən/
Misol: They often play football on Monday. (Ular ko'pincha dushanba kuni futbol o'ynashadi.)
Sometimes (ba'zan) — /ˈsʌm.taɪmz/
Misol: I sometimes read a book in the evening. (Men ba'zan kechqurun kitob o'qiyman.)
Never (hech qachon) — /ˈnev.ər/
Misol: She never eats meat. (U hech qachon go'sht yemaydi.)
Every day (har kuni) — /ˈev.ri deɪ/
Misol: I go to school every day. (Men har kuni maktabga boraman.)
Every week (har hafta) — /ˈev.ri wiːk/
Misol: He plays tennis every week. (U har hafta tennis o'ynaydi.)
Every month (har oy) — /ˈev.ri mʌnθ/
Misol: We visit our grandparents every month. (Biz har oy buvam va bobomni ziyorat qilamiz.)
Every year (har yili) — /ˈev.ri jɪər/
Misol: They go to the sea every year. (Ular har yili dengizga borishadi.)
On Mondays (dushanbalari) — /ɒn ˈmʌn.deɪz/
Misol: She works on Mondays. (U dushanba kunlari ishlaydi.)
Yuqoridagi gaplarni ko'p takrorlang va eslab qoling, sababi bu gaplar sizning speaking ko'nikmangiz boshlang'ich poydevoridir!

3. So'roq shakli (Questions)

So'roq shakli qanday yasaladi?

Present Simple zamonda savol yasash uchun gap boshiga Do yoki Does qo'yiladi.

  • I / You / We / They uchun: Do + S + V + ...?
    Do you play football? (Siz futbol o'ynaysizmi?)
  • He / She / It uchun: Does + S + V (oddiy shakl) + ...?
    Does he play football? (U futbol o'ynaydimi?)

Diqqat! "Does" ishlatilganda fe'lga "-s" qo'shilmaydi.

4. Inkor shakli (Negative)

Inkor shakli qanday yasaladi?

Present Simple zamonda inkor yasash uchun yordamchi fe'ldan keyin not qo'shiladi.

  • I / You / We / They uchun: S + do not (don't) + V + ...
    I do not play football. (Men futbol o'ynamayman.)
  • He / She / It uchun: S + does not (doesn't) + V (oddiy shakl) + ...
    He does not play football. (U futbol o'ynamaydi.)

Diqqat! "Does not" ishlatilganda fe'lga "-s" qo'shilmaydi.

5 ta gap (Tasdiq va Inkor shakli):

I go to school every day. (Men har kuni maktabga boraman.)

He plays football on Fridays. (U juma kunlari futbol o'ynaydi.)
He does not play football on Fridays.
She reads a book every evening. (U har kechqurun kitob o'qiydi.)
She does not read a book every evening.
We have breakfast at 8 AM. (Biz ertalab soat 8 da nonushta qilamiz.)
We do not have breakfast at 8 AM.
They watch TV in the morning. (Ular ertalab televizor ko'rishadi.)
They do not watch TV in the morning.

Interaktiv test (25 ta)

Bo'sh o'rinlarni to'g'ri fe'l shakli, do/does yoki vaqt bilan to'ldiring.

Homework (Uyga vazifa)

1-topshiriq: Key words ishtirokida gaplar

Quyidagi kalit so'zlar ishtirokida o'zbekcha qisqa gaplar yozing va ularni ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:

always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, every week, on Mondays.

2-topshiriq: Reading (Matn va Savollar)

Topshiriq: Quyidagi hikoyani ovoz chiqarib o'qing, so'ngra savollarga javob bering.

Hello! My name is Jasur. I am a student. I have a daily routine. I wake up at 7 o'clock every morning. I get up at 7:10 and brush my teeth. I have breakfast at 8 AM. I usually eat bread and drink tea. I go to school at 8:30. I study English and math at school. I have lunch at 1 PM. After school, I do my homework in the afternoon. On Mondays, I play football with my friends. We play in the park. I have dinner with my family at 7 PM. I watch TV for one hour in the evening. Finally, I go to bed at 10 PM and sleep at night. This is my daily routine.

Savollarga javob bering:

  1. Jasur soat nechada uyg'onadi?
  2. U maktabga soat nechada boradi?
  3. U qaysi kunlari futbol o'ynaydi?
  4. U kechki ovqatni kim bilan yeydi?
  5. U kechqurun nima qiladi?

3-topshiriq: O'z kunlik hayotingizni yozing

Topshiriq: Bugungi barcha yangi so'zlar va strukturadan foydalanib o'z kunlik hayotingizni yozishga harakat qiling va ham rasmga olib ham voice qilib teacherga yuboring. Unutmang Daily routine ni yoddan bilish bu sizga speaking qilish imkonini beradi.
