5-Dars: To Be (bo'lmoq) fe'li

Assalomu alaykum! Bugungi darsimizda ingliz tilining eng asosiy fe'li bo'lgan "To Be" (bo'lmoq) fe'lini o'rganamiz. Bu fe'lni bilmasdan turib, ingliz tilida gapirishning iloji yo'q!

Qisqacha eslatma:

To Be fe'li o'zbek tilida "bo'lmoq" deb tarjima qilinadi. U gapda ikkita asosiy vazifani bajaradi: 1) Kimningdir yoki nimaningdir qanday holatda ekanligini (holat), 2) Ayni damda sodir bo'layotgan ish-harakatni (davomli ish harakat) ifodalaydi. O'zbek tilida bu fe'l ba'zan tushib qoladi (masalan: "Men talabaman" - "Men" va "talabaman" so'zlari orasida fe'l yo'q, lekin ingliz tilida "I am a student" deb aytiladi).

Kishilik olmoshlari (Subject Pronouns)

1-Bo'lim: To Be fe'li qoidasi va strukturasi

To Be fe'li ingliz tilida "bo'lmoq" degan ma'noni anglatadi. U quyidagi ikki holatda ishlatiladi:

1. Holatni ifodalash (State)

Kimningdir yoki nimaningdir qanday ahvolda, qanday ko'rinishda ekanligini bildiradi.

  • I am a student. (Men talabaman) - Kimligim (holatim)
  • She is beautiful. (U chiroyli) - Qanday ko'rinishdaligi (holati)
  • They are at home. (Ular uyda) - Qayerdaligi (holati)

2. Davomli ish harakatni ifodalash (Continuous Actions)

Ayni damda, hozirning o'zida sodir bo'layotgan ish-harakatni bildiradi. Bunda To Be yordamchi fe'l bo'lib xizmat qiladi.

  • I am reading a book. (Men kitob o'qiyapman) - Hozir o'qiyapman
  • He is playing football. (U futbol o'ynayapti) - Hozir o'ynayapti
  • We are learning English. (Biz ingliz tili o'rganyapmiz) - Hozir o'rganyapmiz

Am, Is, Are nima va qaysi olmosh bilan ishlatiladi?

To Be fe'lining hozirgi zamondagi uchta shakli bor: am, is, are. Ular kishilik olmoshlariga qarab tanlanadi:

To Be qanday tarjima qilinadi?

O'zbek tilida "To Be" fe'li alohida so'z sifatida tarjima qilinmaydi, balki gap oxiridagi qo'shimchalarga (-man, -san, -dir, -miz, -siz, -dirlar) aylanadi. Masalan: "I am a doctor" — "Men shifokorman". "He is a doctor" — "U shifokordir" (yoki shunchaki "U shifokor").

2-Bo'lim: Holatni ifodalovchi gaplar (14 ta)

Quyidagi gaplarni ovoz chiqarib o'qing va qanday gap ekanligini tushunishga harakat qiling:

1I am a student.Men talabaman.
2I am tall.Men novchaman.
3He is a teacher.U o'qituvchi.
4He is at home.U uyda.
5She is a doctor.U shifokor.
6She is beautiful.U chiroyli.
7It is a cat.U mushuk.
8It is cold today.Bugun sovuq.
9You are a good friend.Siz yaxshi do'stsiz.
10You are happy.Siz baxtlisiz.
11We are students.Biz talabalar.
12We are in the classroom.Biz sinfdadamiz.
13They are brothers.Ular aka-uka.
14They are very kind.Ular juda mehribon.
Qoida: Yuqoridagi gaplarga yaxshilab e'tibor bering. To Be fe'li aynan qanday hollarda va qay ko'rinishda ishlatilmoqda anglashga harakat qiling. Yuqoridagi 14 gapimiz hammasi aynan holatga bog'liq.

Holat nima? Holat — sizning novchaligingiz yoki uyingizning kattaligi yoki ota-onangizning yoshi kattaligi, hullas, shaxsni yoki buyumni qanday ko'rinishda yoki qanday ahvolda ekanligidir.

Agar tushunmasangiz, qoidani kamida 3 marotaba takrorlang.

3-Bo'lim: Davomli ish harakatni ifodalovchi gaplar (14 ta)

Endi huddi yuqoridagi singari, har bir olmoshga ikkitadan gap tuzamiz, lekin bu gaplarning hammasi davomli ish harakatda bo'ladi. Gaplarni o'qing va ovoz chiqarib takrorlang:

1I am reading a book.Men kitob o'qiyapman.
2I am writing a letter.Men xat yozyapman.
3He is playing football.U futbol o'ynayapti.
4He is sleeping.U uxlayapti.
5She is cooking dinner.U kechki ovqat pishiryapti.
6She is watching TV.U televizor ko'ryapti.
7It is raining.Yomg'ir yog'yapti.
8It is running.U yuguryapti.
9You are singing a song.Siz qo'shiq aytayapsiz.
10You are smiling.Siz kulimsiraysiz.
11We are learning English.Biz ingliz tili o'rganyapmiz.
12We are working.Biz ishlayapmiz.
13They are dancing.Ular raqsga tushyaptilar.
14They are laughing.Ular kulyaptilar.
Eslatma: Tasavvur qiling siz hozir kitob o'qiyapsiz yoki ayni damda mana shu matinni o'qiyapsiz. Davomli ish harakatni anglash shunday: hozirning o'zida qanday ishlar sodir bo'lyapti? Ha, aynan hozirning o'zida atrofga qarang, qanday ish sodir bo'layotganiga e'tibor qiling. Sodir bo'lgan emas yoki endi sodir bo'ladigan ham emas, aynan hozir sodir bo'layotgan ishlar continuous actions (davomli ish harakat)dur. Biz bularni gapirishda, ya'ni davomli ish harakatini gapirishda albatta To Be yordamchi fe'lidan foydalanamiz!

Davomli ish harakat formulasi:

S + To Be + V+ing

S - Ega (I, He, She, It, You, We, They)
To Be - am, is, are
V+ing - Fe'lning -ing qo'shimchasi bilan shakli (reading, playing, cooking...)

1-Interaktiv test (10 ta savol)

To Be fe'liga oid bo'sh o'rinlarni to'ldiring.

4-Bo'lim: To Be ning savol shakli

Formulaga e'tibor bering. Savol shaklini yasash uchun To Be fe'li ega (Subject) dan oldinga o'tadi:

S + To Be + Ving    ➔    To Be + S + Ving?

Oddiy gap: I am reading a book. (Men kitob o'qiyapman.)

Savol gap: Am I reading a book? (Men kitob o'qiyapmanmi?)

Endi misollarga e'tibor beramiz. Avval oddiy gap, keyin savol shakli:

1This is a big house.Bu katta uy.
2Is this a big house?Bu katta uymi?
3He is a doctor.U shifokor.
4Is he a doctor?U shifokormi?
5They are playing football.Ular futbol o'ynashyapti.
6Are they playing football?Ular futbol o'ynashyaptimi?
7She is a nurse.U hamshira.
8Is she a nurse?U hamshirami?
9You are reading a book.Siz kitob o'qiyapsiz.
10Are you reading a book?Siz kitob o'qiyapsizmi?
Qoida: To Be yordamchi fe'li ishtirok etgan gaplarning hammasida savol so'rash uchun To Be yordamchi fe'lini egadan oldinga yoki gapning boshiga qo'yish orqali amalga oshiriladi.

To Be + S + Ving?

Misollarga e'tibor bering (yana 5 ta):

It is a cat. U mushuk. ➔ Is it a cat? U mushukmi?
We are happy. Biz baxtlimiz. ➔ Are we happy? Biz baxtlimizmi?
He is sleeping. U uxlayapti. ➔ Is he sleeping? U uxlayaptimi?
They are students. Ular talabalar. ➔ Are they students? Ular talabami?
I am a teacher. Men o'qituvchiman. ➔ Am I a teacher? Men o'qituvchimanmi?

Daftaringizga bajaring:

Quyidagi 7 gapni daftaringizga savolga aylantirib yozing:

  1. She is a doctor.
  2. They are playing tennis.
  3. He is at home.
  4. We are students.
  5. It is a big house.
  6. You are reading a book.
  7. She is cooking dinner.

5-Bo'lim: To Be ning inkor shakli

Savol bo'limini qanday tushuntirgan bo'lsak, inkor bo'limini ham huddi shunday qilib tushuntiramiz. Inkor gap yasash uchun To Be fe'lidan keyin not so'zini qo'shamiz:

S + To Be + not + Ving

Oddiy gap: I am reading a book. (Men kitob o'qiyapman.)

Inkor gap: I am not reading a book. (Men kitob o'qimayapman.)


Qisqartma shakllari:

  • I am not ➔ I'm not
  • He is not ➔ He isn't
  • She is not ➔ She isn't
  • It is not ➔ It isn't
  • You are not ➔ You aren't
  • We are not ➔ We aren't
  • They are not ➔ They aren't

Misollarga e'tibor bering (5 ta):

He is not a doctor.U shifokor emas.
They are not playing football.Ular futbol o'ynashmayapti.
I am not a student.Men talaba emasman.
She is not cooking dinner.U kechki ovqat pishirmayapti.
We are not happy.Biz baxtli emasmiz.

Reading (O'qish) matni

Topshiriq: Quyidagi matnni ovoz chiqarib o'qishga harakat qiling, o'z ovozingizni yozib oling va teacherga Telegram orqali yuboring.
