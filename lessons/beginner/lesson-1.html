6. Salomlashish va Tanishish (Greetings & Introductions)

Norasmiy (Do'stlar orasida):

  • Hi! — Salom!
  • Hello! — Salom!
  • What's up? — Nima gap? (Norasmiy)

Rasmiy (Kattalar bilan):

  • Good morning! — Xayrli tong! (Ertalab soat 12:00 gacha)
  • Good afternoon! — Xayrli kun! (12:00 dan 18:00 gacha)
  • Good evening! — Xayrli kech! (18:00 dan keyin)

Tanishish:

  • What is your name? — Ismingiz nima?
  • My name is Ali. — Mening ismim Ali.
  • Nice to meet you! — Tanishganimdan xursandman!
  • How are you? — Qalaysiz?
  • I am fine, thank you. — Yaxshiman, rahmat.
