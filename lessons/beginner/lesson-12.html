12-Dars: Places in the city & Directions

Assalomu alaykum! Bugungi, ya'ni yakuniy 12-darsimizda biz shahardagi eng muhim joylarni (Places in the city) va qayerdadir adashib qolsak, yo'l so'rashni (Directions) o'rganamiz. Bu mavzu chet elda sayohat qilganingizda, yangi shaharga borganingizda yoki shunchaki chet ellik do'stingizga yo'l ko'rsatishda sizga juda katta yordam beradi!

Eslatma: Nima uchun shahardagi joylarni va yo'nalishni bilish juda muhim?

Tasavvur qiling, siz Londonda yoki Nyu-Yorkdasiz. Sizga eng yaqin kasalxona, bank yoki eng mazali pitsa pishiradigan restoran qayerdaligini bilishingiz kerak. Atrofdagi odamlardan "Kechirasiz, qayerda...?" deb so'rash va ularning javobini tushunish (masalan, "O'ngga buriling", "To'g'riga yuring") sizni vaqt va asablarni tejaydi. Shahar infrastuzilmasi va yo'nalish so'zlari ingliz tilining eng amaliy va hayotiy qismidir.

1. Shahardagi joylar (Places in the city) - 30 ta

Quyida har bir joy nomi, uning tarjimasi, talaffuzi, dinamikasi va misol gapi keltirilgan. Misol gaplar orqali so'zni qanday ishlatishni o'rganib olasiz.

Misol: I need to go to the hospital. (Men kasalxonaga borishim kerak.)

22Swimming pool/ˈswɪm.ɪŋ puːl/Suzish havzasi

Misol: The gym is near my house. (Sport zali uyimga yaqin.)

28Coffee shop/ˈkɒf.i ʃɒp/Qahvaxona

2. Yo'nalish so'rash (Directions) - Savol va Javob

Quyida yo'l so'rash va unga javob berishning 10 ta asosiy dialoglari keltirilgan. Har bir dialog dinamikaga ega, uni eshitib, takrorlang.

A: Excuse me, where is the bank? (Kechirasiz, bank qayerda?)
B: Go straight and turn left. (To'g'riga yuring va chapga buriling.)
A: Where is the hospital? (Kasalxona qayerda?)
B: It is next to the pharmacy. (U dorixonaning yonida.)
A: How do I get to the park? (Bog'ga qanday borsam bo'ladi?)
B: Turn right at the traffic lights. (Svetoforda o'ngga buriling.)
A: Is the supermarket near here? (Supermarket bu yerga yaqinmi?)
B: Yes, it is on the left. (Ha, u chap tomonda.)
A: Where is the nearest bus stop? (Eng yaqin avtobus bekati qayerda?)
B: It is behind the cafe. (U kafening orqasida.)
A: How can I get to the museum? (Muzeyga qanday borishim mumkin?)
B: Go along this street. (Shu ko'cha bo'ylab yuring.)
A: Excuse me, where is the train station? (Kechirasiz, vokzal qayerda?)
B: It is opposite the hotel. (U mehmonxonaning ro'parasida.)
A: Where is the post office? (Pochta bo'limi qayerda?)
B: Turn left at the bank. (Bankda chapga buriling.)
A: Is there a pharmacy near here? (Bu yerga yaqin dorixona bormi?)
B: Yes, it is on the corner. (Ha, u burchakda.)
A: Where is the zoo? (Zoologiya bog'i qayerda?)
B: Go past the school. (Maktabdan o'tib yuring.)

3. Amaliyot: Yo'nalish xaritasi

Tasavvur qiling, siz quyidagi xaritada turibsiz. Sizning uyingiz (Home) eng pastda. Atrofingizda turli joylar bor. Quyidagi gaplarni o'qib, qaysi joyga qanday borishni o'rganing.

[Home] --- (Go straight) ---> [Park] --- (Turn left) ---> [Bank]
|
(Turn right)
|
[Pharmacy] --- (Go past) ---> [Hospital]
From Home, go straight to the park.Uydan to'g'riga bog'ga boring.
From the park, turn left to the bank.Bog'dan bankka buriling.
From Home, turn right to the pharmacy.Uydan o'ngga dorixonaga buriling.
From the pharmacy, go past the hospital.Dorixonadan kasalxonadan o'tib yuring.

7. Reading (Matn va Savollar)

Topshiriq: Quyidagi matnni ovoz chiqarib o'qing, so'ngra savollarga javob bering.

Hello! My name is Sardor. I live in Tashkent. My city is very big and beautiful. There are many places in my city. There is a big park near my house. I go to the park every Sunday. The park is next to the library. I like reading books, so I often go to the library. The library is very quiet. There is a supermarket opposite the park. My mother buys food there. The hospital is behind the supermarket. My father works in the hospital. He is a doctor. The bank is next to the post office. The bus stop is in front of the bank. I usually go to school by bus. The school is far from my house. The train station is in the city center. It is very busy. I love my city!

Savollarga javob bering:

  1. Sardor qayerda yashaydi?
  2. U qaysi kuni bog'ga boradi?
  3. Bog' nimaning yonida?
  4. Supermarket qayerda?
  5. Uning otasi qayerda ishlaydi?
  6. Bank nimaning yonida?
  7. Avtobus bekati qayerda?
  8. Maktab uyiga yaqinmi?
  9. Vokzal qayerda?
  10. Sardor maktabga qanday boradi?
