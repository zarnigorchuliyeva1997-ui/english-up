11-Dars: To Be & Present Simple Mustahkamlash

Assalomu alaykum! Bugungi darsimizda biz oldingi darslarda o'rgangan ikkita eng muhim mavzu — To Be (bo'lmoq) fe'li va Present Simple (Hozirgi oddiy zamon) ni chuqur takrorlaymiz va mustahkamlaymiz. Bu dars sizning bilimlaringizni mustahkam poydevorga aylantiradi!

Eslatma: Nima uchun bu mavzularni takrorlash kerak?

To Be va Present Simple — ingliz tilining eng asosiy ustunlaridir. To Be fe'li orqali biz kimligimiz, qanday holatda ekanligimiz va nima qilayotganimizni aytamiz. Present Simple orqali esa kundalik odatlarimiz va doimiy ishlarimiz haqida gapiramiz. Bu ikkala mavzuni mukammal bilish sizga oddiy suhbatlarni bemalol olib borish imkonini beradi.

1. To Be (bo'lmoq) fe'li - Nazariya

To Be fe'li hozirgi zamonda uchta shaklga ega: am, is, are. Ular kishilik olmoshlariga qarab tanlanadi:

  • I + am (Men ...man)
  • He / She / It + is (U ...dir)
  • You / We / They + are (Siz/Biz/Ular ...siz/miz/dirlar)

Qisqartmalar: I'm, He's, She's, It's, You're, We're, They're.

Tasdiq gap: I am a student. (Men talabaman.)

Inkor gap: I am not a student. (Men talaba emasman.)

Savol gap: Am I a student? (Men talabamanmi?)

To Be ikki holatda ishlatiladi:

  • Holatni ifodalash: I am tall. (Men novchaman.) She is beautiful. (U chiroyli.)
  • Davomli ish harakatni ifodalash: I am reading a book. (Men kitob o'qiyapman.)

7. Present Simple - Nazariya

Present Simple — doimiy takrorlanadigan ish-harakatlar, kundalik odatlar, umumiy qonuniyatlar haqida gapirishda ishlatiladi.

Tasdiq gap: S + V (He/She/It uchun V-s/es).

  • I/You/We/They + V (I play, They read)
  • He/She/It + V-s/es (He plays, She reads)

Inkor gap: S + do/does + not + V.

  • I do not play. (Men o'ynamayman.)
  • He does not play. (U o'ynamaydi.)

Savol gap: Do/Does + S + V?

  • Do you play? (Siz o'ynaysizmi?)
  • Does he play? (U o'ynaydimi?)

Kalit so'zlar: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, every week, on Mondays, etc.

12. Homework (Uyga vazifa)

1-topshiriq: To Be va Present Simple gaplar tuzish

Quyidagi so'zlar ishtirokida 20 ta gap tuzing (10 tasi To Be, 10 tasi Present Simple):

happy, student, teacher, read, play, go, have, work, beautiful, tall, always, usually, never, every day, on Mondays.

2-topshiriq: Reading (Matn va Savollar)

Topshiriq: Quyidagi matnni ovoz chiqarib o'qing, so'ngra savollarga javob bering.

Hello! My name is Laziza. I am a student. I am 14 years old. I live in Tashkent. I have a big family. My father is a doctor. He works in a hospital. My mother is a teacher. She works at school. I have a brother and a sister. My brother is 10 years old. He plays football every day. My sister is 5 years old. She likes ice cream. I like reading books and playing tennis. I usually wake up at 7 o'clock. I go to school at 8 o'clock. I have lunch at 1 PM. I do my homework in the afternoon. I have dinner with my family at 7 PM. I go to bed at 10 PM. This is my daily routine.

Savollarga javob bering:

  1. Laziza necha yoshda?
  2. Uning otasi kim?
  3. Uning onasi qayerda ishlaydi?
  4. Uning akasi nima qilishni yaxshi ko'radi?
  5. Laziza soat nechada uyg'onadi?
  6. U soat nechada maktabga boradi?
  7. U kechki ovqatni kim bilan yeydi?
  8. U soat nechada yotadi?
  9. Uning opasi nimalarni yaxshi ko'radi?
  10. Laziza nimalarni yaxshi ko'radi?
