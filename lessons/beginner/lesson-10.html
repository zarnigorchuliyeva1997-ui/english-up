10-Dars: Food and Drinks

Assalomu alaykum! Bugungi darsimizda biz ingliz tilida ovqat va ichimliklar nomlarini, shuningdek, o'z xohish-istaklarimizni (I like / I don't like) qanday ifodalashni o'rganamiz. Bu mavzu kundalik hayotda, sayohatda va restoranda juda asqotadi!

Eslatma: Nima uchun ovqat va ichimliklarni bilish kerak?

Ovqatlanish — bu inson hayotining ajralmas qismi. Chet elda sayohat qilganingizda, restoranda ovqat buyurtma qilish, do'stlaringizni mehmonga taklif qilish yoki shunchaki o'z didingiz haqida gapirish uchun ushbu so'zlarni bilish juda muhim. Shuningdek, "I like" va "I don't like" iboralari orqali siz o'z his-tuyg'ularingizni va afzalliklaringizni ifodalashni o'rganasiz, bu esa muloqotning asosidir.

3. I like / I don't like qoidasi

O'z xohish-istaklarimizni ifodalash

Ovqat va ichimliklar haqida gapirganda, biz ko'pincha "yaxshi ko'raman" yoki "yaxshi ko'rmayman" deb aytamiz. Ingliz tilida bu quyidagicha ifodalanadi:

  • I like + ot — Men ...ni yaxshi ko'raman.
    I like apples. (Men olmalarni yaxshi ko'raman.)
  • I don't like + ot — Men ...ni yaxshi ko'rmayman.
    I don't like coffee. (Men qahvani yaxshi ko'rmayman.)
  • Do you like + ot? — Siz ...ni yaxshi ko'rasizmi?
    Do you like tea? (Siz choyni yaxshi ko'rasizmi?)

Diqqat! Agar ot ko'plikda bo'lsa (apples, bananas), fe'l o'zgarmaydi. "I like apple" emas, "I like apples" deyish to'g'ri.

5. Interaktiv test (25 ta)

Quyidagi testda ovqat/ichimlik, like/don't like va savol shakllariga oid savollar berilgan. To'g'ri javobni yozing.

2-topshiriq: Reading (Matn va Savollar)

Topshiriq: Quyidagi hikoyani ovoz chiqarib o'qing, so'ngra savollarga javob bering.

Hello! My name is Malika. I have a big family. We like different food. I like apples, bananas and oranges. I don't like fish. My father likes meat and rice. He doesn't like onions. My mother likes vegetables and salad. She doesn't like fast food. My brother likes pizza and chocolate. He doesn't like soup. We all like ice cream! We usually have breakfast at 8 AM. We have lunch at 1 PM. We have dinner at 7 PM. What about you? Do you like ice cream?

Savollarga javob bering:

  1. Malika qanday ovqatlarni yaxshi ko'radi?
  2. Uning otasi qanday ovqatlarni yaxshi ko'radi?
  3. Uning onasi nimalarni yaxshi ko'rmaydi?
  4. Uning akasi nimalarni yaxshi ko'radi?
  5. Ular soat nechada tushlik qilishadi?
