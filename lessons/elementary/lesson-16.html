2. ENGLISH SENTENCE STRUCTURE

Ingliz tilida oddiy darak gap odatda quyidagi tartibda tuziladi:

SUBJECT + VERB + OBJECT

1. Subject — Ega

Gapda ish-harakatni bajaruvchi shaxs yoki narsa.

I — Men
You — Sen / Siz
He — U (erkak)
She — U (ayol)
We — Biz
They — Ular

2. Verb — Fe'l

Harakat yoki holatni bildiradi.

Work — ishlamoq
Read — o‘qimoq
Write — yozmoq
Speak — gapirmoq
Learn — o‘rganmoq

3. Object — To‘ldiruvchi

Harakat kimga yoki nimaga qaratilganini bildiradi.

Books — kitoblar
English — ingliz tili
Water — suv
Food — ovqat

Gapni qismlarga ajratamiz

I read books.
I = Subject
read = Verb
books = Object
Men kitoblar o‘qiyman.
She drinks water.
She = Subject
drinks = Verb
water = Object
U suv ichadi.
They learn English.
They = Subject
learn = Verb
English = Object
Ular ingliz tilini o‘rganishadi.

4. POSITIVE SENTENCES — DARAK GAPLAR

Darak gap biror ish-harakat bajarilishini yoki holat mavjudligini tasdiqlaydi.

Subject + Verb + Object

Present Simple bilan

I work every day. — Men har kuni ishlayman.
You speak English. — Siz inglizcha gapirasiz.
We study together. — Biz birgalikda o‘qiymiz.
They live in London. — Ular Londonda yashashadi.

He / She / It qoidasi

Present Simple darak gapida He, She, It bilan asosiy fe'lga odatda -s yoki -es qo‘shiladi.

He / She / It + Verb(s/es)
He works every day. — U har kuni ishlaydi.
She reads books. — U kitoblar o‘qiydi.
He watches TV. — U televizor ko‘radi.
She goes to school. — U maktabga boradi.

-s qo‘shilishi

Work → Works
Read → Reads
Play → Plays
Drink → Drinks

-es qo‘shilishi

Odatda -sh, -ch, -ss, -x, -o bilan tugagan ayrim fe'llarga -es qo‘shiladi.

Watch → Watches
Wash → Washes
Go → Goes
Fix → Fixes

-y qoidasi

Undosh + y bilan tugagan fe'llarda y harfi ies ga o‘zgaradi.

Study → Studies
Try → Tries
Carry → Carries

Unli + y bo‘lsa, faqat -s qo‘shiladi.

Play → Plays
Enjoy → Enjoys

5. NEGATIVE SENTENCES — INKOR GAPLAR

Inkor gap biror ish-harakat bajarilmasligini bildiradi.

Subject + do/does + not + V1

DO NOT / DON'T

I, You, We, They bilan ishlatiladi.

I do not work here.
Men bu yerda ishlamayman.
You don't understand me.
Siz meni tushunmaysiz.
We don't drink coffee.
Biz qahva ichmaymiz.
They don't play tennis.
Ular tennis o‘ynashmaydi.

DOES NOT / DOESN'T

He, She, It bilan ishlatiladi.

He does not work here.
U bu yerda ishlamaydi.
She doesn't like coffee.
U qahvani yoqtirmaydi.
It doesn't work.
U ishlamaydi.
ENG MUHIM QOIDA:
Doesn't ishlatilganda asosiy fe'lga -s yoki -es qo‘shilmaydi.

He doesn't works. ❌
He doesn't work. ✅

She doesn't likes tea. ❌
She doesn't like tea. ✅

16. LISTENING 1 — MY DAILY ROUTINE

Hello! My name is David. I am twenty years old. I live in London. I wake up at seven o'clock. I eat breakfast and drink tea. I go to work at eight. I work in a small shop. I like my job. In the evening, I study English. I want to speak English very well.

Tarjimasi

Salom! Mening ismim David. Men yigirma yoshdaman. Londonda yashayman. Soat yettida uyg‘onaman. Nonushta qilaman va choy ichaman. Soat sakkizda ishga boraman. Kichik do‘konda ishlayman. Ishimni yoqtiraman. Kechqurun ingliz tilini o‘rganaman. Ingliz tilida juda yaxshi gapirishni xohlayman.

📓 DAFTARGA YOZING

Listening matnini to‘liq ko‘chiring. Undan 5 ta savol tuzing. Matnni o‘zbek tiliga tarjima qiling.

17. LISTENING 2 — A NEW STUDENT

Sarah is a new student. She studies English every day. Her teacher is Mr Brown. She has two friends in her class. They speak English together. Sarah likes reading books. She also watches English videos. She wants to improve her English.

Tarjimasi

Sarah yangi o‘quvchi. U har kuni ingliz tilini o‘rganadi. Uning o‘qituvchisi Mr Brown. Sinfida ikkita do‘sti bor. Ular birgalikda inglizcha gaplashishadi. Sarah kitob o‘qishni yoqtiradi. Shuningdek, inglizcha videolar ko‘radi. U ingliz tilini yaxshilashni xohlaydi.

📓 DAFTARGA YOZING

Matnni ko‘chiring. Undan 5 ta WH-question tuzing. Har bir savolga matndan foydalanib javob yozing.
