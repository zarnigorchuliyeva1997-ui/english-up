✍️ 2. ENGLISH SENTENCE STRUCTURE

Ingliz tilida oddiy darak gapning asosiy tartibi quyidagicha:

SUBJECT + VERB + OBJECT

Subject: Ega — ish-harakatni bajaruvchi.

Verb: Fe'l — bajarilayotgan harakat.

Object: To‘ldiruvchi — harakat ta'sir qilayotgan narsa yoki shaxs.

I read books.
Men kitoblar o‘qiyman.
She drinks water.
U suv ichadi.
They play football.
Ular futbol o‘ynashadi.

Gap qismlarini tushunamiz

I = Subject
read = Verb
books = Object

Ingliz tilida odatda ega fe'ldan oldin keladi. O‘zbek tilidagi gap tartibini to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri ingliz tiliga ko‘chirmang.

Daftarga yozing

1. Subject — Ega.
2. Verb — Fe'l.
3. Object — To‘ldiruvchi.
4. Formula: Subject + Verb + Object.
5. I read books. — Men kitob o‘qiyman.
6. She likes tea. — U choyni yoqtiradi.

📖 3. POSITIVE SENTENCES

Positive sentence — darak gap. Biror ish-harakat sodir bo‘lishini yoki holatni tasdiqlaydi.

Subject + Verb + Object
I speak English.
Men inglizcha gapiraman.
He works every day.
U har kuni ishlaydi.
We learn English.
Biz ingliz tilini o‘rganamiz.

He / She / It qoidasi

Present Simple zamonida He, She, It bilan asosiy fe'lga odatda -s yoki -es qo‘shiladi.

He / She / It + Verb(s/es)
He plays football. U futbol o‘ynaydi.
She watches TV. U televizor ko‘radi.
It works well. U yaxshi ishlaydi.

Daftarga yozing

I work.
You work.
We work.
They work.
He works.
She works.
It works.

📖 4. NEGATIVE SENTENCES

Negative sentence — inkor gap. Biror ish-harakat bajarilmasligini bildiradi.

Subject + do/does + not + Verb

Do not (don't)

I, You, We, They bilan ishlatiladi.

I do not drink coffee.
Men qahva ichmayman.
They don't work here.
Ular bu yerda ishlashmaydi.

Does not (doesn't)

He, She, It bilan ishlatiladi.

He does not play football.
U futbol o‘ynamaydi.
She doesn't like milk.
U sutni yoqtirmaydi.
MUHIM QOIDA!
Doesn't ishlatilganda asosiy fe'lga -s qo‘shilmaydi.

He doesn't plays. ❌
He doesn't play. ✅

Daftarga yozing

I don't work.
You don't work.
We don't work.
They don't work.
He doesn't work.
She doesn't work.
It doesn't work.

❓ 5. YES / NO QUESTIONS

Yes/No Questions — javobi Yes yoki No bilan boshlanadigan savollar.

Do / Does + Subject + Verb?

DO bilan savol

Do you speak English?
Siz inglizcha gapirasizmi?
Do they live here?
Ular shu yerda yashaydilarmi?
Do we need water?
Bizga suv kerakmi?

DOES bilan savol

Does he work here?
U shu yerda ishlaydimi?
Does she speak English?
U inglizcha gapiradimi?
YODDA TUTING:
Does + he/she/it + Verb (oddiy shakl)?
Does she likes tea? ❌
Does she like tea? ✅

Qisqa javoblar

Do you work? — Yes, I do.
Ishlaysizmi? — Ha.
Do you work? — No, I don't.
Ishlaysizmi? — Yo‘q.
Does he study? — Yes, he does.
Does she cook? — No, she doesn't.

Daftarga yozing

Do you speak English?
Does he work?
Does she study?
Do they live here?
Yes, I do.
No, I don't.
Yes, he does.
No, she doesn't.

❓ 6. AM / IS / ARE BILAN SAVOL

To be fe'li bilan savol tuzishda am, is yoki are gap boshiga chiqadi.

Am / Is / Are + Subject + ...?
Are you a student? Siz talabamisiz?
Is he your brother? U sizning akangizmi?
Is she happy? U xursandmi?
Are they at home? Ular uydami?
Am I late? Men kech qoldimmi?

Inkor shakli

Subject + am/is/are + not
I am not tired. Men charchamaganman.
He is not busy. U band emas.
They are not ready. Ular tayyor emas.
MUHIM FARQ:
Are you happy? ✅
Do you happy? ❌

Do you work? ✅
Are you work? ❌

Happy — sifat, shuning uchun to be kerak. Work — harakat fe'li, shuning uchun Present Simple savolida do kerak.

Daftarga yozing

Am I...?
Are you...?
Is he...?
Is she...?
Is it...?
Are we...?
Are they...?

❓ 7. WH-QUESTIONS

WH-Questions — ma'lumot olish uchun beriladigan savollar.

WhatNima?
WhereQayerda? Qayerga?
WhenQachon?
WhoKim?
WhyNima uchun?
WhichQaysi?
WhoseKimning?
HowQanday? Qanaqa?
How manyNechta?
How muchQancha?
How oldNecha yosh?
How oftenQanchalik tez-tez?

WH + DO / DOES

WH-word + do/does + Subject + Verb?
Where do you live?
Qayerda yashaysiz?
What do you eat?
Nima yeysiz?
When does he work?
U qachon ishlaydi?
Why does she study English?
U nima uchun ingliz tilini o‘rganadi?

WH + AM / IS / ARE

WH-word + am/is/are + Subject?
Where are you?
Siz qayerdasiz?
Who is he?
U kim?
Why are they happy?
Ular nima uchun xursand?

Daftarga yozing

What do you do?
Where do you live?
When do you study?
Why do you learn English?
Who is your teacher?
How old are you?
How often do you read?

🎭 10. DIALOG 1 — MEETING A NEW FRIEND

Yangi do‘st bilan tanishish

Ali: Hello! What is your name?
Salom! Ismingiz nima?
John: My name is John. What is your name?
Mening ismim John. Sizning ismingiz nima?
Ali: My name is Ali. Where are you from?
Mening ismim Ali. Siz qayerdansiz?
John: I am from England. Where do you live?
Men Angliyadanman. Siz qayerda yashaysiz?
Ali: I live in Uzbekistan. Do you speak Uzbek?
Men O‘zbekistonda yashayman. Siz o‘zbekcha gapirasizmi?
John: No, I don't. But I speak English.
Yo‘q. Lekin men inglizcha gapiraman.
Ali: Nice to meet you!
Tanishganimdan xursandman!
John: Nice to meet you too!
Men ham tanishganimdan xursandman!

🎭 11. DIALOG 2 — DAILY LIFE

Kundalik hayot haqida suhbat

Anna: What do you do every day?
Siz har kuni nima qilasiz?
Tom: I work in a shop. What about you?
Men do‘konda ishlayman. Siz-chi?
Anna: I am a student. When do you wake up?
Men talabaman. Siz qachon uyg‘onasiz?
Tom: I wake up at six o'clock.
Men soat oltida uyg‘onaman.
Anna: Do you study English?
Siz ingliz tilini o‘rganasizmi?
Tom: Yes, I do. I study every morning.
Ha. Men har kuni ertalab o‘qiyman.
Anna: Why do you learn English?
Nima uchun ingliz tilini o‘rganyapsiz?
Tom: Because I want to speak English well.
Chunki men ingliz tilida yaxshi gapirishni xohlayman.

🎧 12. LISTENING PRACTICE 1

Matnni avval tarjimasiz tinglang. Keyin yana tinglab, tushungan ma'lumotlaringizni tekshiring.

Listening: My Daily Routine
Hello! My name is David. I am twenty years old. I live in London. I wake up at seven o'clock. I eat breakfast and drink tea. I go to work at eight. I work in a small shop. I like my job. In the evening, I study English. I want to speak English very well.

Matn tarjimasi

Salom! Mening ismim David. Men yigirma yoshdaman. Men Londonda yashayman. Soat yettida uyg‘onaman. Nonushta qilaman va choy ichaman. Soat sakkizda ishga boraman. Men kichik do‘konda ishlayman. Ishimni yoqtiraman. Kechqurun ingliz tilini o‘rganaman. Men ingliz tilida juda yaxshi gapirishni xohlayman.

Listening savollari

🎧 13. LISTENING PRACTICE 2

Listening: A New Student
Sarah is a new student. She studies English every day. Her teacher is Mr Brown. She has two friends in her class. They speak English together. Sarah likes reading books. She also watches English videos. She wants to improve her English.

Tarjimasi

Sarah yangi o‘quvchi. U har kuni ingliz tilini o‘rganadi. Uning o‘qituvchisi Mr Brown. Uning sinfida ikkita do‘sti bor. Ular birgalikda inglizcha gaplashishadi. Sarah kitob o‘qishni yoqtiradi. Shuningdek, inglizcha videolar ko‘radi. U ingliz tilini yaxshilashni xohlaydi.
