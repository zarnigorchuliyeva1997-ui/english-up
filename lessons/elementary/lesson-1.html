📘 2. What is Present Simple?

Present Simple — hozirgi oddiy zamon. U asosan muntazam takrorlanadigan ishlar, kundalik odatlar, umumiy haqiqatlar va doimiy holatlar uchun ishlatiladi.

Asosiy ishlatilish holatlari

1. Daily routines — Kundalik ishlar

I wake up at 6 o'clock every day.

Men har kuni soat 6 da uyg'onaman.

2. Habits — Odatlar

She drinks tea every morning.

U har kuni ertalab choy ichadi.

3. General truths — Umumiy haqiqatlar

The sun rises in the east.

Quyosh sharqdan chiqadi.

4. Permanent situations — Doimiy holatlar

He lives in Uzbekistan.

U O'zbekistonda yashaydi.

5. Feelings and opinions — His-tuyg'ular va fikrlar

I like English.

Men ingliz tilini yoqtiraman.

📗 3. Positive Sentences

Darak gaplarda fe'l odatda o'zining asosiy shaklida ishlatiladi. Lekin He, She, It bilan fe'lga -s yoki boshqa mos qo'shimcha qo'shiladi.

Formula:
I / You / We / They + V1
He / She / It + V-s/es/ies

Misollar

Maxsus fe'l: HAVE

He / She / It bilan have → has bo'ladi.

She has a new phone.

Uning yangi telefoni bor.

📕 5. Negative Sentences

Inkor gaplarda do not yoki does not ishlatiladi.

Formula:
I / You / We / They + do not + V1
He / She / It + does not + V1
Full formShort form
do notdon't
does notdoesn't

I don't drink coffee.

Men qahva ichmayman.

She doesn't work on Sundays.

U yakshanba kunlari ishlamaydi.

They don't live here.

Ular bu yerda yashamaydi.

Eng muhim qoida!
Doesn't ishlatilganda asosiy fe'lga -s qo'shilmaydi.

❌ She doesn't likes tea.
✅ She doesn't like tea.

📘 6. Questions

So'roq gap tuzishda Do yoki Does gap boshiga chiqadi.

Formula:
Do + I / you / we / they + V1?
Does + he / she / it + V1?

Do you speak English?

Siz ingliz tilida gapirasizmi?

Does she work here?

U shu yerda ishlaydimi?

Short Answers — Qisqa javoblar

QuestionPositiveNegative
Do you work?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Does he study?Yes, he does.No, he doesn't.
Do they play?Yes, they do.No, they don't.
Eslab qoling:
Does she likes? ❌
Does she like? ✅

📙 8. Adverbs of Frequency

Bu ravishlar ish-harakat qanchalik tez-tez sodir bo'lishini bildiradi.

AdverbMa'nosiTaxminiy chastota
AlwaysHar doim100%
UsuallyOdatda80–90%
OftenTez-tez60–70%
SometimesBa'zan40–50%
RarelyKamdan-kam10–20%
Hardly everDeyarli hech qachon5–10%
NeverHech qachon0%

Gapda joylashishi

I always wake up early.

Men har doim erta uyg'onaman.

She usually walks to school.

U odatda maktabga piyoda boradi.

They never smoke.

Ular hech qachon chekmaydi.

To be bilan:
Frequency adverb odatda am/is/are dan keyin keladi.

He is always happy.
They are usually busy.

📕 9. Signal Words

Present Simple bilan ko'p ishlatiladigan vaqt iboralari:

  • Every day — Har kuni
  • Every morning — Har tong
  • Every week — Har hafta
  • Every month — Har oy
  • Once a week — Haftada bir marta
  • Twice a week — Haftada ikki marta
  • Three times a month — Oyda uch marta
  • On Mondays — Dushanba kunlari
  • At weekends — Dam olish kunlari
  • In the morning — Ertalab
  • At night — Tunda

I go to the gym three times a week.

Men haftada uch marta sport zaliga boraman.

📘 10. Present Simple: To Be vs Do/Does

Bu ikki tuzilmani adashtirmaslik juda muhim.

To BeAction verbs
I am happy.I work every day.
She is a teacher.She teaches English.
Are you tired?Do you work?
He isn't busy.He doesn't work.
Qoida:
Am / Is / Are bilan odatda Do / Does qo'shilmaydi.

❌ Do you are happy?
✅ Are you happy?

⚠️ 11. Common Mistakes

❌ He go to school.
✅ He goes to school.
❌ She don't like coffee.
✅ She doesn't like coffee.
❌ Does he works here?
✅ Does he work here?
❌ I am go to work every day.
✅ I go to work every day.
❌ She is always go to school.
✅ She always goes to school.

📖 13. Reading Practice

My Daily Routine

My name is Daniel. I live in a small town with my family. I usually wake up at six o'clock in the morning. I wash my face and have breakfast. I go to work at eight o'clock. I work in a shop and meet many people every day. I have lunch at one o'clock. After work, I usually come home and spend time with my family. In the evening, I study English for thirty minutes. I sometimes watch TV, but I never go to bed late. I usually sleep at eleven o'clock.

Tarjimasi

Mening ismim Daniel. Men oilam bilan kichik shaharchada yashayman. Men odatda ertalab soat oltida uyg'onaman. Yuzimni yuvaman va nonushta qilaman. Soat sakkizda ishga boraman. Men do'konda ishlayman va har kuni ko'plab insonlar bilan uchrashaman. Soat birda tushlik qilaman. Ishdan keyin odatda uyga qaytaman va oilam bilan vaqt o'tkazaman. Kechqurun o'ttiz daqiqa ingliz tilini o'rganaman. Ba'zan televizor ko'raman, lekin hech qachon kech uxlamayman. Odatda soat o'n birda uxlayman.
