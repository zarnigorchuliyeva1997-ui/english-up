Present Continuous

Revision & Practice

Hozirgi davomiy zamon: ayni paytda sodir bo‘layotgan va vaqtinchalik davom etayotgan harakatlar.

Bugungi maqsad: Present Continuous zamonida darak, inkor va so‘roq gaplarni to‘g‘ri tuzish, savollarga javob berish va kundalik suhbatda erkin foydalanish.

Dars jarayoni: 0%

1. Salomlashuv va darsga kirish

Hello, everyone! Welcome back to English Up!

Bugun biz Elementary bosqichidagi muhim mavzulardan birini takrorlaymiz va chuqur o‘rganamiz: Present Continuous.

Tasavvur qiling, siz do‘stingizga telefon qildingiz. U hozir nima qilayotganini so‘ramoqchisiz.

What are you doing? — Nima qilyapsan?
I am studying English. — Men ingliz tilini o‘rganyapman.

What is your mother doing? — Onangiz nima qilyapti?
She is cooking dinner. — U kechki ovqat tayyorlayapti.

Are they playing football? — Ular futbol o‘ynashyaptimi?
Yes, they are. — Ha.

Bu gaplarda harakat ayni paytda sodir bo‘lyapti. Ingliz tilida bunday harakatlarni ifodalash uchun Present Continuous ishlatiladi.

DAFTARGA YOZING: Present Continuous — hozir sodir bo‘layotgan yoki hozirgi davrda vaqtincha davom etayotgan harakatni ifodalovchi zamon.

2. Present Continuous nima?

Present — hozirgi, Continuous — davom etayotgan degan ma’noni anglatadi.

PRESENT CONTINUOUS = HOZIR DAVOM ETAYOTGAN HARAKAT

Bu zamon orqali biz biror ish ayni gapirilayotgan vaqtda sodir bo‘layotganini yoki hozirgi davrda vaqtincha davom etayotganini bildiramiz.

1. Ayni hozir sodir bo‘layotgan harakat

I am reading a book now. — Men hozir kitob o‘qiyapman.
She is sleeping. — U uxlayapti.
They are watching TV. — Ular televizor ko‘rishyapti.
The baby is crying. — Chaqaloq yig‘layapti.

2. Hozirgi davrda vaqtincha davom etayotgan ish

Harakat aynan shu soniyada sodir bo‘lishi shart emas. U hozirgi davrda davom etayotgan bo‘lishi mumkin.

I am learning English this month. — Men bu oy ingliz tilini o‘rganyapman.
She is working in Moscow this year. — U bu yil Moskvada ishlayapti.
We are staying with our friends this week. — Biz bu hafta do‘stlarimiznikida turibmiz.

3. O‘zgarib borayotgan vaziyatlar

The weather is getting colder. — Havo sovib boryapti.
Your English is improving. — Ingliz tilingiz yaxshilanib boryapti.
The city is growing. — Shahar kengayib boryapti.

4. Oldindan kelishilgan yaqin kelajak rejalari

I am meeting my friend tomorrow. — Ertaga do‘stim bilan uchrashyapman.
We are visiting our grandparents on Sunday. — Yakshanba kuni buvi-buvamiznikiga boryapmiz.
DAFTARGA YOZING: Present Continuous quyidagi vaziyatlarda ishlatiladi: 1. Ayni hozir sodir bo‘layotgan harakat. 2. Hozirgi davrdagi vaqtinchalik harakat. 3. O‘zgarib borayotgan vaziyat. 4. Kelishilgan yaqin kelajak rejasi.

4. Am / Is / Are — yordamchi fe'llar

Present Continuous zamonining eng muhim qismi — am, is, are. Bu zamonda oddiy fe’lning o‘zi yetarli emas. Bizga yordamchi fe’l ham kerak.

AM / IS / ARE + V-ING
EgaYordamchi fe’lMisol
IamI am working.
HeisHe is working.
SheisShe is working.
ItisIt is working.
YouareYou are working.
WeareWe are working.
TheyareThey are working.

Nega uch xil yordamchi fe'l bor?

Ingliz tilida Present Continuous zamonida yordamchi fe'l ega bilan moslashadi:

  • I bilan faqat am.
  • He, She, It bilan is.
  • You, We, They bilan are.

Bu qoida barcha oddiy ega so‘zlariga ham tegishli:

My father is working. — Dadam ishlayapti.
My parents are cooking. — Ota-onam ovqat tayyorlashyapti.
The dog is sleeping. — It uxlayapti.
The children are playing. — Bolalar o‘ynashyapti.
Eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato:
I is working. ❌
She are reading. ❌
They is playing. ❌

I am working. ✅
She is reading. ✅
They are playing. ✅
DAFTARGA YOZING: I am; He/She/It is; You/We/They are. Har biriga o‘zingizdan bittadan misol yozing.

5. Positive — Darak gap

Darak gap orqali biror shaxs yoki narsa hozir nima qilayotganini aytamiz.

S + AM / IS / ARE + V-ING + O

Formula qismlari

  • S: Subject — ishni bajaruvchi.
  • Am/Is/Are: Ega bilan moslashadigan yordamchi fe’l.
  • V-ing: Asosiy fe’l + ing.
  • O: To‘ldiruvchi, agar gapda kerak bo‘lsa.

Misollar

I am drinking water. — Men suv ichyapman.
You are learning English. — Sen ingliz tilini o‘rganyapsan.
He is playing football. — U futbol o‘ynayapti.
She is washing the dishes. — U idishlarni yuvyapti.
We are watching a film. — Biz film ko‘ryapmiz.
They are cleaning the room. — Ular xonani tozalashyapti.

Qisqartma shakllar — Contractions

To‘liq shaklQisqartma

I'm reading. = I am reading.
She's cooking. = She is cooking.
They're playing. = They are playing.
DAFTARGA YOZING: Positive formulasini va 7 ta qisqartma shaklni yozing. Keyin o‘zingiz haqingizda 5 ta darak gap tuzing.

6. Negative — Inkor gap

He is sleeping.He is not sleeping.U uxlamayapti.
They are playing.They are not playing.Ular o‘ynamayapti.

Qisqartma shakllar

To‘liqQisqartma

E'tibor bering:

DAFTARGA YOZING: Inkor formulasini, qisqartmalarni va yuqoridagi noto‘g‘ri/to‘g‘ri misollarni yozing. So‘ng 5 ta darak gapni inkor gapga aylantiring.

7. Question — So‘roq gap

Biror kishidan hozir nima qilayotganini so‘rash uchun am/is/are gapning boshiga chiqadi.

AM / IS / ARE + S + V-ING + O?

Qanday tuziladi?

Is she cooking? — U ovqat tayyorlayaptimi?

Are they playing? — Ular o‘ynashyaptimi?

WH-questions orqali faqat “ha yoki yo‘q” emas, balki harakat haqida batafsil ma'lumot olamiz.

Savol va javoblar

What are you doing? — Nima qilyapsan?
I am doing my homework. — Uy vazifamni bajaryapman.

WHO haqida muhim qoida

DAFTARGA YOZING: 7 ta WH-so‘zni tarjimasi bilan yozing. Har biri bilan bittadan savol va javob tuzing.

9. -ING qo‘shish qoidalari

Present Continuous da asosiy fe'lga odatda -ing qo‘shiladi. Ammo ayrim fe'llarda imlo o‘zgaradi.

1-qoida: Ko‘pchilik fe'llarga -ing qo‘shiladi

work → working
read → reading
play → playing
watch → watching
eat → eating
cook → cooking

2-qoida: Oxirida -e bo‘lsa, e tushib qoladi

Fe'l oxiridagi talaffuz qilinmaydigan e odatda tushib, -ing qo‘shiladi.

make → making
write → writing
dance → dancing
come → coming
take → taking
drive → driving
Lekin barcha -e bilan tugaydigan fe'llar bir xil emas. Masalan, see → seeing, be → being.

3-qoida: Qisqa unli + bitta undosh bilan tugasa, oxirgi undosh ikki marta yoziladi

run → running
sit → sitting
swim → swimming
get → getting
stop → stopping
put → putting

Bu qoida odatda bitta qisqa unli tovushdan keyin bitta undosh bilan tugaydigan qisqa fe'llarda qo‘llanadi.

4-qoida: -ie bilan tugasa, -ie o‘rniga y keladi

lie → lying
die → dying
tie → tying

5-qoida: Oxirida -y bo‘lsa, y saqlanadi

study → studying
cry → crying
play → playing
enjoy → enjoying
DAFTARGA YOZING: Barcha 5 ta qoidani va misollarni yozing. Keyin quyidagi fe'llarning -ing shaklini mustaqil yozing: sit, dance, study, swim, write, lie, get, make, play, run.

12. Stative Verbs — Odatda Continuous da ishlatilmaydigan fe'llar

Bu juda muhim qoida. Ingliz tilidagi barcha fe'llarni -ing shaklida ishlatib bo‘lmaydi.

Ba'zi fe'llar harakatni emas, balki fikr, his, egalik, sezgi yoki holatni bildiradi. Ular odatda Present Continuous da emas, Present Simple da ishlatiladi.

Fe'lMa'nosiTo‘g‘ri misol
KnowBilmoqI know the answer.
UnderstandTushunmoqI understand you.
BelieveIshonmoqShe believes me.
WantXohlamoqI want some water.
NeedMuhtoj bo‘lmoqWe need help.
LikeYoqtirmoqHe likes music.
LoveSevmoqI love my family.
HateYomon ko‘rmoqShe hates noise.
RememberEslamoqI remember him.
OwnEgalik qilmoqHe owns a house.

Noto‘g‘ri va to‘g‘ri shakllar

I am knowing the answer. ❌
I know the answer. ✅

She is wanting a new phone. ❌
She wants a new phone. ✅

They are understanding English. ❌
They understand English. ✅

Bu fe'llar hech qachon -ing bo‘lmaydimi?

Yo‘q. Ayrim fe'llar ma'nosiga qarab Continuous shaklida ham ishlatilishi mumkin.

I think you are right. — Menimcha, siz haqsiz. (Fikr)
I am thinking about my future. — Men kelajagim haqida o‘ylayapman. (Jarayon)

This soup tastes good. — Bu sho‘rvaning ta'mi yaxshi. (Xususiyat)
The chef is tasting the soup. — Oshpaz sho‘rvaning ta'mini tatib ko‘ryapti. (Harakat)

Think fikr bildirish ma'nosida odatda Simple, o‘ylash jarayonida Continuous bo‘ladi. Have egalik ma'nosida Simple, ovqatlanish yoki vaqt o‘tkazish kabi faoliyatlarda Continuous bo‘lishi mumkin.

DAFTARGA YOZING: Jadvaldagi 10 ta stative verbni tarjimasi bilan yozing. 3 ta noto‘g‘ri va to‘g‘ri misolni ko‘chiring.

13. Present Continuous bilan bog‘liq boshqa muhim holatlar

1. Bir nechta harakat bir vaqtda davom etishi

My mother is cooking while my father is watching TV.
Onam ovqat tayyorlayapti, shu paytda dadam televizor ko‘ryapti.

2. Vaqtinchalik ish va doimiy ish farqi

He works in a bank. — U bankda ishlaydi. (Doimiy ish)
He is working in a shop this month. — U bu oy do‘konda ishlayapti. (Vaqtinchalik)

3. Takroriy harakat va always

Present Continuous bilan always ishlatilsa, ba'zan takroriy va bezovta qiluvchi odatni ifodalaydi.

You are always losing your keys! — Sen kalitlaringni doim yo‘qotib yuribsan!
He is always talking in class. — U darsda doim gapirib o‘tiradi!

4. Kelajakdagi kelishilgan reja

I am meeting my teacher tomorrow. — Ertaga o‘qituvchim bilan uchrashyapman.
We are travelling next week. — Keyingi hafta sayohatga ketyapmiz.

5. Buyruq va undov bilan kuzatilgan harakat

Look! The children are dancing. — Qara! Bolalar raqsga tushishyapti.
Listen! The birds are singing. — Eshit! Qushlar sayrayapti.
DAFTARGA YOZING: Yuqoridagi 5 holatning har biriga bittadan o‘zingizning misolingizni yozing.

16. Reading — A Busy Day at Home

Matnni avval mustaqil o‘qing. Keyin audio orqali tinglang va savollarga javob bering.

A Busy Day at Home

It is Saturday morning. My family is spending time at home. My father is reading a newspaper in the living room. My mother is making breakfast in the kitchen. My younger brother is playing with his toys, and my sister is drawing a beautiful picture. I am sitting near the window and studying English.

Our dog is sleeping under the table. The birds are singing outside, and the sun is shining. My mother is calling us because breakfast is ready. We are not watching television today. We are enjoying our time together.

23. Dars yakuni — What Have We Learned?

  • Present Continuous hozirgi davomiy harakatni ifodalaydi.
  • Positive: S + am/is/are + V-ing.
  • Negative: S + am/is/are + not + V-ing.
  • Question: Am/Is/Are + S + V-ing?
  • WH-question: WH-word + am/is/are + S + V-ing?
  • I bilan am ishlatiladi.
  • He/She/It bilan is ishlatiladi.
  • You/We/They bilan are ishlatiladi.
  • Fe'lga -ing qo‘shish qoidalari mavjud.
  • Stative verbs odatda Continuous da ishlatilmaydi.
  • Present Simple odatlarni, Present Continuous esa hozirgi va vaqtinchalik harakatlarni ifodalaydi.
