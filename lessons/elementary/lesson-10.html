🏡 HOMEWORK — UYGA VAZIFA

Bugungi mavzuni mustahkamlash uchun quyidagi to‘rtta topshiriqni bajaring.

1. GRAMMAR — GRAMMATIKA

  1. IN, ON va AT predloglari yordamida 15 ta gap yozing.
  2. UNDER, ABOVE, BELOW, BETWEEN va BEHIND yordamida 10 ta gap tuzing.
  3. TO, FROM, INTO, OUT OF va THROUGH yordamida 5 ta gap yozing.
  4. Har bir gapning o‘zbekcha tarjimasini yozing.

2. WRITING — YOZISH

Mavzu: My Home and My Daily Routine

Uy va kundalik tartibingiz haqida 12–15 ta inglizcha gap yozing.

Matnda quyidagilardan foydalaning:

  • Kamida 5 ta vaqt predlogi.
  • Kamida 7 ta joy predlogi.
  • Kamida 3 ta harakat predlogi.

Namuna: I wake up at 6 o'clock. My phone is on the table.

3. READING — O‘QISH VA TUSHUNISH

My Daily Life

I wake up at 6 o'clock in the morning. I have breakfast in the kitchen. My phone is on the table and my keys are in my bag. I go to work at 8 o'clock. I work from Monday to Saturday. At lunchtime, I have a meal with my friends. In the evening, I come home and sit next to my family. My children are usually in their rooms. We have dinner at 7 o'clock. At night, I read a book before I go to bed.

Savollar:

  1. What time does the person wake up?
  2. Where does the person have breakfast?
  3. Where are the phone and keys?
  4. What days does the person work?
  5. What does the person do at night?

4. SPEAKING — GAPIRISH

  1. O‘zingizning bir kunlik tartibingizni ingliz tilida aytib bering.
  2. Xonangizdagi 10 ta narsaning qayerda joylashganini tushuntiring.
  3. Uyingiz va maktabingiz yoki ish joyingiz orasidagi joylashuvni tasvirlang.
  4. IN, ON, AT ishlatilgan 10 ta gapni ovoz chiqarib o‘qing.

Speaking namunasi:

I wake up at 7 o'clock. My clothes are in the wardrobe. My books are on the desk. My school is near my house. I go to school in the morning.

📌 Eslatma: Homeworkni shunchaki o‘qib chiqmang. Gaplarni o‘zingiz yozing, ovoz chiqarib ayting va predloglarning nima sababdan ishlatilganini tushuntirishga harakat qiling.
