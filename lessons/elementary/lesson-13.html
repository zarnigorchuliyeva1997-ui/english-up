ELEMENTARY COURSE · LESSON 13

GERUNDS AND INFINITIVES

Verb + -ing / to + Verb

Ingliz tilida fe'llardan keyin boshqa fe'l qanday shaklda keladi?

Enjoy doing? Want to do? Like doing? Like to do?

📚 Grammar · ✍️ Notebook · 🗣 Speaking · 🎧 Listening · 📝 Test

1. Bugungi dars maqsadi

Bugun ingliz tilidagi juda muhim grammatik mavzuni o‘rganamiz. Ba'zi fe'llardan keyin ikkinchi fe'l -ing shaklida, ba'zilaridan keyin esa to + verb shaklida keladi.

Dars yakunida siz:

  • Gerund nima ekanini tushunasiz.
  • Infinitive nima ekanini tushunasiz.
  • Fe'lga -ing qo‘shish qoidalarini bilasiz.
  • Enjoy, finish, avoid, suggest kabi fe'llardan keyin -ing ishlatasiz.
  • Want, need, decide, hope kabi fe'llardan keyin to + verb ishlatasiz.
  • Like, love, hate, prefer bilan ikkala shaklni ham ishlatishni o‘rganasiz.
  • Prepositionlardan keyin nima uchun -ing kelishini tushunasiz.
  • Gerund va infinitive bilan gaplar tuzasiz.
  • Eng ko‘p uchraydigan xatolardan qochasiz.
Daftar qoidasi: Har bir yangi qoida, formula, jadval va misol daftarga yoziladi. Har bir bo‘limdagi mustaqil mashqlar yozma ravishda bajariladi.

2. Avval FE'LLAR haqida tushunib olaylik

3. GERUND nima?

Gerund — fe'lga -ing qo‘shish orqali hosil bo‘ladigan shakl. U gapda ko‘pincha harakat nomi sifatida ishlatiladi.

5. INFINITIVE nima?

Infinitive — fe'lning boshlang‘ich shakli oldidan to qo‘yilgan shakli.

TO + VERB = TO-INFINITIVE

Infinitive gapda qanday ishlatiladi?

I want to learn English.
Men ingliz tilini o‘rganishni xohlayman.

She decided to study.
U o‘qishga qaror qildi.
We hope to travel next year.

6. GERUND va INFINITIVE — asosiy farq

Gerund va infinitive ikkalasi ham fe'lning boshqa shaklini bildiradi. Lekin ulardan qaysi biri ishlatilishi ko‘pincha oldingi fe'lga bog‘liq.

GerundInfinitive
enjoy readingwant to read
finish workingdecide to work
avoid drivinghope to drive
suggest goingplan to go

Quyidagi ikkita gapni taqqoslang:

I enjoy reading books.
Men kitob o‘qishdan zavqlanaman.
I want to read this book.
Men bu kitobni o‘qishni xohlayman.

Enjoy dan keyin reading, want dan keyin to read kelgan. Bu fe'llarning o‘z grammatik talabi bor.

Gerund yoki infinitive tanlashda faqat o‘zbekcha tarjimaga qarab qaror qilmang. Ingliz tilida oldingi fe'lning qaysi shaklni talab qilishini o‘rganish kerak.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Gerund va infinitive taqqoslash jadvalini yozing. Har bir ustunga o‘zingizdan 3 tadan misol qo‘shing.

ENJOY + ING

Enjoy — biror ishni qilishdan zavqlanish.

I enjoy learning English.
Men ingliz tilini o‘rganishdan zavqlanaman.
She enjoys cooking.
U ovqat pishirishdan zavqlanadi.

FINISH + ING

Finish — biror ishni tugatish.

I finished doing my homework.
Men uy vazifamni bajarishni tugatdim.
He finished reading the book.
U kitobni o‘qishni tugatdi.

AVOID + ING

Avoid — biror ishni qilishdan qochish yoki undan saqlanish.

You should avoid eating too much sugar.
Juda ko‘p shakar iste'mol qilishdan saqlanishingiz kerak.
He avoids driving at night.
U tunda mashina haydashdan qochadi.

MIND + ING

Mind — biror ishga qarshi bo‘lish yoki noqulaylik sezish.

Do you mind opening the window?
Derazani ochishga qarshi emasmisiz?
I don't mind waiting.
Kutishga qarshi emasman.

SUGGEST + ING

Suggest — biror ishni taklif qilmoq.

I suggest going by bus.
Avtobusda borishni taklif qilaman.
She suggested studying together.
U birga o‘qishni taklif qildi.

KEEP + ING

Keep — harakatni davom ettirish.

Keep practising English.
Ingliz tilini mashq qilishda davom et.
He keeps asking questions.
U savol berishda davom etadi.

PRACTISE + ING

I practise speaking every day.
Men har kuni gapirishni mashq qilaman.

MISS + ING

I miss playing football with my friends.
Do‘stlarim bilan futbol o‘ynashni sog‘inaman.

9. LIKE, LOVE, HATE, PREFER — ikkala shakl

Bu bo‘lim juda muhim. Chunki ayrim fe'llardan keyin ham gerund, ham infinitive ishlatish mumkin.

Asosiy fe'llar:

  • like — yoqtirmoq
  • love — juda yoqtirmoq
  • hate — yomon ko‘rmoq
  • prefer — afzal ko‘rmoq

LIKE + ING

I like reading books.
Men kitob o‘qishni yoqtiraman.

LIKE + TO + VERB

I like to read before sleeping.
Men uxlashdan oldin o‘qishni yoqtiraman.

Ko‘p vaziyatlarda ikkala shakl ham grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri. Ma'no juda yaqin bo‘lishi mumkin.

LOVE

She loves dancing.
U raqsga tushishni juda yaxshi ko‘radi.
She loves to dance at parties.
U bazmlarda raqsga tushishni juda yaxshi ko‘radi.

HATE

I hate waiting.
Men kutishni yomon ko‘raman.
I hate to wait for a long time.
Men uzoq vaqt kutishni yomon ko‘raman.

PREFER

I prefer walking.
Men piyoda yurishni afzal ko‘raman.
I prefer to walk in the morning.
Men ertalab piyoda yurishni afzal ko‘raman.

Elementary daraja uchun asosiy qoida: Like, love, hate va prefer fe'llaridan keyin ko‘pincha -ing yoki to + verb ishlatish mumkin. Ba'zi kontekstlarda ma'no yoki urg‘u farqi paydo bo‘ladi.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Like, love, hate va prefer fe'llarining ikkala shaklini jadvalga yozing. Har bir fe'l bilan ikkita gap tuzing.

10. PREPOSITION + GERUND

Bu qoidani yaxshilab tushunish kerak: ingliz tilida predlogdan keyin fe'l kelsa, odatda uning -ing shakli ishlatiladi.

PREPOSITION + VERB-ING

Preposition — in, on, at, about, before, after, without, for kabi so‘zlar.

ABOUT + ING

I am thinking about moving to another city.
Boshqa shaharga ko‘chish haqida o‘ylayapman.

BEFORE + ING

Wash your hands before eating.
Ovqatlanishdan oldin qo‘llaringizni yuving.

AFTER + ING

After finishing work, I went home.
Ishni tugatgandan keyin uyga bordim.

WITHOUT + ING

He left without saying goodbye.
U xayrlashmasdan ketdi.

IN + ING

She is interested in learning English.
U ingliz tilini o‘rganishga qiziqadi.

GOOD AT + ING

He is good at playing football.
U futbol o‘ynashni yaxshi biladi.

THANK YOU FOR + ING

Thank you for helping me.
Menga yordam berganingiz uchun rahmat.
Ko‘p uchraydigan xato:
❌ Thank you for help me.
✅ Thank you for helping me.

❌ Before eat, wash your hands.
✅ Before eating, wash your hands.
Yana bir muhim holat: “to” har doim infinitive belgisi emas. Ba'zan u predlog bo‘ladi. Masalan, “look forward to” birikmasidan keyin gerund keladi: I look forward to meeting you.
I look forward to seeing you.
Siz bilan ko‘rishishni intiqlik bilan kutaman.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Preposition + Gerund qoidasini yozing. About, before, after, without, in, at, for va look forward to bilan misollarni ko‘chiring.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING Fe'lni -ing shaklida yozing:
  1. Before _____, wash your hands. (eat)
  2. Thank you for _____ me. (help)
  3. He left without _____ goodbye. (say)
  4. She is interested in _____ English. (learn)
  5. I am good at _____. (cook)
  6. After _____, we went home. (work)
  7. I look forward to _____ you. (meet)

11. PURPOSE — maqsadni bildirish uchun TO

To + verb faqat want yoki need dan keyin emas, biror ishning maqsadini bildirish uchun ham ishlatiladi.

To + verb ko‘pincha “... qilish uchun” ma'nosini beradi.

I went to the shop to buy milk.
Sut sotib olish uchun do‘konga bordim.
She studies hard to pass the exam.
U imtihondan o‘tish uchun qattiq o‘qiydi.
I use my phone to learn English.
Ingliz tilini o‘rganish uchun telefonimdan foydalanaman.
He went outside to get some fresh air.
Toza havo olish uchun tashqariga chiqdi.
TO + VERB = ... QILISH UCHUN

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Purpose qoidasini yozing va yuqoridagi misollarni tarjimasi bilan ko‘chiring.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING Quyidagi gaplarni ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:
  1. Men ingliz tilini o‘rganish uchun kitob sotib oldim.
  2. U pul topish uchun ishlaydi.
  3. Biz do‘stlarimizni ko‘rish uchun bordik.
  4. U imtihondan o‘tish uchun o‘qiydi.
  5. Men suv sotib olish uchun do‘konga bordim.

12. BARE INFINITIVE — TO siz fe'l

Ba'zi vaziyatlarda fe'l oldidan to qo‘yilmaydi. Bunday shakl bare infinitive deb ataladi.

BARE INFINITIVE = VERB (TO YO‘Q)

1. Modal fe'llardan keyin

Can, could, must, should, may, might kabi modal fe'llardan keyin asosiy fe'lning oddiy shakli keladi.

I can swim.
Men suza olaman.
You should study.
Siz o‘qishingiz kerak.
We must go now.
Biz hozir ketishimiz shart.
❌ I can to swim.
✅ I can swim.

❌ You should to study.
✅ You should study.

2. LET + OBJECT + VERB

Let — ruxsat bermoq yoki imkon bermoq.

Let me help you.
Sizga yordam berishimga ruxsat bering.
My parents let me go out.
Ota-onam tashqariga chiqishimga ruxsat berishadi.

3. MAKE + OBJECT + VERB

Make — kimnidir biror ishni qilishga majbur qilmoq yoki sabab bo‘lmoq.

The teacher made us repeat the sentence.
O‘qituvchi bizga gapni takrorlatdi.
Make fe'li majhul nisbatda ishlatilganda “to” qaytadi: We were made to wait. Bu keyingi bosqichda chuqurroq o‘rganiladi.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Bare infinitive ta'rifini yozing. Modal fe'llar, let va make bilan misollarni ko‘chiring.

13. GERUND va INFINITIVE — bir xil fe'l, boshqa ma'no

Ba'zi fe'llardan keyin gerund ham, infinitive ham kelishi mumkin. Lekin ma'no o‘zgaradi. Bu bo‘limga alohida e'tibor bering.

1. STOP

Stop + ING — biror ishni bajarishni to‘xtatmoq.

He stopped smoking.
U chekishni tashladi.

Stop + TO + VERB — boshqa ishni bajarish uchun to‘xtamoq.

He stopped to drink water.
U suv ichish uchun to‘xtadi.

2. REMEMBER

Remember + ING — o'tmishda sodir bo‘lgan ishni eslamoq.

I remember meeting him.
U bilan uchrashganimni eslayman.

Remember + TO + VERB — biror ishni qilishni unutmaslik, eslab qolmoq.

Remember to call your mother.
Onangizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishni unutmang.

3. FORGET

Forget + ING — o'tmishda sodir bo‘lgan ishni eslay olmaslik yoki unutmoq.

I'll never forget meeting you.
Siz bilan uchrashganimni hech qachon unutmayman.

Forget + TO + VERB — bajarilishi kerak bo‘lgan ishni unutmoq.

I forgot to buy bread.
Non sotib olishni unutdim.

4. TRY

Try + ING — muammoni hal qilish uchun bir usulni sinab ko‘rmoq.

Try drinking some water.
Biroz suv ichib ko‘ring.

Try + TO + VERB — biror ishni bajarishga urinmoq, harakat qilmoq.

I tried to open the door.
Eshikni ochishga harakat qildim.
Fe'lING shakliTO shakli
stopishni to‘xtatishbir ish uchun to‘xtash
remembero‘tgan ishni eslashqilishni eslab qolish
forgeto‘tgan ishni unutishqilish kerakligini unutish
tryusulni sinashurinib ko‘rish
Bu ma'no farqlari muhim, lekin hozircha ularni yodlab, misollar orqali tushunib oling. Har bir fe'lning ikki shaklini bir-biriga almashtirib yubormang.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Stop, remember, forget va try fe'llarining ikki shaklini alohida jadvalga yozing. Har biriga misol va tarjima yozing.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING Qavsdagi ma'noga mos shaklni tanlang:
  1. He stopped _____ (chekishni tashladi). (smoke)
  2. She stopped _____ (suv ichish uchun). (drink)
  3. Remember _____ your homework. (bajarishni unutmang) (do)
  4. I remember _____ him last year. (uchrashganimni eslayman) (meet)
  5. I forgot _____ the door. (eshikni qulflashni unutdim) (lock)
  6. Try _____ the computer again. (boshqa usulni sinab ko‘ring) (restart)

14. GERUND — gap boshida va boshqa vazifalarda

Gerund faqat enjoy yoki finish dan keyin kelmaydi. U gapning boshida ham, predloglardan keyin ham ishlatiladi.

1. Gapning egasi sifatida

Reading improves your vocabulary.
O‘qish lug‘at boyligingizni oshiradi.
Walking is good for your health.
Piyoda yurish sog‘liq uchun foydali.

2. Predlogdan keyin

She is interested in learning languages.
U tillarni o‘rganishga qiziqadi.

3. Ayrim birikmalardan keyin

It's worth visiting this place.
Bu joyga borishga arziydi.
I have difficulty understanding him.
Uni tushunishda qiynalaman.
There is no point in arguing.
Bahslashishdan foyda yo‘q.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Uchta vazifani yozing: gap egasi, predlogdan keyin, birikmalardan keyin. Har biriga misol ko‘chiring.

15. GERUND va INFINITIVE bilan inkor gaplar

Gerund va infinitive inkor shaklida ham ishlatilishi mumkin.

NOT + GERUND

NOT + VERB-ING
He admitted not telling the truth.
U haqiqatni aytmaganini tan oldi.
Not studying regularly is a problem.
Muntazam o‘qimaslik muammo.

NOT + TO-INFINITIVE

NOT + TO + VERB
I decided not to go.
Men bormaslikka qaror qildim.
She promised not to be late.
U kechikmaslikka va'da berdi.
Inkor so‘zi not tegishli shakldan oldin keladi: not doing yoki not to do.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Not + gerund va not + infinitive formulalarini yozing. Misollarni ko‘chiring.

20. Yakuniy grammatik xulosa

GERUND = VERB + ING
INFINITIVE = TO + VERB
BARE INFINITIVE = VERB (TO YO‘Q)

Gerund ishlatiladigan asosiy fe'llar:

enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, suggest, keep, practise, consider, miss, admit.

Infinitive ishlatiladigan asosiy fe'llar:

want, need, decide, hope, plan, promise, agree, offer, expect, choose.

Ikkala shakl ham ishlatiladigan fe'llar:

like, love, hate, prefer.

Predlogdan keyin:

before eating, after working, without saying, interested in learning.

Modal fe'llardan keyin:

can swim, should study, must go.

Mavzuning eng asosiy siri: ikkinchi fe'lning shaklini ko‘pincha birinchi fe'l belgilaydi. Shuning uchun fe'llarni alohida emas, balki birikma sifatida yodlang: enjoy doing, want to do, decide to do.

LEARNING ENGLISH

My name is Alex. I enjoy learning English because it helps me communicate with people. I practise speaking every day. I also like reading short stories and listening to English podcasts.

I want to improve my vocabulary. I decided to learn ten new words every day. I need to practise using these words in sentences. Sometimes I make mistakes, but I don't stop learning.

My teacher suggested watching English videos. I hope to speak English confidently in the future. I know that learning a language takes time, so I plan to study regularly.

Savollarga ingliz tilida javob yozing:
  1. What does Alex enjoy doing?
  2. How often does he practise speaking?
  3. What does he want to improve?
  4. How many new words does he plan to learn every day?
  5. What did his teacher suggest?
  6. What does Alex hope to do in the future?
  7. Does he stop learning when he makes mistakes?

Matndan 5 ta gerund va 5 ta infinitive topib, alohida yozing.
