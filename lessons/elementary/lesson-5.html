2. BE GOING TO nima?

Be Going To — ingliz tilida kelajakda amalga oshirishni rejalashtirgan ishlarimiz, niyatlarimiz yoki hozirgi vaziyatga qarab kelajakda sodir bo'lishi kutilayotgan hodisalar haqida gapirish uchun ishlatiladi.

O'zbek tiliga ko'pincha quyidagicha tarjima qilinadi:

  • ... qilmoqchiman
  • ... qilmoqchi
  • ... qilish niyatidaman
  • ... bo'lishi kutilmoqda
Eng muhim fikr: Be Going To kelajakdagi reja yoki niyatni bildirishi mumkin. Shuningdek, hozir ko'rinib turgan dalilga asoslanib kelajak haqida taxmin qilishda ham ishlatiladi.

2.1. Oldindan rejalashtirilgan ishlar

Biror ishni bajarishga avvaldan qaror qilgan bo'lsak, Be Going To ishlatamiz.

I am going to study English tonight.
Men bugun kechqurun ingliz tilini o'rganmoqchiman.
She is going to visit her grandmother.
U buvisini ko'rishga bormoqchi.
We are going to buy a new table.
Biz yangi stol sotib olmoqchimiz.

Bu misollarda odamlar kelajakdagi ish haqida avvaldan reja qilgan. Shuning uchun Be Going To ishlatilgan.

2.2. Niyat va maqsad

Biror ishni qilish niyatimiz borligini aytishda ham ishlatiladi.

I am going to learn Spanish.
Men ispan tilini o'rganmoqchiman.
He is going to become a doctor.
U shifokor bo'lmoqchi.
They are going to start a business.
Ular biznes boshlamoqchi.

2.3. Hozirgi dalilga asoslangan bashorat

Biror hodisaning sodir bo'lishiga hozirgi vaziyatdan dalil ko'rayotgan bo'lsak, Be Going To ishlatamiz.

Look at those dark clouds! It is going to rain.
Ana u qora bulutlarga qara! Yomg'ir yog'ishi kutilmoqda.
Be careful! You are going to fall.
Ehtiyot bo'l! Yiqilib tushishing mumkin.
The glass is at the edge of the table. It is going to fall.
Stakan stol chetida turibdi. U tushib ketishi mumkin.
Eslab qoling: bu yerda shunchaki kelajak haqida fikr bildirilmayapti. Gapiruvchi hozirgi vaziyatda kelajakdagi hodisaga ishora qiluvchi dalilni ko'rmoqda.

4. BE GOING TO va WILL — keng qamrovli taqqoslash

Ingliz tilida Will ham, Be Going To ham kelajak haqida gapirishda ishlatiladi. Lekin ularning qo'llanish holatlari bir xil emas. Ikkalasini ham shunchaki “kelasi zamon” deb yodlab olish yetarli emas. Qaysi vaziyatda qaysi birini ishlatish kerakligini tushunish muhim.

Asosiy qoida:
Be Going To — avvaldan mavjud bo'lgan reja yoki niyat, shuningdek hozirgi dalilga asoslangan bashorat.

Will — gapirish paytida qabul qilingan qaror, va'da, taklif, yordam berish yoki shaxsiy fikrga asoslangan bashorat kabi holatlar.

4.1. Birinchi asosiy farq: oldindan reja va hozirgi qaror

BE GOING TO

Oldindan rejalashtirilgan

Gapirishdan oldin reja yoki niyat mavjud bo'lgan.

I am going to buy a new phone.

Men yangi telefon sotib olmoqchiman. Bu haqda oldindan qaror qilganman.

WILL

Hozir qabul qilingan qaror

Qaror aynan gapirish vaqtida qabul qilingan.

I'll buy a new phone.

Yangi telefon sotib olaman. Qarorni hozir qabul qilyapman.

Vaziyat: Do'stingiz telefoningiz ishlamayotganini ko'rib, yangi telefon olishingiz kerakligini aytdi.

A: Your phone is very old.
A: Telefoning juda eski.
B: Yes, I'm going to buy a new one next week.
B: Ha, keyingi hafta yangisini sotib olmoqchiman.

Bu yerda oldindan reja bor. Shuning uchun Be Going To ishlatilgan.

A: I can't carry these bags.
A: Bu sumkalarni ko'tara olmayapman.
B: I'll help you.
B: Men senga yordam beraman.

Bu yerda yordam berish qarori gapirish vaqtida qabul qilindi. Shuning uchun Will ishlatilgan.

4.2. Ikkinchi farq: dalilga asoslangan bashorat va shaxsiy fikr

BE GOING TO

Ko'rinib turgan dalil

Hozirgi vaziyatda kelajakdagi hodisaga ishora qiluvchi belgi bor.

Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.

Bulutlarga qara! Yomg'ir yog'ishi kutilmoqda.

WILL

Fikr yoki taxmin

Gapiruvchi o'z fikri, ishonchi yoki taxminini aytadi.

I think it will rain tomorrow.

Menimcha, ertaga yomg'ir yog'adi.

Farqni tushunish uchun quyidagi ikki holatni solishtiring:

Look at that boy! He's going to fall.
Ana u bolaga qara! U yiqilib tushishi mumkin.

Gapiruvchi hozirgi vaziyatni ko'rib turibdi va shu asosda taxmin qilyapti.

I think he will become a good teacher.
Menimcha, u yaxshi o'qituvchi bo'ladi.

Bu gap shaxsiy fikr yoki ishonchni bildiradi.

4.3. WILL qachon ishlatiladi?

1. Gapirish paytida qabul qilingan qaror:

The phone is ringing. I'll answer it.
Telefon jiringlayapti. Men javob beraman.

2. Va'da berish:

I will never forget your help.
Men yordamingizni hech qachon unutmayman.

3. Taklif qilish:

I'll carry your bag.
Sumkangizni men ko'tarib beraman.

4. Yordam berishga tayyorlik:

Don't worry. I will help you.
Xavotir olmang. Men sizga yordam beraman.

5. Shaxsiy fikr va taxmin:

I think our team will win.
Menimcha, jamoamiz g'alaba qozonadi.

6. Kelajak haqidagi umumiy taxmin:

People will use more technology in the future.
Kelajakda odamlar ko'proq texnologiyadan foydalanadi.

4.4. BE GOING TO qachon ishlatiladi?

1. Oldindan tuzilgan reja:

4.7. WILL bilan muhim grammatik farq

Be Going To dan oldin am/is/are ishlatiladi. Will dan oldin esa alohida am/is/are kerak emas.

She will study.
U o'qiydi.
Noto'g'ri: She will is study.
To'g'ri: She will study.

Noto'g'ri: She going to study.
To'g'ri: She is going to study.

4.10. Qaysi birini tanlayman?

8. Reading — My Plans for the Future

Matnni avval mustaqil o'qing. Keyin audio orqali tinglang va mazmunini tushunishga harakat qiling.

My Plans for the Future

My name is Daniel. I am a student, and I have many plans for the future. I am going to improve my English because I want to speak with people from different countries. I am going to study every day and learn new words.

Next month, I am going to join an English course. I am also going to read more books and watch educational videos. My sister is going to help me with my homework.

At the weekend, we are going to visit our grandparents. We are going to spend time together and have dinner with them. I think the future will be exciting. I will work hard and try to achieve my goals.

Reading comprehension

  1. What is Daniel going to improve?
  2. Why is he going to learn English?
  3. When is he going to join an English course?
  4. Who is going to help him?
  5. What are they going to do at the weekend?

9. Listening — A Weekend Plan

Avval matnni tinglang. Keyin sekin talaffuzda tinglang. Tinglayotganingizda Be Going To va Will shakllariga e'tibor bering.

Listening script

Anna is talking to her friend Mark about the weekend.

Anna: What are you going to do this weekend?

Mark: I'm going to visit my uncle on Saturday.

Anna: That's nice. Are you going to stay there all day?

Mark: No, I'm not. I'm going to come home in the evening.

Anna: I'm going to study on Saturday. I have an English test next week.

Mark: Don't worry. You'll do well. I think you will pass the test.

Anna: Thank you! I'll do my best.

Listening questions

  1. Who is Mark going to visit?
  2. When is he going to visit his uncle?
  3. What is Anna going to do on Saturday?
  4. What does Anna have next week?
  5. What does Mark think about Anna's test?

10. Speaking — Talk about your plans

Quyidagi savollarga ovoz chiqarib, to'liq gaplar bilan javob bering. Har bir savolga kamida 2–3 ta gap aytishga harakat qiling.

  1. What are you going to do tomorrow?
  2. What are you going to study next week?
  3. Are you going to learn another language?
  4. Where are you going to travel in the future?
  5. What are you going to do this weekend?
  6. Are you going to read a book tonight?
  7. What are you going to do to improve your English?
  8. What do you think will happen in your future?
Speaking mashqi: javoblaringizni telefoningizdagi ovoz yozish funksiyasi orqali yozib oling. Keyin qayta tinglab, grammatik xatolar va talaffuz ustida ishlang.

12. HOMEWORK — Uyga vazifa

Quyidagi 5 ta vazifani bajaring. Yozma topshiriqlarni daftaringizga yozing.

1-topshiriq — Grammar

Be Going To zamonining barcha formulalarini daftaringizga yozing. Har bir shaklga 5 tadan yangi gap tuzing:

  • Positive — 5 ta gap.
  • Negative — 5 ta gap.
  • Question — 5 ta gap.
  • WH Question — 5 ta gap.

2-topshiriq — Will vs Be Going To

Will va Be Going To orasidagi farqni tushuntirib yozing. Keyin har biriga 5 tadan misol tuzing. Misollaringizda oldindan reja, hozirgi qaror, va'da va dalilga asoslangan bashorat qatnashsin.

3-topshiriq — Vocabulary

40 ta yangi so'zni yodlang. Har bir so'z bilan bittadan yangi gap tuzing. Jami 40 ta gap yozing.

4-topshiriq — English to Uzbek

Quyidagi matnni o'zbek tiliga tarjima qiling:

My family has many plans for next month. My father is going to paint the house, and my mother is going to buy some new furniture. My brother is going to help our father. I am going to study English every evening. We are going to visit our grandparents next Sunday. I think we will have a wonderful time together.

5-topshiriq — Uzbek to English

Quyidagi gaplarni ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:

  1. Men ertaga ingliz tilini o'rganmoqchiman.
  2. U keyingi hafta yangi kitob sotib olmoqchi.
  3. Biz bugun kechqurun televizor ko'rmoqchi emasmiz.
  4. Sen dam olish kunlari qayerga bormoqchisan?
  5. Ular yangi uy qurmoqchi.
  6. Men senga yordam beraman. (Hozir qabul qilingan qaror)
  7. Qara! Yomg'ir yog'ishi mumkin.
  8. Menimcha, u imtihondan o'tadi.
MUHIM: Uyga vazifani bajarganingizdan keyin quyidagi 30 savollik testni ham saytda ishlang. Test javoblarini faqat daftarga yozish yetarli emas — javoblarni saytga kiriting va tekshiring.

13. FINAL TEST — 30 QUESTIONS
