ENGLISH UP
ELEMENTARY COURSE · LESSON 12

THERE IS / THERE ARE
THERE WAS / THERE WERE

Biror joyda nimadir bor yoki mavjud bo‘lishi haqida gapirish.

Bugungi darsda hozirgi va o‘tgan zamonda birlik, ko‘plik, inkor va so‘roq gaplarni o‘rganamiz.

📚 Daftar · ✍️ Yozish · 🗣 Talaffuz · 🎧 Tinglash · 📝 Test

1. Dars maqsadi

Ushbu dars yakunida siz quyidagilarni mustaqil bajara olishingiz kerak:

  • There is va There are farqini tushunish.
  • Birlik va ko‘plikdagi otlarni to‘g‘ri ishlatish.
  • There isn't va There aren't bilan inkor gap tuzish.
  • Is there...? va Are there...? savollarini berish.
  • Some va any so‘zlarini to‘g‘ri ishlatish.
  • There was va There were orqali o‘tmish haqida gapirish.
  • Was there...? va Were there...? savollarini tuzish.
  • There is va It is o‘rtasidagi farqni bilish.
  • Uy, xona, maktab, shahar va boshqa joylarni ingliz tilida tasvirlash.
MUHIM: Bu darsda faqat o‘qib chiqish yetarli emas. Har bir qoida, formula va misol daftarga yoziladi. Mashqlar daftarda bajariladi.

2. Daftar bilan ishlash — majburiy qoida

Darsni boshlashdan oldin daftaringizni tayyorlang. Har bir bo‘limda yozish va mustaqil ishlash topshirig‘i bor.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

1. Sanani yozing.
2. Mavzu nomini yozing: THERE IS / THERE ARE — THERE WAS / THERE WERE.
3. Dars maqsadlarini yozing.
4. Har bir yangi grammatik formulani ko‘chiring.
5. Har bir qoidadan kamida 3 ta misol yozing.
Qat'iy talab: Har bir bo‘limdagi yozma topshiriq bajarilgandan keyingina keyingi bo‘limga o‘ting. Javoblarni shunchaki ko‘z bilan o‘qib chiqish mustaqil bajarish hisoblanmaydi.

3. THERE IS nima?

There is ingliz tilida biror joyda bitta narsa borligini yoki mavjudligini bildirish uchun ishlatiladi.

O‘zbek tiliga odatda bor, mavjud deb tarjima qilinadi.

THERE IS + birlikdagi ot

Qachon ishlatamiz?

  • Bitta odam yoki narsa haqida gapirganda.
  • Biror joyda bitta buyum borligini aytganda.
  • Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan.
  • Sanalmaydigan ot bilan ham.

Misollar

There is a book on the table.
Stol ustida bitta kitob bor.
Talaffuz: Zer iz e buk on ze teybl.
There is a teacher in the classroom.
Sinf xonasida bitta o‘qituvchi bor.
Talaffuz: Zer iz e tiicher in ze klasrum.
There is a cat under the chair.
Stul tagida mushuk bor.
Talaffuz: Zer iz e ket ander ze cheyr.
There is some water in the bottle.
Shishada biroz suv bor.
Talaffuz: Zer iz sam voter in ze botl.
Eslab qoling: There is faqat bitta sanaladigan narsa uchun emas, water, milk, sugar kabi sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ham ishlatiladi.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Formulani yozing: There is + singular noun / uncountable noun.
Yuqoridagi 4 ta misolni inglizchasi, tarjimasi va talaffuzi bilan ko‘chiring.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

There is bilan 5 ta yangi gap tuzing:

1. Xonada bitta stol bor.
2. Oshxonada sut bor.
3. Bog‘da bitta daraxt bor.
4. Sumkada bitta ruchka bor.
5. Stakan ichida suv bor.

4. THERE ARE nima?

There are biror joyda ikki yoki undan ortiq narsa borligini bildirish uchun ishlatiladi.

THERE ARE + ko‘plikdagi ot

Ingliz tilida ko‘plikdagi otlar ko‘pincha -s yoki -es bilan tugaydi. Lekin ayrim otlarning ko‘plik shakli o‘zgaradi.

Misollar

There are two books on the table.
Stol ustida ikkita kitob bor.
Talaffuz: Zer ar tu buks on ze teybl.
There are three chairs in the room.
Xonada uchta stul bor.
Talaffuz: Zer ar sri cheyrz in ze rum.
There are many students at school.
Maktabda ko‘plab o‘quvchilar bor.
Talaffuz: Zer ar meni styudents et skul.
There are five apples in the basket.
Savatda beshta olma bor.
Talaffuz: Zer ar fayv eplz in ze basket.
Diqqat! There are dan keyin odatda ko‘plikdagi sanaladigan ot keladi: books, chairs, students, apples.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

There are + plural noun formulasini yozing. 4 ta misolni ko‘chiring.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING There are bilan quyidagi gaplarni ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:
  1. Xonada to‘rtta deraza bor.
  2. Stolda ikkita telefon bor.
  3. Maktabda ko‘p o‘quvchilar bor.
  4. Oshxonada uchta stul bor.
  5. Sumkada beshta kitob bor.

Ko‘plik yasash qoidalari

1. Ko‘pchilik otlarga -s qo‘shiladi:

book → books; pen → pens; table → tables.

2. -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z bilan tugagan otlarga ko‘pincha -es qo‘shiladi:

bus → buses; box → boxes; watch → watches.

3. Undosh + y bilan tugagan otlarda y → ies:

baby → babies; city → cities.

4. Unli + y bo‘lsa, faqat -s qo‘shiladi:

boy → boys; toy → toys.

5. Ayrim otlar butunlay o‘zgaradi:

child → children; man → men; woman → women.

Bu qoidalar otning ko‘plik shaklini yasash uchun kerak. There are ishlatganda otning to‘g‘ri ko‘plik shaklini yozing.

7. THERE IS / THERE ARE — inkor shakli

Biror joyda narsa yo‘qligini aytmoqchi bo‘lsak, inkor shaklidan foydalanamiz.

There is not = There isn't

THERE IS NOT + birlikdagi ot

There is not shaklining qisqartmasi — There isn't.

There isn't a TV in my bedroom.
Mening yotoqxonamda televizor yo‘q.
There isn't a bank near my house.
Uyim yaqinida bank yo‘q.

There are not = There aren't

THERE ARE NOT + ko‘plikdagi ot
There aren't any books on the desk.
Parta ustida hech qanday kitob yo‘q.
There aren't any students in the classroom.
Sinf xonasida hech qanday o‘quvchi yo‘q.
Oddiy shaklInkor shakli
There is a book.There isn't a book.
There are books.There aren't books.
There is some water.There isn't any water.
Eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato:
❌ There aren't a book.
✅ There isn't a book.

❌ There isn't two chairs.
✅ There aren't two chairs.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

There isn't va There aren't formulalarini, qisqartmalarini va misollarini yozing.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING Quyidagi gaplarni inkor shakliga aylantiring:
  1. There is a computer in the room.
  2. There are five pens in the bag.
  3. There is some milk in the fridge.
  4. There are two cars outside.
  5. There is a park near my house.

8. THERE IS / THERE ARE — so‘roq shakli

Biror joyda narsa bor yoki yo‘qligini so‘rash uchun is yoki are gap boshiga chiqadi.

Is there...? — birlik

IS + THERE + birlikdagi ot + ...?
Is there a bank near here?
Shu yaqin atrofda bank bormi?
Is there a bathroom in this house?
Bu uyda hammom bormi?

Are there...? — ko‘plik

ARE + THERE + ko‘plikdagi ot + ...?
Are there any shops near your house?
Uyingiz yaqinida do‘konlar bormi?
Are there any students in the classroom?
Sinf xonasida o‘quvchilar bormi?

Qisqa javoblar

SavolHaYo‘q
Is there a book?Yes, there is.No, there isn't.
Are there books?Yes, there are.No, there aren't.
Muhim: Qisqa javobda “Yes, there is a book” deyish shart emas. Oddiy qisqa javob: “Yes, there is.”
❌ Yes, there are not.
✅ No, there aren't.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Is there va Are there formulalarini yozing. Jadvalni daftarga ko‘chiring.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING Quyidagi gaplarni so‘roq shakliga aylantiring:
  1. There is a school near my house.
  2. There are three windows in the room.
  3. There is a computer on the desk.
  4. There are some shops in this street.
  5. There is some water in the glass.
Har bir savolga Yes va No shaklida qisqa javob yozing.

9. SOME va ANY

Some va any miqdorni aniq aytmasdan “bir oz”, “bir nechta”, “qandaydir” ma'nolarini beradi.

SOME — asosan darak gaplarda

There is va There are bilan darak gaplarda ko‘p ishlatiladi.

There are some apples in the basket.
Savatda bir nechta olma bor.
There is some juice in the glass.
Stakanda biroz sharbat bor.

ANY — asosan inkor va so‘roq gaplarda

There aren't any eggs in the fridge.
Muzlatkichda hech qanday tuxum yo‘q.
Are there any children in the park?
Bog‘da bolalar bormi?
There isn't any sugar in the tea.
Choyda shakar yo‘q.
Gap turiKo‘p ishlatiladigan so‘zMisol
DaraksomeThere are some books.

So‘roqanyAre there any books?
Eslatma: Some so‘roq gaplarda ham taklif yoki iltimosda ishlatilishi mumkin. Hozircha asosiy qoida sifatida darak gapda some, inkor va oddiy so‘roq gapda any ni o‘rganing.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Some va any qoidalarini jadval bilan yozing. Har biriga 3 tadan misol ko‘chiring.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING Bo‘sh joyga some yoki any yozing:

  2. There isn't _____ milk in the fridge.
  3. Are there _____ students here?
  4. There is _____ bread on the table.
  5. There aren't _____ chairs in the room.

10. SANALADIGAN VA SANALMAYDIGAN OTLAR

There is va There are ni tanlashda otning sanaladigan yoki sanalmaydigan ekaniga ham e'tibor berish kerak.

Sanaladigan otlar — Countable nouns

Bularni dona-dona sanash mumkin: one apple, two apples, three books.

  • one book — bitta kitob
  • two books — ikkita kitob
  • one chair — bitta stul
  • three chairs — uchta stul

Sanalmaydigan otlar — Uncountable nouns

Bularni odatda dona-dona sanamaymiz. Masalan:

  • water — suv
  • milk — sut
  • rice — guruch
  • sugar — shakar
  • bread — non
  • money — pul
  • information — ma'lumot
  • furniture — mebel
THERE IS + UNCOUNTABLE NOUN
There is some water in the bottle.
Shishada biroz suv bor.
There is some rice in the kitchen.
Oshxonada biroz guruch bor.
Juda muhim:
❌ There are some water.
✅ There is some water.

Water sanalmaydigan ot bo‘lgani uchun There is ishlatiladi.

Sanalmaydigan narsani idish yoki birlik bilan sanash

Masalan, water so‘zini bevosita sanamaymiz, lekin bottle yoki glass ni sanashimiz mumkin.

There are two bottles of water.
Ikkita shisha suv bor.
There are three glasses of milk.
Uchta stakan sut bor.
There is a bottle of water.
Bitta shisha suv bor.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar ro‘yxatini ikki ustunda yozing. There is bilan 3 ta sanalmaydigan otli gap tuzing.

11. A / AN bilan ishlatilishi

Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot oldidan ko‘pincha a yoki an ishlatiladi.

  • a — undosh tovush bilan boshlanadigan so‘zlardan oldin: a book, a chair, a teacher.
  • an — unli tovush bilan boshlanadigan so‘zlardan oldin: an apple, an egg, an orange.
There is a book on the desk.
Parta ustida bitta kitob bor.
There is an apple in the bag.
Sumkada bitta olma bor.
A/an faqat birlikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan ishlatiladi.
❌ There is a books.
✅ There are books.
❌ There are an apple.
✅ There is an apple.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

A va an qoidalarini, misollarni va xatolarni daftarga yozing.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING To‘g‘ri variantni tanlab yozing:
  1. There is (a / an) orange.
  2. There is (a / an) chair.
  3. There are (a / an) books.
  4. There is (a / an) egg.
  5. There is (a / an) computer.

13. THERE WAS — o‘tgan zamon, birlik

Endi biror narsa hozir emas, balki o'tmishda bor bo‘lganini aytishni o‘rganamiz.

There was — o'tgan zamondagi birlik yoki sanalmaydigan ot bilan ishlatiladi.

THERE WAS + birlikdagi ot / sanalmaydigan ot

Tarjimasi: bor edi, mavjud edi.

There was a book on the table yesterday.
Kecha stol ustida kitob bor edi.
There was a big tree in the garden.
Bog‘da katta daraxt bor edi.
There was some milk in the fridge.
Muzlatkichda biroz sut bor edi.
There was a teacher in the classroom.
Sinf xonasida o‘qituvchi bor edi.

There was ko‘pincha quyidagi vaqt so‘zlari bilan keladi:

  • yesterday — kecha
  • last week — o‘tgan hafta
  • last year — o‘tgan yil
  • two days ago — ikki kun oldin
  • in 2020 — 2020-yilda

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

There was formulasini yozing. Yuqoridagi 4 ta misolni tarjimasi bilan ko‘chiring.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING There was yordamida 5 ta gap tuzing. Quyidagi so‘zlardan foydalaning: book, tree, teacher, water, computer.

14. THERE WERE — o‘tgan zamon, ko‘plik

There were o'tmishda ikki yoki undan ortiq narsa mavjud bo‘lganini bildiradi.

THERE WERE + ko‘plikdagi ot
There were five students in the classroom yesterday.
Kecha sinfxonada beshta o‘quvchi bor edi.
There were many cars in the street.
Ko‘chada ko‘p mashinalar bor edi.
There were two cats in the garden.
Bog‘da ikkita mushuk bor edi.
There were some apples on the table.
Stolda bir nechta olma bor edi.
Hozirgi zamonO‘tgan zamon
There is a book.There was a book.
There are books.There were books.
There is some water.There was some water.

Yodlash uchun:
IS → WAS
ARE → WERE
There is/are hozirgi zamon, There was/were o'tgan zamon.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

There were formulasini va misollarni yozing. Hozirgi va o'tgan zamon jadvalini ko‘chiring.
📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING Quyidagi gaplarni o'tgan zamonga aylantiring:
  1. There are two cars outside.
  2. There is a dog in the garden.
  3. There are many students at school.
  4. There is some milk in the fridge.
  5. There are three books on the table.

18. THERE IS va IT IS — farqi

Bu ikki ibora ko‘pincha chalkashtiriladi. Ularning vazifasi boshqa-boshqa.

There is a book on the table.

Yana bir misol:

20. Kundalik dialoglar

Dialog 1 — Uy haqida

1-TOPSHIRIQ — GRAMMAR

Quyidagi topshiriqlarni daftarda bajaring.

A. To‘g‘ri shaklni tanlang:
  1. There (is / are) a book on the table.
  2. There (is / are) five students in the room.
  3. There (was / were) a tree in the garden.
  4. There (was / were) many people at the market.
  5. There (isn't / aren't) any milk.
  6. There (wasn't / weren't) any chairs.
B. Inkor shakliga aylantiring:
  1. There is a computer in the room.
  2. There are three windows.
  3. There was a shop here.
  4. There were many children.
C. So‘roq shakliga aylantiring:

  3. There was a teacher.

2-TOPSHIRIQ — WRITING

My House and My Town mavzusida kamida 15 ta inglizcha gap yozing.

Talablar:

  • Kamida 5 ta There is / There are gaplari.
  • Kamida 3 ta inkor gap.
  • Kamida 3 ta so‘roq gap.
  • Kamida 4 ta There was / There were gaplari.
  • Kamida 5 ta joy predlogidan foydalaning.

Har bir gapning o‘zbekcha tarjimasini ham yozing.

3-TOPSHIRIQ — READING

Matnni kamida 3 marta o‘qing. Keyin daftarga ko‘chirib, tarjima qiling.

MY OLD HOUSE

There is a small garden near my house. There are three trees in the garden. There is a big table in the kitchen. There are four chairs around the table. There is a small shop next to my house.

There was a beautiful park near my old house. There were many children in the park. There was a small lake behind the park. There weren't many cars in that street.

Now there are more shops and houses in my town. There is also a new school near the market.

Savollarga ingliz tilida javob yozing:

  1. Is there a garden near the house?
  2. How many trees are there in the garden?
  3. Where is the shop?
  4. Was there a park near the old house?
  5. Were there many children in the park?
  6. Are there more shops in the town now?

4-TOPSHIRIQ — SPEAKING

Quyidagi mavzularda ovoz chiqarib gapiring. Har bir mavzuda kamida 1 daqiqa gapirishga harakat qiling.

  1. Describe your room.
  2. Describe your house.
  3. Describe your school or workplace.
  4. Talk about your town in the past.
  5. Talk about what there is and isn't in your neighbourhood.

Gapirishda There is, There are, There was, There were, isn't, aren't, wasn't va weren't shakllaridan foydalaning.
