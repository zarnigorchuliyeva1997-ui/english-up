1. PRONOUNS — OLMOSHLAR NIMA?

Pronoun — otning o‘rnida ishlatiladigan so‘z. O‘zbek tilida ham, ingliz tilida ham olmoshlar mavjud.

Olmosh nima uchun kerak?

Biror odam yoki narsaning nomini qayta-qayta aytmaslik uchun uning o‘rniga olmosh ishlatamiz.

Ali is a student. Ali is 15 years old. Ali studies English.
Ali o‘quvchi. Ali 15 yoshda. Ali ingliz tilini o‘rganadi.
Bu gaplarda Ali ismi uch marta takrorlangan.
Ali is a student. He is 15 years old. He studies English.
Ali o‘quvchi. U 15 yoshda. U ingliz tilini o‘rganadi.
He so‘zi Ali ismining o‘rnida ishlatildi. Gap tabiiyroq bo‘ldi.

3. SUBJECT PRONOUNS QANDAY ISHLATILADI?

1-qoida: Odam ismini olmosh bilan almashtirish

Madina is a doctor. She works in a hospital.

Madina shifokor. U kasalxonada ishlaydi.

Tom and Jack are friends. They play football.

Tom va Jack do‘stlar. Ular futbol o‘ynaydi.

2-qoida: Bir nechta odam haqida gapirganda

  • I + you = We (agar o‘zimiz ham guruh ichida bo‘lsak)
  • He + she = They
  • Ali + I = We
  • My parents = They

3-qoida: It qachon ishlatiladi?

It odatda narsa, buyum, hayvon, ob-havo, vaqt va masofani ifodalashda ishlatiladi.

It is cold today.

Bugun sovuq.

It is five o'clock.

Soat besh.

It is a big dog.

Bu katta it.

5. SUBJECT VA OBJECT PRONOUNS FARQI

Bu ikki guruhni chalkashtirmaslik juda muhim. Ularning asosiy farqi — gapdagi vazifasi.

Subject = harakatni bajaruvchi.
Object = harakat qaratilgan shaxs yoki narsa.

I see him.

Men uni ko‘ryapman.

I — ko‘rayotgan odam. Him — ko‘rilayotgan odam.

She helps us.

U bizga yordam beradi.

She — yordam beruvchi. Us — yordam olayotganlar.

They know her.

Ular uni taniydi.

They — ish-harakat egasi. Her — ish-harakat obyekti.

Taqqoslang

SubjectObject
I like him.He likes me.
She knows us.We know her.
They help me.I help them.
We visit them.They visit us.

Gap tuzilishi

Subject + Verb + Object

I + love + you.

She + likes + him.

They + help + us.

Noto‘g‘ri: Me like English.
To‘g‘ri: I like English.

Noto‘g‘ri: She helps I.
To‘g‘ri: She helps me.

7. POSSESSIVE ADJECTIVES QOIDALARI

1-qoida: Egalik sifati + ot

Possessive Adjective dan keyin ot keladi.

my book — mening kitobim

your phone — sening telefoning

his car — uning mashinasi

our school — bizning maktabimiz

2-qoida: My, his, her so‘zlari odamning jinsiga yoki egasiga qarab tanlanadi

His va her narsa yoki buyumning jinsiga emas, egasiga qarab tanlanadi.

John has a car. His car is new.

Johnning mashinasi bor. Uning mashinasi yangi.

Mary has a cat. Her cat is white.

Maryning mushugi bor. Uning mushugi oq.

3-qoida: Its — hayvon yoki narsaga tegishli egalik

The bird is in its cage.

Qush o‘z qafasida.

The company changed its name.

Kompaniya o‘z nomini o‘zgartirdi.

4-qoida: Possessive Adjective oldidan artikl qo‘yilmaydi

To‘g‘ri: My book, her house, their car.
Noto‘g‘ri: The my book, a her house, the their car.

Chunki my, her, their so‘zlari allaqachon qaysi narsa haqida gap ketayotganini aniqlab beradi.

5-qoida: Birlik va ko‘plik otlar bilan

My friend is here.

Mening do‘stim shu yerda.

My friends are here.

Mening do‘stlarim shu yerda.

Egalik sifati otning birlik yoki ko‘plik ekaniga qarab o‘zgarmaydi.

10. APOSTROPHE S — 'S EGALIK SHAKLI

Ingliz tilida egalikni faqat my, your, his kabi so‘zlar bilan emas, balki otga 's qo‘shish orqali ham ifodalash mumkin.

1-qoida: Birlikdagi odam yoki hayvon nomiga 's qo‘shiladi

Ali's book

Alining kitobi

Mary's phone

Maryning telefoni

The dog's food

Itning ovqati

2-qoida: Ismga 's qo‘shiladi

My brother's room is clean.

Akamning xonasi toza.

Sarah's mother is a teacher.

Sarahning onasi o‘qituvchi.

3-qoida: Ko‘plikdagi ot -s bilan tugasa, faqat apostrof qo‘yiladi

The students' books

O‘quvchilarning kitoblari.

My parents' house

Ota-onamning uyi.

4-qoida: Ko‘plik shakli -s bilan tugamasa, 's qo‘shiladi

Children's toys

Bolalarning o‘yinchoqlari.

Men's clothes

Erkaklarning kiyimlari.

Women's bags

Ayollarning sumkalari.

5-qoida: Ikki kishiga bitta umumiy narsa tegishli bo‘lsa

Ali and Vali's house

Ali va Valining umumiy uyi.

6-qoida: Har birining alohida narsasi bo‘lsa

Ali's and Vali's houses

Alining va Valining alohida uylari.

Taqqoslang:
Ali's book = Alining kitobi.
The students' books = O‘quvchilarning kitoblari.

13. BY MYSELF VA MYSELF FARQI

Bu ikki shakl o‘xshash ko‘rinsa ham, gapdagi ma'nosi farq qiladi.

Myself — o‘zimni / o‘zim

Harakatning o‘zimizga qaratilganini yoki ishni shaxsan bajarganimizni bildirishi mumkin.

I introduced myself.

Men o‘zimni tanishtirdim.

I painted the room myself.

Xonani o‘zim bo‘yadim.

By myself — yolg‘iz / hech kimning yordamisiz

I live by myself.

Men yolg‘iz yashayman.

She went to school by herself.

U maktabga yolg‘iz bordi.

He fixed the computer by himself.

U kompyuterni hech kimning yordamisiz tuzatdi.

Yodda saqlang:
myself — o‘zim / o‘zimni.
by myself — yolg‘iz / o‘zim mustaqil ravishda.

14. ITS VA IT'S — MUHIM FARQ

Bu ikki so‘z talaffuzda bir xil, ammo yozilishi va grammatik vazifasi boshqa.

ITS — egalik

Its — it olmoshining egalik shakli. Ma'nosi: uning (narsa yoki hayvonga tegishli).

The cat is eating its food.

Mushuk o‘z ovqatini yeyapti.

The company changed its logo.

Kompaniya o‘z logotipini o‘zgartirdi.

IT'S — IT IS yoki IT HAS qisqartmasi

It's cold today.

Bugun sovuq.

It's = It is.

It's a beautiful day.

Bugun chiroyli kun.

It's = It is.

It's been a long day.

Uzoq kun bo‘ldi.

It's = It has.

Tez tekshirish usuli:
Agar it's o‘rniga it is yoki it has qo‘yish mumkin bo‘lsa, apostrofli shakl to‘g‘ri bo‘lishi mumkin. Egalikni bildirsa, its yoziladi.
Its tail is long. — Uning dumi uzun.
It's a long tail. — Bu uzun dum.

15. OLMOSHLAR BILAN SAVOL TUZISH

Olmoshlar oddiy gaplarda ham, savollarda ham ishlatiladi. Savol tuzishda gapning fe'liga va zamoniga e'tibor berish kerak.

Am / Is / Are + Subject + ...?

Are you a student?

Is she your sister?

U sening opangmi/singlingmi?

Are they your friends?

Ular sening do‘stlaringmi?

Do you know him?

U unga yordam beradimi?

Do they visit us?

Ular biznikiga kelishadimi?

Who bilan

Who is your teacher?

O‘qituvching kim?

Who helps you?

Senga kim yordam beradi?

Whose bilan

Whose phone is this?

Bu kimning telefoni?

Whose keys are these?

Bular kimning kalitlari?

16. KUNDALIK HAYOTDA OLMOSHLAR

Endi barcha olmoshlarni oddiy hayotiy vaziyatlarda qo‘llaymiz.

Vaziyat 1: Oila haqida

This is my family. My father is a driver. He is kind. My mother is a teacher. She is very patient. I love them.

Bu mening oilam. Otam haydovchi. U mehribon. Onam o‘qituvchi. U juda sabrli. Men ularni yaxshi ko‘raman.

Vaziyat 2: Do‘stlar haqida

I have a good friend. His name is Daniel. He often visits me. I help him with English, and he helps me with maths.

Mening yaxshi do‘stim bor. Uning ismi Daniel. U tez-tez meni ko‘rgani keladi. Men unga ingliz tilidan yordam beraman, u esa menga matematikadan yordam beradi.

Vaziyat 3: Buyumlar haqida

This is my phone. It is new. That phone is yours. The black phone is his, and the white one is hers.

Bu mening telefonim. U yangi. Ana u telefon seniki. Qora telefon uniki, oq telefon esa uniki.

Vaziyat 4: O‘zingizni tanishtirish

My name is Alex. I am a student. I live with my family. My parents are kind. They always help me. I study English by myself every day.

Mening ismim Alex. Men o‘quvchiman. Oilam bilan yashayman. Ota-onam mehribon. Ular doim menga yordam berishadi. Men har kuni ingliz tilini mustaqil o‘rganaman.

Vaziyat 5: Kichik dialog

A: Is this your book?

Bu sening kitobingmi?

B: No, it isn't mine. It's hers.

Yo‘q, bu meniki emas. Bu uniki.

A: Whose bag is that?

U kimning sumkasi?

B: It's my brother's bag.

Bu akamning sumkasi.

17. ENG KO‘P UCHRAYDIGAN XATOLAR

1. I va Me

❌ Me am a student.
✅ I am a student.

I — gap egasi. Me — to‘ldiruvchi.

2. He va Him

❌ I like he.
✅ I like him.

Like fe'lidan keyin bu gapda object pronoun kerak.

3. She va Her

❌ Her is my sister.
✅ She is my sister.

Her bu yerda ega bo‘la olmaydi. Ega shakli — she.

4. My va Mine

❌ This is mine book.
✅ This is my book.
✅ This book is mine.

5. Their va Theirs

❌ This is theirs house.
✅ This is their house.
✅ This house is theirs.

6. His va He's

❌ He's car is new.
✅ His car is new.

He's = He is yoki He has. His = uning.

7. Its va It's

❌ The dog eats it's food.
✅ The dog eats its food.

8. Our va Ours

❌ This is ours house.
✅ This is our house.
✅ This house is ours.

9. Them va They

❌ Them are my friends.
✅ They are my friends.

10. Myself va Me

❌ Please help myself.
✅ Please help me.

Help fe'lidan keyin oddiy object pronoun ishlatiladi. Myself faqat ma'no o‘ziga qaytganda yoki ta'kidlashda kerak bo‘ladi.

5 ta asosiy qoida

  1. Subject Pronouns — harakatni bajaruvchi: I, you, he, she, it, we, they.
  2. Object Pronouns — harakat qaratilgan shaxs yoki narsa: me, you, him, her, it, us, them.
  3. Possessive Adjectives — otdan oldin keladi: my book, her phone.
  4. Possessive Pronouns — otning o‘rnida ishlaydi: mine, hers, ours.
  5. Reflexive Pronouns — harakatning o‘ziga qaytishini bildiradi: myself, yourself, themselves.
O‘zingizni tekshiring:

1. Men uni taniyman. → I know him/her.

2. Bu mening kitobim. → This is my book.

3. Bu kitob meniki. → This book is mine.

4. U o‘zini tanishtirdi. → He/She introduced himself/herself.

5. Bu kimning telefoni? → Whose phone is this?

Oilangiz va do‘stlaringiz haqida ingliz tilida 12–15 ta gap yozing.

Quyidagi so‘zlardan foydalaning:

I, he, she, we, they, me, him, her, us, my, his, her, our, their, mine, yours.

Yozuvingizda quyidagilar bo‘lishi kerak:

  • Oilangiz a'zolarini tanishtiring.
  • Ularning kasbi yoki mashg‘uloti haqida yozing.
  • Do‘stlaringiz haqida 2–3 ta gap yozing.
  • Kimningdir narsasi haqida gapiring.
  • Kamida ikkita Reflexive Pronoun ishlating.

Namuna boshlanishi:

My name is _____. I live with my family. My father is _____. He is _____. My mother is _____. She is _____. I love my family because they always help me.

Ismim _____. Men oilam bilan yashayman. Otam _____. U _____. Onam _____. U _____. Men oilamni yaxshi ko‘raman, chunki ular doim menga yordam berishadi.

📖 TASK 3 — READING

Matnni kamida uch marta o‘qing. Birinchi marta umumiy ma'noni tushuning, ikkinchi marta olmoshlarni toping, uchinchi marta ovoz chiqarib o‘qing.

MY FRIEND AND HIS FAMILY

My name is Adam. I have a good friend. His name is Ben. He is a student. His family is very friendly. His father is a doctor, and his mother is a teacher. They live in a big house.

Ben has a sister. Her name is Anna. She is twelve years old. She loves her brother, and he always helps her. Their parents are proud of them.

One day, Ben showed me his new bicycle. He said, "This bicycle is mine, but that blue one is my brother's." I liked his bicycle very much. We cleaned the bicycles ourselves. Then we went to the park together.

Matn bo‘yicha savollar

  1. What is the boy's name?
  2. What is his friend's name?
  3. What does Ben's father do?
  4. What does his mother do?
  5. How old is Anna?
  6. Who helps Anna?
  7. Whose bicycle is blue?
  8. What did the boys clean themselves?

Vazifa: Savollarga ingliz tilida to‘liq gaplar bilan javob bering. Matndan kamida 10 ta olmoshni topib, turini aniqlang.

🎤 TASK 4 — SPEAKING

Quyidagi mavzular bo‘yicha ovoz chiqarib gapiring. Har bir mavzuda kamida 5–6 ta gap aytishga harakat qiling.

Part 1 — About Yourself

  • What is your name?
  • Where do you live?
  • What do you do?
  • What do you like?
  • What do you do by yourself?

Part 2 — My Family

  • Who is in your family?
  • What does your father do?
  • What does your mother do?
  • What do you do together?
  • Why do you love them?

Part 3 — Whose Is It?

  • Whose phone is this?
  • Whose bag is that?
  • Is this your book?
  • Which things are yours?
  • Which things belong to your family?
Speaking talabi: Javoblarni faqat yodlab aytmang. O‘zingiz haqingizda mustaqil gap tuzing. I, me, my, mine, he, him, his, she, her, hers kabi olmoshlarni to‘g‘ri ishlating.
