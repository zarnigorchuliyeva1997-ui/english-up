1. DARSNI BOSHLAYMIZ

Assalomu alaykum! Bugungi darsimizda ingliz tilining juda muhim mavzularidan birini o‘rganamiz: Countable and Uncountable Nouns.

Masalan, biz bitta olma, ikkita olma, uchta olma deb sanay olamiz. Lekin suvni odatda bitta suv, ikkita suv deb sanamaymiz. Suvning miqdorini litr, stakan yoki shisha orqali ifodalaymiz.

I have three apples.
Menda uchta olma bor.
I need some water.
Menga biroz suv kerak.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Countable — sanaladigan otlar. Uncountable — odatda alohida dona sifatida sanalmaydigan otlar.

2. NOUN NIMA?

3. COUNTABLE NOUNS — SANALADIGAN OTLAR

Countable nouns — alohida dona sifatida sanash mumkin bo‘lgan otlar. Ularning birlik va ko‘plik shakli mavjud.

3.1. Singular — birlik

Bitta narsa haqida gapirganda birlik shaklidan foydalanamiz.

a book — bitta kitob
an apple — bitta olma
a chair — bitta stul

Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot oldidan ko‘pincha a/an ishlatiladi. Bu haqda quyida batafsil o‘rganamiz.

3.2. Plural — ko‘plik

Ikki yoki undan ortiq dona bo‘lsa, ko‘plik shaklidan foydalanamiz.

one book → two books
one apple → three apples
one chair → four chairs

3.3. Ko‘plik yasash qoidalari

QoidaBirlikKo‘plik
Ko‘pincha -sbookbooks
-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z → -esbox, busboxes, buses
Undosh + y → iescity, babycities, babies
Unli + y → -sboy, toyboys, toys
Ko‘p -f/-fe → -veswife, leafwives, leaves

Eslatma: -f/-fe bilan tugagan barcha so‘zlar -ves bo‘lavermaydi. Masalan: roof → roofs, belief → beliefs.

DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Countable nouns birlik va ko‘plik shakliga ega. Ko‘plik odatda -s yoki -es bilan yasaladi, ammo irregular plurals alohida yodlanadi.

3.5. Countable bilan ishlatiladigan ifodalar

  • a / an — bitta: a pen, an egg.
  • one, two, three... — sonlar: two books, five students.
  • many — ko‘p: many cars.
  • a few / few — bir nechta / juda kam: a few friends.
  • How many? — nechta?: How many books?
There are five students in the room.
Xonada beshta o‘quvchi bor.
How many brothers do you have?
Nechta aka-ukangiz bor?

4. UNCOUNTABLE NOUNS — SANALMAYDIGAN OTLAR

Uncountable nouns — odatda alohida dona sifatida sanalmaydigan moddalar, materiallar, suyuqliklar yoki mavhum tushunchalardir.

Masalan, water, milk, rice, sugar, information, advice. Ularni odatda to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri 1, 2, 3 deb sanamaymiz.

I drink water every day.
Men har kuni suv ichaman.
She needs some information.
Unga biroz ma’lumot kerak.

4.1. Uncountable otlarning asosiy xususiyatlari

  1. Odatdagi ma’noda ko‘plik -s qo‘shimchasini olmaydi.
  2. Odatda a/an bilan ishlatilmaydi.
  3. Miqdori some, much, a little kabi so‘zlar bilan ifodalanadi.
  4. Birlikdagi fe’l bilan ishlatiladi: The water is cold.
  5. Alohida dona sanash kerak bo‘lsa, o‘lchov yoki idish so‘zlaridan foydalanamiz.
The milk is cold.
Sut sovuq.
This information is useful.
Bu ma’lumot foydali.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Uncountable nouns odatda -s olmaydi, a/an bilan ishlatilmaydi va birlikdagi fe’l bilan keladi.

Muhim: “news” oxirida -s bo‘lsa ham, odatda sanalmaydigan birlik ot hisoblanadi.

The news is interesting.
Yangilik qiziqarli.

Furniture ham odatda uncountable. “Furnitures” demaymiz. Alohida buyumlarni aytish uchun “pieces of furniture” deymiz.

DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: information, advice, furniture, homework, money, luggage, equipment, news — odatda uncountable. Ularni ko‘plikka -s qo‘shib ishlatmang.

4.3. Uncountable narsalarni qanday sanaymiz?

5. A / AN — BIRLIKDAGI SANALADIGAN OTLAR

A va an ikkalasi ham “bir” ma’nosini beradi. Ular faqat birlikdagi sanaladigan ot oldidan ishlatiladi.

5.1. A qachon ishlatiladi?

So‘z undosh tovush bilan boshlansa, odatda a ishlatamiz.

a book — bir kitob
a car — bir mashina
a university — bir universitet

5.2. An qachon ishlatiladi?

So‘z unli tovush bilan boshlansa, odatda an ishlatamiz.

an apple — bir olma
an egg — bir tuxum
an hour — bir soat

E’tibor: A yoki an tanlashda harfga emas, talaffuzdagi birinchi tovushga qaraymiz. “University” /juː/ tovushi bilan boshlanadi, shuning uchun “a university”. “Hour” so‘zida h talaffuz qilinmaydi, shuning uchun “an hour”.

5.3. A/An qayerda ishlatilmaydi?

  • Ko‘plik ot bilan: a books — noto‘g‘ri.
  • Uncountable ot bilan: an information — noto‘g‘ri.
  • To‘g‘risi: a book, books, some information.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: A + undosh tovush bilan boshlanadigan birlik sanaladigan ot. An + unli tovush bilan boshlanadigan birlik sanaladigan ot. A/An ko‘plik va uncountable ot bilan ishlatilmaydi.

6. SOME / ANY

Some va any miqdori aniq ko‘rsatilmagan narsalar haqida gapirganda ishlatiladi. Ikkalasi ham countable plural va uncountable otlar bilan kelishi mumkin.

6.1. SOME — odatda darak gaplarda

Some — bir nechta, biroz, ma’lum miqdorda degan ma’noni beradi.

I have some friends.
Mening bir nechta do‘stim bor.
There is some milk in the fridge.
Muzlatkichda biroz sut bor.

6.2. ANY — odatda inkor va so‘roq gaplarda

I don't have any money.
Menda umuman pul yo‘q.
Do you have any questions?
Savollaringiz bormi?

6.3. Some bilan savol berish mumkinmi?

Ha! Taklif yoki iltimos qilganda, ijobiy javob kutayotganimizda some ishlatishimiz mumkin.

Would you like some tea?
Biroz choy xohlaysizmi?
Can I have some water, please?
Iltimos, biroz suv olsam bo‘ladimi?

6.4. Any darak gapda ham ishlatilishi mumkin

Any “istalgan” ma’nosida darak gapda ham ishlatiladi.

You can choose any book.
Istalgan kitobni tanlashingiz mumkin.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Some — odatda darak gapda. Any — odatda so‘roq va inkor gapda. Some taklif va iltimoslarda ham ishlatiladi. Any “istalgan” ma’nosida darak gapda kelishi mumkin.

7. MUCH / MANY

Much va many “ko‘p” degan ma’noni anglatadi. Ularning farqi qaysi ot bilan ishlatilishida.

7.1. MANY — sanaladigan ko‘plik otlar bilan

many books — ko‘p kitoblar
many students — ko‘p o‘quvchilar
many apples — ko‘p olmalar

7.2. MUCH — sanalmaydigan otlar bilan

much water — ko‘p suv
much money — ko‘p pul
much time — ko‘p vaqt

7.3. Much/Many qaysi gaplarda ishlatiladi?

Ular ayniqsa so‘roq va inkor gaplarda juda ko‘p ishlatiladi.

How many students are there?
U yerda nechta o‘quvchi bor?
How much water do you drink?
Qancha suv ichasiz?
I don't have much time.
Menda ko‘p vaqt yo‘q.
There aren't many people here.
Bu yerda ko‘p odam yo‘q.

Darak gapda ham much/many ishlatish mumkin, lekin kundalik suhbatda ko‘pincha a lot of tabiiyroq eshitiladi.

I have a lot of friends.
Mening ko‘p do‘stlarim bor.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: MANY + ko‘plikdagi countable noun. MUCH + uncountable noun. Much/Many ayniqsa so‘roq va inkor gaplarda ishlatiladi.

8. A LOT OF / LOTS OF

A lot of va lots of “ko‘p” degan ma’noni beradi. Ular countable plural hamda uncountable otlar bilan ishlatiladi.

a lot of books
ko‘p kitoblar
a lot of water
ko‘p suv
She has lots of friends.
Uning ko‘p do‘stlari bor.
We have a lot of work today.
Bugun ishimiz ko‘p.

A lot of va lots of kundalik nutqda keng ishlatiladi. “Lots of” biroz norasmiyroq ohangga ega.

DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: A LOT OF / LOTS OF + countable plural yoki uncountable noun. Ikkalasi ham “ko‘p” degani.

9. FEW / A FEW / LITTLE / A LITTLE

Bu to‘rtta so‘z oz miqdorni bildiradi. Lekin ularning qaysi ot bilan kelishi va ma’nosi farq qiladi.

9.1. FEW — sanaladigan ko‘plik otlar bilan

Few — juda kam, deyarli yetarli emas degan salbiy ma’noni beradi.

I have few friends here.
Bu yerda do‘stlarim juda kam.

9.2. A FEW — sanaladigan ko‘plik otlar bilan

A few — bir nechta, oz bo‘lsa ham bor degan ijobiyroq ma’noni beradi.

I have a few friends here.
Bu yerda bir nechta do‘stim bor.

9.3. LITTLE — uncountable otlar bilan

Little — juda oz, deyarli yetarli emas.

We have little time.
Bizda vaqt juda kam.

9.4. A LITTLE — uncountable otlar bilan

A little — biroz, ozgina bo‘lsa ham bor.

We have a little time.
Bizda biroz vaqt bor.

DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: FEW/A FEW — countable plural bilan. LITTLE/A LITTLE — uncountable bilan. “A” qo‘shilishi ma’noni “oz bo‘lsa ham bor” tomonga o‘zgartiradi.

10. HOW MANY / HOW MUCH

Biror narsaning soni yoki miqdorini so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi.

10.1. HOW MANY — nechta?

Ko‘plikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan ishlatiladi.

How many apples do you have?
Nechta olmangiz bor?
How many children are there?
Nechta bola bor?

10.2. HOW MUCH — qancha?

Uncountable ot bilan ishlatiladi. Shuningdek, narxni so‘rashda ham qo‘llanadi.

How much milk do we need?
Bizga qancha sut kerak?
How much is this bag?
Bu sumka qancha turadi?

10.3. Savol tuzilishi

Do/Does bilan:

How many + plural noun + do/does + S + V1?
How many books do you read?
Siz nechta kitob o‘qiysiz?
How much + uncountable noun + do/does + S + V1?
How much water does he drink?
U qancha suv ichadi?

Bu yerda S — Subject (ega), V1 — fe’lning asosiy shakli. Be fe’li bilan esa “How many students are there?” kabi tuzilma ishlatiladi.

DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: HOW MANY + ko‘plikdagi sanaladigan ot. HOW MUCH + sanalmaydigan ot. Narxni so‘rashda ham HOW MUCH ishlatiladi.

15. READING — SHOPPING FOR FOOD

A Day at the Supermarket

Anna goes to the supermarket every Saturday. She usually buys some bread, milk, rice and fruit. Today, she needs a lot of food because her family is coming for dinner.

She buys six apples, four oranges and two bottles of water. She also buys a kilo of rice and a loaf of bread. There isn't much sugar at home, so she buys a small bag of sugar.

At the supermarket, Anna sees her friend Tom. He asks, “How many eggs do you need?” Anna says, “I need ten eggs.” Tom also asks, “How much milk do you drink every week?” Anna answers, “We drink a lot of milk.”

After shopping, Anna goes home and puts the food in the kitchen. She is happy because she has everything she needs.

Reading vocabulary

  • supermarket — supermarket
  • usually — odatda
  • coming for dinner — kechki ovqatga kelayotgan
  • a kilo of — bir kilogramm
  • everything — hamma narsa

Reading questions

1. When does Anna go shopping?

2. How many apples does she buy?

3. What does she buy in bottles?

18. WRITING — MY SHOPPING LIST

Quyidagi mavzu bo‘yicha 80–100 so‘zli kichik matn yozing.

Topic: My Shopping List — Mening xaridlar ro‘yxatim

Matningizda quyidagilar bo‘lishi kerak:

  • What food do you usually buy?
  • How many apples, eggs or other countable things do you need?
  • How much milk, water or rice do you need?
  • Use some, any, much, many and a lot of.
  • Explain why you buy these things.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: 80–100 so‘zli matnni avval daftaringizda yozing. Kamida 5 ta countable va 5 ta uncountable ot ishlating.
