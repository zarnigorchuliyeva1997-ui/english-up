FUTURE SIMPLE — WILL

Kelajak haqida gapirishni o‘rganamiz!

Darsni boshlang

🎯 Dars maqsadi

Ushbu dars oxirida siz:

  • Future Simple nima ekanini tushunasiz.
  • Will yordamida darak, inkor va so‘roq gaplar tuzasiz.
  • WH-questions va qisqa javoblardan foydalanasiz.
  • Kelajak haqidagi taxmin, va’da va takliflarni ifodalay olasiz.
  • Will bilan bog‘liq keng tarqalgan xatolardan qochasiz.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Dars davomida sariq rangli barcha qoidalar, formulalar va belgilangan mashqlarni daftaringizga yozing.
1

Mavzuga kirish

Future Simple nima?

Future Simple — ingliz tilida kelajakda sodir bo‘ladigan ish-harakatlar haqida gapirish uchun ishlatiladigan zamon.

O‘zbek tilida bu zamon ko‘pincha “boraman”, “keladi”, “o‘qiyman”, “yordam beraman” kabi ma’nolarni anglatadi.

I will study tomorrow.
Men ertaga o‘qiyman.
She will call you.
U sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi.
They will travel next week.
Ular keyingi hafta sayohat qilishadi.

Will nima?

Will — kelajak ma’nosini ifodalashda ishlatiladigan yordamchi fe’l. U gapda asosiy fe’lga kelajak ma’nosini beradi.

Muhim: Will dan keyin asosiy fe’l doimo V1 shaklida ishlatiladi.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Future Simple — kelajakdagi ish-harakatlar haqida gapirish uchun ishlatiladi. Uning asosiy yordamchi fe’li WILL hisoblanadi.
2

Formulalar va grammatik strukturalar

Avval belgilarni tushunib olaylik

BelgiInglizcha nomiMa’nosi
SSubjectEga: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
WillAuxiliary verbYordamchi fe’l
V1Verb 1Fe’lning birinchi shakli
OObjectTo‘ldiruvchi
WHQuestion wordWhat, where, when, why, how kabi so‘roq so‘zlari
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: S = Subject, Will = yordamchi fe’l, V1 = fe’lning birinchi shakli, O = Object.

A. Positive — darak gap

Darak gap biror ish-harakat kelajakda sodir bo‘lishini tasdiqlaydi.

S + WILL + V1 + O

Misollar:

I will learn English.
Men ingliz tilini o‘rganaman.
He will play football.
U futbol o‘ynaydi.
We will visit our grandmother.
Biz buvimizni ziyorat qilamiz.

Gapni qismlarga ajratamiz:

I — S (ega)
will — yordamchi fe’l
learn — V1 (asosiy fe’l)
English — O (to‘ldiruvchi)
Eslab qoling: I, you, he, she, it, we, they — barcha egalar bilan WILL ishlatiladi. He yoki she bilan will o‘zgarmaydi.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: S + WILL + V1 + O. Ushbu formulani va uchta misolni yozing.

B. Negative — inkor gap

Inkor gap kelajakda biror ish-harakat sodir bo‘lmasligini bildiradi.

S + WILL NOT + V1 + O

Will not qisqartirilganda won’t bo‘ladi.

I will not go there.
Men u yerga bormayman.
She won't work tomorrow.
U ertaga ishlamaydi.
They will not buy a new car.
Ular yangi mashina sotib olishmaydi.
To‘liq shaklQisqa shakl
will notwon’t
I will notI won’t
He will notHe won’t
We will notWe won’t
Xato: I don't will go.
To‘g‘ri: I will not go.
Future Simple inkorida don't yoki doesn't ishlatilmaydi.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: S + WILL NOT + V1 + O. Will not = won’t. Uchta inkor gap yozing.

C. Question — umumiy so‘roq gap

Ha yoki yo‘q deb javob beriladigan savollarda WILL gap boshiga chiqadi.

WILL + S + V1 + O?
Will you come tomorrow?
Siz ertaga kelasizmi?
Will she help us?
U bizga yordam beradimi?
Will they study English?
Ular ingliz tilini o‘rganishadimi?

Gapni qismlarga ajratamiz:

Will — yordamchi fe’l
you — ega
come — V1
tomorrow — vaqt ko‘rsatkichi
So‘roq gapda WILL egadan oldin keladi. Asosiy fe’l esa WILL dan keyin V1 shaklida qoladi.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: WILL + S + V1 + O? Uchta so‘roq gap yozing.

D. WH-Questions — maxsus so‘roq gaplar

Bu savollar shunchaki “ha” yoki “yo‘q” javobini emas, balki ma’lumotni so‘raydi.

WhyNima uchun?Why will they leave?

What will you study tomorrow?
Sen ertaga nimani o‘rganasan?

Ular qayerda yashashadi?
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: WH-WORD + WILL + S + V1? Kamida beshta WH-savol tuzing.

E. Short Answers — qisqa javoblar

Will bilan berilgan savollarga qisqa javob berish tartibi:

SavolIjobiy javobSalbiy javob
Will you come?Yes, I will.No, I won't.
Will he work?Yes, he will.No, he won't.
Will they study?Yes, they will.No, they won't.
Xato: Yes, I will come. — bu to‘liq javob bo‘lishi mumkin, ammo qisqa javob so‘ralganda “Yes, I will” ishlatiladi. “Yes, I will” dan keyin asosiy fe’l takrorlanmaydi.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Yes, S + will. / No, S + won't. Uchta savolga ikkita qisqa javob yozing.

4

Will qachon ishlatiladi?

1. Kelajak haqidagi taxmin va fikrlar

Kelajakda nima sodir bo‘lishi haqida o‘z fikrimizni yoki taxminimizni aytganda.

I think it will rain tomorrow.
Menimcha, ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘adi.
She will be a good teacher.
U yaxshi o‘qituvchi bo‘ladi.

2. Shu zahoti qabul qilingan qaror

Gapirayotgan paytimizda qaror qabul qilsak, WILL ishlatishimiz mumkin.

The phone is ringing. I will answer it.
Telefon jiringlayapti. Men javob beraman.
I'm tired. I will take a rest.
Charchadim. Dam olaman.

3. Va’da berish

I will help you.
Men sizga yordam beraman.
I will never forget this.
Men buni hech qachon unutmayman.

4. Taklif qilish

I will carry your bag.
Sumkangizni ko‘tarib beraman.
We will help you with your homework.
Uy vazifangizga yordam beramiz.

5. Kelajakdagi holat haqida fikr bildirish

Life will be different in the future.
Kelajakda hayot boshqacha bo‘ladi.
People will use new technology.
Odamlar yangi texnologiyalardan foydalanishadi.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Will ning beshta qo‘llanish holatini va har biriga bittadan misolni yozing.

8

Reading — My Future Plans

Matnni avval mustaqil o‘qing. Keyin audio orqali tinglang.

My name is Adam. I am a student. I will finish school next year. I will study English every day. I think English will help me in the future. I will read books and watch educational videos. I will work hard and learn new things. My family will support me. I will try my best because I want to have a good future.

Reading questions

  1. When will Adam finish school?
  2. What will he study every day?
  3. How will English help him?
  4. Who will support Adam?
  5. Why will he work hard?
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Matnni o‘qing, tarjima qiling va savollarga ingliz tilida javob yozing.
9

Listening — A Busy Weekend

Audio matnini tinglang. Birinchi marta odatiy tezlikda, ikkinchi marta sekinroq tinglang.

Audio matni: This weekend, I will visit my uncle. We will have lunch together. My cousins will play football. I will help my uncle in the garden. In the evening, we will watch a film. I think it will be a wonderful weekend.

Listening questions

  1. Who will the speaker visit?
  2. What will they do together?
  3. What will the cousins play?
  4. Where will the speaker help his uncle?
  5. What will they watch in the evening?
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Audio matnini tinglab, savollarga javob yozing. Tushunmagan so‘zlaringizni belgilang.
10

Speaking — Talk about your future

Quyidagi savollarga ovoz chiqarib, to‘liq gaplar bilan javob bering.

  1. What will you do tomorrow?
  2. What will you study next week?
  3. Where will you go next year?
  4. Will you learn another language?
  5. How will you help your family?
  6. What will your life be like in the future?
  7. Will you read a book this month?
  8. What will you do this evening?
Maslahat: Har bir savolga kamida 2 ta inglizcha gap bilan javob berishga harakat qiling.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Savollardan kamida beshtasiga yozma javob tayyorlang. Keyin javoblaringizni ovoz chiqarib ayting.
11

Writing — My Future

Quyidagi fikrlardan foydalanishingiz mumkin:

  • What will you study?
  • Where will you work?
  • How will you help your family?
  • What will you learn?
  • What will you do next year?

12

Homework — Uy vazifasi

Barcha topshiriqlarni daftarda bajaring. Shoshilmasdan, qoidalarga amal qilib ishlang.

TOPSHIRIQ 1 • GRAMMAR

Quyidagi gaplarni Future Simple darak shaklida yozing.

  1. I / visit / my friend / tomorrow.
  2. She / cook / dinner / tonight.
  3. They / play / football / next week.
  4. We / learn / English.
  5. He / buy / a new phone.
  6. You / meet / your teacher.
  7. My brother / work / tomorrow.
  8. It / rain / soon.
  9. My parents / travel / next month.
  10. I / help / my mother.
TOPSHIRIQ 2 • GRAMMAR

Gaplarni ko‘rsatilgan shaklga o‘zgartiring.

  1. I will go to school. (Negative)
  2. She will read a book. (Question)
  3. They will come tomorrow. (Negative)
  4. He will help us. (Question)
  5. We will travel next year. (Negative)
  6. You will study English. (Question)
  7. My father will work tomorrow. (Negative)
  8. It will be cold. (Question)
  9. She will call me. (Negative)
  10. They will buy a house. (Question)
TOPSHIRIQ 3 • VOCABULARY

Vocabulary bilan ishlash

  1. 40 ta yangi so‘zni yodlang.
  2. Har bir so‘zni ovoz chiqarib 3 martadan o‘qing.
  3. Yangi so‘zlardan foydalanib 10 ta Future Simple gap tuzing.
  4. 10 ta so‘zning o‘zbekcha tarjimasini yopib, inglizchasini eslab yozing.
  5. O‘zingiz uchun 5 ta yangi so‘zdan iborat kichik test tuzing.
TOPSHIRIQ 4 • TRANSLATION

Ingliz tilidan o‘zbek tiliga tarjima qiling.

Tomorrow will be a busy day. I will wake up early and have breakfast. Then I will go to school. I will meet my friends and study English. After school, I will help my mother at home. In the evening, I will read a book and do my homework. I think tomorrow will be a good day.

TOPSHIRIQ 5 • TRANSLATION

O‘zbek tilidan ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

Ertaga men erta turaman. Men nonushta qilaman va ishga boraman. Ishdan keyin do‘stim bilan uchrashaman. Biz birga ingliz tilini o‘rganamiz. Kechqurun men uyga qaytaman va kitob o‘qiyman. Men kelajakda yaxshi mutaxassis bo‘laman. Men oilamga yordam beraman.

📒 ESLATMA: Barcha topshiriqlarni daftarda bajaring. Grammatik qoidalarni va formulalarni qayta ko‘rib chiqing.
