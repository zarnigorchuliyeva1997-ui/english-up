1 SHOPPING

🛍️ Shopping — Do‘konda xarid qilish

Shopping — xarid qilish degani. Ingliz tilida do‘konga kirganda, mahsulot narxini so‘raganda, o‘lcham yoki rang tanlaganda ishlatiladigan iboralarni bilish juda muhim.

Asosiy iboralar

How much is it?
Bu qancha turadi?
/hau mach iz it/
How much are they?
Bular qancha turadi?
/hau mach ar zey/
Can I help you?
Sizga yordam bera olamanmi?
/ken ay help yu/
I'm just looking.
Shunchaki ko‘rib yuribman.
/aym jast luking/
Can I try it on?
Buni kiyib ko‘rsam bo‘ladimi?
/ken ay tray it on/
Do you have a smaller size?
Kichikroq o‘lchami bormi?
/du yu hev e smoler sayz/
It's too expensive.
Bu juda qimmat.
/its tu ikspensiv/
Can I pay by card?
Karta orqali to‘lasam bo‘ladimi?
/ken ay pey bay kard/

Muhim grammatik qoliplar

How much + is + birlikdagi ot?

How much is this shirt? — Bu ko‘ylak qancha turadi?

How much is that phone? — Anavi telefon qancha turadi?

How much + are + ko‘plikdagi ot?

How much are these shoes? — Bu oyoq kiyimlar qancha turadi?

How much are those apples? — Anavi olmalar qancha turadi?

Can I + fe’lning birinchi shakli?

Can I try it? — Sinab ko‘rsam bo‘ladimi?

Can I pay now? — Hozir to‘lasam bo‘ladimi?

Can I see it? — Uni ko‘rsam bo‘ladimi?

Eslab qoling: Can I...? odobli ruxsat so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi. Undan keyin fe'lning oddiy shakli keladi: Can I go? Can I sit? Can I ask?

🗣️ Dialogue 1 — Buying a T-shirt

Do‘konda futbolka sotib olish

Customer and Shop Assistant
Assistant: Hello! Can I help you?
Salom! Sizga yordam bera olamanmi?
Customer: Yes, please. I'm looking for a T-shirt.
Ha, iltimos. Men futbolka qidiryapman.
Assistant: What colour would you like?
Qaysi rangni xohlaysiz?
Customer: I'd like a blue one.
Ko‘k ranglisini xohlayman.
Assistant: What size do you need?
Qaysi o‘lcham kerak?
Customer: Medium, please. Can I try it on?
O‘rtacha o‘lcham, iltimos. Kiyib ko‘rsam bo‘ladimi?
Assistant: Of course. The fitting room is over there.
Albatta. Kiyinish xonasi anavi yerda.
Customer: It fits perfectly. How much is it?
Juda mos keldi. Bu qancha turadi?
Assistant: It's twenty dollars.
Yigirma dollar.
Customer: Great. I'll take it. Can I pay by card?
Ajoyib. Buni olaman. Karta orqali to‘lasam bo‘ladimi?
Assistant: Certainly. Thank you!
Albatta. Rahmat!

🎧 Listening 1 — Buying a T-shirt

Dialogni avval tarjimasiz tinglang. Keyin savollarga javob bering.

1. What is the customer looking for?

2. What colour does the customer want?

3. How much is the T-shirt?

Dialogue 2 — Buying Groceries

Customer and Shop Assistant
Customer: Excuse me, where can I find milk?
Kechirasiz, sutni qayerdan topsam bo‘ladi?
Assistant: It's in aisle three, next to the cheese.
Uchinchi qatorda, pishloq yonida.
Customer: Thank you. How much is a bottle?
Rahmat. Bir shishasi qancha turadi?
Assistant: It's two dollars.
Ikki dollar.
Customer: I'll take two bottles, please.
Ikkita shisha olaman, iltimos.
Assistant: Anything else?
Yana biror narsa kerakmi?
Customer: Yes, I'd like some bread and apples.
Ha, biroz non va olma olmoqchiman.
Assistant: Here you are. Is that everything?
Marhamat. Shu hammasimi?
Customer: Yes, thank you. Where can I pay?
Ha, rahmat. Qayerda to‘lasam bo‘ladi?
Assistant: At the checkout, near the entrance.
Kirish joyi yaqinidagi kassada.

🎧 Listening 2 — Buying Groceries

1. Where is the milk?

2. How many bottles of milk does the customer buy?

3. Where can the customer pay?

2 TRAVELLING

✈️ Travelling — Sayohat va transport

Sayohat paytida manzil so‘rash, chipta olish, transport jadvalini tushunish va mehmonxonada gaplashish uchun quyidagi iboralar kerak bo‘ladi.

Where is the station?
Vokzal qayerda?
/wer iz ze steyshn/
I'd like a ticket.
Chipta olmoqchiman.
/ayd layk e tiket/
One way or return?
Bir tomonga yoki borib-kelish?
/wan wey or ritern/
What time does it leave?
U soat nechada jo‘naydi?
/wot taym daz it liiv/
How long does it take?
Qancha vaqt ketadi?
/hau long daz it teyk/
Where should I get off?
Qayerda tushishim kerak?
/wer shud ay get of/
I'm looking for...
Men ... qidiryapman.
/aym luking for/
Could you help me?
Menga yordam bera olasizmi?
/kud yu help mi/

Yo‘l so‘rashda ishlatiladigan so‘zlar

Turn left. — Chapga buriling.
Turn right. — O‘ngga buriling.
Go straight ahead. — To‘g‘riga boring.
It's next to the bank. — Bankning yonida.
It's opposite the hotel. — Mehmonxonaning ro‘parasida.
It's near the bus stop. — Avtobus bekati yaqinida.

Grammatik qolip: How long does it take?

Biror joyga yetib borish uchun qancha vaqt ketishini so‘rashda ishlatiladi.

How long does it take to get to the airport?
Aeroportga yetib borish qancha vaqt oladi?
It takes thirty minutes.
O‘ttiz daqiqa vaqt ketadi.
It takes two hours by bus.
Avtobusda ikki soat ketadi.

🗣️ Dialogue 1 — At the Train Station

Passenger and Ticket Clerk
Passenger: Good morning. I'd like a ticket to London, please.
Xayrli tong. Londonga chipta olmoqchiman.
Clerk: Certainly. One way or return?
Albatta. Bir tomonga yoki borib-kelish?
Passenger: A return ticket, please.
Borib-kelish chiptasi, iltimos.
Clerk: When would you like to travel?
Qachon sayohat qilmoqchisiz?
Passenger: Tomorrow morning.
Ertaga ertalab.
Clerk: The train leaves at nine fifteen.
Poyezd soat to‘qqiz-u o‘n beshda jo‘naydi.
Passenger: How long does it take?
Qancha vaqt ketadi?
Clerk: About two hours.
Taxminan ikki soat.
Passenger: How much is the ticket?
Chipta qancha turadi?
Clerk: It's forty pounds.
Qirq funt.
Passenger: Here you are. Thank you.
Mana, marhamat. Rahmat.

🎧 Listening 1 — At the Train Station

1. Where does the passenger want to go?

2. When does the passenger want to travel?

3. How long does the journey take?

Dialogue 2 — At the Hotel

Guest and Receptionist
Guest: Hello. I have a reservation.
Salom. Menda bron bor.
Receptionist: Welcome! What's your name, please?
Xush kelibsiz! Ismingiz nima?
Guest: My name is Alex Brown.
Mening ismim Alex Brown.
Receptionist: Let me check. Yes, a room for two nights.
Tekshirib ko‘ray. Ha, ikki kechaga xona.
Guest: That's right. Is breakfast included?
To‘g‘ri. Nonushta narxga kiritilganmi?
Receptionist: Yes, breakfast is from seven to ten.
Ha, nonushta yettidan o‘ngacha.
Guest: Great. What time is check-out?
Ajoyib. Xonani topshirish vaqti nechada?
Receptionist: Before eleven in the morning.
Ertalab soat o‘n birdan oldin.
Guest: Thank you. Could I have the room key?
Rahmat. Xona kalitini olsam bo‘ladimi?
Receptionist: Of course. Here you are. Enjoy your stay!
Albatta. Marhamat. Yaxshi dam oling!

🎧 Listening 2 — At the Hotel

1. How many nights is the room booked for?

2. Is breakfast included?

3. What time is check-out?

3 AT THE RESTAURANT

🍽️ At the Restaurant — Restoranda

Restoranda taom buyurtma qilish, menyu so‘rash va hisobni to‘lashda muloyim iboralardan foydalanamiz.

Could I see the menu?
Menyuni ko‘rsam bo‘ladimi?
What do you recommend?
Nimani tavsiya qilasiz?
I'd like to order.
Buyurtma bermoqchiman.
I'll have the chicken.
Tovuq go‘shtlisini olaman.
Anything to drink?
Ichishga biror narsa olasizmi?
Could we have the bill?
Hisobni olsak bo‘ladimi?
It was delicious.
Juda mazali edi.
I'm allergic to nuts.
Yong‘oqqa allergiyam bor.

Muloyim so‘rov: Could I...?

Could I...? — odobli tarzda biror narsani so‘rash yoki ruxsat olish uchun ishlatiladi.

Could I have some water? — Biroz suv olsam bo‘ladimi?
Could I see the menu? — Menyuni ko‘rsam bo‘ladimi?
Could we sit here? — Shu yerga o‘tirsak bo‘ladimi?
Could I...? odatda Can I...? ga qaraganda muloyimroq eshitiladi. Restoran va xizmat ko‘rsatish joylarida juda foydali.

🗣️ Dialogue 1 — Ordering Food

Waiter and Customer
Waiter: Good evening. Welcome to our restaurant.
Xayrli kech. Restoranimizga xush kelibsiz.
Customer: Thank you. Could I see the menu, please?
Rahmat. Menyuni ko‘rsam bo‘ladimi?
Waiter: Of course. Here you are.
Albatta. Marhamat.
Customer: What do you recommend?
Nimani tavsiya qilasiz?
Waiter: Our grilled chicken is very popular.
Bizning grilda pishirilgan tovuqimiz juda mashhur.
Customer: That sounds good. I'll have that.
Yaxshi ekan. Shuni olaman.
Waiter: Would you like something to drink?
Ichishga biror narsa xohlaysizmi?
Customer: Yes, a glass of orange juice, please.
Ha, bir stakan apelsin sharbati, iltimos.
Waiter: Certainly. I'll be right back.
Albatta. Hozir qaytaman.
Customer: Thank you.
Rahmat.

🎧 Listening 1 — Ordering Food

1. What does the customer ask to see?

2. What food does the waiter recommend?

3. What does the customer drink?

Dialogue 2 — Asking for the Bill

Customer and Waiter
Waiter: Was everything all right?
Hammasi yaxshimi edi?
Customer: Yes, everything was delicious.
Ha, hammasi juda mazali edi.
Waiter: I'm glad you enjoyed it.
Sizga yoqqanidan xursandman.
Customer: Could we have the bill, please?
Hisobni olsak bo‘ladimi?
Waiter: Certainly. Here you are.

4 MAKING PLANS

📅 Making Plans — Rejalar tuzish

Do‘stlar bilan uchrashuv belgilash, taklif qilish, vaqt va joy haqida kelishish kundalik muloqotning muhim qismidir.

Are you free tomorrow?
Ertaga bo‘shmisiz?
Would you like to come?
Kelishni xohlaysizmi?
Let's meet at six.
Keling, soat oltida uchrashamiz.
That sounds great.
Bu ajoyib fikr ekan.
I'm afraid I can't.
Afsuski, qila olmayman.
Maybe another time.
Balki boshqa safar.
What about Saturday?
Shanba kuniga nima deysiz?
See you then!
Unda ko‘rishguncha!

Let's + Verb

Let's birgalikda biror ish qilishni taklif etishda ishlatiladi. Undan keyin fe'lning birinchi shakli keladi.

Let's go shopping. — Keling, xarid qilgani boramiz.
Let's watch a film. — Keling, film tomosha qilamiz.
Let's have dinner. — Keling, kechki ovqat qilamiz.
Noto‘g‘ri: Let's to go. To‘g‘ri: Let's go. Let's dan keyin “to” qo‘yilmaydi.

Would you like to...?

Bu ibora birovga muloyim taklif berish uchun ishlatiladi.

Would you like to join us? — Bizga qo‘shilishni xohlaysizmi?
Would you like to have coffee? — Qahva ichishni xohlaysizmi?
Would you like to go out? — Tashqariga chiqishni xohlaysizmi?

Taklifga javob berish

Yes, I'd love to. — Ha, bajonidil.
That sounds wonderful. — Juda ajoyib eshitilyapti.
Sorry, I'm busy. — Kechirasiz, bandman.
I'm afraid I can't make it. — Afsuski, bora olmayman.

🗣️ Dialogue 1 — Making Weekend Plans

Two Friends
Tom: Hi, Anna! Are you free this Saturday?
Salom, Anna! Shu shanba kuni bo‘shmisan?
Anna: Yes, I am. Why?
Ha, bo‘shman. Nima edi?
Tom: Would you like to go to the cinema?
Kinoga borishni xohlaysanmi?
Anna: That sounds great! What film do you want to watch?
Ajoyib fikr! Qaysi filmni ko‘rmoqchisan?
Tom: There's a new comedy film.
Yangi komediya filmi bor.
Anna: Perfect! What time shall we meet?
Zo‘r! Soat nechada uchrashamiz?
Tom: Let's meet at five o'clock.
Keling, soat beshda uchrashamiz.
Anna: Where shall we meet?
Qayerda uchrashamiz?
Tom: In front of the cinema.
Kinoteatr oldida.
Anna: Great. See you on Saturday!
Ajoyib. Shanba kuni ko‘rishamiz!

🎧 Listening 1 — Weekend Plans

1. What day are they making plans for?

2. Where do they want to go?

3. What time will they meet?

Dialogue 2 — Changing Plans

Two Friends
Lisa: Hi, Mark. Are we still meeting tonight?
Salom, Mark. Bugun kechqurun hali ham uchrashamizmi?
Mark: I'm sorry, but I can't make it tonight.
Kechirasan, bugun kechqurun bora olmayman.
Lisa: Oh, that's a shame. Is everything okay?
Afsus. Hammasi joyidami?
Mark: Yes, I just have a lot of work to do.
Ha, shunchaki bajarishim kerak bo‘lgan ishlarim ko‘p.
Lisa: No problem. What about tomorrow?
Muammo yo‘q. Ertaga-chi?
Mark: Tomorrow works for me.
Ertaga men uchun qulay.
Lisa: Great. Let's meet at six.
Ajoyib. Soat oltida uchrashamiz.
Mark: Perfect. See you tomorrow!
Zo‘r. Ertaga ko‘rishamiz!

5 EVERYDAY CONVERSATIONS

💬 Kundalik suhbatlar

Ingliz tilida yaxshi muloqot qilish uchun faqat grammatikani bilish yetarli emas. Suhbatni boshlash, savol berish, javob qaytarish va suhbatni davom ettirishni ham o‘rganish kerak.

Suhbatni boshlash

How's it going?
Ishlar qalay?
How have you been?
Qalaysiz? Ishlaringiz qanday?
Nice to see you.
Sizni ko‘rganimdan xursandman.
Long time no see!
Anchadan beri ko‘rishmadik!

Suhbatni davom ettirish

Really? — Rostdanmi?
That's interesting. — Bu qiziqarli ekan.
Tell me more. — Batafsilroq aytib bering.
What happened next? — Keyin nima bo‘ldi?
What do you think? — Siz nima deb o‘ylaysiz?

Tushunmaganda ishlatiladigan iboralar

Could you repeat that, please? — Iltimos, qaytara olasizmi?
Could you speak more slowly? — Sekinroq gapira olasizmi?
What does this mean? — Bu nimani anglatadi?
I'm not sure I understand. — Tushunganimga ishonchim komil emas.
Let me think. — Bir o‘ylab ko‘ray.
Maslahat: Suhbatda tushunmay qolsangiz, jim turmang. “Could you repeat that?” yoki “Could you speak more slowly?” deb so‘rang.

🗣️ Dialogue 1 — Meeting an Old Friend

Two Friends
John: Hi, David! Long time no see!
Salom, David! Anchadan beri ko‘rishmadik!
David: John! It's great to see you!
John! Seni ko‘rganimdan xursandman!
John: How have you been?
Qalaysan? Ishlaring qanday?
David: I've been good, thanks. What about you?
Yaxshi yuribman, rahmat. O‘zing-chi?
John: I'm doing well. Are you still working at the bank?
Yaxshiman. Hali ham bankda ishlayapsanmi?
David: No, I have a new job now.
Yo‘q, hozir yangi ishim bor.
John: Really? That's interesting! What do you do?
Rostdanmi? Qiziqarli ekan! Nima ish qilasan?
David: I work for a travel company.
Sayohat kompaniyasida ishlayman.
John: That sounds exciting!
Juda qiziqarli ekan!
David: It is! We should meet again soon.
Ha! Yaqinda yana uchrashishimiz kerak.

🎧 Listening 1 — Meeting an Old Friend

1. Where did David work before?

2. Where does David work now?

3. What do the friends want to do?

Dialogue 2 — Asking for Help

Two People
Visitor: Excuse me. Could you help me, please?
Kechirasiz. Menga yordam bera olasizmi?
Local person: Of course. What's the problem?
Albatta. Muammo nimada?
Visitor: I'm looking for the city library.
Shahar kutubxonasini qidiryapman.
Local person: It's not far from here.
Bu yerdan uzoq emas.
Visitor: Could you tell me how to get there?
U yerga qanday borishni ayta olasizmi?
Local person: Go straight and turn left at the traffic lights.
To‘g‘riga boring va svetoforda chapga buriling.
Visitor: Is it next to the museum?
U muzeyning yonidami?
Local person: Yes, it's opposite the museum.
Ha, muzeyning ro‘parasida.
Visitor: Thank you very much!
Katta rahmat!
Local person: You're welcome. Have a nice day!
Arzimaydi. Kuningiz yaxshi o‘tsin!

🎧 Listening 2 — Asking for Help

1. What is the visitor looking for?

2. Where should the visitor turn left?

3. Where is the library?

6 SPEAKING PRACTICE

🎤 Speak English!

Quyidagi savollarga ingliz tilida ovoz chiqarib javob bering. Har bir savolga kamida 2–3 ta gap bilan javob berishga harakat qiling.

1. What do you usually buy at the supermarket?

Odatda supermarketdan nimalar xarid qilasiz?

2. Do you prefer travelling by bus or by train? Why?

Avtobusda yoki poyezdda sayohat qilishni afzal ko‘rasizmi? Nima uchun?

3. What food do you usually order at a restaurant?

Restoranda odatda qanday taom buyurtma qilasiz?

4. How do you make plans with your friends?

Do‘stlaringiz bilan qanday qilib rejalar tuzasiz?

5. What do you say when you don't understand someone?

Birovni tushunmaganingizda nima deysiz?
