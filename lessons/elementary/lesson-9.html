1. COMPARATIVES & SUPERLATIVES nima?

Kundalik hayotda biz doimo narsalarni taqqoslaymiz. Masalan:

  • Bu uy uydan kattaroq.
  • Akmal mendan balandroq.
  • Bu kitob eng qiziqarli kitob.
  • Bugun kechagidan sovuqroq.
  • U oiladagi eng yosh bola.

Ingliz tilida bunday taqqoslashlar uchun sifatlarning maxsus shakllari ishlatiladi.

1.1. Comparative — qiyosiy daraja

Comparative ikki inson, predmet, joy yoki hodisani taqqoslash uchun ishlatiladi. O‘zbek tilidagi -roq ma'nosiga yaqin.

Ali is taller than Vali.

Ali Validan balandroq.

My house is bigger than your house.

Mening uyim sening uyingdan kattaroq.

1.2. Superlative — orttirma daraja

Superlative uch yoki undan ortiq inson yoki predmet orasida eng yuqori yoki eng past darajani ko‘rsatadi. O‘zbek tilidagi eng ma'nosiga yaqin.

Ali is the tallest boy in the class.

Ali sinfdagi eng baland bola.

This is the most interesting book.

Bu eng qiziqarli kitob.

1.3. Uchta darajani tushunib oling

DarajaEnglishUzbek
Positivetallbaland
Comparativetallerbalandroq
Superlativethe tallesteng baland
Positivebeautifulchiroyli
Comparativemore beautifulchiroyliroq
Superlativethe most beautifuleng chiroyli
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

Positive — sifatning oddiy shakli: tall.

Comparative — ikki narsani taqqoslash: taller than.

Superlative — guruh ichida eng yuqori daraja: the tallest.

Comparative = -roq. Superlative = eng.

3.7. Qaysi sifatga -ER, qaysisiga MORE?

Eng asosiy qoida:

  • Ko‘p bir bo‘g‘inli sifatlar: -er.
  • Ko‘p uzun sifatlar: more + adjective.

Uzun sifat: more + adjective + than.

Than — taqqoslashda -dan ma'nosini beradi.

4. COMPARATIVE VA SUPERLATIVE IMLO QOIDALARI

Bu qism juda muhim. Chunki o‘quvchilar ko‘pincha sifatga qo‘shimchani noto‘g‘ri qo‘shadi.

4.1. Bir bo‘g‘inli sifatlar

Odatda -er va -est qo‘shiladi.

old → older → the oldest

fast → faster → the fastest

small → smaller → the smallest

4.2. Oxiri -e bilan tugasa

Faqat -r va -st qo‘shiladi.

nice → nicer → the nicest

large → larger → the largest

4.3. Undosh + y bilan tugasa

y harfi i ga o‘zgaradi.

easy → easier → the easiest

happy → happier → the happiest

4.4. Oxirgi undosh ikki marta yozilsa

big → bigger → the biggest

hot → hotter → the hottest

thin → thinner → the thinnest

4.5. Uzun sifatlar

Comparative uchun more, superlative uchun most ishlatiladi.

beautiful → more beautiful → the most beautiful

expensive → more expensive → the most expensive

comfortable → more comfortable → the most comfortable

📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

Comparative va Superlative yasashda so‘zning oxirgi harflariga e'tibor bering. Har bir sifatga bir xil usulda qo‘shimcha qo‘shilmaydi.

5.3. Nega SUPERLATIVE oldidan THE keladi?

Superlative odatda bir guruh ichida yagona eng yuqori darajani ko‘rsatadi. Shu sababli u bilan ko‘pincha the ishlatiladi.

He is the oldest person in the family.

U oiladagi eng keksa inson.

This is the best day of my life.

Bu hayotimdagi eng yaxshi kun.

Diqqat: Superlative oldidan the ko‘pincha ishlatiladi, lekin gap tuzilishiga qarab ayrim istisnolar mavjud. Hozircha asosiy qoida sifatida the + superlative shaklini eslab qoling.

5.4. IN va OF bilan ishlatish

Superlative bilan guruhni ko‘rsatishda ko‘pincha in va of ishlatiladi.

IN — joy, hudud, tashkilot yoki guruh ichida.

She is the smartest student in the class.

U sinfdagi eng aqlli o‘quvchi.

This is the biggest city in the country.

Bu mamlakatdagi eng katta shahar.

OF — aniq guruh yoki bir nechta narsalar orasida.

He is the tallest of the three brothers.

U uch aka-ukaning eng balandi.

This is the best of all.

Bu hammasining ichida eng yaxshisi.

5.5. SUPERLATIVE bilan EVER

Ever so‘zi hayot davomida yoki hozirgacha bo‘lgan tajribani ta'kidlash uchun superlative bilan ishlatilishi mumkin.

This is the best film I have ever seen.

Bu men ko‘rgan filmlar ichidagi eng yaxshisi.

It is the most beautiful place I have ever visited.

Bu men tashrif buyurgan eng chiroyli joy.

📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING — SUPERLATIVE

Superlative — uch yoki undan ortiq narsalar orasida eng yuqori yoki eng past darajani bildiradi.

Qisqa sifat: the + adjective + est.

Uzun sifat: the most + adjective.

Superlative bilan ko‘pincha the ishlatiladi.

IN — guruh yoki joy ichida; OF — aniq guruh orasida.

6. IRREGULAR ADJECTIVES — NOTO‘G‘RI SHAKLLAR

Ba'zi sifatlar Comparative va Superlative darajalarida oddiy qoidalarga bo‘ysunmaydi. Ularni yodlab olish kerak.

6.1. GOOD → BETTER → THE BEST

Good — yaxshi. Better — yaxshiroq. The best — eng yaxshi.

Your English is better than before.

Ingliz tilingiz avvalgidan yaxshiroq.

This is the best book for beginners.

Bu boshlovchilar uchun eng yaxshi kitob.

6.2. BAD → WORSE → THE WORST

Bad — yomon. Worse — yomonroq. The worst — eng yomon.

The weather is worse today.

Bugun ob-havo yomonroq.

That was the worst day of the week.

Bu haftaning eng yomon kuni edi.

6.3. FAR → FARTHER / FURTHER

Farther ko‘proq jismoniy masofani bildiradi. Further esa masofa bilan birga qo‘shimcha, yanada yoki batafsil ma'nosida ham ishlatiladi. Ayrim vaziyatlarda ikkalasi ham masofa uchun ishlatilishi mumkin.

My school is farther than yours.

Mening maktabim senikidan uzoqroq.

For further information, contact us.

Qo‘shimcha ma'lumot uchun biz bilan bog‘laning.

6.4. LITTLE → LESS → THE LEAST

Bu shakllar miqdor yoki darajaning kamligini bildiradi.

I have less free time than you.

Menda sendagidan kamroq bo‘sh vaqt bor.

This is the least expensive option.

Bu eng kam xarajatli variant.

6.5. MUCH / MANY → MORE → THE MOST

Much va many ko‘p ma'nosini beradi. Ularning Comparative shakli more, Superlative shakli the most.

I have more books than my brother.

Menda akamnikidan ko‘proq kitob bor.

She has the most friends in our group.

U guruhimizda eng ko‘p do‘stga ega.

📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

GOOD → BETTER → THE BEST.

BAD → WORSE → THE WORST.

FAR → FARTHER / FURTHER → THE FARTHEST / THE FURTHEST.

LITTLE → LESS → THE LEAST.

MUCH / MANY → MORE → THE MOST.

Bu shakllar oddiy -er va -est qoidasi bilan yasalmasligini unutmang.

7. KENGAYTIRILGAN TAQQOSLASH TUZILMALARI

7.1. AS ... AS — kabi / ...chalik

Ikki inson yoki predmetning bir xil darajada ekanini bildirish uchun as + adjective + as ishlatiladi.

Subject + be + as + adjective + as + noun/pronoun

Ali is as tall as Vali.

Ali Vali kabi baland.

This book is as interesting as that one.

Bu kitob anavisi kabi qiziqarli.

My room is as large as yours.

Mening xonam senikidek katta.

As ... as tuzilmasida sifat oddiy shaklda qoladi. Taller yoki more beautiful ishlatmaymiz.

❌ Ali is as taller as Vali.
✅ Ali is as tall as Vali.

7.2. NOT AS ... AS — ...chalik emas

Biror narsa boshqasiga teng darajada emasligini bildiramiz.

Subject + be + not as + adjective + as

This car is not as fast as that car.

Bu mashina anavi mashinachalik tez emas.

English is not as difficult as I thought.

Ingliz tili men o‘ylaganchalik qiyin emas.

7.3. LESS ... THAN — kamroq / unchalik emas

Biror sifatning darajasi boshqasiga nisbatan pastroq ekanini ko‘rsatadi.

less + adjective + than

This exercise is less difficult than the last one.

Bu mashq avvalgisiga qaraganda kamroq qiyin.

This hotel is less expensive than that one.

Bu mehmonxona anavisidan arzonroq.

7.4. THE SAME AS — bilan bir xil

Ikki narsaning bir xil ekanini yoki o‘xshashligini bildirish uchun ishlatiladi.

My phone is the same as yours.

Mening telefonim senikiga bir xil.

We are the same age.

Biz teng yoshdamiz.

7.5. DIFFERENT FROM — dan farqli

Ikki narsa bir xil emasligini ko‘rsatadi.

My opinion is different from yours.

Mening fikrim senikidan farq qiladi.

This city is different from my hometown.

Bu shahar mening tug‘ilgan shahrimdan farq qiladi.

7.6. MUCH / FAR / A LOT + COMPARATIVE

Comparative darajani yanada kuchaytirish uchun much, far yoki a lot ishlatiladi. Ular ko‘pincha ancha, juda ham ma'nosini beradi.

much / far / a lot + comparative adjective

This house is much bigger than mine.

Bu uy menikidan ancha kattaroq.

English is far more interesting now.

Ingliz tili hozir ancha qiziqarliroq.

This phone is a lot cheaper.

Bu telefon ancha arzonroq.

7.7. A LITTLE / SLIGHTLY + COMPARATIVE

Taqqoslashdagi farq kichikligini bildirish uchun a little yoki slightly ishlatiladi.

This room is a little bigger.

Bu xona biroz kattaroq.

Today is slightly colder.

Bugun biroz sovuqroq.

7.8. EVEN + COMPARATIVE

Even taqqoslashni kuchaytiradi va hatto undan ham ma'nosini beradi.

This book is even better than the first one.

Bu kitob birinchisidan hatto yaxshiroq.

7.9. THE MORE..., THE MORE...

Bu tuzilma bir narsa ortishi bilan boshqa narsa ham ortishini bildiradi.

The + comparative ..., the + comparative ...

The more you practise, the better you become.

Qancha ko‘p mashq qilsang, shuncha yaxshilanib borasan.

The older I get, the wiser I become.

Yoshim ulg‘aygan sari dono bo‘lib boraman.

7.10. COMPARATIVE AND COMPARATIVE

Biror holat asta-sekin o‘zgarib borayotganini bildirish uchun comparative shaklni takrorlaymiz.

Comparative + and + Comparative

It is getting colder and colder.

Havo tobora sovuqlashib bormoqda.

English is becoming more and more interesting.

Ingliz tili tobora qiziqarli bo‘lib bormoqda.

7.11. ONE OF THE + SUPERLATIVE + PLURAL NOUN

Biror kishi yoki narsa bir guruhdagi eng yaxshi yoki eng muhimlardan biri ekanini bildiradi.

One of the + superlative + plural noun

London is one of the most famous cities in the world.

London dunyodagi eng mashhur shaharlardan biridir.

She is one of the best students in our school.

U maktabimizdagi eng yaxshi o‘quvchilardan biri.

Muhim: One of the dan keyin ot ko‘plikda keladi: one of the best students, one of the biggest cities.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING — KENGAYTIRILGAN TUZILMALAR

AS + adjective + AS — ... kabi / ...chalik.

NOT AS + adjective + AS — ...chalik emas.

LESS + adjective + THAN — kamroq.

THE SAME AS — bir xil.

DIFFERENT FROM — dan farqli.

MUCH / FAR / A LOT + comparative — ancha ...roq.

A LITTLE / SLIGHTLY + comparative — biroz ...roq.

THE + comparative, THE + comparative — qancha ..., shuncha ...

Comparative + and + comparative — tobora ...roq.

ONE OF THE + superlative + plural noun — eng ...lardan biri.

8. COMPARATIVE VA SUPERLATIVE GAP TUZILISHI

8.1. BE FE'LI BILAN

Comparative va Superlative sifatlar ko‘pincha am/is/are/was/were fe'llaridan keyin keladi.

Subject + am/is/are + comparative + than + object Subject + am/is/are + the + superlative + noun/group

My sister is younger than me.

Singlim mendan yoshroq.

My sister is the youngest in our family.

Singlim oilamizdagi eng yoshi kichigi.

8.2. OTHER VERBS BILAN

Taqqoslash faqat be fe'li bilan cheklanmaydi. Boshqa fe'llar bilan ham ishlatiladi.

I work harder than my brother.

Men akamdan ko‘ra ko‘proq mehnat qilaman.

She speaks more clearly than me.

U mendan aniqroq gapiradi.

8.3. INKOR GAP

This book isn't more expensive than that one.

Bu kitob anavisidan qimmatroq emas.

He isn't the tallest student.

U eng baland o‘quvchi emas.

8.4. SAVOL GAP

Is your house bigger than mine?

Uying menikidan kattaroqmi?

Who is the oldest in your family?

Oilangizda kim eng katta?

Which city is the most beautiful?

Qaysi shahar eng chiroyli?

📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

Comparative: Subject + be / verb + comparative + than + object.

Superlative: Subject + be + the + superlative + group/place.

Savol yasashda be fe'li egadan oldinga chiqadi: Is it bigger?

9. ENG KO‘P UCHRAYDIGAN XATOLAR

10. TAQQOSLASHNI O‘RGANISH UCHUN NAMUNAVIY MATNLAR

10.1. Two Cities

My hometown is smaller than the capital city. The capital is more crowded and more expensive. However, my hometown is quieter and cleaner. The people in my town are very friendly. The capital has more shops, bigger buildings and better public transport. I think both cities are interesting, but I prefer my hometown because life is more peaceful there.

10.2. My Two Friends

Aziz and Jamshid are my friends. Aziz is taller than Jamshid, but Jamshid is younger. Aziz is more serious, while Jamshid is funnier. Both are hardworking and helpful. Jamshid is the fastest runner in our group. Aziz is the most careful student. They are different, but both are excellent friends.

10.3. O‘zingizni tekshiring

  1. Which city is smaller?
  2. Why does the writer prefer the hometown?
  3. Who is taller, Aziz or Jamshid?
  4. Who is the fastest runner?
  5. Who is the most careful student?

40 ta so‘zning barchasini lug‘at daftaringizga yozing. Inglizcha so‘z, talaffuz, o‘zbekcha tarjima va misol gapni birga o‘rganing.

12. FOUR SKILLS — 4 KO‘NIKMA

12.1. READING — A Visit to the City

Last weekend, I visited two cities with my family. The first city was smaller and quieter than the second one. It had beautiful parks and clean streets. The second city was bigger, busier and more modern. It had the largest shopping centre in the region.

We visited many interesting places. The most beautiful place was an old park near the river. The food in the second city was more expensive, but the restaurants were more comfortable. My brother thought the second city was better, but I preferred the first one.

For me, a city does not have to be the biggest or the most modern to be a wonderful place. Sometimes, smaller towns are more peaceful and friendlier.

Reading questions

  1. Which city was quieter?
  2. Which city was more modern?
  3. What was the most beautiful place?
  4. Which city had more expensive food?
  5. Which city did the writer prefer?
  6. Does the writer think the biggest city is always the best?

12.2. LISTENING — Choosing a New Phone

Dialogue:

Tom: I want to buy a new phone. This one is cheaper than that one.

Anna: Yes, but the other phone has a better camera.

Tom: Is it much more expensive?

Anna: A little. But it is also faster and has a bigger screen.

Tom: Which one is the best choice?

Anna: It depends on what you need. The cheaper phone is good, but the more expensive one is more powerful.

Listening questions

  1. What does Tom want to buy?
  2. Which phone is cheaper?
  3. Which phone has a better camera?
  4. Which phone has a bigger screen?
  5. What does Anna say about the best choice?

12.3. SPEAKING — GAPIRISH MASHQI

Quyidagi savollarga ingliz tilida ovoz chiqarib javob bering. Har bir javobda kamida 2 ta gap ishlatishga harakat qiling.

  1. Is your hometown bigger or smaller than the capital city?
  2. Who is the oldest person in your family?
  3. What is the most interesting book you have read?
  4. Is English easier or more difficult than you expected?
  5. Which is more comfortable, a car or a bus?
  6. What is the best day of the week for you?
  7. Are you more confident now than last year?
  8. What is the most important thing in your life?
  9. Which is better for learning: reading or listening?
  10. What is one of the most beautiful places you know?
Namuna:
"My hometown is smaller than the capital city. It is quieter and less crowded. I think it is one of the most peaceful places."

12.4. WRITING — Compare Two Places

Mavzu: Compare two cities, towns or places you know.

100–150 so‘z yozing. Matningizda quyidagilardan foydalaning:

  • kamida 4 ta Comparative;
  • kamida 2 ta Superlative;
  • as...as yoki not as...as;
  • more yoki less;
  • than, in yoki of.

Yozishni boshlash uchun namuna:

My hometown is smaller than the capital, but it is quieter and more peaceful.

Mening shahrim poytaxtdan kichikroq, ammo tinchroq va osoyishtaroq.

13. HOMEWORK — UYGA VAZIFA

Uyga vazifa bitta bo‘limdan iborat. Unda 5 ta topshiriq bor. Barcha ishlarni daftarda bajaring.

📒 1-TOPSHIRIQ — GRAMMAR

Qavs ichidagi sifatlarni Comparative shakliga o‘zgartirib, gaplarni to‘ldiring.

  1. My brother is ______ than me. (tall)
  2. This book is ______ than that one. (interesting)
  3. Today is ______ than yesterday. (hot)
  4. English is ______ than I expected. (easy)
  5. This bag is ______ than mine. (heavy)
  6. A train is ______ than a bus. (fast)
  7. My room is ______ than yours. (large)
  8. This phone is ______ than that one. (expensive)
  9. She is ______ than her sister. (happy)
  10. Health is ______ than money. (important)
📒 2-TOPSHIRIQ — GRAMMAR

Qavs ichidagi sifatlarni Superlative shakliga o‘zgartirib, gaplarni to‘ldiring.

  1. He is ______ boy in the class. (tall)
  2. This is ______ book in the shop. (cheap)
  3. She is ______ student in our group. (careful)
  4. It is ______ day of the year. (hot)
  5. This is ______ place in the city. (beautiful)
  6. He is ______ person in his family. (old)
  7. That was ______ film I have ever seen. (interesting)
  8. This is ______ question in the test. (easy)
  9. It is ______ building in the town. (big)
  10. She is one of ______ students in the school. (good)
📒 3-TOPSHIRIQ — VOCABULARY

Bugungi 40 ta so‘zni yodlang. Har bir so‘z bilan bittadan o‘z gaplaringizni tuzing. Jami 40 ta gap yozing.

Kamida 10 ta gapda Comparative yoki Superlative shaklidan foydalaning.
