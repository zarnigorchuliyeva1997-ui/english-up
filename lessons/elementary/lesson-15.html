Assalomu alaykum, aziz o‘quvchi!

Bugungi darsimizda ingliz tilidagi eng muhim zamonlardan biri — Past Simple bilan chuqur tanishamiz. Bu zamon yordamida kecha nima qilganimizni, o‘tgan hafta qayerga borganimizni, bolaligimizda nimalar bilan shug‘ullanganimizni ayta olamiz.

2 Past Simple nima?

Past Simple — o‘tmishda sodir bo‘lgan va o‘tmishda tugagan ish-harakat yoki holatni ifodalovchi zamon.

Subject + Verb 2 + Object / Complement

Bu yerda:

  • Subject — ega: I, you, he, she, we, they.
  • Verb 2 — fe’lning ikkinchi shakli.
  • Object — to‘ldiruvchi yoki gapning qolgan qismi.

Misollar

I watched TV yesterday.Men kecha televizor ko‘rdim.
She visited her grandmother.U buvisinikiga bordi.
They played football last Sunday.Ular o‘tgan yakshanba futbol o‘ynashdi.
We learned English last year.Biz o‘tgan yili ingliz tilini o‘rgandik.

2.1. Past Simple qachon ishlatiladi?

1. O‘tmishda tugagan ish-harakat.

I cleaned my room yesterday.Men kecha xonamni tozaladim.

2. Aniq o‘tgan vaqt bilan.

He arrived two hours ago.U ikki soat oldin yetib keldi.

3. O‘tmishda ketma-ket sodir bo‘lgan voqealar.

I woke up, washed my face and had breakfast.Men uyg‘ondim, yuzimni yuvdim va nonushta qildim.

4. O‘tmishdagi odat yoki takrorlangan ish.

When I was a child, I played outside every day.Men bolaligimda har kuni tashqarida o‘ynardim.

5. O‘tmishdagi holat yoki fakt.

My family lived in a small village.Mening oilam kichik qishloqda yashagan.

2.2. Past Simple va hozirgi zamon farqi

Present SimplePast Simple
I work every day.
Men har kuni ishlayman.		I worked yesterday.
Men kecha ishladim.
She goes to school.
U maktabga boradi.		She went to school.
U maktabga bordi.
We eat breakfast.
Biz nonushta qilamiz.		We ate breakfast.
Biz nonushta qildik.

Present Simple odat yoki umumiy holatni bildiradi. Past Simple esa o‘tmishda sodir bo‘lib tugagan ishni bildiradi.

3 Past Simple gap shakllari

3.1. Darak gap — Positive

Subject + Verb 2 + ...

Darak gapda asosiy fe’lning ikkinchi shakli ishlatiladi.

I visited my friend.Men do‘stimnikiga bordim.
She cooked dinner.U kechki ovqat tayyorladi.
They bought a new car.Ular yangi mashina sotib olishdi.
He wrote a letter.U xat yozdi.
Muhim: darak gapda I, you, he, she, it, we, they uchun fe’lning o‘tgan shakli bir xil ishlatiladi. He/she/it uchun alohida -s qo‘shilmaydi.

3.2. Inkor gap — Negative

Subject + did not (didn't) + Verb 1 + ...

Inkor gapda did not yoki uning qisqa shakli didn't ishlatiladi. Undan keyin fe’lning birinchi shakli keladi.

I didn't watch TV.Men televizor ko‘rmadim.
She didn't go to school.U maktabga bormadi.
They didn't buy bread.Ular non sotib olishmadi.
He didn't understand me.U meni tushunmadi.
Eng muhim qoida!

Didn't ishlatilganidan keyin Verb 2 emas, Verb 1 ishlatiladi.

❌ I didn't went.

✅ I didn't go.

3.3. So‘roq gap — Question

Did + Subject + Verb 1 + ...?

So‘roq gap boshida Did keladi. Asosiy fe’l birinchi shaklda bo‘ladi.

Did you work yesterday?Siz kecha ishladingizmi?
Did she call you?U sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qildimi?
Did they arrive on time?Ular o‘z vaqtida yetib kelishdimi?
Did he see the film?U filmni ko‘rdimi?

3.4. Qisqa javoblar — Short Answers

Qisqa javobda asosiy fe’lni qayta aytish shart emas. Did yoki didn't bilan javob beramiz.

4 Was / Were — Past Simple of To Be

Was va Were — am/is/are fe’llarining o‘tgan zamondagi shakllari.

Hozirgi zamonO‘tgan zamonEga
AmWasI
IsWasHe / She / It
AreWereYou / We / They
Was — I, he, she, it bilan.
Were — you, we, they bilan.

4.1. Darak gap

Subject + was / were + ...
I was tired yesterday.Men kecha charchagan edim.
She was at home.U uyda edi.
We were happy.Biz xursand edik.
They were at school.Ular maktabda edilar.

4.2. Inkor gap

Subject + was not / were not + ...
To‘liq shaklQisqa shakl
was notwasn't
were notweren't
He wasn't at work.U ishda emas edi.
They weren't ready.Ular tayyor emas edilar.

4.3. So‘roq gap

Was / Were + Subject + ...?
Was she ill yesterday?U kecha kasal edimi?
Were you busy?Siz band edingizmi?

Qisqa javoblar:

  • Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
  • Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.

4.4. Was/Were va Did farqi

Bu ikki yordamchi bir xil vazifani bajarmaydi.

Was he at home?U uyda edimi?
Did he go home?U uyga bordimi?

Birinchi gapda joylashuv yoki holat so‘ralmoqda. Ikkinchi gapda harakat so‘ralmoqda.

❌ Did you were tired?
✅ Were you tired?
❌ Did she was happy?
✅ Was she happy?
❌ Did he at home?
✅ Was he at home?

4.5. There was / There were

O‘tmishda biror narsa bor bo‘lganini bildirish uchun ishlatiladi.

There was + birlik ot
There were + ko‘plik ot
There was a book on the table.Stol ustida kitob bor edi.
There were three children in the garden.Bog‘da uchta bola bor edi.
There wasn't any milk.Sut yo‘q edi.
Were there many people?Odamlar ko‘p edimi?
✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Was/Were jadvali, inkor va so‘roq formulalari, There was/There were qoidalari va misollarni yozing.

5 Regular va Irregular Verbs

5.1. Regular Verbs — to‘g‘ri fe’llar

Regular fe’llarning Past Simple shakli odatda -ed qo‘shimchasi yordamida yasaladi.

Verb 1 + ed = Verb 2

clean → cleanedtozalamoq → tozaladi

open → openedochmoq → ochdi

-ed uch xil talaffuz qilinadi:

5.4. Irregular Verbs — noto‘g‘ri fe’llar

Irregular fe’llar odatda -ed bilan yasalmaydi. Ularning o‘tgan zamon shaklini alohida o‘rganish kerak.

✅ went, ate, bought — to‘g‘ri.

7 Qo‘shimcha muhim qoidalar

7.1. Used to — o‘tmishdagi odat

Used to o‘tmishda muntazam bo‘lgan, ammo hozir odatda mavjud bo‘lmagan odat yoki holatni ifodalaydi.

Subject + used to + Verb 1
I used to play football.Men ilgari futbol o‘ynardim.
She used to live here.U ilgari shu yerda yashardi.

Inkor va so‘roq shakllarida did ishlatilganda used to → use to bo‘ladi.

Subject + didn't use to + V1
Did + subject + use to + V1?
Did you use to play tennis?Siz ilgari tennis o‘ynarmidingiz?
7.2. Was born / Were born

Tug‘ilganlikni aytish uchun be born iborasining o‘tgan shakli ishlatiladi.

I was born in Uzbekistan.Men O‘zbekistonda tug‘ilganman.
They were born in 2010.Ular 2010-yilda tug‘ilgan.
When were you born?Siz qachon tug‘ilgansiz?
7.3. Could — o'tmishdagi qobiliyat

Could o'tmishda qila olgan qobiliyatni ifodalaydi.

Subject + could + Verb 1
I could swim when I was six.Men olti yoshimda suza olardim.
She couldn't read at that age.U o‘sha yoshda o‘qiy olmasdi.
Could you ride a bike?Siz velosiped hayday olarmidingiz?
7.4. Past Simple bilan bog‘lovchilar
  • And — va: I got up and had breakfast.
  • But — lekin: I wanted to go, but I stayed home.
  • Because — chunki: I stayed home because I was tired.
  • Then — keyin: I finished work, then I went home.
  • After that — shundan keyin: After that, we watched a film.
  • Before — oldin: I called her before I left.
  • Finally — nihoyat: Finally, we arrived home.
7.5. Past Simple bilan so‘roq so‘zlari

What, where, when, why, who, how, how long kabi so‘zlar savol boshida keladi.

What did you do last weekend?O‘tgan dam olish kunlari nima qildingiz?
Who did you visit?Kimnikiga bordingiz?
How did you get there?U yerga qanday bordingiz?
7.6. Subject questions — ega haqida savol

Who gapning egasi bo‘lsa, odatda Did ishlatilmaydi. Who + Verb 2 shakli qo‘llanadi.

Who called you?Sizga kim qo‘ng‘iroq qildi?
Who broke the window?Derazani kim sindirdi?

Ammo kimnidir obyekt sifatida so‘rasak, did ishlatiladi:

Who did you call?Siz kimga qo‘ng‘iroq qildingiz?
✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Har bir qo‘shimcha qoidaning formulasini va kamida 2 ta misolini yozing.

10 Reading — A Busy Day Yesterday

Matnni avval mustaqil o‘qing. Keyin tarjimasini tushunishga harakat qiling.

A Busy Day Yesterday

Yesterday was a very busy day for me. I woke up at six o'clock in the morning. First, I washed my face and brushed my teeth. Then I made breakfast for my family. We ate eggs, bread and cheese. After breakfast, I went to work by bus. I arrived at work at nine o'clock. I met many customers and helped them. At lunchtime, I ate a sandwich and drank some water. I finished work at seven in the evening. After work, I went to the supermarket and bought some food. When I got home, my children were happy to see me. We had dinner together and talked about our day. Later, I helped my children with their homework. Finally, I read a book and went to bed at eleven o'clock. I was tired, but I was happy because I spent time with my family.

Matn tarjimasi

Kecha men uchun juda band kun bo‘ldi. Men ertalab soat oltida uyg‘ondim. Avval yuzimni yuvdim va tishlarimni tozaladim. Keyin oilam uchun nonushta tayyorladim. Biz tuxum, non va pishloq yedik. Nonushtadan keyin avtobusda ishga bordim. Ishga soat to‘qqizda yetib keldim. Ko‘plab mijozlar bilan uchrashdim va ularga yordam berdim. Tushlik paytida sendvich yedim va suv ichdim. Kechqurun soat yettida ishimni tugatdim. Ishdan keyin supermarketga bordim va oziq-ovqat sotib oldim. Uyga kelganimda bolalarim meni ko‘rganidan xursand bo‘lishdi. Biz birga kechki ovqat yedik va kunimiz haqida suhbatlashdik. Keyin bolalarimga uy vazifalarida yordam berdim. Nihoyat, kitob o‘qidim va soat o‘n birda uxlashga yotdim. Men charchagan edim, lekin oilam bilan vaqt o‘tkazganim uchun xursand edim.

11 Listening — My Last Weekend

Quyidagi matnni ovoz chiqarib tinglang. Birinchi marta tinglaganda umumiy mazmunni tushunishga harakat qiling. Ikkinchi marta tinglaganda tafsilotlarga e’tibor bering.

Ovoz qurilmangizdagi inglizcha ovozga bog‘liq. Agar ovoz chiqmasa, qurilmaning matnni ovozga aylantirish sozlamalarini tekshiring.

My Last Weekend

Last weekend, I visited my uncle. I travelled to his village with my family. We left home early in the morning and arrived there before noon. My uncle welcomed us and prepared a delicious lunch. After lunch, we walked around the village. I took some beautiful photos and talked to my cousins. In the evening, we watched an interesting film. On Sunday, I helped my uncle in his garden. We planted some flowers and watered the trees. I returned home in the afternoon. I enjoyed my weekend because I spent a wonderful time with my relatives.

14 Katta mashqlar to‘plami

16 HOMEWORK — Uy vazifasi

Uy vazifasiga jiddiy yondashing!

Bugungi darsdagi bilimlarni mustahkamlash uchun barcha to‘rtta topshiriqni bajaring. Javoblar daftarga yozilishi va ovoz chiqarib mashq qilinishi kerak.

TASK 1 • GRAMMAR

Grammatika bo‘yicha katta topshiriq

A. 20 ta darak gap tuzing. Kamida 10 tasida irregular fe’l ishlating.

B. 15 ta inkor gap tuzing. Didn't dan keyin V1 ishlatilishiga e’tibor bering.

C. 15 ta so‘roq gap tuzing. Kamida 8 tasida WH-question ishlating.

D. Was/Were bilan 15 ta gap tuzing: 5 ta darak, 5 ta inkor, 5 ta so‘roq.

E. 20 ta fe’lning V1 va V2 shaklini yodlang. Ertasi kuni o‘zingizni tekshiring.

F. 15 ta xato gapni toping va to‘g‘rilang. Har bir xato sababini yozing.

TASK 3 • READING & LISTENING

O‘qish va tinglash

Reading: “My Last Holiday” mavzusida 180–220 so‘zli matn yozing yoki o‘zingizga mos matn tanlang. Uni o‘qing, tarjima qiling va 10 ta Past Simple fe’lini ajrating.

Listening: Darsdagi “My Last Weekend” matnini kamida 3 marta tinglang. Birinchi marta umumiy mazmunni, ikkinchi marta fe’llarni, uchinchi marta talaffuzni kuzating.

Comprehension: Tinglagan matn bo‘yicha 10 ta savol tuzing va ularga javob yozing.

Retelling: Matnni o‘z so‘zlaringiz bilan 8–10 ta gapda qayta hikoya qiling.

DAFTARDA BAJARING
TASK 4 • SPEAKING & WRITING

Gapirish va yozish

Speaking 1: “What did I do yesterday?” mavzusida 2 daqiqa gapiring.

Speaking 2: “My last weekend” mavzusida 2–3 daqiqalik hikoya tayyorlang.

Speaking 3: Darsdagi 20 ta savolga ovoz chiqarib javob bering.

Writing 1: “A Memorable Day in My Life” mavzusida 150–180 so‘z yozing.

Writing 2: “My Childhood” mavzusida 100–120 so‘z yozing. Used to va could dan foydalaning.

Writing 3: O‘zingiz haqingizda 10 ta WH-question va javob yozing.
