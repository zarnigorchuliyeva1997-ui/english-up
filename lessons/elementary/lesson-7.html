1. LESSON INTRODUCTION

Assalomu alaykum! Bugungi darsimiz ingliz tilidagi eng muhim grammatik mavzulardan biri — ARTICLES.

O‘zbek tilida ko‘pincha “kitob”, “bir kitob”, “o‘sha kitob” deb aytamiz. Ingliz tilida esa ot oldidan kichik so‘zlar — artikllar ishlatiladi.

I have a book.
Menda bir kitob bor.
The book is interesting.
O‘sha kitob qiziqarli.
Books are useful.
Kitoblar foydali.

Uchta misolni taqqoslang:

  • a book — qandaydir bitta kitob, hali aniq emas;
  • the book — ma’lum, aniq kitob;
  • books — umumiy kitoblar, artiklsiz.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Ingliz tilida uch xil holat bor: A/AN — noaniq artikl; THE — aniq artikl; ZERO ARTICLE — artikl ishlatilmaydi.

2. ARTICLES NIMA?

Article — ot oldidan kelib, biz gapirayotgan narsa aniqmi yoki noaniqmi ekanini ko‘rsatadigan grammatik vosita.

Ingliz tilida uchta asosiy holatni o‘rganamiz:

ArticleVazifasiMisol
ANoaniq, birlikdagi sanaladigan ota car
ANNoaniq, birlikdagi sanaladigan otan apple
THEAniq, ma’lum otthe car
ZERO ARTICLEArtiklsiz ishlatishCars are useful.

2.1. Nega artikl kerak?

Chunki artikl gapning ma’nosini o‘zgartirishi mumkin.

I saw a dog.
Men bir itni ko‘rdim. (Qaysi itligi hali noma’lum.)
The dog was black.
O‘sha it qora edi. (Endi qaysi it haqida gap ketayotgani ma’lum.)

Birinchi gapda it haqida ilk bor gapiryapmiz. Ikkinchi gapda esa o‘sha itni nazarda tutyapmiz.

2.2. Articles qaysi so‘z bilan bog‘lanadi?

Artikllar asosan otlar (nouns) bilan bog‘lanadi. Ba’zan sifat bilan ot orasida keladi.

a car
mashina
a beautiful car
chiroyli mashina
an interesting book
qiziqarli kitob
the beautiful house
o‘sha chiroyli uy
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Article ot oldidan keladi. Agar sifat ot oldidan kelsa, article sifatning oldiga qo‘yiladi: a beautiful house, an interesting story.

2.3. Eng muhim cheklov

DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: A/AN — faqat birlikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan. THE — birlik, ko‘plik va sanalmaydigan ot bilan ishlatilishi mumkin.

3. A — NOANIQ ARTIKL

A “bir” degan ma’noga yaqin. Biz biror narsani birinchi marta tilga olayotganimizda yoki aynan qaysi narsa ekanligi muhim bo‘lmaganda ishlatamiz.

3.1. A qachon ishlatiladi?

Otning talaffuzdagi birinchi tovushi undosh bo‘lsa, birlikdagi sanaladigan ot oldidan a qo‘yamiz.

a book — bir kitob
a car — bir mashina
a teacher — bir o‘qituvchi
a house — bir uy
a student — bir o‘quvchi
a phone — bir telefon

3.2. Birinchi marta tilga olingan narsa

I saw a bird in the garden.
Men bog‘da bir qushni ko‘rdim.
She bought a dress.
U bir ko‘ylak sotib oldi.
He has a new job.
Uning yangi ishi bor.

Bu misollarda qaysi qush, qaysi ko‘ylak yoki qaysi ish ekani hali aniqlashtirilmagan.

3.3. Kasb va lavozimlar

Biror kishining kasbini aytganda birlikdagi kasb nomi oldidan odatda a/an ishlatiladi.

I am a teacher.
Men o‘qituvchiman.
She is a doctor.
U shifokor.
He is an engineer.
U muhandis.
My father is a driver.
Mening otam haydovchi.

3.4. Biror narsaning turi yoki guruhiga mansublik

A whale is a mammal.
Kit sutemizuvchi hayvondir.
A rose is a flower.
Atirgul gul hisoblanadi.

3.5. Bir dona yoki bir birlik ma’nosida

I need a pen.
Menga bitta ruchka kerak.
Can I have a ticket?
Bitta chipta olsam bo‘ladimi?

3.6. A bilan ishlatiladigan vaqt va miqdor ifodalari

twice a week
haftasiga ikki marta
once a day
kuniga bir marta
60 kilometres an hour
soatiga 60 kilometr
three times a month
oyiga uch marta
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: A ishlatiladi: 1) birlik sanaladigan ot bilan; 2) birinchi marta tilga olingan noaniq narsa bilan; 3) kasbni aytganda; 4) tur yoki guruhni ifodalaganda; 5) once a day, twice a week kabi ifodalarda.

3.7. A qachon ishlatilmaydi?

  • Ko‘plikdagi ot bilan: a books — noto‘g‘ri.
  • Uncountable ot bilan: a milk — oddiy ma’noda noto‘g‘ri.
  • Ism oldidan: a John — odatda noto‘g‘ri.
  • Ko‘plikdagi umumiy ma’noda: Dogs are friendly.
Lekin “a coffee” kabi ifodalar buyurtma yoki bir finjon qahva ma’nosida ishlatilishi mumkin. Bu yerda ichimlikning bir porsiyasi nazarda tutiladi.

4.2. Nega A UNIVERSITY, lekin AN UMBRELLA?

Bu juda muhim qoida. Biz harfga emas, tovushga qaraymiz.

University so‘zi yozuvda U harfi bilan boshlanadi, ammo talaffuzda boshida /j/ tovushi bor. Bu tovush “yes” so‘zining boshidagi tovushga o‘xshaydi. Shuning uchun undosh tovush hisoblanadi.

a university

Umbrella esa /ʌ/ unli tovushi bilan boshlanadi.

an umbrella
bir soyabon

4.3. H harfi bilan boshlanadigan so‘zlar

Ba’zi H bilan boshlanadigan so‘zlarda H talaffuz qilinmaydi. Shuning uchun an ishlatiladi.

4.4. Qisqartmalar va harflar

Qisqartma qanday talaffuz qilinishiga qarab A yoki AN ishlatiladi.

DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: A yoki AN tanlashda yozuvdagi harfga emas, talaffuzdagi birinchi tovushga qaraymiz. Unli tovush — AN; undosh tovush — A.

5. THE — ANIQ ARTIKL

THE — aniq artikl. Gapiruvchi va tinglovchi qaysi narsa yoki shaxs haqida gap ketayotganini bilsa, the ishlatiladi.

THE birlik, ko‘plik va sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ishlatilishi mumkin.

5.1. Oldin tilga olingan narsa

Bu eng muhim qoida: birinchi marta a/an, keyingi safar the.

I saw a dog.
Men bir itni ko‘rdim.
The dog was very big.
O‘sha it juda katta edi.
She bought a book. The book is expensive.
U bir kitob sotib oldi. O‘sha kitob qimmat.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Biror narsani birinchi marta noaniq tilga olsak A/AN, keyin o‘sha narsani yana eslasak THE ishlatamiz.

5.2. Vaziyatdan ma’lum bo‘lgan narsa

Ba’zan narsa oldin aytilmagan bo‘lsa ham, vaziyatdan qaysi narsa ekani ma’lum bo‘ladi.

Close the door, please.
Iltimos, eshikni yoping. (Qaysi eshikligi vaziyatdan ma’lum.)
Turn off the light.
Chiroqni o‘chiring.
Where is the bathroom?
Hojatxona qayerda?

5.3. Dunyoda yagona bo‘lgan narsalar

Odatda yagona yoki hammaga ma’lum bo‘lgan obyektlar bilan THE ishlatiladi.

The sun is very hot.

5.4. Aniqlovchi bilan kelgan otlar

Ot qaysi ekanligi qo‘shimcha ma’lumot bilan aniq ko‘rsatilsa, THE ishlatilishi mumkin.

The book on the table is mine.
Stol ustidagi kitob meniki.
The man in the blue shirt is my father.
Ko‘k ko‘ylakdagi erkak mening otam.
The girl who lives next door is a student.
Qo‘shni uyda yashaydigan qiz o‘quvchi.

5.5. Eng yuqori daraja — Superlatives

5.6. Tartib sonlari bilan

First, second, third kabi tartib sonlar oldidan odatda THE ishlatiladi.

5.7. THE bilan birlik, ko‘plik va uncountable

The apple is red.
O‘sha olma qizil.
The apples are red.
O‘sha olmalar qizil.
The water is cold.
O‘sha suv sovuq.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: THE ishlatiladi: 1) oldin aytilgan narsa bilan; 2) vaziyatdan aniq bo‘lgan narsa bilan; 3) yagona obyektlar bilan; 4) aniqlovchi bilan aniq ko‘rsatilgan ot bilan; 5) superlative va tartib sonlari oldidan.

6. ZERO ARTICLE — ARTIKL ISHLATILMAYDIGAN HOLATLAR

Ingliz tilida har bir ot oldidan artikl qo‘yilavermaydi. Ba’zan A, AN yoki THE ishlatmaslik kerak. Bu holat Zero Article deyiladi.

6.1. Ko‘plikdagi otlar umumiy ma’noda

Biror guruh yoki tur haqida umumiy gapirsak, artikl ishlatmaymiz.

Dogs are friendly animals.
Itlar do‘stona hayvonlardir.
Books are important.
Kitoblar muhim.
Teachers help students.
O‘qituvchilar o‘quvchilarga yordam beradilar.

6.2. Uncountable otlar umumiy ma’noda

Water is important for life.
Suv hayot uchun muhim.
Milk contains calcium.
Sut tarkibida kalsiy bor.
Money cannot buy happiness.
Pul baxtni sotib ololmaydi.

Agar aniq suv yoki aniq pul haqida gapirsak, THE ishlatamiz:

The water in this bottle is cold.
Bu shishadagi suv sovuq.

6.3. Shaxs ismlari

Odatda odamlarning ismlari oldidan artikl qo‘yilmaydi.

John is my friend.
John mening do‘stim.
Mary lives in London.
Mary Londonda yashaydi.

6.4. Ko‘pchilik mamlakat va shahar nomlari

I live in Uzbekistan.
Men O‘zbekistonda yashayman.
Paris is a beautiful city.
Parij chiroyli shahar.
She visited Germany.
U Germaniyaga tashrif buyurdi.

6.5. Fanlar, tillar va sport turlari

I study English.
Men ingliz tilini o‘rganaman.
Mathematics is difficult.
Matematika qiyin.
He plays football.
U futbol o‘ynaydi.
She likes music.
U musiqani yoqtiradi.

6.6. Ovqatlanish vaqtlari

Breakfast, lunch, dinner umumiy ma’noda kelganda ko‘pincha artiklsiz ishlatiladi.

I have breakfast at seven.
Men soat yettida nonushta qilaman.
We have lunch at school.
Biz maktabda tushlik qilamiz.
Dinner is ready.
Kechki ovqat tayyor.

Agar aniq bir ovqatlanish tadbirini nazarda tutsak, artikl kelishi mumkin:

The dinner we had yesterday was delicious.
Kecha yegan kechki ovqatimiz mazali edi.

6.7. Kunlar, oylar va bayramlar

I work on Monday.
Men dushanba kuni ishlayman.
My birthday is in July.
Tug‘ilgan kunim iyul oyida.
We celebrate New Year.
Biz Yangi yilni nishonlaymiz.
DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: Umumiy ma’nodagi ko‘plik otlar, uncountable otlar, ko‘pchilik ism, shahar va mamlakat nomlari, tillar, fanlar, sport turlari va odatiy ovqatlanish nomlari oldidan odatda artikl ishlatilmaydi.

7.9. Artikllarni tanlash algoritmi

9. READING — A VISIT TO A NEW CITY

A Day in London

Last summer, Daniel visited London for the first time. He stayed in a small hotel near the city centre. On the first morning, he had breakfast at the hotel and then went to the British Museum.

He saw an interesting exhibition about history. After that, he walked to a beautiful park. The park was full of people, and the weather was sunny.

Daniel took a photo of the famous clock tower and visited a small bookshop. He bought a book about English history. In the evening, he had dinner at a restaurant near the river.

Daniel enjoyed the trip. He wants to visit the city again because London has many interesting places.

Reading questions

1. Where did Daniel stay?

2. What did he visit first?

3. What did he buy?

4. Why does he want to visit London again?

12. WRITING — A PERSON I ADMIRE

Topic: A Person I Admire — Men hurmat qiladigan inson.

80–120 so‘zli matn yozing. Matningizda quyidagilarga javob bering:

  • Who is this person?
  • Is he/she a teacher, a doctor, a worker or something else?
  • What does this person do?
  • Why do you admire this person?
  • What is the most important thing you have learned from him/her?

Matnda kamida 3 ta A/AN, 3 ta THE va 3 ta artiklsiz ishlatilgan misol bo‘lsin.

DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING: 80–120 so‘zli matnni daftaringizda yozing. A/AN, THE va ZERO ARTICLE ishlatilgan joylarni alohida belgilang.
