2. ADJECTIVE GAPDA QAYERDA KELADI?

Sifat ingliz tilida asosan ikki muhim o‘rinda ishlatiladi.

A) Otning oldidan keladi

Adjective + Noun
a big house — katta uy
a good student — yaxshi o‘quvchi
an interesting book — qiziqarli kitob
a new car — yangi mashina
a small garden — kichik bog‘

Eslab qoling: odatda sifat va ot orasiga boshqa so‘z kiritilmaydi, sifat otning oldidan keladi.

B) To be fe’lidan keyin keladi

Subject + am/is/are + Adjective
I am happy. — Men xursandman.
He is tired. — U charchagan.
They are friendly. — Ular do‘stona.
The food is delicious. — Ovqat mazali.
My parents are busy. — Ota-onam band.

Yana qaysi fe’llardan keyin sifat keladi?

Quyidagi fe’llardan keyin sifat kelishi mumkin:

  • look — ko‘rinmoq
  • feel — his qilmoq
  • seem — tuyulmoq
  • sound — eshitilmoq
  • smell — hid taratmoq
  • taste — ta’mi bo‘lmoq
  • become — bo‘lib qolmoq
  • get — bo‘lib qolmoq
You look happy. — Sen xursand ko‘rinyapsan.
I feel good. — Men o‘zimni yaxshi his qilyapman.
The soup smells nice. — Sho‘rvaning hidi yoqimli.
The weather became cold. — Havo sovib ketdi.
Bu fe’llardan keyin har doim ravish ishlatish shart emas. Masalan, “You look beautifully” emas, “You look beautiful” deyiladi. Bu yerda look — ko‘rinmoq ma’nosida.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Ikki asosiy formulani va sifat bilan ishlatiladigan fe’llarni yozing.

📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

Quyidagi so‘zlardan foydalanib 12 ta gap yozing: happy, tired, beautiful, delicious, cold, nice, busy, friendly, good, interesting, clean, ready.

3. ADVERB — RAVISH

Adverb — harakat qanday, qachon, qayerda yoki qay darajada bajarilishini bildiradi.

Ko‘pincha fe’lni izohlaydi va “qanday qilib?” savoliga javob beradi.

**Adverb = ravish**
slowly — sekin
quickly — tez
carefully — ehtiyotkorlik bilan

Taqqoslang

He is a careful driver. — U ehtiyotkor haydovchi. (Adjective)
He drives carefully. — U ehtiyotkorlik bilan haydaydi. (Adverb)
She is a slow runner. — U sekin yuguruvchi.
She runs slowly. — U sekin yuguradi.

Ravishlarning asosiy turlari

PlaceQayerda?here, there, outside
FrequencyQanchalik tez-tez?always, often, never
DegreeQay darajada?very, quite, too

Adverb ta’rifi va beshta ravish turini jadval bilan ko‘chiring.

4. ADJECTIVE + LY = ADVERB

Ko‘plab ravishlar sifatga -ly qo‘shimchasini qo‘shish orqali yasaladi.

Adjective + -ly = Adverb

Qoida 1: Oddiy holat — ly qo‘shiladi

slow → slowly; quick → quickly; careful → carefully.

Qoida 2: -y bilan tugagan sifatlar

Ko‘p hollarda y harfi i ga o‘zgaradi va -ly qo‘shiladi.

happy → happily — xursand holda
easy → easily — osonlik bilan
angry → angrily — jahldor tarzda
busy → busily — band tarzda

Qoida 3: -le bilan tugagan ayrim sifatlar

Oxiridagi e tushib, -y qo‘shiladi.

simple → simply — oddiy tarzda
gentle → gently — muloyimlik bilan

Qoida 4: -ic bilan tugagan ko‘p sifatlar

Odatda -ally qo‘shiladi.

basic → basically — asosan
automatic → automatically — avtomatik ravishda
dramatic → dramatically — keskin tarzda
Har bir -ly bilan tugagan so‘z ravish emas. Masalan, friendly, lovely, lonely — sifatlar. Ularni ravish deb qabul qilmang.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

To‘rtta yasash qoidasini va barcha misollarni yozing.

📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

Quyidagi sifatlarning ravish shaklini yozing: slow, happy, easy, careful, simple, angry, quiet, basic, polite, beautiful, gentle, quick.

Har bir yangi ravish bilan bittadan gap tuzing.

5. -LY QO‘SHIMCHASISIZ RAVISHLAR

Ba’zi ravishlar sifat bilan bir xil shaklda bo‘ladi. Ularga -ly qo‘shilmaydi.

AdjectiveAdverbTarjima

He is a fast runner. — U tez yuguruvchi.
He runs fast. — U tez yuguradi.
She works hard. — U astoydil ishlaydi.
I get up early. — Men erta turaman.
Don't arrive late. — Kech kelma.
MUHIM: “fastly” odatda ishlatilmaydi. “He runs fast” deb aytamiz.

Hard va hardly farqi

He works hard. — U astoydil ishlaydi.
He hardly works. — U deyarli ishlamaydi.

Hardly — “deyarli ... emas” ma’nosini beradigan alohida ravish. U hard so‘zining oddiy -ly shakli emas.

Late va lately farqi

I arrived late. — Men kech keldim.
I haven't seen him lately. — Men uni oxirgi paytlarda ko‘rmadim.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Bir xil shakldagi ravishlarni, hard/hardly va late/lately farqlarini yozing.

📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

Fast, hard, early, late, straight so‘zlari bilan 10 ta gap tuzing. Hard va hardly, late va lately bilan alohida gaplar yozing.

6. GOOD VA WELL — ENG MUHIM FARQ

Good — sifat. Biror odam yoki narsaning yaxshi ekanini bildiradi.

Well — ko‘pincha ravish. Ish-harakat yaxshi bajarilishini bildiradi.

GoodWell
a good studentstudy well
a good singersing well
a good cookcook well
She is a good student. — U yaxshi o‘quvchi.
She studies well. — U yaxshi o‘qiydi.
He is a good driver. — U yaxshi haydovchi.
He drives well. — U mashinani yaxshi haydaydi.

Well sifat ham bo‘lishi mumkin

Well ba’zan insonning sog‘lig‘i yaxshi ekanini bildiradi.

I am well. — Men sog‘lomman / ahvolim yaxshi.
She isn't feeling well. — Uning mazasi yo‘q.
“I'm good” kundalik ingliz tilida “ahvolim yaxshi” ma’nosida ishlatilishi mumkin. Ammo grammatik farqni o‘rganayotganda good — sifat, well — ravish ekanini eslab qoling.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Good va well ta’riflarini, farqlarini va barcha misollarni yozing.

📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

Good yoki well bilan 10 ta gap tuzing. Har birida nima uchun shu shaklni tanlaganingizni yozing.

7. ADVERB GAPDA QAYERDA KELADI?

A) Harakat fe’lidan keyin

Subject + Verb + Adverb

She sings beautifully. — U chiroyli kuylaydi.
They work carefully. — Ular ehtiyotkorlik bilan ishlaydi.

B) To‘ldiruvchidan keyin ham kelishi mumkin

She speaks English fluently. — U ingliz tilida ravon gapiradi.
He opened the door quietly. — U eshikni sekin ochdi.

Always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never so‘zlari bilan har biriga ikkitadan gap tuzing.

8. VERY, TOO, ENOUGH, QUITE, REALLY

VERY — juda

Very sifat yoki ravishning darajasini kuchaytiradi.

This book is very interesting. — Bu kitob juda qiziqarli.
She speaks very slowly. — U juda sekin gapiradi.

TOO — haddan tashqari / ortiqcha

Too ko‘pincha kerakli yoki maqbul darajadan ortiqlikni bildiradi.

The tea is too hot. — Choy juda issiq (ichish uchun ortiqcha issiq).
He speaks too quickly. — U haddan tashqari tez gapiradi.

ENOUGH — yetarli

Enough sifatdan keyin keladi, ot bilan esa odatda otdan oldin keladi.

Adjective + enough
She is old enough. — U yetarlicha katta.
The room is big enough. — Xona yetarlicha katta.
Enough + Noun
We have enough time. — Bizda yetarli vaqt bor.
There is enough food. — Yetarli ovqat bor.

REALLY — haqiqatan / juda

It is really beautiful. — Bu haqiqatan chiroyli.
He runs really fast. — U haqiqatan juda tez yuguradi.

QUITE — ancha / anchagina

The test is quite easy. — Test anchagina oson.
She speaks quite clearly. — U ancha ravshan gapiradi.

SO — shunchalik / juda

I am so tired. — Men juda charchaganman.
He runs so quickly. — U shunchalik tez yuguradi.
Too va very bir xil emas. Very — juda; too — haddan tashqari, ko‘pincha muammo yoki noqulaylik borligini anglatadi.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Very, too, enough, really, quite va so qoidalarini yozing.

📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

Har bir so‘z bilan ikkitadan gap yozing. Enough bilan sifat va otga alohida misol yozing.

-ED: insonning his-tuyg‘usi yoki holatini bildiradi.
-ING: his-tuyg‘uni keltirib chiqaradigan narsa yoki vaziyatni tasvirlaydi.

10. COMPARATIVE ADJECTIVES — QIYOSIY SIFATLAR

Ikki odam, narsa yoki joyni taqqoslash uchun Comparative ishlatiladi.

Subject + be + comparative adjective + than + object

1-qoida: Qisqa sifatga -er qo‘shiladi

OddiyQiyosiyTarjima
smallsmallerkichikroq
talltallerbalandroq
fastfastertezroq
shortshorterqisqaroq
oldolderkattaroq/yoshi ulug‘roq
My house is smaller than yours. — Mening uyim senikidan kichikroq.

2-qoida: Oxiri -e bo‘lsa, faqat -r qo‘shiladi

large → larger; nice → nicer; safe → safer.

This room is larger than that room. — Bu xona anavi xonadan kattaroq.

3-qoida: Undosh + unli + undosh

Ayrim qisqa so‘zlarda oxirgi undosh ikki marta yoziladi.

big → bigger; hot → hotter; thin → thinner.

Today is hotter than yesterday. — Bugun kechagidan issiqroq.

4-qoida: -y bilan tugasa

y → i + er.

happy → happier; easy → easier; busy → busier.

This exercise is easier than the last one. — Bu mashq avvalgisidan osonroq.

5-qoida: Uzun sifatlarga more

Ko‘p uzun sifatlardan oldin more ishlatiladi.

beautiful → more beautiful; expensive → more expensive; interesting → more interesting.

This book is more interesting than that book. — Bu kitob anavisidan qiziqarliroq.

6-qoida: Noto‘g‘ri shakllar

AdjectiveComparative
goodbetter
badworse
farfarther / further
littleless
much / manymore
My English is better than before. — Ingliz tilim avvalgidan yaxshiroq.
This result is worse than expected. — Bu natija kutilganidan yomonroq.
More better yoki more easier demang. Better va easier so‘zlarining o‘zi qiyosiy shakldir.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Comparative formulasini, barcha yasash qoidalarini va jadvalni yozing.

📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

Quyidagi sifatlarning qiyosiy shaklini yozing: tall, large, big, hot, happy, easy, beautiful, expensive, good, bad, far, interesting.

Kamida 10 ta taqqoslash gapini yozing.

11. SUPERLATIVE ADJECTIVES — ORTTIRMA DARAJA

Uch yoki undan ortiq odam yoki narsalar ichida eng yuqori darajani bildirish uchun Superlative ishlatiladi.

Subject + be + the + superlative adjective

1-qoida: Qisqa sifatlarga -est

small → the smallest; tall → the tallest; fast → the fastest.

He is the tallest student in the class. — U sinfdagi eng baland o‘quvchi.

2-qoida: -e bilan tugasa, -st

large → the largest; nice → the nicest.

3-qoida: Oxirgi undosh ikki marta yoziladi

big → the biggest; hot → the hottest.

4-qoida: -y → iest

happy → the happiest; easy → the easiest; busy → the busiest.

5-qoida: Uzun sifatlardan oldin the most

beautiful → the most beautiful; expensive → the most expensive; interesting → the most interesting.

6-qoida: Noto‘g‘ri shakllar

OddiyQiyosiyOrttirma
goodbetterthe best
badworsethe worst
farfarther/furtherthe farthest/furthest
littlelessthe least
much/manymorethe most
She is the best student. — U eng yaxshi o‘quvchi.
This is the most expensive phone. — Bu eng qimmat telefon.
Today is the hottest day. — Bugun eng issiq kun.
Superlative oldidan odatda THE ishlatiladi: the best, the biggest, the most beautiful.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Superlative formulasini, barcha qoidalarni va jadvalni yozing.

📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

12 ta sifatni Superlative shakliga aylantiring va kamida 8 ta gap tuzing.

12. AS ... AS VA NOT AS ... AS

Ikki narsa yoki odamning teng yoki teng emasligini ifodalash uchun ishlatiladi.

as + adjective + as = ... kabi / ...chalik
My brother is as tall as my father. — Akam otamdek baland bo‘yli.
This book is as interesting as that one. — Bu kitob anavisi kabi qiziqarli.
not as + adjective + as = ...chalik emas
This car is not as expensive as that car. — Bu mashina anavichalik qimmat emas.
I am not as tired as yesterday. — Men kechagichalik charchamaganman.

As ... as orasida sifatning oddiy shakli ishlatiladi. “As taller as” yoki “as more beautiful as” demaymiz.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

Ikki formulani va misollarni yozing.

📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

As ... as bilan 5 ta, not as ... as bilan 5 ta gap tuzing.

13. SO VA SUCH

So va such kuchaytirish uchun ishlatiladi, lekin gapda turlicha qo‘llanadi.

so + adjective / adverb
She is so beautiful. — U juda chiroyli.
He runs so quickly. — U juda tez yuguradi.
such + (a/an) + adjective + noun
She is such a kind person. — U juda mehribon inson.
It is such an interesting book. — Bu juda qiziqarli kitob.
They are such good friends. — Ular juda yaxshi do‘stlar.

Farqi: so dan keyin odatda sifat yoki ravish; such dan keyin esa otli birikma keladi.

✍️ DAFTARGA YOZING

So va such formulalarini va misollarni yozing.

📝 DAFTARDA BAJARING

So bilan 5 ta, such bilan 5 ta gap tuzing.
