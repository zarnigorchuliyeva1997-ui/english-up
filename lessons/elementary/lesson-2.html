Have Got / Has Got

Biror narsaga ega bo‘lish, kimdadir nima borligini aytish va so‘rash.

Bugungi maqsad: Have got va Has got shakllarini tushunish, to‘g‘ri gap tuzish va kundalik suhbatda ishlatish.

Dars jarayoni: 0%

1. Salomlashuv va darsga kirish

Hello, everyone! Welcome back to English Up!

Bugun biz juda kerakli mavzuni o‘rganamiz. Tasavvur qiling: siz yangi do‘stingiz bilan tanishyapsiz. U sizdan oilangiz, telefoningiz, mashinangiz yoki uy hayvoningiz haqida so‘raydi.

Masalan, siz shunday demoqchisiz:

I have got a phone. — Menda telefon bor.
She has got a brother. — Uning akasi yoki ukasi bor.
We have got a big house. — Bizning katta uyimiz bor.

Mana shu fikrlarni ingliz tilida ifodalash uchun Have got va Has got dan foydalanamiz.

Dars davomida har bir qoidani misollar bilan ko‘rib chiqamiz. Shoshilmang: avval ma’noni tushuning, keyin gap tuzishni mashq qiling.

2. Have got / Has got nimani anglatadi?

Have got va Has got — ingliz tilida egalikni, ya’ni kimdadir biror narsa borligini bildirish uchun ishlatiladigan grammatik shakllar.

O‘zbek tilida biz odatda “Menda kitob bor”, “Unda mashina bor”, “Bizning oilamiz katta” deymiz. Ingliz tilida bu fikrni have got yoki has got bilan ifodalash mumkin.

HAVE GOT / HAS GOT = BOR, EGA
I have got a book.
Menda kitob bor.

He has got a car.
Unda mashina bor.

They have got two children.
Ularning ikkita farzandi bor.

Bu shakl qayerdan kelgan?

Have ingliz tilida “ega bo‘lmoq / bor bo‘lmoq” ma’nosini beradigan fe’l. Got esa get fe’lining uchinchi shakli bo‘lib, bu birikmada have bilan birga kelib, egalik ma’nosini ifodalaydi.

Shuning uchun have got ni alohida-alohida “have = bor, got = bor” deb tarjima qilmaymiz. Ular birgalikda bitta grammatik shakl hosil qiladi va o‘zbekchaga odatda “bor” yoki “ega” deb tarjima qilinadi.

Muhim: “I have got a pen” va “I have a pen” ko‘pincha bir xil ma’noni beradi: “Menda ruchka bor”. Ushbu darsda biz aynan have got / has got shakllarini o‘rganamiz.

3. Nega HAVE GOT va HAS GOT farq qiladi?

Ingliz tilida hozirgi zamonda ayrim fe’llar ega (subject) ga qarab shaklini o‘zgartiradi. Have fe’li ham shunday fe’llardan biridir.

Present Simple mavzusida ham ko‘rganimizdek, he, she, it bilan fe’l shakli o‘zgarishi mumkin. Have fe’lining he/she/it bilan ishlatiladigan shakli — has.

HAVE → HAS (faqat HE / SHE / IT bilan)

Nega aynan shu olmoshlar?

I, you, we, they bilan have ishlatiladi. He, she, it bilan esa has ishlatiladi. Bu ingliz tilidagi fe’l tuslanish qoidasi: uchinchi shaxs birlikda have shakli has ga o‘zgaradi.

Bu qoida faqat olmoshlar bilan cheklanmaydi. Ega bitta erkak kishi, bitta ayol kishi yoki bitta narsa/hayvon bo‘lsa ham has got ishlatiladi.

My father has got a car. — Dadamning mashinasi bor.
My mother has got a phone. — Onamning telefoni bor.
The dog has got a long tail. — Itning uzun dumi bor.
My friends have got bicycles. — Do‘stlarimning velosipedlari bor.
Esda saqlang: bitta odam yoki bitta narsa — he/she/it qoidasi bo‘yicha has got. I, you, we, they yoki ko‘plikdagi egalar bilan have got.

5. Inkor gap — Negative

Biror narsa yo‘qligini aytmoqchi bo‘lsak, have not got yoki has not got ishlatamiz.

Subject + have / has + not + got + object
DarakInkorTarjima
I have got a car.I have not got a car.Menda mashina yo‘q.
She has got a dog.She has not got a dog.Unda it yo‘q.
They have got a garden.They have not got a garden.Ularda bog‘ yo‘q.

Qisqartma inkor shakllari

  • have not got → haven't got
  • has not got → hasn't got
I haven't got a brother. — Mening akam/ukam yo‘q.
He hasn't got a computer. — Unda kompyuter yo‘q.
We haven't got enough time. — Bizda yetarli vaqt yo‘q.

Yodda tuting: inkor gapda not so‘zi have yoki has dan keyin, got dan oldin keladi.

7. WH-questions — batafsil savollar

Faqat “bor yoki yo‘q?” deb so‘rash bilan cheklanmaymiz. Nima, kim, nechta kabi savollarni ham berishimiz mumkin.

Question word + have / has + subject + got ...?
What have you got? — Senda nima bor?
What has she got? — Unda nima bor?
How many brothers have you got? — Nechta aka-ukang bor?
How many children have they got? — Ularning nechta farzandi bor?
Who has got my book? — Mening kitobim kimda?

How many sanaladigan narsalar ko‘pligi haqida so‘raydi: brothers, sisters, books, cars, children.

What narsa yoki buyum haqida so‘raydi. Who esa odam haqida so‘raydi.

8. Have got va Has got qayerlarda ishlatiladi?

Bu shakl faqat buyumlar haqida emas. U turli vaziyatlarda ishlatiladi:

1) Oila va qarindoshlar

I've got two sisters. — Mening ikkita singlim/opam bor.
My father has got a brother. — Dadamning akasi/ukasi bor.

2) Buyumlar va egalik

We have got a new television. — Bizda yangi televizor bor.
She has got a beautiful dress. — Unda chiroyli ko‘ylak bor.

3) Tashqi ko‘rinish va tana a’zolari

He has got brown eyes. — Uning ko‘zlari jigarrang.
The cat has got a long tail. — Mushukning uzun dumi bor.

4) Uy va xonalar

Our house has got three bedrooms. — Uyimizda uchta yotoqxona bor.
They have got a big kitchen. — Ularning katta oshxonasi bor.

5) Kasallik yoki holatni ifodalash

I've got a headache. — Boshim og‘riyapti.
She has got a cold. — U shamollagan.
Bu misollarda ham asosiy ma’no — odamda yoki narsada biror xususiyat, narsa yoki holat mavjudligi.

9. Have got va Have ning farqi

Have got va have egalik ma’nosida ko‘pincha bir-birining o‘rnida ishlatiladi:

I have got a car. = I have a car.
She has got a sister. = She has a sister.
They have got a house. = They have a house.

Ammo have fe’li boshqa ma’nolarda ham ishlatiladi: ovqatlanmoq, ichmoq, vaqt o‘tkazmoq va boshqalar.

I have breakfast at 7. — Men soat 7 da nonushta qilaman.
We have lunch together. — Biz birga tushlik qilamiz.
She has a shower every morning. — U har kuni ertalab dush qabul qiladi.
Bunday harakatlarda odatda have got ishlatilmaydi. Masalan, “I have got breakfast at 7” deb “Men soat 7 da nonushta qilaman” ma’nosida aytmaymiz.

It has got green eyes and a long tail. We have got a comfortable house. Our house has got three bedrooms, a kitchen and a living room. I have got my own room. It is not very big, but I like it.   1. Have you got a big family?

  5. Has your best friend got a bicycle?

19. Dars yakuni — What have we learned?

  • Have got va has got egalikni ifodalaydi.
  • I / You / We / They bilan have got ishlatiladi.
  • He / She / It bilan has got ishlatiladi.
  • Inkor: haven't got / hasn't got.
  • Savol: Have / Has gap boshiga chiqadi.
  • Qisqa javob: Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.
  • WH-savollar: What have you got? / How many ... have they got?
Excellent work!
Bugun siz ingliz tilida egalikni ifodalashni, savol berishni va inkor gap tuzishni o‘rgandingiz. Endi bu shakllarni kundalik suhbatlarda ishlatishga harakat qiling.
