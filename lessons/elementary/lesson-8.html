1. Modal Verbs nima?

Ingliz tilida Modal Verbs — bu asosiy fe'lga qo‘shimcha ma'no beradigan yordamchi fe'llardir. Ular biror ishni qila olish, qilish kerakligi, ruxsat, maslahat, ehtimollik yoki istakni ifodalaydi.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma'nolarni ko‘pincha quyidagi so‘zlar orqali beramiz:

  • qila olmoq;
  • mumkin;
  • kerak;
  • shart;
  • lozim;
  • balki;
  • maslahat bermoq;
  • xohlamoq yoki iltimos qilmoq.

I can swim.

Men suza olaman.

You should study.

Sen o‘qishing kerak / o‘qishing ma'qul.

You must be careful.

Sen ehtiyot bo‘lishing shart.

MUHIM: Modal fe'lning o‘zi odatda harakatni bildirmaydi. U asosiy fe'lga qo‘shimcha ma'no beradi. Masalan, swim — suzmoq, can swim — suza olmoq.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

Modal verbs — asosiy fe'lga qobiliyat, ruxsat, majburiyat, maslahat, ehtimollik yoki istak ma'nosini qo‘shadigan yordamchi fe'llar.

2. Modal Verbs'ning eng muhim grammatik qoidalari

2.1. Modal fe'ldan keyin V1 keladi

Modal fe'llarning aksariyatidan keyin asosiy fe'lning birinchi shakli — V1 ishlatiladi. V1 — fe'lning oddiy, lug‘atdagi shakli.

Subject + Modal Verb + V1 + Object

Subject — ega; Modal Verb — modal fe'l; V1 — fe'lning birinchi shakli; Object — to‘ldiruvchi.

She can speak English.

U ingliz tilida gapira oladi.

He must work today.

U bugun ishlashi shart.

Nega speak, speaks emas?

Chunki can modal fe'lidan keyin asosiy fe'l doimo V1 shaklida keladi. Ega he, she yoki it bo‘lsa ham, modal fe'ldan keyin -s qo‘shilmaydi.

❌ She can speaks English.
✅ She can speak English.

2.2. Modal fe'llarga -s qo‘shilmaydi

Present Simple'da odatiy fe'llar he/she/it bilan -s oladi. Ammo modal fe'llar bunday o‘zgarmaydi.

EgaTo‘g‘ri shakl
II can swim.
YouYou can swim.
HeHe can swim.
SheShe can swim.
ItIt can work.
WeWe can swim.
TheyThey can swim.

2.3. Modal fe'llardan keyin odatda TO ishlatilmaydi

Can, could, may, might, must, should, will va would kabi modal fe'llardan keyin asosiy fe'l oldidan to qo‘yilmaydi.

❌ I can to swim.
✅ I can swim.

❌ You should to study.
✅ You should study.

Ammo istisno: have to va ought to shakllarida to mavjud. Ular alohida o‘rganiladi.

2.4. Inkor shakli

Modal fe'ldan keyin not qo‘shiladi.

Subject + Modal + not + V1

I cannot drive.

Men mashina hayday olmayman.

You should not worry.

Sen xavotir olmasliging kerak.

2.5. Savol shakli

Modal fe'l savolda egadan oldinga chiqadi. Odatda do/does yordamchi fe'li kerak bo‘lmaydi.

Modal + Subject + V1 + Object + ?

Can you help me?

Menga yordam bera olasanmi?

Should I call him?

Men unga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishim kerakmi?

📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING — UMUMIY QOIDALAR

1. Modal + V1.

2. He / She / It bilan modal fe'lga -s qo‘shilmaydi.

3. Can, could, may, might, must, should, will, would dan keyin odatda to ishlatilmaydi.

4. Inkor: Modal + not + V1.

5. Savol: Modal + Subject + V1?

6. Modal savollarda odatda do/does ishlatilmaydi.

3. CAN va COULD

3.1. CAN — qila olmoq

Can hozirgi zamonda qobiliyat yoki imkoniyatni bildirish uchun ishlatiladi. Biror ishni bajarishga kuchimiz, bilimimiz yoki imkonimiz borligini ko‘rsatadi.

Positive: Subject + can + V1 Negative: Subject + cannot / can't + V1 Question: Can + Subject + V1?

Misollar

I can cook.

Men ovqat pishira olaman.

She can drive a car.

U mashina hayday oladi.

They can speak Uzbek.

Ular o‘zbek tilida gapira oladilar.

He can't swim.

U suza olmaydi.

3.2. CAN — ruxsat so‘rash va berish

Can kundalik suhbatda ruxsat so‘rash yoki ruxsat berishda ham ishlatiladi.

Can I come in?

Kirsam maylimi?

You can use my phone.

Telefonimdan foydalanishing mumkin.

Can I ask a question?

Savol bersam bo‘ladimi?

3.3. CAN — iltimos

Can you...? orqali birovdan biror ishni bajarishni so‘rashimiz mumkin.

Can you open the door?

Eshikni ochib bera olasanmi?

Can you help me, please?

Iltimos, menga yordam bera olasizmi?

3.4. COULD — o'tgan zamondagi qobiliyat

Could ko‘pincha can fe'lining o'tgan zamondagi shakli hisoblanadi. U o'tmishda umumiy qobiliyatimiz bo‘lganini bildiradi.

I could swim when I was five.

Men besh yoshimda suza olardim.

My grandfather could speak Russian.

Bobom rus tilida gapira olardi.

She couldn't read at the age of four.

U to‘rt yoshida o‘qiy olmasdi.

3.5. COULD — muloyim iltimos

Could you...? Can you...? shakliga qaraganda ko‘pincha muloyimroq va odobliroq eshitiladi.

Could you help me?

Menga yordam bera olasizmi?

Could you speak slowly, please?

Iltimos, sekinroq gapira olasizmi?

3.6. CAN va COULD farqi

CanCould
Hozirgi qobiliyatO‘tmishdagi umumiy qobiliyat
Can you help me?Could you help me?
Oddiy iltimosKo‘pincha muloyimroq iltimos

Eslab qoling: Could har doim faqat o'tgan zamon degani emas. U hozirgi suhbatda ham muloyim iltimos yoki ruxsat so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

CAN — qila olmoq, ruxsat, iltimos.

COULD — o'tmishdagi qobiliyat, muloyim iltimos yoki ruxsat so‘rash.

Can + V1; Could + V1.

Inkor: can't / couldn't.

Savol: Can/Could + subject + V1?

4. MAY va MIGHT

4.1. MAY — ruxsat

May ruxsat so‘rashda ishlatiladi. U ko‘pincha can'dan rasmiyroq yoki odobliroq eshitiladi.

May I come in?

Kirsam maylimi?

May I use your pen?

Ruchkangizdan foydalansam maylimi?

You may sit here.

Bu yerga o‘tirishingiz mumkin.

4.2. MAY — ehtimollik

May biror voqea sodir bo‘lishi mumkinligini, lekin bunga to‘liq ishonch yo‘qligini bildiradi.

It may rain today.

Bugun yomg‘ir yog‘ishi mumkin.

He may come tomorrow.

U ertaga kelishi mumkin.

She may be at home.

U uyda bo‘lishi mumkin.

4.3. MIGHT — ehtimol

Might ham ehtimollikni bildiradi. Ko‘p vaziyatlarda may bilan ma'nosi juda yaqin. Might ba'zan ehtimollikni biroz kuchsizroq ko‘rsatadi, lekin bu farq qat'iy foiz bilan o‘lchanmaydi.

I might visit my friend.

Balki do‘stimnikiga borarman.

They might be busy.

Ular band bo‘lishi mumkin.

We might go out tonight.

Balki bugun kechqurun tashqariga chiqarmiz.

4.4. MAY va MIGHT inkor shakli

may not + V1 might not + V1

He may not come.

U kelmasligi mumkin.

It might not work.

Bu ishlamasligi mumkin.

📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

MAY — ruxsat va ehtimollik.

MIGHT — ehtimollik, balki.

May/Might + V1.

Inkor: may not / might not + V1.

May I...? — ruxsat so‘rashning odobli shakli.

5. MUST va HAVE TO

5.1. MUST — qat'iy zarurat yoki majburiyat

Must biror ishni bajarish juda zarur yoki shart ekanini bildiradi. Ko‘pincha gapiruvchi bu majburiyatni kuchli ta'kidlaydi.

Subject + must + V1

I must study English.

Men ingliz tilini o‘rganishim shart.

You must be careful.

Sen ehtiyot bo‘lishing shart.

We must finish this work.

Biz bu ishni tugatishimiz shart.

5.2. MUST — kuchli qoida va ko‘rsatma

You must wear a seat belt.

Xavfsizlik kamarini taqishingiz shart.

Students must follow the rules.

O‘quvchilar qoidalarga amal qilishlari shart.

5.3. HAVE TO — majbur bo‘lmoq / qilishga to‘g‘ri kelmoq

Have to ham majburiyatni bildiradi. Ko‘pincha tashqi qoida, vaziyat, ish yoki sharoit sababli biror ishni bajarish kerakligini ifodalaydi.

Eng muhim farq:

  • Must: gapiruvchi majburiyatni kuchli ta'kidlaydi yoki uni zarur deb hisoblaydi.
  • Have to: vaziyat, qoida yoki tashqi talab sababli majburiyat borligini bildiradi.

Bu farq har doim qat'iy emas. Kundalik ingliz tilida ular ko‘p vaziyatlarda bir-birining o‘rnida ham ishlatilishi mumkin.

5.4. HAVE TO qanday o‘zgaradi?

Have to oddiy modal fe'l kabi o‘zgarmaydi. U Present Simple'da he/she/it bilan has to bo‘ladi.

SubjectShaklMisol
Ihave toI have to work.
Youhave toYou have to study.
Wehave toWe have to leave.
Theyhave toThey have to wait.
Hehas toHe has to work.
Shehas toShe has to cook.
Ithas toIt has to stop.

Nega has to?

Chunki have to oddiy fe'l kabi grammatik o‘zgaradi. Present Simple'da he, she, it bilan have → has bo‘ladi. Bu o‘zgarish must kabi haqiqiy modal fe'llarga tegishli emas.

She has to get up early.

U erta turishi kerak.

I have to go to work.

Men ishga borishim kerak.

5.5. HAVE TO — inkor shakli

Have to bilan inkor yasashda do not yoki does not ishlatiladi.

I / You / We / They + don't have to + V1 He / She / It + doesn't have to + V1

I don't have to work today.

Bugun ishlashim shart emas.

She doesn't have to cook.

U ovqat pishirishi shart emas.

5.6. HAVE TO — savol shakli

Do + I/you/we/they + have to + V1? Does + he/she/it + have to + V1?

Do you have to work tomorrow?

Ertaga ishlashing kerakmi?

Does he have to wear a uniform?

U forma kiyishi shartmi?

Diqqat: Does ishlatilganda has emas, have keladi. Chunki does tarkibida -s grammatik belgisi allaqachon mavjud.

❌ Does she has to work?
✅ Does she have to work?

5.7. MUSTN'T va DON'T HAVE TO — juda muhim farq!

Bu ikkisini aralashtirmang. Ularning ma'nosi butunlay boshqa.

MUSTN'T = QILISH TAQIQLANGAN

Biror ishni bajarish mumkin emas, bunga ruxsat yo‘q.

You mustn't smoke here.

Bu yerda chekish taqiqlangan.

You mustn't touch this.

Bunga tegish mumkin emas.

DON'T HAVE TO = QILISH SHART EMAS

Biror ishni bajarish majburiy emas. Xohlasangiz qilishingiz mumkin, lekin majbur emassiz.

You don't have to come early.

Erta kelishing shart emas.

She doesn't have to work on Sunday.

U yakshanba kuni ishlashi shart emas.

Bu yerda mashina qo‘yish mumkin emas.Bu yerga mashina qo‘yishingiz shart emas.

5.8. MUST va HAVE TO taqqoslash

I must call my mother.

Onamga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishim shart. (Men buni muhim deb bilaman.)

I have to wear a uniform at work.

Ishda forma kiyishim kerak. (Ish joyimning talabi.)

📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

MUST — shart, kuchli zarurat.

HAVE TO — majbur bo‘lmoq, vaziyat yoki qoida sababli kerak bo‘lmoq.

He / She / It + HAS TO.

Inkor: don't have to / doesn't have to.

Savol: Do / Does + subject + have to + V1?

MUSTN'T — taqiqlangan.

DON'T HAVE TO — qilish shart emas.

6. SHOULD va OUGHT TO

6.1. SHOULD — maslahat

Should biror ishni bajarish yaxshi yoki foydali ekanini bildiradi. Maslahat berish, tavsiya qilish yoki to‘g‘ri deb hisoblangan ishni aytishda ishlatiladi.

Subject + should + V1 Subject + should not / shouldn't + V1 Should + Subject + V1?

You should drink more water.

Ko‘proq suv ichishing kerak.

You should learn new words every day.

Har kuni yangi so‘zlar o‘rganishing kerak.

He should see a doctor.

U shifokorga ko‘rinishi kerak.

You shouldn't eat too much sugar.

Juda ko‘p shakar yemasliging kerak.

6.2. SHOULD — savol orqali maslahat so‘rash

What should I do?

Men nima qilishim kerak?

Should we wait here?

Biz shu yerda kutishimiz kerakmi?

Should I buy this book?

Men bu kitobni sotib olishim kerakmi?

6.3. MUST va SHOULD farqi

MustShould

6.4. OUGHT TO — lozim, kerak

Ought to ham maslahat yoki axloqiy jihatdan to‘g‘ri deb hisoblangan ishni bildirish uchun ishlatiladi. Ma'nosi should'ga juda yaqin. Kundalik suhbatda should ko‘proq ishlatiladi.

Subject + ought to + V1

You ought to respect your parents.

Ota-onangni hurmat qilishing lozim.

We ought to help each other.

Biz bir-birimizga yordam berishimiz lozim.

Ought to inkor shakli: ought not to. Savol shakli esa mumkin, lekin kundalik suhbatda kamroq ishlatiladi.

📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

SHOULD — maslahat, tavsiya.

Should + V1.

Shouldn't + V1 — qilmaslik ma'qul.

OUGHT TO — lozim, kerak; should'ga yaqin ma'no.

Ought to + V1.

7. WILL, WOULD, SHALL va NEED

7.1. WILL — kelajak, va'da va tezkor qaror

Will Future Simple mavzusida o‘rganilgan. Bu yerda uning modal fe'l sifatidagi ishlatilishini eslab olamiz.

I will help you.

Men senga yordam beraman.

She will call you later.

U senga keyinroq qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi.

I will not forget this.

Men buni unutmayman.

7.2. WILL — taklif va yordam

I will carry your bag.

Sumkangizni ko‘tarib beraman.

I will open the window.

Derazani ochib beraman.

7.3. WOULD — muloyim iltimos va xohish

Would ko‘pincha muloyim so‘rov, taklif yoki xohishni ifodalaydi.

Would you help me?

Menga yordam bera olasizmi?

Would you like some tea?

Biroz choy xohlaysizmi?

I would like a cup of coffee.

Men bir finjon qahva xohlardim.

Would like — xohlamoqning muloyim shakli. Want ham xohlamoq degani, lekin would like odatda odobliroq eshitiladi.

WantWould like
I want tea.I would like tea.
Men choy xohlayman.Men choy istardim.

7.4. SHALL — taklif va maslahatlashish

Shall ko‘pincha I va we bilan taklif yoki birgalikda qaror qilishda ishlatiladi. Kundalik ingliz tilida, ayniqsa Britaniya inglizchasida uchraydi.

Shall we go?

Ketamizmi?

Shall I open the door?

Eshikni ochaymi?

Shall we start now?

Hozir boshlaymizmi?

7.5. NEED — zarurat

Need asosiy fe'l sifatida ham, ayrim vaziyatlarda modal fe'l sifatida ham ishlatiladi. Elementary darajada eng ko‘p uchraydigan shaklini o‘rganamiz:

Subject + need to + V1

I need to study.

Men o‘qishim kerak.

She needs to sleep.

U uxlashi kerak.

Need to oddiy fe'l kabi o‘zgaradi: he/she/it bilan needs to.

You don't need to hurry.

Shoshilishing shart emas.

Does he need to come?

U kelishi kerakmi?

8. Modal fe'llarda eng ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

1. Modal + to ishlatish
❌ I can to speak.
✅ I can speak.
2. Modal fe'lga -s qo‘shish
❌ He cans swim.
✅ He can swim.
3. Modal bilan do/does ishlatish
❌ Do you can swim?
✅ Can you swim?
4. Modal fe'ldan keyin V2 ishlatish
❌ She can went home.
✅ She can go home.
5. Has to savolida have o‘rniga has ishlatish
❌ Does he has to work?
✅ Does he have to work?
6. Mustn't va don't have to ni adashtirish
Mustn't — taqiqlangan.
Don't have to — majburiy emas.
7. Should'dan keyin to qo‘shish
❌ You should to sleep.
✅ You should sleep.
📒 DAFTARGA YOZIB OLING

Modal fe'llardan keyin V1 ishlatiladi. Modal fe'llarga he/she/it bilan -s qo‘shilmaydi. Modal savollarda do/does ishlatilmaydi. Have to va need to esa oddiy fe'l qoidalariga ko‘ra o‘zgaradi.

10. FOUR SKILLS — 4 KO‘NIKMA

10.1. READING — A Helpful Friend

Ali is a helpful student. He can speak English and he can use a computer. Every day, he has to study after school. He must finish his homework before dinner. Sometimes, his friends ask him for help.

One day, his friend Tom says, "Could you help me with English?" Ali says, "Of course! We can study together." Tom is happy. Ali tells him, "You should practise every day. You don't have to learn everything in one day. You just need to work regularly."

After the lesson, Tom says, "I might become a good English speaker one day." Ali answers, "You can do it if you keep practising!"

Reading questions

  1. What can Ali do?
  2. When does Ali study?
  3. What does Tom ask Ali?
  4. What advice does Ali give Tom?
  5. Does Tom have to learn everything in one day?

10.2. LISTENING — A Conversation

Vazifa: Avval matnni ko‘rmasdan audioni tinglang. Keyin yana bir marta sekin tinglang va savollarga javob bering.

Anna: Can you help me with this exercise?

Ben: Of course. What do you have to do?

Anna: I must finish it today, but I don't understand question three.

Ben: You should read the instructions again. I can explain it to you.

Anna: Thank you! Could you speak slowly, please?

Ben: Sure. We can do it together.

Listening questions

  1. What does Anna need help with?
  2. When must she finish the exercise?
  3. Which question does she not understand?
  4. What should she read again?
  5. How does she ask Ben to speak?

10.3. SPEAKING — Gapirish mashqi

Quyidagi savollarga ingliz tilida ovoz chiqarib javob bering. Javoblaringizni kamida 2–3 ta gap bilan kengaytirishga harakat qiling.

  1. What can you do well?
  2. What couldn't you do when you were a child?
  3. What do you have to do every day?
  4. What should people do to learn English?
  5. What must people do to stay safe?
  6. What might you do next weekend?
  7. Would you like to learn another language?
  8. Could you help a friend with English?
Masalan: "I can speak Uzbek. I can also understand some English. I want to improve my speaking."

10.4. WRITING — My Responsibilities and Abilities

Vazifa: "My Responsibilities and Abilities" mavzusida 100–120 so‘zli kichik matn yozing.

Matningizda quyidagi modal fe'llardan foydalaning:

  • can — qobiliyat;
  • can't — qila olmaslik;
  • have to — kundalik majburiyat;
  • must — muhim zarurat;
  • should — maslahat;
  • might — ehtimollik;
  • would like — istak.

Boshlash uchun namuna:

I can do many things, but I still have a lot to learn.

Men ko‘p narsalarni qila olaman, ammo hali ko‘p narsalarni o‘rganishim kerak.

13. DARS YAKUNI

📒 O‘ZINGIZNI TEKSHIRING
  • Can va could farqini tushuntira olamanmi?
  • May va might yordamida ehtimollik bildira olamanmi?
  • Must va have to farqini bilamanmi?
  • Mustn't va don't have to farqini tushunamanmi?
  • Should yordamida maslahat bera olamanmi?
  • Would you like...? bilan muloyim taklif qila olamanmi?
  • Modal fe'llardan keyin V1 ishlatishni bilamanmi?
