11-DARS: PAST SIMPLE VS PAST CONTINUOUS

Lesson objective: To describe completed actions and actions in progress in the past.

Tarjimasi: O‘tmishda tugallangan va davom etayotgan harakatlarni tasvirlash.

2. Darsdan oldingi bilimlarni esga olish

Yangi mavzuga kirishdan oldin oldingi darslarni eslaymiz.

Past Simple haqida

Past Simple o‘tmishda sodir bo‘lgan va tugagan harakatlar uchun ishlatiladi.

I visited my grandmother yesterday.

Men kecha buvimni ko‘rgani bordim.

Past Continuous haqida

Past Continuous o‘tmishdagi ma’lum bir vaqtda davom etayotgan harakat uchun ishlatiladi.

I was studying at 8 o'clock yesterday.

Men kecha soat sakkizda o‘qiyotgan edim.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Past Simple: I watched TV yesterday.

Past Continuous: I was watching TV at 8 pm.

Har bir gapning o‘zbekcha tarjimasini ham yozing.

3. Grammar — asosiy tushuntirish

3.1. Past Simple nima?

Past Simple — o‘tmishda sodir bo‘lgan va hozirga kelib tugagan harakatlarni ifodalovchi zamon.

Biz bu zamonni quyidagi holatlarda ishlatamiz:

  • Kecha sodir bo‘lgan harakatlar.
  • O‘tgan hafta sodir bo‘lgan voqealar.
  • O‘tgan yili sodir bo‘lgan ishlar.
  • O‘tmishda aniq vaqtda bajarilgan harakatlar.
Example 1:

She cooked dinner yesterday.

U kecha kechki ovqat tayyorladi.

Example 2:

They visited London last year.

Ular o‘tgan yili Londonga tashrif buyurishdi.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Past Simple is used for completed actions in the past.

Past Simple o‘tmishda tugallangan harakatlar uchun ishlatiladi.

Yuqoridagi ikkita misolni tarjimasi bilan ko‘chiring.

3.2. Past Simple — darak gap

Subject + V2 + Object / Complement

Endi formuladagi har bir qismni alohida o‘rganamiz.

S — Subject

Subject — gapning egasi. Harakatni kim bajarayotganini bildiradi.

I, You, He, She, It, We, They yoki ism bo‘lishi mumkin.

I played football yesterday.

Men kecha futbol o‘ynadim.

Bu gapda I — Subject.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Subject = gapning egasi.

Misol: I played football.

V2 — Verb 2

V2 — fe’lning ikkinchi shakli. Past Simple darak gapida asosiy fe’l odatda V2 shaklida keladi.

Regular verbs: play → played, work → worked.

Irregular verbs: go → went, eat → ate.

He went to school.

U maktabga bordi.

Bu gapda went — V2.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

V2 = fe’lning ikkinchi shakli.

go → went; eat → ate; play → played.

Object — To‘ldiruvchi

Object harakat kimga yoki nimaga qaratilganini bildirishi mumkin.

She bought a new phone.

U yangi telefon sotib oldi.

Bu gapda a new phone — Object.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Object = to‘ldiruvchi.

Misol: She bought a book.

3.3. Past Simple — inkor gap

Subject + did not (didn't) + V1 + Object

Inkor gapda did not yoki didn't ishlatiladi. Did yordamchi fe’li ishlatilganda asosiy fe’l V1 shaklida bo‘ladi.

I did not watch TV yesterday.

Men kecha televizor ko‘rmadim.

She didn't go to work.

U ishga bormadi.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Subject = ega.

did not = inkor yordamchi fe’li.

V1 = fe’lning birinchi shakli.

Misol: They didn't play football.

Eslab qoling: Did not ishlatilganda fe’lga -ed qo‘shilmaydi va V2 ishlatilmaydi.

3.4. Past Simple — so‘roq gap

Did + Subject + V1 + Object?

So‘roq gapda Did gap boshiga chiqadi.

Did you visit your friend?

Sen do‘stingni ko‘rgani bordingmi?

Did they finish their homework?

Ular uy vazifasini tugatishdimi?

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Did = so‘roq yordamchi fe’li.

Subject = ega.

V1 = asosiy fe’lning birinchi shakli.

Misol: Did she call you?

3.5. Past Continuous nima?

Past Continuous — o‘tmishdagi ma’lum bir vaqtda davom etayotgan harakatni ifodalovchi zamon.

Bu zamon harakatning aynan o‘sha vaqtda jarayonda bo‘lganini ko‘rsatadi.

Example 1:

I was reading a book at 9 pm.

Men kechki soat 9 da kitob o‘qiyotgan edim.

Example 2:

They were playing football at that time.

Ular o‘sha vaqtda futbol o‘ynayotgan edilar.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Past Continuous is used for an action in progress at a particular time in the past.

Tarjimasi: O‘tmishdagi ma’lum vaqtda davom etayotgan harakat uchun ishlatiladi.

Ikki misolni tarjimasi bilan yozing.

3.6. Past Continuous — darak gap

Subject + was / were + V-ing + Object
S — Subject

Subject — harakatni bajarayotgan shaxs yoki narsa.

She was cooking dinner.

U kechki ovqat tayyorlayotgan edi.

She — Subject.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Subject = ega.

Misol: She was reading.

Was

Was yordamchi fe’li I, He, She, It bilan ishlatiladi.

He was sleeping.

U uxlayotgan edi.

Was — yordamchi fe’l.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Was: I / He / She / It bilan.

Misol: I was working.

Were

Were yordamchi fe’li You, We, They bilan ishlatiladi.

They were studying.

Ular o‘qiyotgan edilar.

Were — yordamchi fe’l.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Were: You / We / They bilan.

Misol: We were learning English.

V-ing

Asosiy fe’lga -ing qo‘shimchasi qo‘shiladi.

read → reading; play → playing; work → working.

She was reading a story.

U hikoya o‘qiyotgan edi.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

V-ing = fe’l + ing.

Misol: They were playing.

3.7. Past Continuous — inkor gap

Subject + was / were + not + V-ing

Inkor gapda was yoki were dan keyin not qo‘shiladi.

I was not sleeping.

Men uxlamayotgan edim.

They weren't watching TV.

Ular televizor ko‘rmayotgan edilar.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Was not = wasn't.

Were not = weren't.

Misol: She wasn't working.

Misol: We weren't sleeping.

3.8. Past Continuous — so‘roq gap

Was / Were + Subject + V-ing?

Were you studying at 7 pm?

Sen kechki soat 7 da o‘qiyotganmiding?

Was she cooking dinner?

U kechki ovqat tayyorlayotgan edimi?

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Was + I / He / She / It + V-ing?

Were + You / We / They + V-ing?

Misol: Was he sleeping?

Misol: Were they working?

4. Past Simple va Past Continuous farqi

Bu darsning eng muhim qismi. Ikkala zamon ham o‘tmish haqida gapiradi, ammo ular harakatni turlicha tasvirlaydi.

Past Simple = tugallangan harakat

I watched a film yesterday.

Men kecha film ko‘rdim.

Past Continuous = o‘tmishda davom etayotgan harakat

I was watching a film at 8 pm.

Men soat 8 da film ko‘rayotgan edim.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Past Simple — What happened? (Nima sodir bo‘ldi?)

Past Continuous — What was happening? (Nima sodir bo‘layotgan edi?)

Yuqoridagi ikkita misolni yozing va farqini o‘z so‘zingiz bilan tushuntiring.

4.1. Bir harakat davom etayotganida boshqa harakat sodir bo‘lishi

Bu holatda odatda Past Continuous davom etayotgan harakatni, Past Simple esa uning orasida sodir bo‘lgan qisqa harakatni ifodalaydi.

I was sleeping when the phone rang.

Telefon jiringlaganida men uxlayotgan edim.

Bu gapda:

  • I was sleeping — davom etayotgan harakat.
  • The phone rang — sodir bo‘lgan qisqa harakat.

She was cooking when I arrived.

Men yetib kelganimda u ovqat tayyorlayotgan edi.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

Past Continuous + when + Past Simple.

Misol: I was reading when he called.

Tarjima: U qo‘ng‘iroq qilganida men kitob o‘qiyotgan edim.

4.2. When ishlatilishi

When — “qachon”, “-ganda” ma’nolarini beradi. Bir harakat boshqa harakat sodir bo‘lgan vaqtni bildirishi mumkin.

I was walking when it started to rain.

Yomg‘ir yog‘a boshlaganida men yurayotgan edim.

When I came home, my mother was cooking.

Men uyga kelganimda onam ovqat tayyorlayotgan edi.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

When = qachon / -ganda.

When I arrived, they were eating.

When she called, I was studying.

4.3. While ishlatilishi

While — “paytida”, “davomida” ma’nolarini beradi. Ko‘pincha davom etayotgan harakatni ifodalaydi.

While I was studying, my brother was watching TV.

Men o‘qiyotgan paytimda akam televizor ko‘rayotgan edi.

While she was cooking, I was cleaning the room.

U ovqat tayyorlayotgan paytda men xonani tozalayotgan edim.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

While = paytida / davomida.

While + Subject + was/were + V-ing.

While I was reading, he was writing.

4.4. When va While farqi

When ko‘pincha sodir bo‘lgan voqea yoki vaqtni ko‘rsatadi. While esa davom etayotgan jarayonni ta’kidlaydi.

I was having breakfast when he arrived.

U kelganida men nonushta qilayotgan edim.

While I was having breakfast, he was reading.

Men nonushta qilayotgan paytimda u kitob o‘qiyotgan edi.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

When — ko‘pincha qisqa sodir bo‘lgan voqea bilan.

While — ko‘pincha davom etayotgan jarayon bilan.

Bu umumiy qoida ekanini unutmang: when va while turli grammatik holatlarda ham ishlatilishi mumkin.

5. V-ing yozilish qoidalari

Past Continuous zamonida fe’lga -ing qo‘shiladi. Biroq ayrim fe’llarda imlo o‘zgarishi mavjud.

1-qoida: Oddiy holatda -ing qo‘shiladi

work → working

read → reading

play → playing

DAFTARGA YOZING:

work — working

read — reading

play — playing

2-qoida: Oxiridagi -e tushib qoladi

make → making

write → writing

come → coming

DAFTARGA YOZING:

make — making

write — writing

come — coming

3-qoida: Ayrim qisqa fe’llarda oxirgi undosh ikki marta yoziladi

run → running

sit → sitting

swim → swimming

DAFTARGA YOZING:

run — running

sit — sitting

swim — swimming

4-qoida: -ie bilan tugagan fe’llar

lie → lying

die → dying

DAFTARGA YOZING:

lie — lying

die — dying

6. Signal words — vaqt ko‘rsatkichlari

Past Simple bilan ko‘p ishlatiladigan iboralar

  • Yesterday — kecha
  • Last night — kecha kechasi
  • Last week — o‘tgan hafta
  • Last month — o‘tgan oy
  • Last year — o‘tgan yil
  • Two days ago — ikki kun oldin
  • In 2020 — 2020-yilda

Past Continuous bilan ko‘p ishlatiladigan iboralar

  • At 7 o'clock yesterday — kecha soat 7 da
  • At that moment — o‘sha paytda
  • This time yesterday — kecha shu vaqtda
  • While — paytida
  • When — qachon / -ganda
  • All evening — butun kech davomida
DAFTARGA YOZING:

Har ikkala zamon uchun kamida 5 tadan signal iborani tarjimasi bilan yozing.

So‘ng har bir zamon uchun 3 tadan mustaqil gap tuzing.

7. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

Wrong: I was go to school.

Correct: I was going to school.

Sabab: was dan keyin Past Continuous da V-ing ishlatiladi.

Wrong: She didn't went home.

Correct: She didn't go home.

Sabab: didn't dan keyin V1 ishlatiladi.

Wrong: They was playing.

Correct: They were playing.

Sabab: They bilan were ishlatiladi.

Wrong: Did you watched TV?

Correct: Did you watch TV?

Sabab: Did bilan V1 ishlatiladi.

DAFTARGA YOZING:

4 ta noto‘g‘ri gapni va ularning to‘g‘ri variantlarini yozing.

Har bir xatoning sababini ham yozing.

12. Reading — o‘qib tushunish

A Day I Will Never Forget

Last Saturday was a very interesting day. I woke up early because I wanted to visit my friend. The weather was beautiful, and the sun was shining.

At ten o'clock, I was walking to the bus stop when I saw an old friend. We were very happy to see each other. We talked for a few minutes and then went to a small café.

While we were drinking coffee, my friend told me about his new job. He started working at a big company two months ago. He really enjoys his work.

After that, we walked around the city. People were shopping, children were playing in the park, and some musicians were performing in the street.

In the evening, I returned home. My family was having dinner when I arrived. I told them about my wonderful day.

It was a simple day, but I will always remember it.
