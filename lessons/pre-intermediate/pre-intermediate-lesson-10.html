2. GRAMMAR — PAST CONTINUOUS

2.1. Past Continuous ta’rifi

Past Continuous — o‘tmishda boshlangan va o‘tmishdagi ma’lum bir vaqtda davom etayotgan harakatni ifodalaydi.

English rule:

We use the Past Continuous to talk about actions that were happening at a particular time in the past.

Example 1:

She was cooking dinner at 7 p.m.

U kechki soat 7 da ovqat tayyorlayotgan edi.

Example 2:

We were watching TV last night.

Biz kecha kechqurun televizor ko‘rayotgan edik.

2.2. Qachon ishlatiladi?

1-holat: O‘tmishdagi aniq vaqtda davom etgan harakat.

Kecha soat 6 da, o‘tgan kuni ertalab yoki boshqa aniq vaqtda nima qilayotganimizni aytishda ishlatiladi.

I was working at 9 o’clock yesterday.

Kecha soat 9 da ishlayotgan edim.

My mother was sleeping at midnight.

Onam yarim tunda uxlayotgan edi.

2-holat: O‘tmishda bir vaqtda davom etgan ikki harakat.

Ikki ish-harakat bir vaqtning o‘zida davom etgan bo‘lsa, Past Continuous ishlatilishi mumkin.

I was reading while my sister was cooking.

Singlim ovqat tayyorlayotgan paytda men kitob o‘qiyotgan edim.

They were talking while we were working.

Biz ishlayotganimizda ular suhbatlashayotgan edi.

3-holat: Davom etayotgan harakatni boshqa qisqa harakat bo‘lib bo‘lishi.

Davom etayotgan ish uchun Past Continuous, uni bo‘lgan qisqa harakat uchun Past Simple ishlatiladi.

I was sleeping when the phone rang.

Telefon jiringlaganida men uxlayotgan edim.

She was walking when it started to rain.

Yomg‘ir yog‘a boshlaganida u yurayotgan edi.

2.3. Darak gap — Affirmative

Darak gapda subjectdan keyin was yoki were, undan keyin fe’lning -ing shakli keladi.

Subject + was/were + Verb-ing

Formula qismlarining ta’rifi

S — Subject: gapning egasi, ya’ni ish-harakatni bajarayotgan shaxs yoki narsa.

I was reading. — Men o‘qiyotgan edim.

Was: I, he, she, it va birlikdagi ega bilan ishlatiladi.

He was working. — U ishlayotgan edi.

Were: you, we, they va ko‘plikdagi ega bilan ishlatiladi.

They were playing. — Ular o‘ynayotgan edi.

V-ing: asosiy fe’lga -ing qo‘shilgan shakl.

She was dancing. — U raqsga tushayotgan edi.

Was va Were jadvali

Subject Yordamchi fe’l
Iwas
He / She / Itwas
You / We / Theywere
Plural nounswere

2.4. Inkor gap — Negative

Inkor gapda was yoki were dan keyin not qo‘shiladi.

Subject + was/were + not + Verb-ing

Subject: gap egasi.

I was not sleeping. — Men uxlamayotgan edim.

Was not: was yordamchi fe’lining inkor shakli.

She was not working. — U ishlamayotgan edi.

Were not: were yordamchi fe’lining inkor shakli.

They were not studying. — Ular dars qilmayotgan edi.

Verb-ing: harakat fe’lining -ing shakli.

We were not watching TV. — Biz televizor ko‘rmayotgan edik.

Qisqartmalar

was not = wasn’t

were not = weren’t

I wasn’t listening. — Men tinglamayotgan edim.

2.5. So‘roq gap — Interrogative

So‘roq gapda was yoki were gap boshiga chiqadi.

Was/Were + Subject + Verb-ing?

Was/Were: yordamchi fe’l gap boshida keladi.

Was he sleeping? — U uxlayotgan edimi?

Subject: savol berilayotgan shaxs yoki narsa.

Were they working? — Ular ishlayotgan edimi?

Verb-ing: harakatning davomiy shakli.

Was she reading? — U o‘qiyotgan edimi?

Qisqa javoblar

Was he studying? — Yes, he was. / No, he wasn’t.

Were you sleeping? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn’t.

2.6. Verb + ing qoidalari

Past Continuousda asosiy fe’lga odatda -ing qo‘shiladi.

work → working

read → reading

play → playing

listen → listening

1-qoida: Odatda -ing qo‘shiladi

Fe’l oxiriga shunchaki -ing qo‘shiladi.

She was cleaning the room. — U xonani tozalayotgan edi.
We were watching a film. — Biz film tomosha qilayotgan edik.

2-qoida: Oxiridagi e harfi tushadi

Ko‘p fe’llarda oxirgi e tushib, -ing qo‘shiladi.

make → making; write → writing; dance → dancing
He was making tea. — U choy tayyorlayotgan edi.
I was writing a letter. — Men xat yozayotgan edim.

3-qoida: Ba’zi qisqa fe’llarda oxirgi undosh ikki marta yoziladi

sit → sitting; run → running; swim → swimming
The boy was running. — Bola yugurayotgan edi.
She was sitting near the window. — U deraza yonida o‘tirgan edi.

4-qoida: -ie bilan tugagan fe’llar

lie → lying; die → dying
The dog was lying on the floor. — It polda yotgan edi.
The plant was dying. — O‘simlik qurib borayotgan edi.

2.7. Time Expressions — vaqt ko‘rsatkichlari

Past Continuous bilan quyidagi vaqt iboralari ko‘p ishlatiladi:

At 7 o’clock yesterday — kecha soat 7 da

At this time yesterday — kecha shu vaqtda

While — ... paytida

When — ... ganida

All evening — butun kech davomida

From 5 to 6 yesterday — kecha 5 dan 6 gacha

At this time yesterday, I was travelling.

Kecha shu vaqtda men sayohat qilayotgan edim.

While I was cooking, my brother was studying.

Men ovqat tayyorlayotganimda, akam dars qilayotgan edi.

2.8. When va While farqi

When: ko‘pincha davom etayotgan harakatni bo‘lib yuborgan qisqa voqea bilan ishlatiladi.
I was walking when I saw my friend.

Do‘stimni ko‘rganimda men yurayotgan edim.

She was sleeping when the door opened.

Eshik ochilganida u uxlayotgan edi.

While: ko‘pincha bir vaqtda davom etayotgan harakatlarni bog‘laydi.
While I was reading, my father was watching TV.

Men o‘qiyotganimda, otam televizor ko‘rayotgan edi.

While they were playing, we were studying.

Ular o‘ynayotgan paytda biz dars qilayotgan edik.

2.9. Eng ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

❌ I was play football.

✅ I was playing football.

Was dan keyin fe’lning -ing shakli ishlatiladi.

❌ They was sleeping.

✅ They were sleeping.

They bilan were ishlatiladi.

❌ Was you working?

✅ Were you working?

You bilan were ishlatiladi.

❌ She wasn't cooked.

✅ She wasn't cooking.

Past Continuousda asosiy fe’l -ing shaklida bo‘ladi.

6. READING — A STRANGE EVENING

Matnni diqqat bilan o‘qing. Past Continuous ishlatilgan gaplarga e’tibor bering.

A Strange Evening

Yesterday evening, Tom was walking home when he heard a strange sound. It was raining, and the streets were almost empty. A woman was standing near a small shop. She was looking for something in her bag.

Tom wanted to help her. While he was talking to the woman, a little dog came out from behind the shop. The woman smiled because she was looking for her dog.

At that moment, a car stopped near them. The driver was asking if everything was all right. The woman thanked Tom and went home with her dog.

Tom was feeling happy because he was helping someone.

A Busy Day at Home

Last Saturday was a busy day for my family. At 9 o’clock in the morning, my mother was cleaning the house. My father was washing his car outside. I was helping my younger brother with his homework.
