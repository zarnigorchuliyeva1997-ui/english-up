1. There is / There are nima?

There is va There are biror joyda nimadir borligini yoki mavjudligini bildirish uchun ishlatiladi.

O‘zbek tilidagi “bor” so‘ziga o‘xshaydi.

There is a book on the table.
Stol ustida bitta kitob bor.
There are three books on the table.
Stol ustida uchta kitob bor.
There is — birlikdagi otlar bilan.
There are — ko‘plikdagi otlar bilan.

Esda tuting: bu yerda asosiy e'tibor kimdir biror narsani qilayotganiga emas, balki biror narsaning mavjudligiga qaratiladi.

2. Affirmative — Darak gap

Formula 1:
There is + a/an + singular noun.
There is a chair in the room.
There is an apple on the table.
Formula 2:
There are + plural noun.
There are two chairs in the room.
There are many students in the classroom.

Qisqartmalar

Full formShort form
There isThere's
There areThere're (odatda qisqartirilmaydi)
Kundalik ingliz tilida “There's” juda ko‘p ishlatiladi. “There are” esa odatda to‘liq shaklda aytiladi.

3. Negative — Inkor gap

There is not + singular noun.
There are not + plural noun.
Full formShort formMa'nosi
There is notThere isn'tYo‘q
There are notThere aren'tYo‘q
There isn't a TV in my bedroom.
Mening yotoqxonamda televizor yo‘q.
There aren't any students in the classroom.
Sinfda hech qanday o‘quvchi yo‘q.
Inkor gaplarda ko‘pincha any ishlatiladi:
There aren't any apples.

4. Questions — So‘roq gap

Formula:
Is there + singular noun...?
Are there + plural noun...?
Is there a bank near here? — Bu yer yaqinida bank bormi?
Are there any shops nearby? — Yaqin atrofda do‘konlar bormi?

Short answers

QuestionPositiveNegative
Is there a park?Yes, there is.No, there isn't.
Are there shops?Yes, there are.No, there aren't.
There is a park? ❌
Is there a park? ✅

5. There is / There are — mixed examples

Bir gapda birlik va ko‘plikdagi otlar birga kelishi mumkin.

There is a sofa and two chairs in the living room.
There are two chairs and a sofa in the living room.

Ko‘pincha fe'l undan keyin kelgan birinchi otga moslashadi. Lekin gapni aniq va sodda tuzish tavsiya etiladi.

8. A va An — Noaniq artikllar

A va An birlikdagi sanaladigan otlardan oldin ishlatiladi. Biror narsani birinchi marta tilga olganimizda ham qo‘llanadi.

A — undosh tovush bilan boshlanadigan talaffuzdan oldin.
a book, a car, a university.
An — unli tovush bilan boshlanadigan talaffuzdan oldin.
an apple, an egg, an hour.
Muhim: harfga emas, tovushga qaraymiz.
a university — “yu” tovushi bilan boshlanadi.
an hour — h talaffuz qilinmaydi.

Misollar

There is a book on the desk.
There is an orange in the fridge.
She is a teacher.
He is an engineer.

9. The — Aniq artikl

The muayyan, aniq narsa haqida gapirganda ishlatiladi.

A / An dan keyin The

I saw a dog. The dog was very friendly.

Birinchi gapda it haqida birinchi marta gapiryapmiz. Ikkinchi gapda esa qaysi it ekanligi ma'lum.

Hamma biladigan yagona narsalar

The sun is bright.
The moon looks beautiful.

Muayyan joy yoki narsa

Please close the door.
The kitchen is clean.
A/An — noma'lum yoki birinchi marta aytilayotgan birlikdagi narsa.
The — aniq, ma'lum narsa.

10. Some va Any

Some va Any miqdori aniq bo‘lmagan narsalarni bildirishda ishlatiladi.

Some — ko‘pincha darak gaplarda.
There are some apples.
There is some milk.
Any — ko‘pincha inkor va so‘roq gaplarda.
There aren't any apples.
Is there any milk?

Some bilan taklif va iltimos

Would you like some tea?
Can I have some water?
Some faqat ko‘plikdagi sanaladigan otlar bilan emas, sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ham ishlatiladi.

11. Much va Many

Many — sanaladigan ko‘plik otlar bilan.
How many books are there?
Much — sanalmaydigan otlar bilan.
How much water is there?

12. A lot of / Lots of

A lot of va lots of ko‘p miqdorni bildiradi. Ikkalasi ham sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ishlatiladi.

There are a lot of books in the library.
There is a lot of water in the bottle.
We have lots of friends.
She has lots of work today.
A lot of kundalik suhbatlarda juda foydali. Many va Much ga qaraganda darak gaplarda tabiiyroq eshitiladi.

13. A few va A little

A few — sanaladigan ko‘plik otlar bilan.
I have a few friends.
A little — sanalmaydigan otlar bilan.

14. How many va How much

How many + plural countable noun...?
How much + uncountable noun...?

Javoblarda son yoki miqdorni aytamiz.

There is a little rice.

🎧 Listening 1 — My New Apartment

Avval tinglang. Keyin dialogni o‘qing. So‘ng yana tinglab, gaplarni takrorlang.

Dialogue

Anna: Welcome to my new apartment, Jack!
Jack: Thank you! It looks lovely. How many rooms are there?
Anna: There are three bedrooms, a living room and a kitchen.
Jack: Is there a balcony?
Anna: Yes, there is. There are some flowers on it.
Jack: That's wonderful. Is there much furniture?
Anna: Not really. There is a sofa, a table and a few chairs.
Jack: Is there a supermarket nearby?
Anna: Yes, there are two supermarkets near the apartment.
Jack: It sounds like a great place to live!

Uzbekcha mazmuni

Anna Jekni yangi kvartirasiga taklif qiladi. Kvartirada uchta yotoqxona, mehmonxona va oshxona bor. Balkonda gullar bor. Uyda hozircha ko‘p mebel yo‘q. Kvartira yaqinida ikkita supermarket joylashgan.

Listening questions

  2. Is there a balcony?
  3. What is on the balcony?
  4. Is there much furniture?
  5. How many supermarkets are nearby?

📖 Reading — A Beautiful Neighbourhood

Matnni diqqat bilan o‘qing. There is / There are va miqdor bildiruvchi so‘zlarni toping.

I live in a quiet neighbourhood. There are many houses and a few small apartment buildings in my area. There is a beautiful park near my home, and there are several trees around it.

There is a supermarket on the main street. It sells a lot of fresh food. There are many different vegetables and fruits, and there is always some fresh bread. There is also a small bakery next to the supermarket.

There aren't many cars in my street, so it is usually quiet. There isn't much noise at night. There are a few cafés and restaurants nearby, but there aren't any large shopping centres.

I like my neighbourhood because there are friendly people and many useful places. There is everything I need for everyday life.

Matnning o‘zbekcha mazmuni

Muallif tinch mahallada yashaydi. Uning hududida ko‘plab uylar va bir nechta kichik ko‘p qavatli binolar bor. Uy yaqinida chiroyli park, asosiy ko‘chada supermarket va uning yonida novvoyxona joylashgan. Mahallada mashinalar va shovqin ko‘p emas. Yaqin atrofda bir nechta kafe va restoranlar bor. Muallif o‘z mahallasini yaxshi ko‘radi, chunki u yerda kundalik hayot uchun kerakli joylar mavjud.

Reading comprehension

  1. Where does the writer live?
  2. What is near the writer's home?
  3. What does the supermarket sell?
  4. What is next to the supermarket?
  5. Are there many cars in the street?
  6. Is there much noise at night?
  7. Are there any shopping centres?
  8. Why does the writer like the neighbourhood?

Reading analysis

Matndan quyidagilarni toping va daftarga yozing:

  • Many ishlatilgan gap.

Keyin matnni o‘zingizning mahallangizga moslab qayta yozing.
