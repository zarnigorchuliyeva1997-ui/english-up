1 Grammar Explanation

MAIN TOPIC

1. Present Continuous nima?

Present Continuous — hozir sodir bo'layotgan yoki ma'lum bir vaqt davomida davom etayotgan harakatlarni ifodalash uchun ishlatiladi.

I am studying English now.
Men hozir ingliz tilini o'rganyapman.
She is talking on the phone.
U telefonda gaplashyapti.
They are playing football.
Ular futbol o'ynashyapti.

2. Present Continuous formulasi

Positive — Darak gap
Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing

I am working.

He is reading.

They are studying.

Negative — Inkor gap
Subject + am/is/are + not + Verb-ing

I am not sleeping.

She is not cooking.

We are not watching TV.

Question — So'roq gap
Am/Is/Are + Subject + Verb-ing?

Are you listening?

Is he working?

Am I speaking correctly?

3. Am / Is / Are ishlatilishi

SubjectHelping verbExample
IamI am reading.
HeisHe is reading.
SheisShe is reading.
ItisIt is raining.
YouareYou are reading.
WeareWe are reading.
TheyareThey are reading.
Esda saqlang!

I bilan faqat AM, He/She/It bilan IS, You/We/They bilan ARE ishlatiladi.

Present Continuous gapida yordamchi fe'lni tashlab ketish mumkin emas.

5. Qachon ishlatiladi?

A. Hozir sodir bo'layotgan harakat

I am writing a message now.

Men hozir xabar yozyapman.

B. Vaqtinchalik vaziyat

He is living with his parents this month.

U shu oy ota-onasi bilan yashayapti.

C. O'zgarayotgan vaziyat

The weather is getting colder.

Ob-havo sovuqlashib boryapti.

D. Kelajakdagi kelishilgan reja

We are meeting our teacher tomorrow.

Biz ertaga o'qituvchimiz bilan uchrashyapmiz.

7. Present Simple va Present Continuous farqi

Present SimplePresent Continuous
I work every day.I am working now.
Men har kuni ishlayman.Men hozir ishlayapman.
She reads books.She is reading a book.
He usually walks.He is walking now.
MUHIM FARQ:

Present Simple — odat, takrorlanadigan ish va umumiy haqiqat.

Present Continuous — ayni paytdagi yoki vaqtinchalik harakat.

2 Listening Practice

Har bir dialogni kamida 3 marta tinglang. Birinchi marta umumiy mazmunni tushuning. Ikkinchi marta tafsilotlarga e'tibor bering. Uchinchi marta eshitgan gaplaringizni takrorlang.

🎧 Listening 1 — A Busy Morning

Daraja: Pre-Intermediate

Mavzu: Morning activities

Dialog matni

Anna: Good morning, Tom. What are you doing?

Tom: I am preparing breakfast.

Anna: Are the children getting ready for school?

Tom: Yes, they are. Emma is brushing her teeth and Jack is looking for his school bag.

Anna: What is your wife doing?

Tom: She is making coffee. We are all getting ready for a busy day.

Listening questions

  1. What is Tom preparing?
  2. What is Emma doing?
  3. What is Jack looking for?
  4. What is Tom's wife making?
  5. Why is the family busy?

🎧 Listening 2 — Plans for the Weekend

Daraja: Pre-Intermediate

Dialog matni

David: Hi, Sarah. Are you doing anything interesting this weekend?

Sarah: Yes, I am visiting my grandparents on Saturday.

David: What are you doing on Sunday?

Sarah: I am meeting some friends in the city centre.

David: Are you going by bus?

Sarah: No, we are travelling by train because it is more comfortable.

David: That sounds great. Are you staying there all day?

Sarah: No, we are coming home in the evening.

Listening questions

  1. Who is Sarah visiting on Saturday?
  2. Who is she meeting on Sunday?
  3. Where are they meeting?
  4. How are they travelling?
  5. When are they coming home?
📒 Listening daftari:

Har ikkala dialogdan kamida 5 ta gapni tanlang. Ularni ingliz tilida daftarga yozing, o'zbek tiliga tarjima qiling va ovoz chiqarib takrorlang.

4 Reading Practice

READING • B1 PREPARATION

A Different Week

My name is Daniel, and I usually work in an office. However, this week is different because I am working from home. My company is changing its office, so all the employees are working remotely.

At the moment, I am sitting in my living room and answering emails. My colleague James is preparing a presentation, and our manager is talking to a new customer.

My wife is also working today. She is teaching English online. Our children are staying with their grandparents because their school is closed for a few days.

We are planning a short family trip for next weekend. We are visiting a small town near the mountains. I am looking forward to this trip because I really need some rest.

New expressions

  • Work from home — uydan ishlamoq
  • Remotely — masofadan
  • At the moment — ayni paytda
  • Answer emails — xatlarga javob bermoq
  • Look forward to — intiqlik bilan kutmoq
  • Short family trip — qisqa oilaviy sayohat
  • Need some rest — biroz dam olishga muhtoj bo'lmoq

Reading comprehension

  1. Where does Daniel usually work?
  2. Why is he working from home this week?
  3. What is James preparing?
  4. Who is teaching English online?
  5. Where are the children staying?
  6. What are they planning for next weekend?
  7. Why is Daniel looking forward to the trip?
📒 Matnni yana bir marta o'qing. Undagi Present Continuous zamonida yozilgan gaplarni toping va daftaringizga ko'chiring.
