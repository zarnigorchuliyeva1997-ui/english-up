📚 2. GRAMMAR — ADJECTIVES & ADVERBS

2.1. What is an adjective?

Adjective — sifat. U otning qandayligini, rangini, hajmini, holatini yoki xususiyatini bildiradi.

Ingliz tilida adjective ko‘pincha otning oldidan keladi.

Adjective + Noun
She has a beautiful house.

Uning chiroyli uyi bor.

He is a hardworking student.

U mehnatkash o‘quvchi.

They bought an expensive car.

Ular qimmat mashina sotib olishdi.

Adjective quyidagi savollarga javob beradi:

  • What kind? — Qanday?
  • Which one? — Qaysi?
  • How many? — Nechta? (miqdor bildiruvchi ayrim so‘zlar ham aniqlovchi vazifasida keladi.)

2.2. Adjectives after linking verbs

Adjective faqat ot oldidan kelmaydi. U be, look, feel, seem, sound, smell, taste, become kabi fe'llardan keyin ham ishlatiladi.

Subject + Linking Verb + Adjective
She is happy.

U baxtli.

You look tired.

Siz charchaganga o‘xshaysiz.

This food smells delicious.

Bu taomning hidi mazali.

The music sounds beautiful.

Musiqa chiroyli eshitiladi.

I feel nervous before exams.

Imtihonlardan oldin asabiylashaman.

Muhim: Linking verbdan keyin shaxs yoki narsaning holatini tasvirlasak, odatda adjective ishlatamiz: I feel good. She looks happy.

2.3. What is an adverb?

Adverb — ravish. U harakatning qanday, qachon, qayerda yoki qay darajada bajarilishini bildiradi.

Subject + Verb + Adverb
She speaks clearly.

U aniq gapiradi.

He drives carefully.

U ehtiyotkorlik bilan mashina haydaydi.

They work hard.

Ular astoydil ishlashadi.

Adverb quyidagi savollarga javob berishi mumkin:

  • How? — Qanday?
  • When? — Qachon?
  • Where? — Qayerda?
  • How often? — Qanchalik tez-tez?
  • To what extent? — Qay darajada?

2.4. Adjective va adverb o‘rtasidagi farq

AdjectiveAdverbTarjima
He is a slow runner.He runs slowly.U sekin yuguradi.
She is a careful driver.She drives carefully.U ehtiyotkor haydaydi.
He is a good singer.He sings well.U yaxshi kuylaydi.
She is a beautiful dancer.She dances beautifully.U chiroyli raqsga tushadi.
He is a fast learner.He learns fast.U tez o‘rganadi.

Bir xil gapda ikkala turdagi so‘z ham kelishi mumkin:

She is a careful student, and she always works carefully.

U ehtiyotkor o‘quvchi va doimo ehtiyotkorlik bilan ishlaydi.

2.5. How to make adverbs with -ly

Ko‘pchilik ravishlar adjectivega -ly qo‘shimchasini qo‘shish orqali yasaladi.

Adjective + ly = Adverb
AdjectiveAdverb
QuickQuickly
SlowSlowly
CarefulCarefully
BeautifulBeautifully
QuietQuietly
SafeSafely
PolitePolitely
SuddenSuddenly

Imlo qoidasi 1: -y bilan tugagan adjective so‘zlarida y harfi i ga almashadi.

Happy → Happily
Easy → Easily
Angry → Angrily

Imlo qoidasi 2: Ayrim -le bilan tugagan so‘zlarda oxirgi e tushadi.

Gentle → Gently
Simple → Simply

Imlo qoidasi 3: Ayrim -ic bilan tugagan so‘zlarga -ally qo‘shiladi.

Basic → Basically
Automatic → Automatically

2.6. Irregular adverbs

Ba'zi ravishlar oddiy -ly qoidasi bilan yasalmaydi. Ularni alohida yodlash kerak.

AdjectiveAdverbMa'nosi

FastFastTez

He is a good player.
U yaxshi o‘yinchi.
He plays well.
U yaxshi o‘ynaydi.
She works hard.
U astoydil ishlaydi.
Hard va hardly bir xil emas. Hard — astoydil, hardly — deyarli ... emas. Masalan: I hardly know him — Men uni deyarli tanimayman.

2.7. Adverbs of frequency

Bu ravishlar harakatning qanchalik tez-tez bajarilishini ko‘rsatadi.

AdverbTarjimaTaxminiy daraja
AlwaysHar doim100%
UsuallyOdatda80–90%
OftenKo‘pincha60–70%
SometimesBa'zan50%
RarelyKamdan-kam10–20%
NeverHech qachon0%

Oddiy fe'llardan oldin keladi:

I usually study English in the evening.
Men odatda kechqurun ingliz tilini o‘rganaman.

To be fe'lidan keyin keladi:

She is always polite.
U har doim odobli.

Sometimes gap boshida yoki oxirida ham ishlatilishi mumkin:

Sometimes I read English books.
Ba'zan inglizcha kitoblar o‘qiyman.

2.8. Adverbs of degree

Bu ravishlar sifat yoki boshqa ravishning darajasini bildiradi.

U nihoyatda tez yuguradi.

Xona yetarlicha iliq.

Bu sumka haddan tashqari og‘ir.

2.9. Adverbs of manner

Harakat qanday bajarilishini bildiradi. Ko‘pincha fe'ldan keyin keladi.

Verb + Adverb of manner
He answered politely.
U muloyimlik bilan javob berdi.
She opened the door quietly.
U eshikni sekingina ochdi.
They completed the task successfully.
Ular vazifani muvaffaqiyatli bajardilar.

2.10. Adverbs of time and place

Time adverbs: today, yesterday, tomorrow, now, soon, later, recently.

Place adverbs: here, there, outside, inside, upstairs, everywhere.

We will meet tomorrow.
Biz ertaga uchrashamiz.
Please wait outside.
Iltimos, tashqarida kuting.
I recently started a new course.
Men yaqinda yangi kurs boshladim.

2.11. Comparative adverbs

Ikki harakatni taqqoslash uchun ishlatiladi.

Short adverb + er + than
Ali runs faster than Vali.
Ali Validan tezroq yuguradi.

Uzun ravishlarda more + adverb + than ishlatiladi.

More + adverb + than
She speaks more clearly than before.
U avvalgidan aniqroq gapiradi.

Istisnolar:

  • Well → Better
  • Badly → Worse
  • Far → Farther / Further
He works better now.
U hozir yaxshiroq ishlaydi.

2.12. Common mistakes

❌ She sings beautiful.
✅ She sings beautifully.
❌ He drives careful.
✅ He drives carefully.
❌ I feel happily.
✅ I feel happy.
❌ She speaks good.
✅ She speaks well.
❌ He works hardly every day.
✅ He works hard every day.

🗣 7. SPEAKING — SPEAK NATURALLY

Speaking bo‘limida javoblarni yodlash emas, mustaqil fikrlash muhim. Har bir savolga 3–5 ta gap bilan javob berishga harakat qiling.

Part 1 — Personal questions

  1. Are you a careful person?
  2. Do you usually wake up early?
  3. How often do you practise English?
  4. Do you speak English confidently?
  5. What makes a lesson interesting?
  6. Do you work hard to achieve your goals?
  7. How do you usually spend your evenings?
  8. Do you learn new words quickly?

Part 2 — Describe a successful learner

Describe a person who learns new things successfully.

You should say:

  • Who the person is.
  • What the person learns.
  • How the person studies.
  • Why you think this person is successful.

Part 3 — Discussion

  1. Why is regular practice important when learning a language?
  2. Do people learn more quickly when they study independently?
  3. How can teachers make lessons more interesting?
  4. Is confidence more important than grammar in speaking?
  5. How can people improve their pronunciation?
  6. Why do some students lose motivation?

Speaking model answer

I think a successful learner needs patience and discipline. A good student studies regularly and listens carefully to the teacher. Sometimes learning can be difficult, but a confident person does not give up easily. I try to improve my English gradually because I want to communicate clearly and naturally.

Menimcha, muvaffaqiyatli o‘quvchiga sabr va intizom kerak. Yaxshi o‘quvchi muntazam shug‘ullanadi va o‘qituvchini diqqat bilan tinglaydi. Ba'zan o‘rganish qiyin bo‘lishi mumkin, lekin o‘ziga ishongan inson osonlikcha taslim bo‘lmaydi. Men ingliz tilimni asta-sekin yaxshilashga harakat qilaman, chunki aniq va tabiiy muloqot qilishni xohlayman.

Speaking self-practice

Telefoningizdagi ovoz yozish vositasidan foydalanib, Part 2 mavzusida 1–2 daqiqalik javob yozib oling.
