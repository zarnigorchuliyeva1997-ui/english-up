📚 2. GRAMMAR — ADJECTIVES

1. What is an Adjective?

Adjective — sifat. U otning qandayligini, rangini, hajmini, holatini yoki xususiyatini bildiradi.

Adjective + Noun

Sifat odatda otning oldidan keladi.

She has a beautiful house.

Uning chiroyli uyi bor.

He is a careful driver.

U ehtiyotkor haydovchi.

This is an interesting book.

Bu qiziqarli kitob.

2. Adjective after the verb TO BE

Sifat faqat otdan oldin kelmaydi. U am, is, are, was, were fe'llaridan keyin ham ishlatiladi.

Subject + am/is/are + Adjective

I am happy.

Men xursandman.

She is intelligent.

U aqlli.

They are busy.

Ular band.

3. Adjectives with other linking verbs

Quyidagi fe'llardan keyin ham sifat ishlatilishi mumkin:

  • Look — ko‘rinmoq
  • Feel — his qilmoq
  • Sound — eshitilmoq
  • Smell — hid taratmoq
  • Taste — ta'mga ega bo‘lmoq
  • Become — bo‘lib qolmoq
  • Get — bo‘lib qolmoq

You look tired.

Siz charchagan ko‘rinasiz.

I feel comfortable.

Men o‘zimni qulay his qilyapman.

The food smells delicious.

Ovqatning hidi mazali.

The weather is getting cold.

Havo sovib bormoqda.

📓 DAFTARGA YOZING

Adjectives haqida quyidagilarni yozing:

  1. Adjective ta'rifi.
  2. Adjective + Noun formulasi.
  3. Subject + be + Adjective formulasi.
  4. 6 ta linking verb.
  5. Yuqoridagi formulalar asosida 8 ta mustaqil gap.

5. Adjective order

Ingliz tilida bir nechta sifat bir otni tasvirlaganda, odatda ma'lum tartibga rioya qilinadi.

Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Color → Origin → Material → Noun

A beautiful small old Italian house.

Chiroyli kichkina eski italyan uyi.

A lovely large round wooden table.

Yoqimli katta dumaloq yog‘och stol.

Eslatma: Bu tartib barcha vaziyatlarda barcha sifatlarni ishlatish kerak degani emas. Odatda 2–3 ta sifat yetarli bo‘ladi.

📘 3. ADVERBS — RAVISHLAR

1. What is an Adverb?

Adverb — ravish. U harakatning qanday, qachon, qayerda yoki qanchalik bajarilishini bildiradi.

Subject + Verb + Adverb

He speaks slowly.

U sekin gapiradi.

She works carefully.

U ehtiyotkorlik bilan ishlaydi.

They arrived early.

Ular erta yetib kelishdi.

2. How to form Adverbs?

Ko‘plab ravishlar sifatga -ly qo‘shimchasini qo‘shish orqali yasaladi.

Adjective + ly = Adverb
Adjective Adverb Ma'nosi
SlowSlowlySekin
QuickQuicklyTez
CarefulCarefullyEhtiyotkorlik bilan
BeautifulBeautifullyChiroyli tarzda
QuietQuietlyJimgina
LoudLoudlyBaland ovozda

3. Spelling rules

Rule 1: -y bilan tugagan sifatlar

y → ily
Happy → Happily
Easy → Easily
Angry → Angrily

Rule 2: -le bilan tugagan ayrim sifatlar

-le → -ly
Simple → Simply
Gentle → Gently
Possible → Possibly

Rule 3: -ic bilan tugagan sifatlar

-ic → -ically
Basic → Basically
Automatic → Automatically
Scientific → Scientifically

Rule 4: -e bilan tugagan sifatlar

Ko‘pchilik sifatlarda -ly qo‘shiladi, lekin ayrimlarida imlo o‘zgaradi.

Nice → Nicely
Safe → Safely
True → Truly

To‘rtta imlo qoidasini alohida yozing. Har bir qoida uchun kamida 5 ta misol keltiring.

4. Irregular adverbs

Ba'zi ravishlar oddiy -ly qoidasi bilan yasalmasligi mumkin.

Adjective Adverb Uzbek
GoodWellYaxshi

EarlyEarlyErta

She is a good singer.

U yaxshi qo‘shiqchi.

She sings well.

U tez yuguruvchi.

DIQQAT: Hard va hardly bir xil ma'noga ega emas. Hard — astoydil, hardly — deyarli ... emas.

He works hard.

U astoydil ishlaydi.

I hardly know him.

Men uni deyarli tanimayman.

5. Adjective or Adverb?

Eng muhim farq: adjective odatda otni yoki shaxsning holatini tasvirlaydi. Adverb esa ko‘pincha harakat qanday bajarilishini tasvirlaydi.

He is careful.

U ehtiyotkor.

Careful — sifat. U qanday inson?

He drives carefully.

U ehtiyotkorlik bilan mashina haydaydi.

Carefully — ravish. U qanday haydaydi?

The test is easy.

Test oson.

She answered easily.

U osonlik bilan javob berdi.

6. Adverbs of frequency

Bu ravishlar ish-harakat qanchalik tez-tez bajarilishini bildiradi.

Adverb Tarjima
AlwaysHar doim
UsuallyOdatda
OftenTez-tez
SometimesBa'zan
RarelyKamdan-kam
NeverHech qachon
Subject + Frequency Adverb + Main Verb

I always drink water in the morning.

Men ertalab har doim suv ichaman.

She usually goes to school by bus.

U odatda maktabga avtobusda boradi.

To be fe'lidan keyin frequency adverb keladi:
Subject + be + Adverb
He is always busy.

📊 4. COMPARATIVE ADVERBS

1. Comparing actions

Ikki kishining yoki narsaning harakatini taqqoslash uchun comparative adverbs ishlatiladi.

Adverb + -er + than

Qisqa ravishlarda -er qo‘shiladi.

He runs faster than me.

U mendan tezroq yuguradi.

She works harder than her brother.

U akasidan ko‘ra astoydilroq ishlaydi.

2. More + Adverb + than

Ko‘pchilik -ly bilan tugagan ravishlar more yordamida taqqoslanadi.

More + Adverb + than

She speaks more clearly than before.

U avvalgidan aniqroq gapiradi.

He drives more carefully than his friend.

U do‘stidan ehtiyotkorroq haydaydi.

3. Irregular comparisons

Positive Comparative Superlative
WellBetterBest
BadlyWorseWorst
FarFarther / FurtherFarthest / Furthest

She sings better than me.

U mendan yaxshiroq kuylaydi.

He behaved worse than yesterday.

U kechagidan yomonroq xulq-atvor ko‘rsatdi.

4. As ... as

Ikki harakat bir xil darajada bajarilishini bildirish uchun ishlatiladi.

As + Adverb + As

He runs as fast as his brother.

U akasi kabi tez yuguradi.

She speaks as clearly as her teacher.

U o‘qituvchisi kabi aniq gapiradi.

5. Less ... than

Biror harakat boshqa harakatga qaraganda kamroq darajada bajarilishini bildiradi.

Less + Adverb + Than

He drives less carefully than his father.

U otasiga qaraganda kamroq ehtiyotkorlik bilan haydaydi.

I speak less confidently than my friend.

Men do‘stimga qaraganda kamroq ishonch bilan gapiraman.

📓 DAFTARGA YOZING

Comparative adverbs bo‘yicha 5 ta formulani yozing.

Har bir formula uchun 3 tadan inglizcha gap va ularning o‘zbekcha tarjimasini yozing.

🎧 6. LISTENING PRACTICE

Listening 1 — A Busy Morning

Matnni avval o‘zingiz o‘qing. Keyin telefoningizdagi matnni ovoz chiqarib o‘qish funksiyasi orqali tinglang.

Every morning, Daniel wakes up early. He usually gets out of bed quickly and prepares his breakfast carefully. He is a responsible person, so he always plans his day. He speaks politely to his family and listens carefully when they talk. He works hard because he wants to improve his life. Sometimes he feels tired, but he never gives up. He believes that small steps can bring big results.

Har kuni ertalab Daniel erta uyg‘onadi. U odatda tezda o‘rnidan turadi va nonushtasini ehtiyotkorlik bilan tayyorlaydi. U mas'uliyatli inson, shuning uchun kunini doimo rejalashtiradi. U oilasi bilan xushmuomalalik bilan gaplashadi va ular gapirganda diqqat bilan tinglaydi. U hayotini yaxshilashni xohlagani uchun astoydil ishlaydi. Ba'zan charchaydi, ammo hech qachon taslim bo‘lmaydi. U kichik qadamlar katta natijalarga olib kelishiga ishonadi.

Listening 1 — Questions

Listening 2 — Learning English

Sarah is learning English because she wants to communicate confidently with people from different countries. At first, she spoke slowly and made many mistakes. However, she practised regularly and listened carefully to English conversations. Her teacher explained grammar clearly and gave her useful advice. Now Sarah speaks more confidently than before. She still makes mistakes, but she understands that learning a language takes time. She works patiently and tries to improve every day.

Sarah turli mamlakatlardan bo‘lgan odamlar bilan ishonchli muloqot qilishni xohlagani uchun ingliz tilini o‘rganmoqda. Avvaliga u sekin gapirgan va ko‘plab xatolarga yo‘l qo‘ygan. Biroq muntazam mashq qilgan va inglizcha suhbatlarni diqqat bilan tinglagan. O‘qituvchisi grammatikani aniq tushuntirgan va unga foydali maslahatlar bergan. Hozir Sarah avvalgidan ko‘ra ishonchliroq gapiradi. U hali ham xatolar qiladi, ammo til o‘rganish vaqt talab qilishini tushunadi. U sabr bilan ishlaydi va har kuni rivojlanishga harakat qiladi.

📚 7. READING PRACTICE

Reading 1 — The Importance of Good Habits

Good habits are important for a successful life. People who manage their time carefully often achieve their goals more easily. A healthy routine helps people feel better and work more effectively. For example, waking up early can give you more time to study or exercise. Reading regularly improves your vocabulary and develops your thinking skills. Speaking confidently also becomes easier when you practise every day. You do not need to change everything immediately. Start slowly, work consistently and remain positive. Small improvements can make a big difference over time.

Yaxshi odatlar muvaffaqiyatli hayot uchun muhimdir. Vaqtini ehtiyotkorlik bilan boshqaradigan insonlar ko‘pincha maqsadlariga osonroq erishadilar. Sog‘lom kundalik tartib odamlarning o‘zini yaxshi his qilishiga va samaraliroq ishlashiga yordam beradi. Masalan, erta uyg‘onish sizga o‘qish yoki mashq qilish uchun ko‘proq vaqt beradi. Muntazam kitob o‘qish so‘z boyligingizni oshiradi va fikrlash qobiliyatingizni rivojlantiradi. Har kuni mashq qilganingizda ishonch bilan gapirish ham osonlashadi. Hammasini darhol o‘zgartirishingiz shart emas. Sekin boshlang, muntazam ishlang va ijobiy bo‘ling. Vaqt o‘tishi bilan kichik yaxshilanishlar katta farq yaratishi mumkin.

Reading 1 — Questions

Reading 2 — A Responsible Student

Tom is a responsible student who studies hard. He does not always understand everything immediately, but he tries patiently. He reads English texts carefully and writes new vocabulary in his notebook. His pronunciation is becoming clearer because he practises aloud. He also listens to English stories regularly. Sometimes his classmates learn faster than him, but Tom does not compare himself with others. He focuses on his own progress. He knows that regular practice is more important than studying for many hours once a week. His main goal is to speak English naturally and confidently.

Tom astoydil o‘qiydigan mas'uliyatli o‘quvchi. U hamma narsani doimo darhol tushunmaydi, ammo sabr bilan harakat qiladi. U inglizcha matnlarni diqqat bilan o‘qiydi va yangi so‘zlarni daftariga yozadi. Ovoz chiqarib mashq qilgani uchun uning talaffuzi tobora aniqroq bo‘lmoqda. Shuningdek, u muntazam inglizcha hikoyalarni tinglaydi. Ba'zan sinfdoshlari undan tezroq o‘rganadi, ammo Tom o‘zini boshqalar bilan taqqoslamaydi. U o‘z rivojlanishiga e'tibor qaratadi. Haftada bir marta ko‘p soat o‘qigandan ko‘ra muntazam mashq qilish muhimroq ekanini biladi. Uning asosiy maqsadi ingliz tilida tabiiy va ishonch bilan gapirishdir.
