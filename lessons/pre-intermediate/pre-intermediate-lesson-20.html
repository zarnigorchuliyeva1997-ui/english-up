3. Grammar — Everyday Communication

Bugun kundalik muloqotda ishlatiladigan 10 ta muhim kommunikativ usulni o‘rganamiz.

1. Asking for Information

Ma'lumot so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi.

Structure: Could you tell me + question?

Example: Could you tell me where the station is?

Tarjimasi: Bekat qayerdaligini ayta olasizmi?

2. Giving Advice

Maslahat berish uchun ishlatiladi.

Structure: You should + verb.

Example: You should practise English every day.

Tarjimasi: Har kuni ingliz tilini mashq qilishing kerak.

3. Making Suggestions

Biror ishni taklif qilish.

Structure: Why don't we + verb?

Example: Why don't we watch an English movie?

Tarjimasi: Nega inglizcha film ko‘rmaymiz?

4. Agreeing and Disagreeing

Fikrga qo‘shilish yoki qo‘shilmaslik.

Agree: I completely agree with you.

Tarjimasi: Men sizga to‘liq qo‘shilaman.

Disagree: I don't think so.

Tarjimasi: Men bunday deb o‘ylamayman.

5. Expressing Opinions

Shaxsiy fikr bildirish.

Structure: In my opinion, + sentence.

Example: In my opinion, learning languages is useful.

Tarjimasi: Mening fikrimcha, tillarni o‘rganish foydali.

6. Making Requests

Muloyimlik bilan iltimos qilish.

Structure: Could you please + verb?

Example: Could you please help me?

Tarjimasi: Iltimos, menga yordam bera olasizmi?

7. Offering Help

Yordam taklif qilish.

Structure: Shall I + verb?

Example: Shall I carry your bag?

Tarjimasi: Sumkangizni ko‘tarib beraymi?

8. Apologizing

Kechirim so‘rash.

Expression: I'm really sorry for being late.

Tarjimasi: Kech qolganim uchun juda afsusdaman.

Response: That's all right.

Tarjimasi: Hechqisi yo‘q.

9. Giving Reasons

Sababni tushuntirish.

Structure: because + reason.

Example: I'm studying English because I want to travel.

Tarjimasi: Sayohat qilishni xohlaganim uchun ingliz tilini o‘rganyapman.

10. Expressing Preferences

O‘z xohish va afzalliklarini bildirish.

Structure: I would rather + verb.

Example: I would rather stay at home today.

Tarjimasi: Bugun uyda qolishni afzal ko‘raman.

5. Reading — The Importance of Communication

Matnni avval mustaqil o‘qing, keyin audio orqali tinglang.

Good Communication in Everyday Life

Communication is an important part of our everyday life. We communicate with our family, friends, teachers and colleagues. Good communication helps people understand each other and build strong relationships.

Sometimes we need to ask for information or explain a problem. We should speak politely and listen carefully. When we disagree with someone, we should respect their opinion.

Learning English can improve our communication skills. It gives us opportunities to meet new people, travel to different countries and learn about other cultures.

We should practise speaking every day. Even a short conversation can help us become more confident. Making mistakes is normal because mistakes are part of learning.

O‘zbekcha tarjimasi

Muloqot kundalik hayotimizning muhim qismidir. Biz oilamiz, do‘stlarimiz, o‘qituvchilarimiz va hamkasblarimiz bilan muloqot qilamiz. Yaxshi muloqot odamlarning bir-birini tushunishiga va mustahkam munosabatlar o‘rnatishiga yordam beradi.

Ba'zida biz ma'lumot so‘rashimiz yoki muammoni tushuntirishimiz kerak. Biz muloyim gapirishimiz va diqqat bilan tinglashimiz kerak. Biror kishi bilan fikrimiz mos kelmasa, uning fikrini hurmat qilishimiz lozim.

Ingliz tilini o‘rganish muloqot ko‘nikmalarimizni yaxshilashi mumkin. Bu bizga yangi insonlar bilan tanishish, turli mamlakatlarga sayohat qilish va boshqa madaniyatlarni o‘rganish imkonini beradi.

Biz har kuni gapirishni mashq qilishimiz kerak. Hatto qisqa suhbat ham o‘zimizga bo‘lgan ishonchimizni oshiradi. Xato qilish tabiiy, chunki xatolar o‘rganishning bir qismidir.

Reading 1 — A New Beginning

Learning a new language can change a person's life. Many people learn English because they want to study, travel or find better opportunities. However, learning a language requires time and patience.

When I started learning English, I found some things difficult. I could understand simple texts, but speaking was a real challenge. I was sometimes afraid of making mistakes. Later, I understood that mistakes are a natural part of learning.

Now I try to practise English every day. I read short stories, listen to conversations and learn useful expressions. I also try to speak English whenever I have an opportunity.

My goal is to communicate confidently with people from different countries. I know that success does not happen in one day. Small steps and regular practice can bring great results.

  • Patience — sabr
  • Challenge — qiyinchilik
  • Natural — tabiiy
  • Expression — ibora

Reading 2 — The Importance of Good Relationships

Good relationships are important in every part of life. We communicate with family members, friends, neighbours and colleagues. These relationships can make our lives happier and more meaningful.

However, building strong relationships is not always easy. Sometimes people have different opinions or misunderstand each other. In these situations, it is important to remain calm and listen carefully.

For example, when a friend has a problem, we should not immediately criticize them. Instead, we can listen, ask questions and offer helpful advice. A kind word can sometimes make a big difference.

Respect, honesty and patience are important qualities in every relationship. We should also learn how to apologize when we make mistakes and forgive other people when necessary.

Good communication helps us solve problems and understand different points of view. It also teaches us to become more responsible and thoughtful people.

Vocabulary to remember

  • Neighbour — qo‘shni
  • Meaningful — mazmunli
  • Misunderstand — noto‘g‘ri tushunmoq

  • Criticize — tanqid qilmoq
  • Honesty — halollik
  • Forgive — kechirmoq
