1. GRAMMAR — Gerunds & Infinitives

Ushbu bo'limda mavzuni noldan boshlab bosqichma-bosqich o'rganamiz. Har bir formulani daftaringizga yozing.

1.1. Gerund nima?

Gerund — fe'lning -ing shakli bo'lib, gapda ko'pincha ot vazifasini bajaradi. O'zbek tiliga ko'pincha "-ish" yoki "-sh" shaklida tarjima qilinadi.

Verb + ING

Formula qismlari:

  • Verb — fe'l, ya'ni harakatni bildiruvchi so'z.
  • ING — fe'lga qo'shiladigan qo'shimcha.
  • Gerund — hosil bo'lgan -ing shakli.
Reading is important.
O'qish muhim.
Swimming is healthy.
Suzish sog'liq uchun foydali.
Eslab qoling: har bir -ing bilan tugagan so'z gerund bo'lavermaydi. Uning gapdagi vazifasiga qarash kerak.

1.2. Gerundning yasalishi

Ko'pchilik fe'llarga shunchaki -ing qo'shiladi.

VerbGerundTarjimasi
ReadReadingO'qish
PlayPlayingO'ynash
WorkWorkingIshlash
StudyStudyingO'qish
CookCookingOvqat pishirish
WatchWatchingTomosha qilish

Imlo qoidalari

1-qoida: Odatdagi fe'llarga -ing qo'shiladi.

Walk → Walking
Yurmoq → Yurish

2-qoida: Oxirida jim e harfi bo'lsa, odatda e tushib qoladi.

Make → Making
Yasamoq → Yasash
Write → Writing
Yozmoq → Yozish

3-qoida: Ba'zi qisqa fe'llarda oxirgi undosh harf ikki marta yoziladi.

Run → Running
Yugurmoq → Yugurish
Sit → Sitting
O'tirmoq → O'tirish

4-qoida: Undosh + y bilan tugagan fe'llarda y saqlanadi.

Study → Studying
O'qimoq → O'qish

1.3. Gerund gapda ega vazifasida

Gerund gap boshida kelib, gapning egasi bo'lishi mumkin. Bunda harakat yoki faoliyat haqida gapiriladi.

Gerund + Verb + Complement

Formula qismlari:

  • Gerund — -ing shaklidagi harakat.
  • Verb — gapning asosiy fe'li.
  • Complement — gapni to'ldiruvchi qism.
Learning English takes time.
Ingliz tilini o'rganish vaqt talab qiladi.
Cooking is my hobby.
Ovqat pishirish mening sevimli mashg'ulotim.

1.4. Gerund fe'ldan keyin kelishi

Ayrim fe'llardan keyin keyingi fe'l -ing shaklida ishlatiladi. Bu fe'llarni yodlab olish kerak.

Subject + Verb + Gerund
Fe'lMa'nosi
EnjoyYoqtirmoq, zavqlanmoq
AvoidQochmoq, chetlab o'tmoq

Enjoy + Gerund

I enjoy reading books.
Men kitob o'qishni yoqtiraman.
She enjoys cooking dinner.
U kechki ovqat tayyorlashni yoqtiradi.

Avoid + Gerund

He avoids eating fast food.
U tez tayyorlanadigan ovqatlarni yeyishdan qochadi.
We avoid making mistakes.
Biz xato qilishdan qochamiz.

Finish + Gerund

I finished doing my homework.
Men uy vazifamni bajarishni tugatdim.
They finished cleaning the room.
Ular xonani tozalashni tugatishdi.

Suggest + Gerund

She suggested going by bus.
U avtobusda borishni taklif qildi.
I suggest studying together.
Men birga o'qishni taklif qilaman.

Mind + Gerund

Do you mind opening the window?
Derazani ochishga qarshi emasmisiz?
I don't mind helping you.
Sizga yordam berishga qarshi emasman.

Keep + Gerund

Keep practicing English.
Ingliz tilini mashq qilishda davom eting.
He keeps asking questions.
U savollar berishda davom etadi.

1.5. Gerund predloglardan keyin

Ingliz tilida predlogdan keyin fe'l ishlatilsa, odatda uning -ing shakli keladi.

Preposition + Gerund

Preposition — in, on, at, about, without, before, after, for kabi so'zlar.

She is good at drawing.
U rasm chizishga yaxshi.
Thank you for helping me.
Menga yordam berganingiz uchun rahmat.
He left without saying goodbye.
U xayrlashmasdan ketdi.
Before going to bed, I read a book.
Uxlashga yotishdan oldin kitob o'qiyman.
After finishing work, I went home.
Ishni tugatgandan keyin uyga ketdim.
Muhim: "look forward to" iborasidagi to predlog hisoblanadi. Shuning uchun undan keyin -ing ishlatiladi.
I look forward to meeting you.
Siz bilan uchrashishni intiqlik bilan kutyapman.

1.6. Infinitive nima?

Infinitive — fe'lning boshlang'ich shakli oldidan to qo'shilishi orqali hosil bo'ladigan shakl.

TO + V1

Formula qismlari:

  • TO — infinitive belgisi.
  • V1 — fe'lning birinchi shakli.
  • To learn — o'rganmoq.
  • To work — ishlamoq.
I want to learn English.
Men ingliz tilini o'rganishni xohlayman.
She decided to study abroad.
U chet elda o'qishga qaror qildi.

Ayrim fe'llardan keyin ikkinchi fe'l to + V1 shaklida keladi.

Want + Infinitive

I want to speak English.
Men ingliz tilida gapirishni xohlayman.
They want to travel abroad.
Ular chet elga sayohat qilishni xohlashadi.

Decide + Infinitive

He decided to change his job.
U ishini o'zgartirishga qaror qildi.
We decided to stay at home.
Biz uyda qolishga qaror qildik.

Hope + Infinitive

I hope to see you soon.
Sizni tez orada ko'rishga umid qilaman.
She hopes to pass the exam.
U imtihondan o'tishga umid qilmoqda.

Plan + Infinitive

I plan to study tonight.
Men bugun kechqurun o'qishni rejalashtiryapman.
They plan to buy a new house.
Ular yangi uy sotib olishni rejalashtirishmoqda.

Promise + Infinitive

I promise to help you.
Men sizga yordam berishga va'da beraman.
He promised to call me.
U menga qo'ng'iroq qilishga va'da berdi.

Agree + Infinitive

She agreed to come early.
U erta kelishga rozi bo'ldi.
We agreed to work together.
Biz birga ishlashga rozi bo'ldik.

Manage + Infinitive

I managed to finish the work.
Men ishni tugatishga muvaffaq bo'ldim.
He managed to find his keys.
U kalitlarini topishga muvaffaq bo'ldi.

1.8. Infinitive maqsadni bildirish uchun

Biror ish nima maqsadda bajarilayotganini tushuntirish uchun to + V1 ishlatiladi.

Subject + Verb + TO + V1
I went to the shop to buy some bread.
Men non sotib olish uchun do'konga bordim.
She studies hard to pass the exam.
U imtihondan o'tish uchun qattiq o'qiydi.
He works to support his family.
U oilasini ta'minlash uchun ishlaydi.

1.9. Bare Infinitive — To'siz infinitive

Ba'zi fe'llardan keyin to ishlatilmaydi. Bunda fe'lning birinchi shakli keladi.

Modal Verb + V1

Can, could, should, must, may, might, will, would kabi modal fe'llardan keyin to qo'yilmaydi.

I can swim.
Men suza olaman.
You should study more.
Siz ko'proq o'qishingiz kerak.
She must leave now.
U hozir ketishi kerak.

Make va let fe'llaridan keyin ham obyekt bilan bare infinitive ishlatiladi.

My parents let me go out.
Ota-onam tashqariga chiqishimga ruxsat berishadi.
The teacher made us repeat the sentence.
O'qituvchi bizga gapni takrorlatdi.

1.10. Gerund va Infinitive ikkalasi ham ishlatiladigan fe'llar

Ayrim fe'llardan keyin ham -ing, ham to + V1 ishlatilishi mumkin. Ba'zilarida ma'no deyarli bir xil bo'ladi.

VerbGerundInfinitive
LikeLike readingLike to read
LoveLove swimmingLove to swim
HateHate waitingHate to wait
PreferPrefer walkingPrefer to walk
StartStart workingStart to work
BeginBegin rainingBegin to rain
ContinueContinue studyingContinue to study
I like reading books.
Men kitob o'qishni yoqtiraman.
I like to read before sleeping.
Men uxlashdan oldin kitob o'qishni yoqtiraman.
Like, love, hate va prefer fe'llarida ikkala shakl ko'pincha o'xshash ma'noda ishlatiladi. Lekin ayrim vaziyatlarda uslubiy farq bo'lishi mumkin.

1.11. Ma'nosi o'zgaradigan fe'llar

Bu qism juda muhim. Ba'zi fe'llardan keyin -ing yoki to + V1 ishlatilishi gapning ma'nosini o'zgartiradi.

STOP + GERUND

Biror ishni butunlay to'xtatish.

He stopped smoking.
U chekishni tashladi.
She stopped talking.
U gapirishni to'xtatdi.

STOP + TO + VERB

Boshqa ishni bajarish uchun to'xtash.

He stopped to smoke.
U chekish uchun to'xtadi.
I stopped to answer the phone.
Men telefonga javob berish uchun to'xtadim.

REMEMBER + GERUND

O'tmishda sodir bo'lgan ishni eslash.

I remember meeting him.
Men u bilan uchrashganimni eslayman.
She remembers visiting London.
U Londonga borganini eslaydi.

REMEMBER + TO + VERB

Biror ishni bajarishni unutmaslik.

Remember to call your mother.
Onangizga qo'ng'iroq qilishni unutmang.
Remember to lock the door.
Eshikni qulflashni unutmang.

FORGET + GERUND

O'tmishda sodir bo'lgan ishni eslay olmaslik.

I will never forget meeting you.
Siz bilan uchrashganimni hech qachon unutmayman.
He forgot telling me the story.
U menga hikoyani aytganini unutdi.

FORGET + TO + VERB

Biror ishni bajarishni unutish.

I forgot to buy milk.
Men sut sotib olishni unutdim.
She forgot to send the email.
U elektron xat yuborishni unutdi.

TRY + GERUND

Muammoni hal qilish uchun biror usulni sinab ko'rish.

Try drinking some water.
Biroz suv ichib ko'ring.
Try using a dictionary.
Lug'atdan foydalanib ko'ring.

TRY + TO + VERB

Biror ishni bajarishga harakat qilish.

I tried to open the door.
Men eshikni ochishga harakat qildim.
She tried to understand the lesson.
U darsni tushunishga harakat qildi.

1.12. Need + Gerund / Infinitive

Need odatda to + V1 bilan ishlatiladi. Need + gerund esa ayrim vaziyatlarda passiv ma'noni bildiradi.

I need to clean my room.
Men xonamni tozalashim kerak.
The room needs cleaning.
Xonani tozalash kerak.

1.13. Gerund va Infinitive taqqoslash

GerundInfinitive
I enjoy reading.I want to read.
She avoids driving.She plans to drive.
He finished working.He decided to work.
We suggested walking.We hope to walk.
They keep learning.They need to learn.
Esda saqlang: har bir fe'lning qaysi shaklni talab qilishini o'rganish kerak. Faqat tarjimaga qarab -ing yoki to tanlab bo'lmaydi.

1.14. Eng ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

Noto'g'riTo'g'ri
I enjoy to read.I enjoy reading.
I want learning.I want to learn.
She decided going.She decided to go.
He avoids to drive.He avoids driving.
I look forward to meet you.I look forward to meeting you.
She can to swim.She can swim.

4. LISTENING PRACTICE

Har bir matnni avval tinglang. Keyin qayta tinglab, mazmunini tushunishga harakat qiling.

Listening 1 — Learning English

Daraja: Pre-Intermediate

My name is Daniel. I enjoy learning English because I want to travel around the world. Every morning, I spend thirty minutes reading English books. I also practice speaking with my friends. Sometimes I find grammar difficult, but I never stop trying. My goal is to speak English confidently.

Listening 2 — My Future Plans

Daraja: Pre-Intermediate

Anna has many plans for the future. She wants to become a teacher. She enjoys helping children and teaching new things. She decided to study hard because she hopes to enter university. She knows that learning takes time, so she keeps practicing every day. She also plans to improve her English.

Listening Questions

1. Why does Daniel enjoy learning English?

2. How long does Daniel read English books every morning?

3. What does Anna want to become?

4. Why does Anna study hard?

5. READING — The Importance of Learning

Matnni avval mustaqil o'qing. Yangi grammatik shakllarni topishga harakat qiling.

Learning Every Day

Learning a new language is an interesting journey. Many people enjoy studying English because it helps them communicate with people from different countries.

Some students prefer reading books, while others like watching videos. Both methods can help people improve their language skills. However, it is important to practice every day.

When I started learning English, I found speaking difficult. I was afraid of making mistakes. Then I decided to practice with my friends. I started listening to English conversations and reading short stories.

I hope to become a confident English speaker. I know that improving my language skills takes time. I will continue studying because I want to achieve my goals.

Learning English is not always easy, but it is worth trying. The most important thing is to keep learning and never give up.

O'rganish — har kungi jarayon

Yangi til o'rganish qiziqarli sayohatdir. Ko'p odamlar ingliz tilini o'rganishni yoqtirishadi, chunki bu ularga turli mamlakatlardagi odamlar bilan muloqot qilishga yordam beradi.

Ba'zi o'quvchilar kitob o'qishni afzal ko'rishadi, boshqalari esa video tomosha qilishni yoqtirishadi. Har ikkala usul ham til ko'nikmalarini yaxshilashga yordam beradi. Biroq har kuni mashq qilish muhim.

Men ingliz tilini o'rganishni boshlaganimda, gapirishni qiyin deb topdim. Xato qilishdan qo'rqardim. Keyin do'stlarim bilan mashq qilishga qaror qildim. Inglizcha suhbatlarni tinglashni va qisqa hikoyalar o'qishni boshladim.

Men ingliz tilida ishonch bilan gapiradigan bo'lishga umid qilaman. Til ko'nikmalarimni yaxshilash vaqt talab qilishini bilaman. Maqsadlarimga erishishni xohlaganim uchun o'qishni davom ettiraman.

Ingliz tilini o'rganish har doim ham oson emas, lekin bunga harakat qilish arziydi. Eng muhimi — o'rganishda davom etish va hech qachon taslim bo'lmaslik.

Reading Comprehension

1. Why do many people enjoy studying English?

2. What do some students prefer?

3. What was difficult for the writer?

4. What did the writer decide to do?

5. What is the most important thing?
