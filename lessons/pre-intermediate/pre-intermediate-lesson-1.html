1 GRAMMAR — GRAMMATIKA

Bugungi darsda o‘zimizni tanishtirish, boshqa odamlar haqida gapirish, narsalarning kimga tegishli ekanini bildirishni o‘rganamiz.

1. TO BE — AM / IS / ARE

To be fe’li o‘zbek tiliga ko‘pincha “bo‘lmoq” deb tarjima qilinadi. Hozirgi zamonda ega bilan mos shakl ishlatiladi.

SubjectTo beMisol
IamI am a student.
He / She / ItisShe is my sister.
You / We / TheyareThey are friends.
I am happy. — Men xursandman.
He is at home. — U uyda.
We are ready. — Biz tayyormiz.

2. NEGATIVE — INKOR SHAKLI

Inkor gap hosil qilish uchun to be fe’lidan keyin not qo‘shiladi.

are notaren'tThey aren't here.
She isn't my teacher. — U mening o‘qituvchim emas.
We aren't late. — Biz kechikmadik.

3. QUESTIONS — SO‘ROQ SHAKLI

To be fe’li gap boshiga chiqadi. Keyin ega keladi.

Are you a student? — Siz talabamisiz?
Is she your mother? — U sizning onangizmi?
Am I right? — Men haqmanmi?

Short answers:

  • Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
  • Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
  • Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

8. SUBJECT VA OBJECT FARQI

Subject — ish-harakatni bajaruvchi. Object — ish-harakat qaratilgan shaxs yoki narsa.

She helps him.
She — Subject, him — Object.
They know us.
They — Subject, us — Object.
He likes her.
He — Subject, her — Object.

9. BIR NECHTA QOIDANI BIRLASHTIRAMIZ

My brother is a student. His school is near our house.
Akam talaba. Uning maktabi bizning uyimiz yaqinida.
We are friends. They know us.
Biz do‘stmiz. Ular bizni taniydi.
This is her notebook, but that one is mine.
Bu uning daftari, ammo anavi meniki.
Our parents are kind. We love them.
Ota-onamiz mehribon. Biz ularni yaxshi ko‘ramiz.
Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar:
  • ❌ She are happy. → ✅ She is happy.
  • ❌ I is a student. → ✅ I am a student.
  • ❌ This is mine book. → ✅ This is my book.
  • ❌ He helps I. → ✅ He helps me.
  • ❌ This book is my. → ✅ This book is mine.

2 NOTEBOOK — DAFTAR ISHI

QAT’IY TALAB: Ushbu bo‘limdagi topshiriqlarni daftarga yozish shart. Faqat o‘qib chiqish yoki telefonda bajarish yetarli emas.
  1. To be fe’lining am, is, are shakllarini jadval qilib yozing.
  2. To be ning darak, inkor va so‘roq shakllaridan 5 tadan misol yozing.
  3. 7 ta Personal Pronouns va ularning tarjimasini yozing.
  4. 7 ta Object Pronouns va ularning tarjimasini yozing.
  5. Possessive Adjectives va Possessive Pronouns jadvalini yozing.
  6. Subject va Object farqini o‘z misollaringiz bilan tushuntiring.
  7. Bugungi grammatikadan foydalanib 15 ta mustaqil inglizcha gap tuzing.
Daftarga yozib bo‘lgach, quyidagi belgilash katagiga bosing.

3 LISTENING — TINGLAB TUSHUNISH

Har bir mashqdagi matnni avval tinglang. Keyin matnni ochib tekshiring. Audio tugmasi qurilmangizdagi inglizcha ovozdan foydalanadi.

🎧 Listening 1 — Introducing Myself

O‘zini tanishtirish

Hello! My name is Daniel. I am twenty-four years old. I am from Canada, but I live in a small city now. I am an English student. My English teacher is very friendly. She helps me with grammar and speaking. My favourite subject is English because it is useful and interesting. My best friend is Mark. He is also a student. We are in the same class. Our classroom is small, but it is comfortable. I am happy because my English is getting better every day.

Tarjima: Salom! Mening ismim Daniel. Men yigirma to‘rt yoshdaman. Men Kanadadanman, lekin hozir kichik shaharda yashayman. Men ingliz tili o‘quvchisiman. Ingliz tili o‘qituvchim juda do‘stona. U menga grammatika va gapirishda yordam beradi. Mening sevimli fanim ingliz tili, chunki u foydali va qiziqarli. Eng yaqin do‘stim Mark. U ham o‘quvchi. Biz bir sinfda o‘qiymiz. Sinfimiz kichik, ammo qulay. Men xursandman, chunki ingliz tilim har kuni yaxshilanmoqda.

Listening 1 — Savollar

  1. What is the boy's name?
  2. How old is he?
  3. Where is he from?
  4. Who helps him with English?
  5. Why does he like English?

🎧 Listening 2 — My Family

Oila haqida suhbat

My name is Anna, and this is my family. We are a family of five. My father's name is Robert. He is a doctor. My mother's name is Susan. She is a teacher. I have one brother and one sister. My brother, Tom, is sixteen years old. He is funny and helpful. My sister, Lily, is ten. She is very creative. Our home is not very big, but it is warm and comfortable. We love each other. At weekends, we are usually together. My parents are very important to me, and I am proud of my family.

Tarjima: Mening ismim Anna va bu mening oilam. Biz besh kishilik oilamiz. Otamning ismi Robert. U shifokor. Onamning ismi Susan. U o‘qituvchi. Mening bitta akam va bitta singlim bor. Akam Tom o‘n olti yoshda. U quvnoq va yordam berishni yaxshi ko‘radi. Singlim Lily o‘n yoshda. U juda ijodkor. Uyimiz unchalik katta emas, ammo issiq va qulay. Biz bir-birimizni yaxshi ko‘ramiz. Dam olish kunlari odatda birga bo‘lamiz. Ota-onam men uchun juda muhim va men oilam bilan faxrlanaman.

Listening 2 — Savollar

  1. How many people are in Anna's family?
  2. What is her father's job?
  3. What is her mother's job?
  4. How old are Tom and Lily?
  5. What does Anna say about her family?

5 READING — O‘QISH

My Family, My Life and My Goals

Mening oilam, hayotim va maqsadlarim

My name is Alex, and I am twenty-three years old. I am from a small town, but now I am a student at a college in a big city. My life is quite busy, but I am happy with it. I live with my parents and my younger sister. Our home is comfortable, and our family is very friendly.

My father is a careful and responsible person. He is an engineer. His job is sometimes difficult, but he is always patient. My mother is a teacher. She is kind and helpful. Her students love her because she is friendly and honest. My sister Emma is fifteen. She is independent and confident. She is interested in music and art.

My family is important to me because they always support me. When I have a problem, I usually talk to my parents. Their advice is useful, and I believe them. My relationship with my sister is also good. We are different in some ways, but we have similar interests. We both enjoy films and travelling.

I am especially interested in learning English. It is not always easy, but I want to improve my language skills. My English teacher is very patient. She helps us become more confident. Our lessons are interesting, and we usually work together. My classmates are friendly, and they help me when I have questions.

At the moment, I am working hard because I have several goals. First, I want to speak English confidently. Second, I want to study at a good university. Finally, I want to have a successful career. I know that success takes time, patience and hard work. I believe in myself, and I am ready to learn something new every day.

O‘qish tartibi: Matnni birinchi marta lug‘atga qaramasdan o‘qing. Ikkinchi marta yangi so‘zlarga e’tibor bering. Uchinchi marta ovoz chiqarib o‘qing.
