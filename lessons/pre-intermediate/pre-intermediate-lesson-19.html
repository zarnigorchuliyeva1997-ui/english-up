03

Grammar 1 — Direct Speech

Ko‘chirma gap nima?

Direct Speech — bu odamning aynan o‘z so‘zlarini o‘zgartirmasdan keltirishdir. Odatda qo‘shtirnoq ichida yoziladi.

Subject + say/says + "exact words"
Tom said, "I am tired."
Tom: “Men charchadim”, dedi.
Anna said, "I like English."
Anna: “Men ingliz tilini yoqtiraman”, dedi.
David said, "I live in London."
David: “Men Londonda yashayman”, dedi.

Direct Speech belgilarini eslab qol

  • Odamning aynan aytgan so‘zlari ishlatiladi.
  • Ko‘pincha qo‘shtirnoq ishlatiladi.
  • Gapning zamoni o‘zgartirilmaydi.
Daftarga yoz: Direct Speech ta'rifini va yuqoridagi uchta misolni ko‘chir.
04

Grammar 2 — Reported Speech

O‘zlashtirma gap nima?

Reported Speech — boshqa odam aytgan gapni mazmunini saqlagan holda o‘z so‘zimiz bilan yetkazishdir. Bunda qo‘shtirnoq olib tashlanadi va ko‘pincha zamon bir pog‘ona orqaga o‘zgaradi.

Subject + said (that) + reported clause
Direct: Tom said, "I am tired."
Tom: “Men charchadim”, dedi.
Reported: Tom said that he was tired.
Tom charchaganini aytdi.
Direct: Anna said, "I like English."
Anna: “Men ingliz tilini yoqtiraman”, dedi.
Reported: Anna said that she liked English.
Anna ingliz tilini yoqtirishini aytdi.
That so‘zi ko‘pincha ishlatiladi, lekin darak gaplarda uni tushirib qoldirish ham mumkin.
Tom said that he was tired.
Tom said he was tired.
Ikkala gap ham grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri.

Direct va Reported Speech farqi

Direct Speech Reported Speech
"I am happy." He said he was happy.
"I work here." She said she worked there.
"I have a car." He said he had a car.
"I will come." She said she would come.
Daftarga yoz: Direct Speech va Reported Speech ta'riflarini, formulani va jadvaldagi to‘rtta misolni yozib ol.
05

Grammar 3 — Tense Changes

Zamonlarning o‘zgarishi

Agar reporting verb o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lsa (said, told, asked), ko‘pincha ichki gapning zamoni bir pog‘ona orqaga suriladi. Bu hodisa backshift deb ataladi.

1. Present Simple → Past Simple

am / is / are → was / were
work / works → worked
Direct: "I work every day."
“Men har kuni ishlayman.”
Reported: He said that he worked every day.
U har kuni ishlashini aytdi.
Direct: "She is busy."
“U band.”
Reported: He said that she was busy.
U uning band ekanini aytdi.

2. Present Continuous → Past Continuous

am / is / are + verb-ing
↓
was / were + verb-ing
Direct: "I am studying English."
“Men ingliz tilini o‘rganyapman.”
Reported: She said that she was studying English.
U ingliz tilini o‘rganayotganini aytdi.
Direct: "They are waiting for us."
“Ular bizni kutishyapti.”
Reported: He said that they were waiting for us.
U ularning bizni kutayotganini aytdi.

3. Present Perfect → Past Perfect

have / has + V3
↓
had + V3
Direct: "I have finished my work."
“Men ishimni tugatdim.”
Reported: He said that he had finished his work.
U ishini tugatganini aytdi.
Direct: "She has lost her keys."
“U kalitlarini yo‘qotib qo‘ydi.”
Reported: He said that she had lost her keys.
U uning kalitlarini yo‘qotib qo‘yganini aytdi.

4. Past Simple → Past Perfect

V2 → had + V3
Direct: "I bought a new phone."
“Men yangi telefon sotib oldim.”
Reported: He said that he had bought a new phone.
U yangi telefon sotib olganini aytdi.
Direct: "We visited our uncle."
“Biz amakimiznikiga bordik.”
Reported: They said that they had visited their uncle.
Ular amakilarinikiga borganlarini aytishdi.

5. will → would

will + V1 → would + V1
Direct: "I will call you."
“Men senga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.”
Reported: He said that he would call me.
U menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishini aytdi.
Direct: "We will help you."
“Biz senga yordam beramiz.”
Reported: They said that they would help me.
Ular menga yordam berishlarini aytishdi.

6. Modal verbs o‘zgarishi

Direct Speech Reported Speech
cancould
maymight
willwould
shallshould / would
musthad to (majburiyat ma'nosida)
couldcould (odatda o‘zgarmaydi)
shouldshould (odatda o‘zgarmaydi)
wouldwould (odatda o‘zgarmaydi)
Direct: "I can swim."
“Men suza olaman.”
Reported: He said that he could swim.
U suza olishini aytdi.
Direct: "You must study."
“Sen o‘qishing shart.”
Reported: He said that I had to study.
U mening o‘qishim shartligini aytdi.
Yodda tut! Backshift har doim ham shart emas. Agar gap hanuz haqiqat bo‘lsa yoki reporting verb hozirgi zamonda bo‘lsa, zamon o‘zgarmasligi mumkin.
Direct: "The Earth goes around the Sun."
“Yer Quyosh atrofida aylanadi.”
Reported: The teacher said that the Earth goes around the Sun.
O‘qituvchi Yer Quyosh atrofida aylanishini aytdi.
Daftarga yoz: Barcha zamon o‘zgarishlari jadvalini va har bir zamondan kamida bittadan misolni ko‘chir.

Grammar 4 — Pronoun Changes

Olmoshlarning o‘zgarishi

Reported Speech ishlatilganda olmoshlar gapning ma'nosiga qarab o‘zgaradi. Kim gapirganini va kim haqida gap ketayotganini aniqlash juda muhim.

Direct Reported (misolga qarab)
Ihe / she
wethey
myhis / her
ourtheir
mehim / her
usthem
youI / we / he / she / they
Direct: Mary said, "I love my family."
Mary: “Men oilamni yaxshi ko‘raman”, dedi.
Reported: Mary said that she loved her family.
Mary oilasini yaxshi ko‘rishini aytdi.
Direct: John said, "We are ready."
John: “Biz tayyormiz”, dedi.
Reported: John said that they were ready.
John ularning tayyor ekanini aytdi.
Direct: Peter said to me, "You are helpful."
Peter menga: “Sen yordam beruvchisan”, dedi.
Reported: Peter told me that I was helpful.
Peter menga yordam beruvchi ekanimni aytdi.
Olmoshlarni mexanik tarzda almashtirma. Har doim gapiruvchi va tinglovchini aniqlab ol.
Daftarga yoz: Jadvalni va uchta Direct / Reported misol juftligini yoz. Keyin “I”, “my”, “we” olmoshlari qatnashgan o‘zingning uchta gapingni tuz.

Grammar 5 — Time and Place Changes

Vaqt va joy ifodalarining o‘zgarishi

Gap boshqa vaqtda yoki boshqa joyda yetkazilayotgan bo‘lsa, ayrim vaqt va joy so‘zlari ham o‘zgarishi mumkin.

Direct Speech Reported Speech
nowthen
todaythat day
yesterdaythe day before
tomorrowthe next day / the following day
last weekthe week before
next weekthe following week
agobefore
herethere
thisthat
thesethose
Direct: "I am busy now."
“Men hozir bandman.”
Reported: She said that she was busy then.
U o‘sha paytda band ekanini aytdi.
Direct: "I will see you tomorrow."
“Men seni ertaga ko‘raman.”
Reported: He said that he would see me the next day.
U meni keyingi kuni ko‘rishini aytdi.
Direct: "We came here yesterday."
“Biz kecha bu yerga keldik.”
Reported: They said that they had gone there the day before.
Ular oldingi kuni u yerga borganlarini aytishdi.
Bu o‘zgarishlar vaziyatga bog‘liq. Agar gap ayni joyda va ayni vaqtda yetkazilsa, “here”, “today” yoki “tomorrow” kabi so‘zlar o‘zgarmasligi ham mumkin.
Daftarga yoz: Vaqt va joy jadvalini to‘liq ko‘chir. “today”, “tomorrow”, “here” so‘zlari qatnashgan uchta yangi misol tuz.
08

Grammar 6 — Reported Statements

Darak gaplarni yetkazish

Darak gaplarni Reported Speech shakliga aylantirishda odatda said yoki told ishlatiladi.

Subject + said + (that) + subject + changed verb

Said bilan ishlatish

She said, "I am hungry."
U: “Men ochman”, dedi.
She said that she was hungry.
U ochligini aytdi.

Told bilan ishlatish

Subject + told + person + (that) + clause
He told me, "I need help."
U menga: “Menga yordam kerak”, dedi.
He told me that he needed help.
U menga yordam kerakligini aytdi.
Muhim farq:
He said that he was tired. — To‘g‘ri.
He told that he was tired. — Noto‘g‘ri.
He told me that he was tired. — To‘g‘ri.

Yana ko‘proq misollar

Direct: "I speak three languages."
“Men uchta tilda gaplashaman.”
Reported: She said that she spoke three languages.
U uchta tilda gaplashishini aytdi.
Direct: "My brother is learning German."
“Akam nemis tilini o‘rganyapti.”
Reported: He said that his brother was learning German.
U akasi nemis tilini o‘rganayotganini aytdi.
Daftarga yoz: Said va told formulalarini, to‘g‘ri va noto‘g‘ri misollarni yoz. So‘ng o‘zing uchta darak gapni Reported Speech shakliga aylantir.

Grammar 7 — Reported Questions

So‘roq gaplarni yetkazish

Birovning bergan savolini boshqa odamga yetkazishda Reported Questions ishlatiladi. Bu yerda savol tartibi oddiy darak gap tartibiga o‘zgaradi.

asked + if / whether + subject + verb

1. Yes / No questions

Javobi “yes” yoki “no” bo‘ladigan savollarda if yoki whether ishlatiladi.

Direct: "Are you tired?"
“Sen charchadingmi?”
Reported: He asked me if I was tired.
U mendan charchaganimni so‘radi.
Direct: "Do you speak English?"
“Sen ingliz tilida gaplashasanmi?”
Reported: She asked me whether I spoke English.
U mendan ingliz tilida gaplashishimni so‘radi.

2. Wh- questions

What, where, when, why, who, how kabi savol so‘zlari saqlanadi. Lekin gap ichida savol tartibi ishlatilmaydi.

asked + question word + subject + verb
Direct: "Where do you live?"
“Sen qayerda yashaysan?”
Reported: He asked me where I lived.
U mendan qayerda yashashimni so‘radi.
Direct: "What are you doing?"
“Sen nima qilyapsan?”
Reported: She asked me what I was doing.
U mendan nima qilayotganimni so‘radi.
Direct: "Why did you leave early?"
“Nega erta ketding?”
Reported: He asked me why I had left early.
U mendan nega erta ketganimni so‘radi.
Eng muhim xato:
He asked me where did I live. — Noto‘g‘ri.
He asked me where I lived. — To‘g‘ri.
Reported Question ichida do/does/did yordamchi fe'llari odatda ishlatilmaydi.
Daftarga yoz: Yes/No Questions va Wh-Questions formulalarini, barcha misollarni yoz. Ayniqsa savol tartibi bilan darak gap tartibi o‘rtasidagi farqni belgilab qo‘y.
10

Grammar 8 — Commands and Requests

Buyruq va iltimoslarni yetkazish

Buyruq yoki iltimosni boshqalarga yetkazishda tell / ask + object + to + verb tuzilmasidan foydalanamiz.

told / asked + person + to + V1
Direct: "Open the door."
“Eshikni och.”
Reported: He told me to open the door.
U menga eshikni ochishni buyurdi.
Direct: "Please help me."
“Iltimos, menga yordam ber.”
Reported: She asked me to help her.
U mendan unga yordam berishimni so‘radi.

Negative commands

told / asked + person + not to + V1
Direct: "Don't touch that."
“Anavi narsaga tegma.”
Reported: He told me not to touch that.
U menga anavi narsaga tegmaslikni aytdi.
Direct: "Don't be late."
“Kech qolma.”
Reported: My teacher told me not to be late.
O‘qituvchim menga kech qolmaslikni aytdi.
Buyruq uchun told, muloyim iltimos uchun asked ko‘p ishlatiladi. Vaziyatga qarab ikkalasi ham qo‘llanishi mumkin.
Daftarga yoz: Ijobiy va inkor buyruqlar formulalarini hamda to‘rtta misolni yoz.
11

Grammar 9 — Say vs Tell

Ikki fe'lning farqi

Say va tell ikkalasi ham “aytmoq” ma'nosini beradi. Lekin ularning gapdagi ishlatilishi farq qiladi.

SAY

Odatda kimga aytilgani ko‘rsatilmaydi.

She said that she was happy.

TELL

Odatda kimga aytilgani ko‘rsatiladi.

She told me that she was happy.
Correct Incorrect
He said hello.He told hello.
He told me the truth.He said me the truth.
She said that she was ill.She told that she was ill.
She told us a story.She said us a story.
My father told me to be careful.
Otam menga ehtiyot bo‘lishni aytdi.
My father said that I should be careful.
Otam ehtiyot bo‘lishim kerakligini aytdi.
Daftarga yoz: Say va Tell farqini, jadvaldagi to‘g‘ri va noto‘g‘ri shakllarni yoz. O‘zing ham har bir fe'l bilan ikkitadan gap tuz.

📖 10. Reading — A Conversation About a Surprise

📓 Daftaringizga matnni yozing. Har bir gapni o‘qing, tarjima qiling va Reported Speech shakllarini toping.

Read the text

Last Friday, Anna met her friend Daniel at a café.

Anna said, "I have some exciting news."

Daniel asked, "What has happened?"

Anna replied, "My brother has got a new job."

Daniel said, "That is wonderful!"

Anna explained, "He will move to London next month."

Daniel asked, "Will he live alone?"

Anna answered, "No, he will share a flat with his friend."

Daniel said, "I hope he enjoys his new life."

Sentence-by-sentence pronunciation

1. Last Friday, Anna met her friend Daniel at a café.

2. Anna said, "I have some exciting news."

3. Daniel asked, "What has happened?"

4. Anna replied, "My brother has got a new job."

5. Daniel said, "That is wonderful!"

6. Anna explained, "He will move to London next month."

7. Daniel asked, "Will he live alone?"

8. Anna answered, "No, he will share a flat with his friend."

9. Daniel said, "I hope he enjoys his new life."

Reading comprehension

1. When did Anna meet Daniel?

2. What was Anna's exciting news?

3. Where will her brother move?

4. Who will he share a flat with?

🎧 11. Listening Practice

📓 Avval audioni tinglang. Keyin eshitgan ma’lumotlaringizni daftaringizga yozing. Birinchi marta matnga qaramasdan tinglashga harakat qiling.

Listening 1 — A Teacher's Announcement

Mavzu: e’lonni boshqalarga yetkazish

Listen carefully. You can choose slow or normal speed.

The teacher said that the next lesson would start at nine o'clock. She told the students that they had to bring their books. She also explained that the classroom had changed. One student asked where the new classroom was. The teacher replied that it was on the second floor. She reminded everyone that the test would take place on Friday.

Transcript:

The teacher said that the next lesson would start at nine o'clock.

She told the students that they had to bring their books.

She also explained that the classroom had changed.

One student asked where the new classroom was.

The teacher replied that it was on the second floor.

She reminded everyone that the test would take place on Friday.

Listening 1 — Questions

1. What time would the next lesson start?

2. What did the students have to bring?

3. Where was the new classroom?

4. When would the test take place?

Listening 2 — A Phone Conversation

Mavzu: telefon orqali aytilgan gaplarni yetkazish

Mark called Sarah and said that he was running late. He explained that the bus had not arrived. Sarah told him that she was already at the station. Mark asked her to wait for him. Sarah said that she would wait near the main entrance. Mark thanked her and promised that he would arrive soon.

Transcript:

Mark called Sarah and said that he was running late.

He explained that the bus had not arrived.

Sarah told him that she was already at the station.

Mark asked her to wait for him.

Sarah said that she would wait near the main entrance.

Mark thanked her and promised that he would arrive soon.

Listening 2 — Questions

1. Who called Sarah?

2. Why was Mark late?

3. Where was Sarah?

4. Where would Sarah wait?

Listening 3 — Change the speaker

Listen to the sentences and choose the correct reported version.

1. "I am learning English because I want to study abroad."

3. "Can you help me with this exercise?"
