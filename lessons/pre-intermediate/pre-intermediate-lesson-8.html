LESSON OBJECTIVE

🎯 Bugungi dars maqsadi

Bugun biz Present Perfect zamonini o‘rganamiz. Ushbu zamon ingliz tilida juda muhim hisoblanadi. U orqali o'tmishda sodir bo‘lgan, lekin hozirgi vaqt bilan aloqasi bor voqealar haqida gapiramiz.

Remember:

Present Perfect = Past action + Present connection.

Ya'ni o'tmishda sodir bo'lgan ishning hozirgi vaqt bilan aloqasi mavjud.

Bugun nimalarni o‘rganamiz?

  • Have va Has yordamchi fe'llari.
  • Fe'llarning uchinchi shakli — V3.
  • Darak, inkor va so‘roq gaplar.
  • Ever, Never, Already, Yet, Just.
  • Since va For.
  • Been va Gone farqi.
  • Present Perfect va Past Simple farqiga kirish.

1. PRESENT PERFECT FORMULASI

Present Perfect zamonida asosiy yordamchi fe'llarimiz HAVE va HAS hisoblanadi.

Subject + HAVE / HAS + V3 + Object
SubjectYordamchi fe'lMisol
IHaveI have worked.
YouHaveYou have worked.
WeHaveWe have worked.
TheyHaveThey have worked.
HeHasHe has worked.
SheHasShe has worked.
ItHasIt has worked.

Positive — Darak gap

Subject + have/has + V3

I have finished my homework.

Men uy vazifamni tugatdim.

She has visited London.

U Londonga tashrif buyurgan.

They have bought a new car.

Ular yangi mashina sotib olishgan.

We have learned a lot today.

Biz bugun ko‘p narsa o‘rgandik.

Negative — Inkor gap

Subject + have/has + not + V3

I have not finished my work.

Men ishimni tugatmadim.

He has not called me.

U menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmadi.

They haven't arrived yet.

Ular hali yetib kelishmadi.

Qisqartmalar:

  • Have not = Haven't
  • Has not = Hasn't

Question — So‘roq gap

Have/Has + Subject + V3 + Object?

Have you finished your homework?

Uy vazifangni tugatdingmi?

Has she arrived?

U yetib keldimi?

Have they seen this movie?

Ular bu filmni ko‘rishganmi?

Short Answers — Qisqa javoblar

Have you eaten? — Yes, I have.

Ovqatlandingmi? — Ha.

Has he arrived? — No, he hasn't.

U yetib keldimi? — Yo‘q.

Have they called? — Yes, they have.

Ular qo‘ng‘iroq qilishdimi? — Ha.

Wh-Questions — Maxsus so‘roq gaplar

Wh-word + have/has + subject + V3?

What have you done?

Nima qilding?

Where has she gone?

U qayerga ketdi?

How many books have you read?

Nechta kitob o‘qigansan?

Regular Verbs

Regular fe'llarda V3 odatda -ed qo‘shimchasi orqali hosil qilinadi.

Work → Worked

I have worked.

Play → Played

She has played.

Clean → Cleaned

We have cleaned.

Visit → Visited

They have visited.

Open → Opened

He has opened it.

Study → Studied

I have studied English.

Important: Har bir fe'lga -ed qo‘shib bo‘lmaydi. Irregular verbs shakllarini alohida o‘rganish kerak.

Already va Yet farqi

Already ko‘pincha darak gaplarda ishlatiladi.

Yet ko‘pincha inkor va so‘roq gaplarning oxirida keladi.

I have already done it.

Men buni allaqachon bajardim.

I haven't done it yet.

Men buni hali bajarmadim.

Ever va Never

Have you ever eaten Korean food?

Hech koreys taomini yeb ko‘rganmisan?

I have never eaten Korean food.

Men hech qachon koreys taomini yemaganman.

Just

My mother has just arrived.

Onam hozirgina yetib keldi.

4. SINCE VA FOR

Bu ikki so‘z biror holat qancha vaqtdan beri davom etayotganini ifodalash uchun ishlatiladi.

SINCE = boshlanish nuqtasi
  • Since Monday — Dushanbadan beri.
  • Since 2020 — 2020-yildan beri.
  • Since yesterday — Kechadan beri.
  • Since morning — Ertalabdan beri.
  • Since childhood — Bolalikdan beri.
FOR = vaqt davomiyligi
  • For two days — Ikki kundan beri.
  • For three years — Uch yildan beri.
  • For an hour — Bir soatdan beri.
  • For a long time — Uzoq vaqtdan beri.
  • For ten minutes — O‘n daqiqadan beri.

I have studied English for six years.

Men olti yildan beri ingliz tilini o‘rganaman.

She has lived here since 2021.

U 2021-yildan beri shu yerda yashaydi.

We have known each other for ten years.

Biz bir-birimizni o‘n yildan beri bilamiz.

Eslab qoling:

Since — qachondan boshlanganini bildiradi.

For — qancha vaqt davom etganini bildiradi.

5. BEEN VA GONE

He has gone to London.

U Londonga ketgan (hozir bu yerda emas).

He has been to London.

U Londonda bo‘lgan (tajribasi bor, qaytib kelgan).

Been to: Biror joyga borib qaytgan tajriba.

Gone to: Biror joyga ketgan va hali qaytmagan.

BEEN TO

I have been to Turkey.

Men Turkiyada bo‘lganman.

GONE TO

My father has gone to work.

Dadam ishga ketgan.

6. PRESENT PERFECT VA PAST SIMPLE

Present Perfect o'tmishda sodir bo‘lgan, hozir bilan aloqasi bor ishlar uchun ishlatiladi. Past Simple esa tugagan o'tmishdagi aniq vaqt bilan ishlatiladi.

Present PerfectPast Simple
Have/Has + V3V2
Vaqt aniq ko‘rsatilmaydiO‘tgan vaqt aniq ko‘rsatiladi
I have seen this film.I saw this film yesterday.
Men bu filmni ko‘rganman.Men bu filmni kecha ko‘rdim.

Past Simple signal words

  • Yesterday — Kecha.
  • Last week — O‘tgan hafta.
  • Last year — O‘tgan yil.
  • Two days ago — Ikki kun oldin.
  • In 2020 — 2020-yilda.

Taqqoslash

I have lost my phone.

Telefonimni yo‘qotib qo‘ydim (hozir topilmagan).

I lost my phone yesterday.

Kecha telefonimni yo‘qotdim.

She has visited Paris.

U Parijda bo‘lgan.

She visited Paris last summer.

U o‘tgan yozda Parijga tashrif buyurgan.

Important: Yesterday, last week, ago kabi tugagan vaqt ko‘rsatkichlari bilan odatda Past Simple ishlatiladi.

8. COMMON MISTAKES — KENG TARQALGAN XATOLAR

❌ Noto‘g‘ri: She have finished.

✅ To‘g‘ri: She has finished.

Sabab: She bilan has ishlatiladi.

❌ Noto‘g‘ri: I have went.

✅ To‘g‘ri: I have gone.

Sabab: Present Perfect uchun V3 kerak.

❌ Noto‘g‘ri: Did you have eaten?

✅ To‘g‘ri: Have you eaten?

Sabab: Present Perfect so‘rog‘ida have/has gap boshiga chiqadi.

❌ Noto‘g‘ri: I have seen him yesterday.

✅ To‘g‘ri: I saw him yesterday.

Sabab: Yesterday bilan Past Simple ishlatiladi.

❌ Noto‘g‘ri: He has not went.

✅ To‘g‘ri: He has not gone.

Sabab: Inkor gapda ham V3 ishlatiladi.

14. LISTENING PRACTICE

Quyidagi matnni avval o‘zingiz o‘qing. Keyin telefoningizdagi matnni ovoz chiqarib o‘qish funksiyasidan foydalanib tinglang.

Hello! My name is Daniel. I have studied English for three years. I have learned many new words, and I have read several English books. I have also watched English videos. Recently, I have started speaking with my friends in English. I haven't become fluent yet, but I have improved a lot. I have never given up because I want to speak English confidently.

Listening questions

  1. How long has Daniel studied English?
  2. What has he read?
  3. What has he started recently?
  4. Has he become fluent yet?
  5. Why hasn't he given up?

15. READING PRACTICE

PRE-INTERMEDIATE STORY

A New Life

Anna has always wanted to improve her life. She has worked hard for many years. Recently, she has decided to learn English because she wants to communicate with people from different countries.

She has already bought some English books. She has also downloaded a language-learning application. Every morning, she studies new words and reads short stories.

Anna hasn't learned everything yet, but she has made good progress. She has met some new friends who also study English. They have practiced speaking together several times.

Anna has understood one important thing: success doesn't happen in one day. She has decided to continue learning every day.

Vocabulary from the story

Improve — Yaxshilamoq
Communicate — Muloqot qilmoq
Application — Ilova
Progress — Rivojlanish
Several — Bir nechta
Decide — Qaror qilmoq

Reading comprehension

  1. Why has Anna decided to learn English?
  2. What has she already bought?
  3. When does she study new words?
  4. Who has she met?
  5. What important thing has Anna understood?

IMPORTANT EXTRA TASK

Quyidagi inglizcha matnni o‘zbek tiliga tarjima qiling. Matnni shoshilmasdan o‘qing, har bir gapning mazmunini tushunishga harakat qiling.

The Importance of Never Giving Up

Learning a new language is not always easy. Sometimes, people feel tired or disappointed because they cannot understand everything. However, successful learners never give up. They understand that every small step is important.

I have studied English for several years, and I have learned many useful things. I have made mistakes, but I have also improved my skills. I have discovered that practice is more important than simply reading grammar rules.

Recently, I have started spending more time on listening and speaking. I have watched English videos, listened to conversations and learned new expressions. I haven't reached my final goal yet, but I have made progress.

Everyone has a different learning journey. Some people learn quickly, while others need more time. The most important thing is to continue learning and believe in your progress. If you work consistently, you will achieve your goals one day.
