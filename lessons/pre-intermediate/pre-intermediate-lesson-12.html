Assalomu alaykum! English Up platformasining 12-darsiga xush kelibsiz!

Bugun biz ingliz tilida juda ko‘p ishlatiladigan modal fe'llarni o‘rganamiz.

Modal verbs kundalik suhbatlarda, ishda, o‘qishda va turli vaziyatlarda fikrimizni aniq ifodalashga yordam beradi.

Bugungi darsning maqsadlari

  • Should yordamida maslahat berish.
  • Must yordamida kuchli majburiyatni ifodalash.
  • Have to yordamida zarurat va majburiyatni aytish.
  • Might yordamida ehtimolni ifodalash.
  • Modal fe'llar bilan darak, inkor va so‘roq gaplar tuzish.
  • Yangi so‘zlarni gaplarda ishlatish.
  • Reading, Listening, Speaking va Writing ko‘nikmalarini mashq qilish.
📓 MUHIM TALAB: Har bir grammatika qoidasini, formulasini va misollarni daftaringizga yozing. Faqat o‘qib ketmang. Yozish bilimni mustahkamlashga yordam beradi.

2. What are Modal Verbs?

English rule: We use modal verbs to express ability, possibility, permission, advice, obligation and necessity.

O‘zbekcha izoh: Modal fe'llar asosiy fe'lga qo‘shimcha ma'no beradi. Masalan, maslahat, majburiyat, ruxsat yoki ehtimolni bildiradi.

You should study every day.
Siz har kuni o‘qishingiz kerak (maslahat).
She might come tomorrow.
U ertaga kelishi mumkin (ehtimol).

Modal verbs haqida muhim qoidalar

Ko‘pchilik modal fe'llardan keyin fe'lning birinchi shakli — V1 ishlatiladi.
Subject + Modal Verb + V1 + Object

Subject: gapning egasi — I, You, He, She, It, We, They.

Modal Verb: should, must, might kabi modal fe'l.

V1: fe'lning birinchi shakli — go, study, work, read.

Object: harakat ta'sir qiladigan shaxs yoki narsa.

We should learn English.
Biz ingliz tilini o‘rganishimiz kerak.
He must finish his work.
U ishini tugatishi shart.
Eslab qoling: should, must va might dan keyin odatda “to” qo‘yilmaydi. Masalan: “She should study”, “She should to study” emas.
📓 Daftarga yozing: Modal verbs ta'rifi, asosiy formulasi va yuqoridagi ikkita misolni ko‘chirib yozing.

3. SHOULD — Advice and Suggestions

English rule: We use should to give advice, suggestions and recommendations.

O‘zbekcha izoh: Should kimlargadir maslahat berish yoki biror ishni bajarish foydali ekanini aytish uchun ishlatiladi.

1. Affirmative — Darak gap

Subject + should + V1 + Object

Formula qismlari:

  • Subject — gapning egasi.
  • Should — maslahat bildiruvchi modal fe'l.
  • V1 — fe'lning birinchi shakli.
  • Object — gapning to‘ldiruvchisi.
You should drink more water.
Siz ko‘proq suv ichishingiz kerak.
He should visit his parents.
U ota-onasini ko‘rgani borishi kerak.

2. Negative — Inkor gap

English rule: We use should not or shouldn't to say that something is not a good idea.

Subject + should not + V1 + Object

Should not qisqartirilgan shaklda shouldn't bo‘ladi.

You should not eat too much sugar.
Siz juda ko‘p shakar yemasligingiz kerak.
They shouldn't be late.
Ular kech qolmasliklari kerak.

3. Question — So‘roq gap

English rule: We put should before the subject to make a question.

Should + Subject + V1 + Object + ?
Should I call the doctor?
Men shifokorga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishim kerakmi?
Should we leave now?
Biz hozir ketishimiz kerakmi?

Should bilan javob berish

Yes, Subject + should.
No, Subject + shouldn't.
Should I study tonight?
Bugun kechqurun o‘qishim kerakmi?
Yes, you should.
Ha, o‘qishingiz kerak.
📓 Daftarga yozing: Should ning darak, inkor va so‘roq formulalarini yozing. Har biriga o‘zingiz 2 tadan misol tuzing.

4. MUST — Strong Obligation

English rule: We use must to express a strong obligation, necessity or an important rule.

O‘zbekcha izoh: Must kuchli majburiyatni yoki bajarilishi juda muhim bo‘lgan ishni bildiradi. Ko‘pincha gapiruvchi biror ishni juda zarur deb hisoblaydi.

1. Affirmative — Darak gap

Subject + must + V1 + Object

Subject: ishni bajaruvchi shaxs.

Must: kuchli majburiyat.

V1: fe'lning birinchi shakli.

Object: harakatga tegishli narsa yoki shaxs.

You must wear a seat belt.
Siz xavfsizlik kamarini taqishingiz shart.
Students must respect their teachers.
O‘quvchilar o‘qituvchilarini hurmat qilishlari shart.

2. Negative — Inkor gap

English rule: We use must not or mustn't to express prohibition.

Subject + must not + V1 + Object

Muhim: Mustn't — biror ishni qilish mumkin emas, taqiqlangan degani.

You mustn't smoke here.
Bu yerda chekish mumkin emas.
Children must not play with dangerous objects.
Bolalar xavfli buyumlar bilan o‘ynamasliklari kerak.

3. Question — So‘roq gap

Must + Subject + V1 + Object + ?
Must I finish this today?
Men buni bugun tugatishim shartmi?
Must we bring our books?
Biz kitoblarimizni olib kelishimiz shartmi?
Mustn't va don't have to bir xil emas. Mustn't — taqiqlangan. Don't have to — qilish majburiy emas.
📓 Daftarga yozing: Must ning uchta formulasini va mustn't bilan don't have to o‘rtasidagi farqni yozing.

5. HAVE TO — Necessity and Obligation

English rule: We use have to to talk about rules, duties and situations when something is necessary.

O‘zbekcha izoh: Have to tashqi sabab, qoida, ish yoki vaziyat tufayli bajarilishi kerak bo‘lgan majburiyatni ifodalaydi.

1. Affirmative — Darak gap

I / You / We / They + have to + V1
He / She / It + has to + V1

Have to I, you, we, they bilan ishlatiladi.

Has to he, she, it bilan ishlatiladi.

I have to work tomorrow.
Men ertaga ishlashim kerak.
She has to study for her exam.
U imtihoniga tayyorlanishi kerak.

2. Negative — Inkor gap

English rule: We use don't have to or doesn't have to when something is not necessary.

I / You / We / They + don't have to + V1
He / She / It + doesn't have to + V1
You don't have to come early.
Siz erta kelishingiz shart emas.
He doesn't have to cook today.
U bugun ovqat pishirishi shart emas.

3. Question — So‘roq gap

Do + I / you / we / they + have to + V1?
Does + he / she / it + have to + V1?
Do you have to work on Sunday?
Yakshanba kuni ishlashingiz kerakmi?
Does she have to wear a uniform?
U forma kiyishi kerakmi?
So‘roq va inkor gaplarda does ishlatilganda has to emas, have to ishlatiladi. Masalan: Does he have to go?
📓 Daftarga yozing: Have to va has to farqini, inkor va so‘roq formulalarini yozing.

6. MIGHT — Possibility

English rule: We use might to talk about something that is possible but not certain.

O‘zbekcha izoh: Might biror hodisa sodir bo‘lishi mumkinligini, lekin bunga to‘liq ishonch yo‘qligini bildiradi.

1. Affirmative — Darak gap

Subject + might + V1 + Object
It might rain tomorrow.
Ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘ishi mumkin.
We might visit our friends.
Biz do‘stlarimiznikiga borishimiz mumkin.

2. Negative — Inkor gap

Subject + might not + V1 + Object
He might not come today.
U bugun kelmasligi mumkin.
They might not understand the question.
Ular savolni tushunmasliklari mumkin.

3. Question — So‘roq gap

Might bilan so‘roq gap tuzish mumkin, lekin kundalik suhbatda ehtimol haqida savol berish uchun boshqa shakllar ham ko‘p ishlatiladi.

Might + Subject + V1 + Object + ?
Might it rain tonight?
Bugun kechasi yomg‘ir yog‘ishi mumkinmi?
Might she need some help?
Unga yordam kerak bo‘lishi mumkinmi?
📓 Daftarga yozing: Might ning darak, inkor va so‘roq formulalarini yozing. Ehtimolni ifodalovchi 3 ta gap tuzing.

7. Comparison — Modal Verbs

Endi to‘rtta modal shaklning ma'nosini taqqoslaymiz.

SHOULD

Maslahat yoki tavsiya.

You should sleep early.
Siz erta uxlashingiz kerak (maslahat).

MUST

Kuchli majburiyat yoki qat'iy qoida.

You must follow the rules.
Siz qoidalarga amal qilishingiz shart.

HAVE TO

Vaziyat yoki tashqi talab tufayli zarurat.

I have to go to work.
Men ishga borishim kerak.

MIGHT

Ehtimol, aniq bo‘lmagan imkoniyat.

She might be busy.
U band bo‘lishi mumkin.
Esda tuting:
Should = maslahat.
Must = kuchli majburiyat.
Have to = zarurat yoki tashqi talab.
Might = ehtimol.
📓 Daftarga yozing: Har bir modal fe'lning ma'nosini va o‘zingiz tuzgan bittadan misol gapni yozing.

9. Reading Practice

A Busy Week

Daniel is a student. He has a busy week. He has to go to school every morning. He must do his homework and respect his teachers. He should read English books every day because he wants to improve his English. Sometimes he feels tired, so he might take a short break. His parents say that he should sleep early and eat healthy food. On Saturday, he doesn't have to go to school. He might visit his grandparents or play football with his friends.

Reading Questions

1. What does Daniel have to do every morning?

2. Why should Daniel read English books?

3. What might Daniel do when he feels tired?

4. Does Daniel have to go to school on Saturday?

Anna: What are you going to do this weekend?
Mark: I might visit my uncle.
Anna: Do you have to work on Saturday?

Anna: You should finish it today.

Listening Check

1. What must the student do?

2. What might Mark do at the weekend?

5. Reading Homework

A New Opportunity

Emma wants to improve her English because she hopes to study abroad one day. She knows that she should practise every day. She has to attend English classes twice a week. Her teacher says that students must complete their assignments on time. Emma sometimes feels nervous when she speaks English. However, she knows that mistakes are a normal part of learning. This weekend, she might join an English conversation club. Her friend says she should try it because it could help her become more confident. Emma doesn't have to be perfect. She only has to keep learning and practising.

Answer the questions

1. Why does Emma want to improve her English?
2. How often does she attend English classes?
3. What must students do according to the teacher?
4. What might Emma do this weekend?
5. Why should she join the conversation club?
