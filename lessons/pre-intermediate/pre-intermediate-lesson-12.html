LESSON 12 — MODAL VERBS

Should • Must • Have to • Might • May

Grammar | Vocabulary | Listening | Reading | Writing | Speaking

Bugungi darsimizda ingliz tilidagi juda muhim grammatik mavzulardan biri — Modal Verbsni o‘rganamiz.

Modal verbs — bu asosiy fe'lga qo‘shimcha ma'no beradigan yordamchi fe'llardir. Ular maslahat, majburiyat, ruxsat, taqiq, imkoniyat va ehtimollikni ifodalashda ishlatiladi.

Nima uchun bu mavzu muhim?

Kundalik hayotda biz doimo quyidagi fikrlarni bildiramiz:

You should study every day.

Sen har kuni o‘qishing kerak (maslahat).

You must follow the rules.

Sen qoidalarga rioya qilishing shart.

I might visit my friend tomorrow.

Men ertaga do‘stimnikiga borishim mumkin.

Bugungi dars maqsadlari

  • Modal verbs grammatikasini tushunish.
  • Har bir strukturani mustaqil tuza olish.
  • Ingliz tilidagi qoidalarni daftarga yozish.
  • Maslahat va majburiyatlarni ifodalash.
  • Ehtimollik haqida gapirish.
  • Real hayotdagi suhbatlarda modal verbs ishlatish.
DAFTARGA YOZING!

Modal verbs are auxiliary verbs that express ability, possibility, permission, advice, obligation and necessity.

Modal fe'llar qobiliyat, ehtimollik, ruxsat, maslahat, majburiyat va zaruratni ifodalovchi yordamchi fe'llardir.

PART 1 — GRAMMAR

Har bir strukturani alohida o‘rganamiz. Formulalarni daftarga yozing va misollarni ovoz chiqarib o‘qing.

1. SHOULD — Maslahat berish

Advice • Recommendation

1.1. Should nima?

Should biror kishiga maslahat berish, tavsiya qilish yoki to‘g‘ri deb hisoblangan ishni aytish uchun ishlatiladi.

We use SHOULD to give advice or make recommendations.

Biz SHOULD dan maslahat berish yoki tavsiya qilish uchun foydalanamiz.

1.2. Positive structure — Darak gap

Subject + SHOULD + V1 + Object

Formula izohi:

  • Subject — ega (I, You, He, She, We, They).
  • Should — maslahat bildiruvchi modal fe'l.
  • V1 — fe'lning birinchi shakli.
  • Object — gapning to‘ldiruvchisi.
1. You should drink more water.

Sen ko‘proq suv ichishing kerak.

2. She should study English every day.

U har kuni ingliz tilini o‘rganishi kerak.

1.3. Negative structure — Inkor gap

Subject + SHOULD NOT + V1 + Object

Should not qisqartirilgan shaklda shouldn't bo‘ladi.

1. You shouldn't eat too much fast food.

Sen juda ko‘p tez tayyorlanadigan ovqat yemasliging kerak.

2. He shouldn't go to bed late.

U kech uxlashga yotmasligi kerak.

1.4. Question structure — So‘roq gap

SHOULD + Subject + V1 + Object + ?

Should gap boshiga chiqadi va maslahat so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi.

1. Should I call the doctor?

Men shifokorga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishim kerakmi?

2. Should we start now?

Biz hozir boshlashimiz kerakmi?

1.5. Short answers — Qisqa javoblar

Yes, Subject + should.
No, Subject + shouldn't.
Should I learn new words?

Yes, you should.

Ha, o‘rganishing kerak.

Should he drive so fast?

No, he shouldn't.

Yo‘q, u bunday tez haydamasligi kerak.

DAFTARGA YOZING!

We use should to give advice, make suggestions and express what is a good idea.

Structure: Subject + should + V1.

Negative: Subject + shouldn't + V1.

Question: Should + Subject + V1?

MUHIM GRAMMATIK QOIDA!

Should dan keyin fe'l doimo V1 shaklida keladi. He, She, It bilan ham fe'lga -s qo‘shilmaydi.

Correct: She should work.

Incorrect: She should works.

2. MUST — Kuchli majburiyat

Strong Obligation

2.1. Must nima?

Must kuchli majburiyat, zarurat yoki so‘zlovchi uchun juda muhim bo‘lgan talabni ifodalashda ishlatiladi.

We use MUST to express strong obligation or necessity.

Biz MUST dan kuchli majburiyat yoki zaruratni ifodalash uchun foydalanamiz.

2.2. Positive structure — Darak gap

Subject + MUST + V1 + Object

Formula izohi:

  • Subject — ish-harakatni bajaruvchi ega.
  • Must — qat'iy majburiyat bildiradi.
  • V1 — fe'lning birinchi shakli.
  • Object — to‘ldiruvchi.
1. You must respect your parents.

Sen ota-onangni hurmat qilishing shart.

2. Students must complete their homework.

O‘quvchilar uy vazifalarini bajarishlari shart.

2.3. Negative structure — Qat'iy taqiq

Subject + MUST NOT + V1 + Object

Must not qisqartirilgan shaklda mustn't bo‘ladi.

DIQQAT!

Mustn't — biror ishni qilish qat'iyan taqiqlanganini bildiradi. Bu don't have to bilan bir xil emas.

1. You mustn't smoke here.

Bu yerda chekish qat'iyan taqiqlangan.

2. Children mustn't play in the road.

Bolalar yo‘lda o‘ynamasliklari kerak.

2.4. Question structure — So‘roq gap

MUST + Subject + V1 + Object + ?
1. Must I finish this work today?

Men bu ishni bugun tugatishim shartmi?

2. Must we wear uniforms?

Biz forma kiyishimiz shartmi?

2.5. Short answers — Qisqa javoblar

Yes, Subject + must.
No, Subject + needn't.
Must I attend the meeting?

Yes, you must.

Ha, qatnashishing shart.

Must we leave now?

No, you needn't.

Yo‘q, hozir ketishingiz shart emas.

DAFTARGA YOZING!

We use must to express strong obligation, necessity and rules.

Structure: Subject + must + V1.

Negative: Subject + mustn't + V1.

Question: Must + Subject + V1?

ESLAB QOLING!

Must dan keyin do, does yoki did ishlatilmaydi.

Correct: He must study.

Incorrect: He must to study.

Incorrect: He must studies.

3. HAVE TO — Majburiyat va zarurat

Obligation • Necessity

3.1. Have to nima?

Have to tashqi qoida, ish, maktab, qonun yoki vaziyat sababli bajarilishi kerak bo‘lgan majburiyatni ifodalaydi.

We use HAVE TO to talk about obligations and necessities, especially when they come from outside circumstances.

Biz HAVE TO dan tashqi sharoit yoki qoidalar sababli yuzaga keladigan majburiyat va zaruratlarni ifodalash uchun foydalanamiz.

3.2. Positive structure — Darak gap

I / You / We / They + HAVE TO + V1
He / She / It + HAS TO + V1

Formula izohi:

  • I, You, We, They — HAVE TO ishlatadi.
  • He, She, It — HAS TO ishlatadi.
  • V1 — fe'lning birinchi shakli.
1. I have to wake up early every morning.

Men har kuni ertalab erta uyg‘onishim kerak.

2. She has to wear a uniform at school.

U maktabda forma kiyishi kerak.

3.3. Negative structure — Inkor gap

I / You / We / They + DON'T HAVE TO + V1
He / She / It + DOESN'T HAVE TO + V1

Don't have to va doesn't have to majburiyat yo‘qligini bildiradi.

1. I don't have to work on Sunday.

Men yakshanba kuni ishlashim shart emas.

2. He doesn't have to cook dinner today.

U bugun kechki ovqat tayyorlashi shart emas.

3.4. Question structure — So‘roq gap

DO + I / YOU / WE / THEY + HAVE TO + V1?
DOES + HE / SHE / IT + HAVE TO + V1?
1. Do you have to study tonight?

Bugun kechqurun o‘qishing kerakmi?

2. Does she have to work tomorrow?

U ertaga ishlashi kerakmi?

3.5. Short answers — Qisqa javoblar

Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Do you have to get up early?

Yes, I do.

Ha, shunday qilishim kerak.

Does he have to wear a uniform?

No, he doesn't.

Yo‘q, unga forma kiyish shart emas.

DAFTARGA YOZING!

We use have to to express obligations and necessities.

Positive: I have to work. / She has to work.

Negative: I don't have to work. / She doesn't have to work.

Question: Do you have to work? / Does she have to work?

MUHIM!

Doesn't dan keyin has to emas, have to ishlatiladi.

Correct: She doesn't have to go.

Incorrect: She doesn't has to go.

4. MIGHT — Ehtimollik

Possibility

4.1. Might nima?

Might kelajakda yoki hozir biror hodisa sodir bo‘lishi ehtimoli borligini, lekin bunga ishonch to‘liq emasligini ifodalaydi.

We use MIGHT to talk about something that is possible but not certain.

Biz MIGHT dan sodir bo‘lishi mumkin, lekin aniq bo‘lmagan vaziyatlarni ifodalash uchun foydalanamiz.

4.2. Positive structure — Darak gap

Subject + MIGHT + V1 + Object

Formula izohi:

  • Subject — ega.
  • Might — ehtimollik bildiradi.
  • V1 — fe'lning birinchi shakli.
  • Object — to‘ldiruvchi.
1. It might rain tomorrow.

Ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘ishi mumkin.

2. I might visit my uncle this weekend.

Men shu dam olish kunlari amakimnikiga borishim mumkin.

4.3. Negative structure — Inkor gap

Subject + MIGHT NOT + V1 + Object
1. She might not come to the party.

U ziyofatga kelmasligi mumkin.

2. We might not have enough time.

Bizda yetarli vaqt bo‘lmasligi mumkin.

4.4. Question structure — So‘roq gap

MIGHT + Subject + V1 + Object + ?
1. Might he be late?

U kechikishi mumkinmi?

2. Might they need our help?

Ularga bizning yordamimiz kerak bo‘lishi mumkinmi?

4.5. Might be + noun/adjective

Subject + MIGHT BE + Noun / Adjective
1. He might be tired.

U charchagan bo‘lishi mumkin.

2. She might be a teacher.

U o‘qituvchi bo‘lishi mumkin.

DAFTARGA YOZING!

We use might to express possibility and uncertainty.

Positive: Subject + might + V1.

Negative: Subject + might not + V1.

Question: Might + Subject + V1?

ESLAB QOLING!

Might dan keyin to yoki fe'lning -s shakli ishlatilmaydi.

Correct: He might come.

Incorrect: He might to come.

Incorrect: He might comes.

5. MAY — Ehtimollik va ruxsat

Possibility • Permission

5.1. May nima?

May ikki asosiy ma'noda ishlatiladi:

  • Ehtimollik — biror narsa sodir bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • Ruxsat — biror ishni qilishga ruxsat so‘rash yoki berish.

5.2. Positive structure — Darak gap

Subject + MAY + V1 + Object
1. It may snow tonight.

Bugun kechasi qor yog‘ishi mumkin.

2. I may travel next month.

Men keyingi oy sayohat qilishim mumkin.

5.3. Negative structure — Inkor gap

Subject + MAY NOT + V1 + Object
1. He may not know the answer.

U javobni bilmasligi mumkin.

2. They may not arrive today.

Ular bugun yetib kelmasliklari mumkin.

5.4. Question structure — Ruxsat so‘rash

MAY + Subject + V1 + Object + ?
1. May I come in?

Kirsam maylimi?

2. May I use your phone?

Telefoningizdan foydalansam maylimi?

5.5. Permission — Ruxsat berish

Subject + MAY + V1
1. You may leave now.

Siz hozir ketishingiz mumkin (ruxsat).

2. Students may use dictionaries.

O‘quvchilar lug‘atlardan foydalanishlari mumkin.

DAFTARGA YOZING!

We use may to express possibility and formal permission.

Positive: Subject + may + V1.

Negative: Subject + may not + V1.

Question: May + Subject + V1?

PART 2 — GRAMMAR COMPARISON

Endi modal fe'llarning ma'nolari va ishlatilishidagi farqlarni o‘rganamiz.

1. SHOULD vs MUST

SHOULDMUST
MaslahatKuchli majburiyat
You should study.You must study.
O‘qishing kerak (maslahat).O‘qishing shart.
1. You should exercise regularly.

Sen muntazam mashq qilishing kerak (maslahat).

2. You must wear a seat belt.

Sen xavfsizlik kamarini taqishing shart.

2. MUST vs HAVE TO

Must ko‘pincha so‘zlovchi his qilayotgan kuchli majburiyatni bildiradi. Have to esa tashqi qoida yoki vaziyat tufayli yuzaga kelgan majburiyatni bildiradi.

MUSTHAVE TO
I must call my mother.I have to wear a uniform.
Men onamga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishim shart.Men forma kiyishim kerak.
1. I must remember to buy some bread.

Men non sotib olishni eslab qolishim kerak.

2. We have to show our tickets at the entrance.

Biz kirishda chiptalarimizni ko‘rsatishimiz kerak.

3. MUSTN'T vs DON'T HAVE TO

Bu ikki strukturani adashtirmang!

Mustn't — qilish taqiqlangan.

Don't have to — qilish majburiy emas.

1. You mustn't use your phone during the exam.

Imtihon paytida telefon ishlatish taqiqlangan.

2. You don't have to bring food.

Ovqat olib kelishing shart emas.

4. MIGHT vs MAY

Ikkalasi ham ehtimollikni bildiradi. Ko‘p vaziyatlarda bir-birining o‘rnida ishlatilishi mumkin. Might ba'zan kamroq ishonchni bildiradi, lekin bu qat'iy foiz qoidasi emas.

1. It might rain this evening.

Bugun kechqurun yomg‘ir yog‘ishi mumkin.

2. It may rain this evening.

Bugun kechqurun yomg‘ir yog‘ishi mumkin.

5. SHOULD vs HAVE TO

1. You should read more books.

Sen ko‘proq kitob o‘qishing kerak (maslahat).

2. You have to attend the meeting.

Sen yig‘ilishda qatnashishing kerak (majburiyat).

PART 5 — READING

A Responsible Student

Tom is a responsible student. He gets up early every morning because he has to arrive at school on time. He must prepare his books and homework before leaving home.

Tom's teacher often gives him useful advice. She says that students should read books every day and shouldn't spend too much time playing computer games. Tom follows her advice because he wants to improve his English.

At school, students must follow the rules. They mustn't run in the corridors or make too much noise during lessons. However, they may ask questions whenever they need help.

Tom sometimes studies with his friends after school. They might visit the library or work together on a project. Tom believes that everyone should work hard and help each other.
