1. GRAMMAR — Past Simple

1.1. Past Simple nima?

Past Simple o‘tmishda sodir bo‘lgan va hozirga kelib tugagan harakatlarni ifodalaydi.

I visited my grandmother yesterday.
Men kecha buvimnikiga bordim.
She watched a film last night.
U kecha kechqurun film tomosha qildi.
We travelled to Samarkand last year.
Biz o‘tgan yili Samarqandga sayohat qildik.

1.2. Darak gap — Positive

Formula: Subject + Verb 2 + Object

Darak gapda fe’lning o‘tgan zamon shakli ishlatiladi.

I worked yesterday. — Men kecha ishladim.
He played football. — U futbol o‘ynadi.
They went to school. — Ular maktabga borishdi.

1.3. Regular Verbs — To‘g‘ri fe’llar

1.4. -ed qo‘shish qoidalari

1-qoida: Odatda fe’lga -ed qo‘shiladi.

play → played
help → helped
visit → visited

2-qoida: Fe’l -e bilan tugasa, faqat -d qo‘shiladi.

live → lived
love → loved
dance → danced

3-qoida: Undosh + y bilan tugasa, y o‘rniga ied yoziladi.

study → studied
try → tried
cry → cried

4-qoida: Ayrim qisqa fe’llarda oxirgi undosh ikki marta yoziladi.

stop → stopped
plan → planned
Eslab qoling: play → played, chunki play so‘zida y dan oldin unli harf bor.

1.6. Inkor gap — Negative

Formula: Subject + did not (didn't) + V1

Inkor gapda didn't ishlatiladi. Undan keyin fe’lning birinchi shakli keladi.

I didn't go to school yesterday.
Men kecha maktabga bormadim.
She didn't watch TV.
U televizor ko‘rmadi.
They didn't buy a new car.
Ular yangi mashina sotib olishmadi.
Juda muhim!
I didn't went — XATO
I didn't go — TO‘G‘RI
Did yordamchi fe’li ishlatilganda asosiy fe’l V1 shaklida qoladi.

1.7. So‘roq gap — Questions

Formula: Did + Subject + V1 + ...?
Did you work yesterday?
Kecha ishladingizmi?
Did she visit her friend?
U dugonasini ko‘rgani bordimi?
Did they arrive on time?
Ular vaqtida yetib kelishdimi?

1.9. WH-Questions

WH-so‘zlar yordamida o‘tgan voqea haqida batafsil savol beramiz.

Where did you go? — Qayerga bordingiz?
What did she buy? — U nima sotib oldi?
When did they arrive? — Ular qachon yetib kelishdi?
Why did he leave? — U nima uchun ketdi?
Who did you meet? — Siz kim bilan uchrashdingiz?

1.10. Was / Were

Was va were — to be fe’lining o‘tgan zamon shakllari.

SubjectPast form
Iwas
He / She / Itwas
Youwere
Wewere
Theywere
I was tired yesterday.
Men kecha charchagan edim.
They were at home.
Ular uyda edi.

Inkor: was not = wasn't; were not = weren't.

She wasn't at school.
U maktabda emas edi.

So‘roq: Was/Were + subject ...?

Were you busy yesterday?
Kecha band edingizmi?
Was/were bilan did ishlatilmaydi.
Were you tired? — To‘g‘ri.
Did you were tired? — Xato.

1.12. Past Simple va Present Simple farqi

Present SimplePast Simple
I work every day.I worked yesterday.
She goes to school.She went to school yesterday.
We eat at home.We ate at home last night.
He plays football.He played football yesterday.
Esda tuting:
Present Simple — odat, muntazam ish yoki umumiy haqiqat.
Past Simple — o‘tmishda sodir bo‘lgan va tugagan ish.

2. LISTENING — Tinglab tushunish

Avval audio matnini tinglang. Birinchi tinglashda matnni ochmang. Keyin matnni ko‘rib, yana tinglang.

🎧 Listening 1 — A Busy Saturday

Ikki do‘st o‘tgan shanba kuni haqida suhbatlashmoqda.

Anna: Hi, David! What did you do last Saturday?

David: I had a very busy day. I woke up at seven and cleaned my room.

Anna: Did you go anywhere?

David: Yes, I went to the city centre with my brother.

Anna: What did you buy?

David: I bought a new jacket and some books.

Anna: Did you have lunch there?

David: Yes, we ate at a small Italian restaurant. The food was delicious.

Anna: That sounds great!

David: In the evening, I watched a film and went to bed early.

🎧 Listening 2 — My First Day at Work

Bir kishi yangi ish joyidagi birinchi kuni haqida gapirmoqda.

Last Monday was my first day at a new job. I felt a little nervous in the morning, but my colleagues were very friendly. I arrived at the office at eight thirty. My manager showed me around the building and introduced me to the team. Then I learned how to use the computer system. At lunchtime, I went to a café with two colleagues. We talked about our hobbies and families. I finished work at five o'clock. I was tired, but I felt happy because everything went well.

Listening savollari

1. What time did David wake up?

2. Where did David go?

3. What did David buy?

4. How did the speaker feel on his first day at work?

5. What time did the speaker finish work?

4. READING — A Weekend in the Countryside

Matnni avval mustaqil o‘qing. Keyin notanish so‘zlarni aniqlang va mazmunini tushunishga harakat qiling.

A Weekend in the Countryside

Last weekend, my family and I visited my grandparents in the countryside. We left home early on Saturday morning. The weather was sunny, and the journey was pleasant.

When we arrived, my grandmother prepared a delicious breakfast. My grandfather showed us his garden. He grew tomatoes, potatoes, carrots and many other vegetables. I helped him water the plants.

In the afternoon, my cousins came to visit us. We played football in the garden and took some photos. Later, we sat together and talked about our lives.

On Sunday, we had lunch together. My grandmother cooked my favourite meal. Before we left, I thanked my grandparents for their hospitality. We returned home in the evening. I felt tired, but I really enjoyed the weekend.

Reading comprehension

1. When did the family visit the grandparents?

2. Where did the grandparents live?

3. What did the grandfather grow?

4. What did the children do in the afternoon?

5. How did the writer feel at the end?

8. HOMEWORK — Uy vazifasi

QAT’IY TALAB: Uy vazifasini daftarga yozib bajaring. Faqat o‘qib chiqish yoki javoblarni ko‘rib qo‘yish uy vazifasi bajarildi degani emas.

1-vazifa — Grammar

  • Past Simple formulasini yodlang.
  • Regular fe’llarning -ed qoidalarini daftarga yozing.
  • Kamida 25 ta irregular fe’lning V1 va V2 shaklini yodlang.
  • 10 ta darak, 10 ta inkor va 10 ta so‘roq gap tuzing.

2-vazifa — Vocabulary

  • Darsdagi 30 ta yangi so‘zni daftarga yozing.
  • Inglizcha so‘z, talaffuz va o‘zbekcha tarjimasini yozing.
  • Har bir so‘zni ovoz chiqarib o‘qing.
  • Kamida 15 ta so‘z bilan gap tuzing.

3-vazifa — Listening

  • Listening 1 ni kamida 3 marta tinglang.
  • Listening 2 ni kamida 3 marta tinglang.
  • Har bir matndan 5 tadan yangi ibora yozib oling.
  • Dialoglarni ovoz chiqarib takrorlang.

4-vazifa — Reading

  • Reading matnini ikki marta o‘qing.
  • Matn mazmunini o‘zbek tilida tushuntirib bering.
  • Matn asosida 5 ta inglizcha gap yozing.

5-vazifa — Writing

  • My Last Weekend mavzusida 100–120 so‘z yozing.
  • Matnda kamida 10 ta Past Simple fe’l ishlating.
  • Kamida 2 ta inkor va 2 ta so‘roq gap yozing.

6-vazifa — Speaking

Quyidagi savollarga ovoz chiqarib, to‘liq gaplar bilan javob bering:

  1. What did you do yesterday?
  2. What time did you wake up?
  3. Where did you go last weekend?
  4. What did you eat yesterday?
  5. Who did you talk to?
  6. Did you learn anything new last week?
  7. What was the best day of your last week?
  8. How was your last holiday?

7-vazifa — Takrorlash

Oldingi darslardagi Present Simple va Present Continuous mavzularini ham takrorlang. Ularni Past Simple bilan taqqoslang.
