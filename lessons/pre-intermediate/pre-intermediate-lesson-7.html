📖 GRAMMAR — 1. Comparative Adjectives

Comparative adjectives ikki shaxs, narsa, joy yoki hodisani taqqoslash uchun ishlatiladi.

QOIDA: Bir narsaning boshqa narsadan qanday farq qilishini aytish uchun comparative shaklidan foydalanamiz.
Subject + verb + comparative adjective + than + object

A. Qisqa sifatlar: -er

Ko'pchilik bir bo'g'inli sifatlarning oxiriga -er qo'shiladi.

📖 2. Superlative Adjectives

📖 4. Equality — As...as

Ikki narsaning teng darajada ekanini ifodalash uchun ishlatiladi.

as + adjective + as
My house is as big as yours.
Mening uyim senikidek katta.
She is as intelligent as her brother.
U akasidek aqlli.

Not as...as

Teng emaslikni yoki pastroq darajani bildiradi.

not as + adjective + as
This city is not as crowded as London.
Bu shahar London kabi gavjum emas.
My phone is not as expensive as yours.
Telefonim senikidek qimmat emas.

As much as / As many as

As much as — sanalmaydigan otlar bilan. As many as — sanaladigan ko'plik otlar bilan.
I don't have as much time as you.
Menda sendagidek ko'p vaqt yo'q.
She has as many books as I do.
Unda menikidek ko'p kitob bor.

London is larger and busier than many small towns. However, life in a small town can be quieter and less expensive. Some people prefer big cities because they offer more opportunities. Others prefer smaller places because they are more peaceful and comfortable.

🎧 Listening strategy

  • Matnni birinchi marta umumiy mazmun uchun tinglang.

  • Matnni ovoz chiqarib takrorlang.

📖 Reading — The Best Place to Live

Read carefully. Pay attention to comparative and superlative adjectives.

People have different opinions about the best place to live. Some prefer large cities, while others enjoy small towns. Large cities are usually more crowded and more expensive than small towns. However, they often provide better job opportunities, bigger hospitals and more educational institutions.

Small towns are generally quieter and safer. Houses may be cheaper, and people often know their neighbours better. Life can be less stressful because there is less traffic and noise.

Living in the countryside is another choice. The countryside is often more peaceful than cities. There is usually more fresh air and beautiful natural scenery. However, public transport may be less convenient.

There is no perfect place for everyone. The best place depends on personal needs, lifestyle and priorities. A student may prefer a large city because it offers more educational opportunities. A family may choose a small town because it is quieter and more comfortable.

In the end, the most important thing is to find a place where you feel comfortable, safe and happy.

Reading comprehension — 15 questions

🗣 Dialogue 1 — Which City Is Better?

Ali: Which city do you prefer, London or a small town?
John: I prefer a small town because it is quieter and less crowded.
Ali: But London has more job opportunities.
John: That's true. London is also more exciting.
Ali: So, which one is better?
John: It depends on your lifestyle. For work, London may be better. For a peaceful life, a small town is more suitable.

🗣 Dialogue 2 — Finding the Best Place

Anna: Excuse me, where is the nearest supermarket?
Tom: It is next to the bank, opposite the post office.
Anna: Is it far from here?
Tom: No, it is only five minutes away.
Anna: How can I get there?
Tom: Walk along this street, go past the pharmacy and turn right.
Anna: Thank you very much!
Tom: You're welcome!
