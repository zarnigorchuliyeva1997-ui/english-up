📚 1. GRAMMAR — ZERO CONDITIONAL

1.1. What is Zero Conditional?

English explanation: We use the Zero Conditional to talk about general truths, scientific facts and situations that are always true.

O‘zbekcha izoh: Zero Conditional biror shart bajarilganda doimo yoki odatda sodir bo‘ladigan natijani ifodalash uchun ishlatiladi.

IF + PRESENT SIMPLE, PRESENT SIMPLE

Formula tarkibi:

  • If — agar.
  • S (Subject) — ega.
  • V1 (Verb) — fe'lning birinchi shakli.
  • O (Object) — to‘ldiruvchi.
If you heat water, it boils.

Agar suvni isitsangiz, u qaynaydi.

If plants don't get water, they die.

Agar o‘simliklar suv olmasa, ular nobud bo‘ladi.

Daftarga yozing! Zero Conditional ta'rifi, inglizcha qoida, formula va yuqoridagi ikkita misolni grammatik daftaringizga yozing.

1.2. When do we use Zero Conditional?

Zero Conditional quyidagi holatlarda ishlatiladi:

A. General truths — Umumiy haqiqatlar

If you don't eat, you get hungry.

Agar ovqat yem asangiz, qorningiz ochadi.

If people exercise, they feel better.

Agar odamlar mashq qilsa, o‘zlarini yaxshiroq his qilishadi.

B. Scientific facts — Ilmiy faktlar

If ice gets warm, it melts.

Agar muz isisa, u eriydi.

If you heat ice, it melts.

Agar muzni qizdirsangiz, u eriydi.

C. Repeated situations — Takrorlanadigan holatlar

If I drink coffee at night, I can't sleep.

Agar kechasi qahva ichsam, uxlay olmayman.

If my brother studies, he learns new things.

Agar akam dars qilsa, yangi narsalarni o‘rganadi.

Daftarga yozing! General truths, scientific facts va repeated situations bo‘limlarining izohlarini hamda barcha misollarini yozing.

1.3. Affirmative sentences — Darak gaplar

English explanation: We use the Present Simple in both parts of the Zero Conditional sentence.

O‘zbekcha: Zero Conditional darak gapida If qatnashgan qismda ham, natijani bildirgan qismda ham Present Simple ishlatiladi.

If + S + V1(s/es) + O, S + V1(s/es) + O.

Formulaning izohi:

  • If — shartni bildiradi.
  • S — ega.
  • V1 — fe'lning birinchi shakli.
  • s/es — he, she, it bilan qo‘shiladi.
  • O — to‘ldiruvchi.
If he works hard, he earns money.

Agar u qattiq ishlasa, pul topadi.

If water reaches 100 degrees Celsius, it boils.

Agar suv 100 darajaga yetsa, qaynaydi.

1.4. Negative sentences — Inkor gaplar

English explanation: We use do not or does not to make negative sentences in the Present Simple.

O‘zbekcha: Inkor gaplarda yordamchi fe'llar do not (don't) yoki does not (doesn't) ishlatiladi. Does bilan asosiy fe'lga s/es qo‘shilmaydi.

If + S + don't/doesn't + V1, S + don't/doesn't + V1.
If you don't water flowers, they don't grow well.

Agar gullarni sug‘ormasangiz, ular yaxshi o‘smaydi.

If she doesn't sleep enough, she feels tired.

Agar u yetarlicha uxlamasa, charchoq his qiladi.

Eslab qoling: Doesn't dan keyin fe'lga s yoki es qo‘shilmaydi. Masalan: doesn't work — to‘g‘ri; doesn't works — noto‘g‘ri.

1.5. Questions — So‘roq gaplar

English explanation: We use do or does to form Present Simple questions.

O‘zbekcha: Zero Conditional so‘roq gaplarida Do yoki Does yordamchi fe'llari ishlatiladi.

What happens if + S + V1?
What happens if you heat water?

Suvni isitsangiz nima sodir bo‘ladi?

Do/Does + S + V1 + if + S + V1?
Do you feel tired if you sleep late?

Kech uxlasangiz, charchoq his qilasizmi?

Does he get hungry if he skips breakfast?

Agar u nonushta qilmasa, qorni ochadimi?

1.6. If and When — farqi

If — agar. Shartni ifodalaydi.

When — qachonki. Odatda sodir bo‘lishi kutiladigan holatni ifodalaydi.

If I see him, I say hello.

Agar uni ko‘rsam, salom beraman.

When I get home, I take off my shoes.

Uyga kelganimda, oyoq kiyimimni yechaman.

1.7. Comma — Vergul qoidasi

Agar If bilan boshlangan qism gap boshida kelsa, undan keyin vergul qo‘yiladi.

If it rains, the ground gets wet.

Agar yomg‘ir yog‘sa, yer ho‘l bo‘ladi.

Agar If qismi gap oxirida kelsa, odatda vergul qo‘yilmaydi.

The ground gets wet if it rains.

Agar yomg‘ir yog‘sa, yer ho‘l bo‘ladi.

1.8. If clause at the beginning and at the end

If you mix blue and yellow, you get green.

Agar ko‘k va sariq ranglarni aralashtirsangiz, yashil rang hosil bo‘ladi.

You get green if you mix blue and yellow.

Ko‘k va sariq ranglarni aralashtirsangiz, yashil rang hosil bo‘ladi.

1.9. Zero Conditional vs First Conditional

ZERO: If + Present Simple, Present Simple

Umumiy haqiqatlar va odatiy natijalar uchun.

FIRST: If + Present Simple, will + V1

Kelajakda sodir bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan vaziyatlar uchun.

Zero: If you heat ice, it melts.

Muzni isitsangiz, eriydi.

First: If it rains tomorrow, I will stay home.

Ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘sa, uyda qolaman.

GRAMMAR DAFTAR TOPSHIRIG‘I: 1-bo‘limdagi barcha qoidalar, formulalar, formulalar izohi va misol gaplarni daftaringizga to‘liq yozing. O‘zingiz ham har bir struktura uchun bittadan yangi gap tuzing.
