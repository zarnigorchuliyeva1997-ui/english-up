1. What is Passive Voice?

Passive Voice — gapda ish-harakatni bajarayotgan shaxsdan ko‘ra, ish-harakatning o‘zi yoki ta’sirlangan narsa muhim bo‘lgan grammatik shakl.

Active Voice — aniq nisbat

Subject + Verb + Object

Tom writes a letter.

Tom xat yozadi.

Passive Voice — majhul nisbat

Object + be + V3

A letter is written by Tom.

Xat Tom tomonidan yoziladi.

Active: The chef cooks the food.

Passive: The food is cooked by the chef.

Active: People speak English here.

Passive: English is spoken here.

Active: My mother cleans the room.

Passive: The room is cleaned by my mother.

Qachon Passive Voice ishlatiladi?

  • Ishni kim bajargani noma’lum bo‘lsa.
  • Ishni kim bajargani muhim bo‘lmasa.
  • Natija yoki hodisaning o‘zi muhim bo‘lsa.
  • Rasmiy, ilmiy yoki yangilik uslubida yozilganda.

My phone was stolen. — Telefonim o‘g‘irlangan.

The road is being repaired. — Yo‘l ta’mirlanmoqda.

2. Passive Voice formula

Passive Voice yasashda asosiy yordamchi fe’l — BE. Asosiy fe’l esa doimo V3 shaklida keladi.

Subject + BE + Past Participle (V3)

Present Simple Passive

am / is / are + V3

Biror ish muntazam bajarilishi yoki umumiy faktni ifodalaydi.

The room is cleaned every day.

Xona har kuni tozalanadi.

English is taught at this school.

Bu maktabda ingliz tili o‘qitiladi.

These cars are made in Germany.

Bu mashinalar Germaniyada ishlab chiqariladi.

Letters are delivered in the morning.

Xatlar ertalab yetkazib beriladi.

Present Simple — negative

am / is / are + not + V3

This product is not sold here. — Bu mahsulot bu yerda sotilmaydi.

These rooms are not used. — Bu xonalardan foydalanilmaydi.

Present Simple — questions

Am / Is / Are + subject + V3?

Is English spoken here? — Bu yerda ingliz tilida gaplashiladimi?

Are the windows cleaned every week? — Derazalar har hafta tozalanadimi?

Past Simple Passive

was / were + V3

O‘tmishda bajarilgan ish-harakatni ifodalaydi.

The bridge was built in 1990.

Ko‘prik 1990-yilda qurilgan.

My bag was found yesterday.

Sumkam kecha topildi.

These pictures were taken last summer.

Bu rasmlar o‘tgan yozda olingan.

Past Simple — negative

was / were + not + V3

The letter was not sent. — Xat yuborilmadi.

The shops were not opened yesterday. — Do‘konlar kecha ochilmadi.

Past Simple — questions

Was / Were + subject + V3?

Was the house built in 2005? — Uy 2005-yilda qurilganmi?

Were the documents signed? — Hujjatlar imzolanganmi?

Future Simple Passive

will be + V3

The work will be finished tomorrow.

Ish ertaga tugatiladi.

A new school will be built here.

Bu yerda yangi maktab quriladi.

The results will be announced soon.

Natijalar tez orada e’lon qilinadi.

Future Simple — negative and questions

will not be + V3

The meeting will not be cancelled. — Uchrashuv bekor qilinmaydi.

Will + subject + be + V3?

Will the project be completed this year? — Loyiha bu yil yakunlanadimi?

Present Perfect Passive

has / have been + V3

Natijasi hozirga aloqador bo‘lgan ish-harakatni ifodalaydi.

The room has been cleaned.

Xona tozalangan.

The emails have been sent.

Elektron xatlar yuborilgan.

The problem has been solved.

Muammo hal qilingan.

Present Perfect — negative and questions

has / have not been + V3

The report has not been finished yet. — Hisobot hali tugatilmagan.

Has / Have + subject + been + V3?

Has the food been prepared? — Ovqat tayyorlanganmi?

Four main tenses — comparison

TenseFormulaExample
Present Simpleam/is/are + V3It is made.
Past Simplewas/were + V3It was made.
Future Simplewill be + V3It will be made.
Present Perfecthas/have been + V3It has been made.

3. How to change Active into Passive

Active gapni Passive shaklga o‘zgartirish uchun quyidagi bosqichlarni bajaring:

  1. Active gapdagi Objectni toping.
  2. Objectni gap boshiga olib chiqing.
  3. Gap zamoniga mos BE fe’lini tanlang.
  4. Asosiy fe’lni V3 shakliga o‘tkazing.
  5. Kerak bo‘lsa, bajaruvchini by bilan yozing.

Active: Sarah wrote the story.

1. Object: the story

2. New subject: The story

3. Past Simple BE: was

4. V3: written

Passive: The story was written by Sarah.

Active: They will open the new shop.

Passive: The new shop will be opened.

Active: Someone has stolen my bicycle.

Passive: My bicycle has been stolen.

4. Using BY

By ish-harakatni kim yoki nima bajarganini ko‘rsatadi. Bajaruvchi muhim bo‘lmasa, uni yozmaslik mumkin.

The book was written by George Orwell.

Kitob George Orwell tomonidan yozilgan.

The window was broken by the boy.

Deraza bola tomonidan sindirilgan.

My wallet was found.

Hamyonim topildi.

Remember: people, someone, they, everybody kabi bajaruvchilar noma’lum yoki umumiy bo‘lsa, Passive gapda ko‘pincha tushirib qoldiriladi.

5. Common mistakes

❌ The room is clean every day.

✓ The room is cleaned every day.

Passive shaklda BE + V3 kerak. Clean — bu yerda fe’lning V1 shakli.

❌ The house was build in 2010.

✓ The house was built in 2010.

Build fe’lining V3 shakli built.

❌ The work will finished tomorrow.

✓ The work will be finished tomorrow.

Future Passive formulasida will be + V3 ishlatiladi.

❌ The letters have sent.

✓ The letters have been sent.

Present Perfect Passive: have/has been + V3.

11. Speaking — Talk about your world

Quyidagi savollarga ingliz tilida ovoz chiqarib javob bering. Har bir javob kamida 2–3 gapdan iborat bo‘lsin.

1. What is made in your country?

Answer example: Many different products are made in my country.

2. What was built in your town recently?

Answer example: A new shopping centre was built in my town.

3. What is cleaned every day in your home?

Answer example: The kitchen is cleaned every day.

4. What has been changed in your life recently?

Answer example: My daily routine has been changed recently.

5. What will be improved in your city?

Answer example: More roads will be improved in my city.
