📚 2. WHAT ARE RELATIVE CLAUSES?

Relative Clauses nima?

Relative Clauses — biror shaxs, narsa, hayvon yoki joy haqida qo‘shimcha ma'lumot beruvchi ergash gaplardir.

Ular ikkita alohida gapni birlashtirib, bitta mazmunli va murakkab gap hosil qilishga yordam beradi.

Oddiy gaplarni birlashtirish

The woman is my teacher.

She lives next door.

Ayol mening o‘qituvchim. U qo‘shni uyda yashaydi.

The woman + WHO + lives next door + is my teacher.

The woman who lives next door is my teacher.

Qo‘shni uyda yashaydigan ayol mening o‘qituvchim.

👤 3. WHO — PEOPLE

WHO insonlar haqida qo‘shimcha ma'lumot berish uchun ishlatiladi.

Person + WHO + Verb + ...

Examples

The man who works here is very friendly.

Bu yerda ishlaydigan erkak juda samimiy.

I know a girl who speaks four languages.

Men to‘rtta tilda gapiradigan qizni taniyman.

The teacher who helped me is very kind.

Menga yordam bergan o‘qituvchi juda mehribon.

People who exercise regularly are often healthier.

Muntazam mashq qiladigan odamlar ko‘pincha sog‘lomroq bo‘lishadi.

WHO with different tenses

The woman who lives here is my aunt.

Bu yerda yashaydigan ayol mening xolam.

The man who called me yesterday is my friend.

Kecha menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan erkak mening do‘stim.

I have a friend who will help us tomorrow.

Ertaga bizga yordam beradigan do‘stim bor.

WHO as the object

Who relative clause ichida object vazifasida ham kelishi mumkin.

Person + WHO + Subject + Verb

The woman who I met yesterday is a doctor.

Kecha men uchratgan ayol shifokor.

The boy who we invited is my cousin.

Biz taklif qilgan bola mening amakivachcham.

WHO object bo‘lib kelganda, defining relative clause ichida uni ba'zan tushirib qoldirish mumkin:
The woman I met yesterday is a doctor.
📓 DAFTARGA YOZING

WHO mavzusidagi qoidalarni va 6 ta misolni yozing.

O‘zingiz haqida, oilangiz haqida va tanishlaringiz haqida WHO yordamida 5 ta yangi gap tuzing.

📦 4. WHICH — THINGS AND ANIMALS

WHICH narsalar va hayvonlar haqida ma'lumot berish uchun ishlatiladi.

Thing / Animal + WHICH + Verb ...

The book which is on the table is mine.

Stol ustida turgan kitob meniki.

I bought a phone which has a good camera.

Men yaxshi kameraga ega telefon sotib oldim.

The dog which lives in our garden is very friendly.

Bog‘imizda yashaydigan it juda samimiy.

This is the computer which I use for work.

Bu men ish uchun foydalanadigan kompyuter.

WHICH as subject

Thing + WHICH + Verb

The car which belongs to my father is new.

Otamga tegishli mashina yangi.

WHICH as object

Thing + WHICH + Subject + Verb

The movie which we watched was interesting.

Biz tomosha qilgan film qiziqarli edi.

The laptop which I bought yesterday works perfectly.

Kecha sotib olgan noutbukim juda yaxshi ishlaydi.

WHICH object bo‘lib kelgan defining clause ichida tushirib qoldirilishi mumkin:
The movie we watched was interesting.
📓 DAFTARGA YOZING

WHICH ning subject va object holatlarini yozing. Har biriga 4 tadan mustaqil gap tuzing.

🔗 5. THAT — PEOPLE AND THINGS

THAT odamlar, narsalar va hayvonlar haqida ma'lumot berishi mumkin. Ko‘pincha defining relative clauses ichida WHO yoki WHICH o‘rnida ishlatiladi.

Person / Thing + THAT + Verb ...

The man that lives here is my neighbour.

Bu yerda yashaydigan erkak mening qo‘shnim.

The book that I bought is interesting.

Men sotib olgan kitob qiziqarli.

The dog that barked last night belongs to our neighbour.

Kecha tunda hurigan it qo‘shnimizga tegishli.

WHO vs THAT

WHOTHAT
The woman who works here...The woman that works here...
The boy who helped me...The boy that helped me...

WHICH vs THAT

WHICHTHAT
The phone which I bought...The phone that I bought...
The book which you need...The book that you need...
Muhim qoida: THAT odatda vergul bilan ajratilgan Non-defining Relative Clauses ichida ishlatilmaydi.

THAT cannot always replace WHO or WHICH

Non-defining Relative Clauses ichida WHO yoki WHICH ishlatiladi, THAT emas.

My brother, who lives in London, is a teacher.

Londonda yashaydigan akam o‘qituvchi.

📓 DAFTARGA YOZING

WHO, WHICH va THAT farqlarini jadval shaklida yozing.

Har biri bilan 4 tadan gap tuzing.

📍 6. WHERE — PLACES

WHERE joylar haqida qo‘shimcha ma'lumot berish uchun ishlatiladi.

Place + WHERE + Subject + Verb

This is the school where I studied.

Bu men o‘qigan maktab.

The restaurant where we had dinner was expensive.

Biz kechki ovqat qilgan restoran qimmat edi.

I visited the city where my parents were born.

Men ota-onam tug‘ilgan shaharga tashrif buyurdim.

This is the shop where I buy fresh vegetables.

Bu men yangi sabzavotlar sotib oladigan do‘kon.

WHERE vs WHICH

Bu ikki so‘zning farqini tushunish juda muhim.

This is the house where I live.

Bu men yashaydigan uy.

WHERE — yashash joyi ma'nosini beradi.

This is the house which I bought.

Bu men sotib olgan uy.

WHICH — sotib olingan narsani bildiradi.

WHERE and prepositions

WHERE o‘rnida ba'zan preposition + WHICH ham ishlatilishi mumkin.

This is the house where I live.

This is the house in which I live.

Bu men yashaydigan uy.

The hotel where we stayed was comfortable.

The hotel at which we stayed was comfortable.

Biz turgan mehmonxona qulay edi.

📓 DAFTARGA YOZING

WHERE formulasini, WHERE va WHICH farqini yozing.

O‘zingiz biladigan 5 ta joy haqida WHERE bilan gap tuzing.

🎓 7. ADVANCED RELATIVE CLAUSES

1. WHOSE — POSSESSION

WHOSE egalikni bildiradi. Odamlar va ba'zan narsalar bilan ishlatiladi.

Person + WHOSE + Noun + Verb

I know a boy whose father is a doctor.

Otasi shifokor bo‘lgan bolani taniyman.

The woman whose car was stolen called the police.

Mashinasini o‘g‘irlab ketishgan ayol politsiyaga qo‘ng‘iroq qildi.

She is a student whose English is excellent.

U ingliz tili a'lo darajada bo‘lgan o‘quvchi.

2. WHOM — OBJECT

WHOM insonlarga nisbatan object vazifasida ishlatiladi. Ko‘proq rasmiy ingliz tilida uchraydi.

Person + WHOM + Subject + Verb

The man whom I met yesterday was friendly.

Kecha men uchratgan erkak samimiy edi.

The woman whom we invited couldn't come.

Biz taklif qilgan ayol kela olmadi.

Oddiy suhbatda WHOM o‘rniga WHO yoki tushirib qoldirilgan shakl ham ishlatiladi.

3. DEFINING RELATIVE CLAUSES

Bu clause gapdagi shaxs yoki narsani aniq belgilab beradi. Uni olib tashlasak, kim yoki nima haqida gap ketayotgani noaniq bo‘lib qolishi mumkin.

Noun + Relative Pronoun + Necessary Information

The students who study regularly get better results.

Muntazam o‘qiydigan o‘quvchilar yaxshiroq natijalar olishadi.

The phone that I bought yesterday is expensive.

Kecha sotib olgan telefonim qimmat.

Defining clause odatda vergul bilan ajratilmaydi.

4. NON-DEFINING RELATIVE CLAUSES

Bu clause qo‘shimcha ma'lumot beradi. Asosiy gapning ma'nosi usiz ham tushunarli bo‘ladi.

Noun, + Relative Pronoun + Extra Information, + Main Clause

My father, who is very hardworking, gets up early.

Juda mehnatkash bo‘lgan otam erta turadi.

London, which is the capital of the UK, attracts many tourists.

Buyuk Britaniya poytaxti bo‘lgan London ko‘plab sayyohlarni jalb qiladi.

Non-defining clause ichida THAT ishlatilmaydi. Vergullar muhim ahamiyatga ega.

5. OMISSION — RELATIVE PRONOUNNI TUSHIRIB QOLDIRISH

Agar relative pronoun defining clause ichida object vazifasida ishlatilsa, ko‘pincha tushirib qoldirilishi mumkin.

The book that I read was excellent.

The book I read was excellent.

Men o‘qigan kitob ajoyib edi.

The man who we met was kind.

The man we met was kind.

Biz uchratgan erkak mehribon edi.

Relative pronoun subject bo‘lib kelganida uni odatda tushirib qoldirib bo‘lmaydi:
The man who lives here is my uncle.
The man lives here is my uncle. — noto‘g‘ri.

6. PREPOSITIONS IN RELATIVE CLAUSES

Preposition + whom / which shakli rasmiyroq uslubda ishlatiladi.

The person to whom I spoke was helpful.

Men gaplashgan inson yordam berdi.

The company for which he works is famous.

U ishlaydigan kompaniya mashhur.

Oddiy suhbatda preposition odatda gap oxirida keladi.

The person who I spoke to was helpful.

The company which he works for is famous.

7. RELATIVE CLAUSES WITH QUANTIFIERS

Some of, many of, most of, all of kabi iboralar bilan whom va which ishlatilishi mumkin.

I have three brothers, two of whom live abroad.

Mening uchta akam-ukam bor, ulardan ikkitasi chet elda yashaydi.

She bought ten books, most of which were interesting.

U o‘nta kitob sotib oldi, ularning aksariyati qiziqarli edi.

📓 ADVANCED GRAMMAR — DAFTARGA YOZISH
  1. WHOSE va WHOM formulalarini yozing.
  2. Defining va Non-defining farqini yozing.
  3. Relative Pronoun omission qoidasini yozing.
  4. Preposition + whom/which shaklini yozing.
  5. Har bir bo‘lim uchun kamida 3 ta gap tuzing.

🎧 9. LISTENING PRACTICE

Listening 1 — The Man Who Changed His Life

There was a man named David who lived in a small town. He worked in a shop that belonged to his uncle. Although he enjoyed his job, he wanted to learn new skills. One day, he met a teacher who encouraged him to study English. David started taking lessons at a school where students practised speaking every day. He also bought a book which explained basic grammar. The people who supported him helped him stay motivated. After several months, David could communicate with foreigners more confidently. He realised that the decision which he had made was one of the best decisions in his life.

David ismli bir erkak kichik shaharchada yashardi. U amakisiga tegishli do‘konda ishlardi. Ishidan zavqlansa ham, yangi ko‘nikmalarni o‘rganishni xohlardi. Bir kuni u ingliz tilini o‘rganishga undagan o‘qituvchini uchratdi. David har kuni o‘quvchilar gapirishni mashq qiladigan maktabda dars olishni boshladi. Shuningdek, asosiy grammatikani tushuntiradigan kitob sotib oldi. Uni qo‘llab-quvvatlagan odamlar motivatsiyasini saqlashga yordam berishdi. Bir necha oydan keyin David chet elliklar bilan ishonchliroq muloqot qila oldi. U qabul qilgan qarori hayotidagi eng yaxshi qarorlardan biri ekanini tushundi.

Listening 2 — A School That Makes a Difference

Anna studies at a school which focuses on practical education. The teachers who work there encourage students to think independently. Every student has a project that helps them develop useful skills. Anna has a friend whose dream is to become an engineer. They often visit a library where they can find information about science and technology. Their teacher, who has worked in education for twenty years, gives them useful advice. Anna believes that the knowledge which she gains at school will help her build a successful future. She wants to become someone who can make a positive difference in society.

Anna amaliy ta'limga e'tibor qaratadigan maktabda o‘qiydi. U yerda ishlaydigan o‘qituvchilar o‘quvchilarni mustaqil fikrlashga undaydi. Har bir o‘quvchining foydali ko‘nikmalarni rivojlantirishga yordam beradigan loyihasi bor. Annaning muhandis bo‘lishni orzu qiladigan do‘sti bor. Ular ko‘pincha ilm-fan va texnologiya haqida ma'lumot topish mumkin bo‘lgan kutubxonaga borishadi. Ta'lim sohasida yigirma yil ishlagan o‘qituvchisi ularga foydali maslahatlar beradi. Anna maktabda oladigan bilimlari unga muvaffaqiyatli kelajak qurishda yordam berishiga ishonadi. U jamiyatda ijobiy o‘zgarish qila oladigan inson bo‘lishni xohlaydi.

📚 10. READING PRACTICE

Reading 1 — The Importance of Education

Education is one of the most important things that can change a person's life. People who continue learning often discover opportunities that they did not know existed. A good teacher is someone who can explain difficult ideas clearly and encourage students to ask questions. Schools where students feel comfortable can create an environment which supports learning. However, education is not limited to classrooms. Books that people read, experiences which they gain and people whom they meet can also teach valuable lessons. A person who wants to develop should remain curious and open to new ideas. Knowledge which is used in everyday life becomes especially valuable.

Ta'lim inson hayotini o‘zgartira oladigan eng muhim narsalardan biridir. O‘rganishni davom ettiradigan odamlar ko‘pincha mavjudligini bilmagan imkoniyatlarni kashf etadilar. Yaxshi o‘qituvchi qiyin g‘oyalarni aniq tushuntira oladigan va o‘quvchilarni savol berishga undaydigan insondir. O‘quvchilar o‘zlarini qulay his qiladigan maktablar o‘rganishni qo‘llab-quvvatlaydigan muhit yaratishi mumkin. Biroq ta'lim sinfxonalar bilan cheklanmaydi. Odamlar o‘qiydigan kitoblar, orttiradigan tajribalar va uchratadigan insonlar ham qimmatli saboqlar berishi mumkin. Rivojlanishni xohlagan inson qiziquvchan va yangi g‘oyalarga ochiq bo‘lishi kerak. Kundalik hayotda qo‘llanadigan bilim ayniqsa qimmatli bo‘ladi.

Reading 2 — A Journey to Success

Success is a journey which requires patience, discipline and hard work. People who achieve their goals usually have clear plans that help them move forward. A person whose goals are realistic can measure progress more easily. Friends who offer support can make difficult situations less stressful. There are also moments when people face problems which seem impossible to solve. However, those who remain determined often discover solutions. The environment where people work and study can influence their habits. That is why it is important to spend time with people who encourage positive behaviour. Success does not always belong to those who are the fastest. It often belongs to those who continue when things become difficult.

Muvaffaqiyat sabr, intizom va mehnat talab qiladigan sayohatdir. Maqsadlariga erishadigan odamlar odatda oldinga harakat qilishga yordam beradigan aniq rejalarga ega. Maqsadlari real bo‘lgan inson rivojlanishini osonroq baholay oladi. Qo‘llab-quvvatlaydigan do‘stlar qiyin vaziyatlarni kamroq stressli qilishi mumkin. Ba'zan odamlar hal qilish imkonsizdek tuyuladigan muammolarga duch kelishadi. Biroq qat'iyatli bo‘lib qoladiganlar ko‘pincha yechimlarni topadilar. Odamlar ishlaydigan va o‘qiydigan muhit ularning odatlariga ta'sir qilishi mumkin. Shuning uchun ijobiy xulq-atvorni rag‘batlantiradigan insonlar bilan vaqt o‘tkazish muhim. Muvaffaqiyat doimo eng tezkor odamlarga nasib etmaydi. Ko‘pincha qiyinchilik paytida ham davom etadigan insonlar muvaffaqiyatga erishadi.
