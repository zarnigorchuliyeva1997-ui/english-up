1. INTRODUCTION — Darsga kirish

Present Simple ingliz tilidagi eng muhim zamonlardan biridir. U faqat har kuni takrorlanadigan ishlarni emas, balki doimiy holatlar, umumiy haqiqatlar, fikrlar, jadval va odatlarni ham ifodalaydi.

Bugungi asosiy maqsad: Present Simple qoidalarini bilish bilan cheklanmasdan, undan mustaqil gapirish, savol berish va yozishda foydalanishni o'rganish.

Beginner va Pre-Intermediate farqi

Beginner:

I work every day.

Men har kuni ishlayman.

Pre-Intermediate:

I usually work during the day, but I sometimes work late when we have a lot of customers.

Men odatda kunduzi ishlayman, ammo mijozlar ko'p bo'lganda ba'zan kechgacha ishlayman.

Present Simple qachon ishlatiladi?

  1. Takrorlanadigan odatlar.
  2. Kundalik tartib.
  3. Doimiy holatlar.
  4. Umumiy haqiqatlar.
  5. Fikrlar va his-tuyg'ular.
  6. Jadval asosidagi kelajak voqealari.
  7. Ko'rsatmalar va yo'riqnomalar.

2. POSITIVE SENTENCES — Darak gaplar

Darak gaplarda biror ish bajarilishi yoki holat mavjudligi aytiladi.

I / You / We / They + V1
He / She / It + V1 + s/es

1-qoida: I, You, We, They

I study English every evening.

Men har kuni kechqurun ingliz tilini o'rganaman.

They live in a small town.

Ular kichik shaharchada yashaydi.

We usually have dinner together.

Biz odatda birga kechki ovqat qilamiz.

You speak English very well.

Siz ingliz tilida juda yaxshi gapirasiz.

2-qoida: He, She, It

Uchinchi shaxs birlikda asosiy fe'lga odatda -s yoki -es qo'shiladi.

He works in a hospital.

U kasalxonada ishlaydi.

She teaches English.

U ingliz tilidan dars beradi.

My brother watches TV every evening.

Akam har oqshom televizor ko'radi.

The shop opens at nine.

Do'kon soat to'qqizda ochiladi.

3. NEGATIVE SENTENCES — Inkor gaplar

Biror ish bajarilmasligini aytish uchun do not yoki does not ishlatiladi.

I / You / We / They + do not + V1
He / She / It + does not + V1

Qisqartmalar:

  • Do not = Don't
  • Does not = Doesn't

I don't drink coffee.

Men qahva ichmayman.

We don't work on Sundays.

Biz yakshanba kunlari ishlamaymiz.

She doesn't like cold weather.

U sovuq ob-havoni yoqtirmaydi.

My father doesn't watch TV in the morning.

Otam ertalab televizor ko'rmaydi.

Eng ko'p uchraydigan xato

❌ He doesn't works.
✅ He doesn't work.

❌ She don't like tea.
✅ She doesn't like tea.

❌ They doesn't study.
✅ They don't study.

7. PRESENT SIMPLE vs PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Bu ikki zamonni farqlash Pre-Intermediate bosqichida juda muhim.

Present SimplePresent Continuous
Doimiy odatHozir sodir bo'layotgan ish
I work every day.I am working now.
She usually cooks.She is cooking now.
They live here.They are staying here this week.

Taqqoslash misollari

I usually drink tea, but today I am drinking coffee.

Men odatda choy ichaman, ammo bugun qahva ichyapman.

He works in a bank, but this week he is working from home.

U bankda ishlaydi, lekin bu hafta uydan ishlayapti.

My sister studies English every day. She is studying now.

Singlim har kuni ingliz tilini o'rganadi. U hozir o'qiyapti.

9. SENTENCE BUILDING — Gap tuzish

Quyidagi gaplarni o'rganing. Keyin ularni o'zingizning hayotingizga moslashtiring.

I usually start my day with a cup of tea.

Men odatda kunimni bir piyola choy bilan boshlayman.

My brother works hard because he wants to support his family.

Akam oilasini qo'llab-quvvatlashni xohlagani uchun qattiq ishlaydi.

She rarely goes out because she prefers staying at home.

U uyda qolishni afzal ko'rgani uchun kamdan-kam tashqariga chiqadi.

Although I feel tired after work, I study English every evening.

Ishdan keyin charchagan bo'lsam ham, har oqshom ingliz tilini o'rganaman.

My parents always encourage me to achieve my goals.

Ota-onam meni maqsadlarimga erishishga doimo ruhlantiradi.

I don't usually watch television because I have many things to do.

Men odatda televizor ko'rmayman, chunki qiladigan ishlarim ko'p.

Our English lesson starts at nine, so I never arrive late.

Ingliz tili darsimiz soat to'qqizda boshlanadi, shuning uchun men hech qachon kechikmayman.

He believes that regular practice improves his speaking skills.

U muntazam mashq qilish uning speaking ko'nikmalarini yaxshilashiga ishonadi.

10. READING — A Balanced Daily Life

My Daily Routine and Lifestyle

My name is Daniel, and I live in a small town with my family. I usually wake up at half past six because I like starting my day early. After I get up, I wash my face, brush my teeth and prepare breakfast. I normally have eggs, bread and a cup of tea.

I work in a local company. My working day starts at nine o'clock and finishes at six. I often communicate with different people, answer emails and solve problems. Although my job sometimes becomes stressful, I enjoy learning new things and improving my skills.

After work, I usually return home and spend some time with my family. We have dinner together and talk about our day. My children sometimes tell me interesting stories about school.

In the evening, I try to study English for at least thirty minutes. I read short articles, listen to conversations and practise speaking. I believe that regular practice makes a big difference.

Before I go to bed, I usually prepare my plans for the next day. I don't always have a perfect routine, but I try to maintain healthy habits. For me, a balanced lifestyle means having enough time for work, education, family and rest.

🇺🇿 O'zbekcha tarjimasi

Matn tarjimasini ochish

Mening ismim Daniel va men oilam bilan kichik shaharchada yashayman. Men odatda ertalabki soat olti yarimda uyg'onaman, chunki kunimni erta boshlashni yoqtiraman. Turganimdan keyin yuzimni yuvaman, tishlarimni tozalayman va nonushta tayyorlayman. Men odatda tuxum, non va bir piyola choy ichaman.

Men mahalliy kompaniyada ishlayman. Ish kunim soat to'qqizda boshlanadi va oltida tugaydi. Men ko'pincha turli odamlar bilan muloqot qilaman, elektron xatlarga javob beraman va muammolarni hal qilaman. Ishim ba'zan stressli bo'lsa-da, yangi narsalarni o'rganish va ko'nikmalarimni rivojlantirish menga yoqadi.

Ishdan keyin odatda uyga qaytaman va oilam bilan vaqt o'tkazaman. Biz birga kechki ovqat qilamiz va kunimiz haqida suhbatlashamiz. Farzandlarim ba'zan menga maktabdagi qiziqarli voqealarni aytib berishadi.

Kechqurun kamida o'ttiz daqiqa ingliz tilini o'rganishga harakat qilaman. Qisqa maqolalar o'qiyman, suhbatlarni tinglayman va gapirishni mashq qilaman. Muntazam mashq katta farq qilishiga ishonaman.

Uxlashdan oldin odatda keyingi kun uchun rejalarimni tayyorlayman. Menda har doim ham mukammal kun tartibi bo'lmaydi, lekin sog'lom odatlarni saqlashga harakat qilaman. Men uchun muvozanatli hayot tarzi ish, ta'lim, oila va dam olish uchun yetarli vaqtga ega bo'lishni anglatadi.

11. LISTENING — Real-life conversations

Dialoglarni avval ingliz tilida o'qing. Keyin telefoningizdagi matnni ovoz chiqarib o'qish funksiyasidan yoki boshqa o'quvchidan foydalanib, tinglash mashqini bajaring.

Dialogue 1 — Daily Habits

Anna: What time do you usually wake up?

Tom: I usually wake up at seven, but I sometimes get up earlier.

Anna: Do you have breakfast at home?

Tom: Yes, I do. I normally eat eggs and bread.

Anna: Do you exercise in the morning?

Tom: Not every day. I usually go for a walk three times a week.

Anna: That sounds healthy!

Dialogue 1 tarjimasi

Anna: Odatda soat nechada uyg'onasiz?

Tom: Odatda soat yettida uyg'onaman, lekin ba'zan ertaroq turaman.

Anna: Uyda nonushta qilasizmi?

Tom: Ha. Odatda tuxum va non yeyman.

Anna: Ertalab mashq qilasizmi?

Tom: Har kuni emas. Odatda haftada uch marta sayr qilaman.

Anna: Bu sog'lom odat ekan!

Dialogue 2 — Work and Lifestyle

David: What do you do for a living?

Maria: I work as a teacher at a secondary school.

David: Do you enjoy your job?

Maria: Yes, I do. I enjoy helping students learn new things.

David: What do you usually do after work?

Maria: I usually prepare lessons and spend time with my family.

David: Do you have time for hobbies?

Maria: Yes. I sometimes read novels and listen to podcasts.

Dialogue 2 tarjimasi

David: Qanday ish bilan shug'ullanasiz?

Maria: Men o'rta maktabda o'qituvchi bo'lib ishlayman.

David: Ishingiz sizga yoqadimi?

Maria: Ha. O'quvchilarga yangi narsalarni o'rganishda yordam berish menga yoqadi.

David: Ishdan keyin odatda nima qilasiz?

Maria: Odatda darslarni tayyorlayman va oilam bilan vaqt o'tkazaman.

David: Sevimli mashg'ulotlaringizga vaqtingiz bormi?

Maria: Ha. Ba'zan romanlar o'qiyman va podkastlar tinglayman.
