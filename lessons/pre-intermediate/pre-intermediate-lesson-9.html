1. GRAMMAR — PRESENT PERFECT

Present Perfect nima?

Present Perfect — o‘tmishda sodir bo‘lgan, lekin natijasi yoki aloqasi hozirgi vaqt bilan bog‘liq bo‘lgan ish-harakatni ifodalash uchun ishlatiladigan zamon.

📘 English definition — Daftarga yozing:

We use the Present Perfect to talk about past actions that have a connection with the present.

Biz Present Perfect zamonidan o‘tmishda sodir bo‘lgan va hozirgi vaqt bilan aloqasi mavjud bo‘lgan ish-harakatlar haqida gapirish uchun foydalanamiz.

✅ Present Perfect formulasi

Subject + HAVE / HAS + V3

Subject — ega.

Have / Has — yordamchi fe’l.

V3 — fe’lning uchinchi shakli (Past Participle).

SubjectYordamchi fe’lMisol
IhaveI have worked.
YouhaveYou have worked.
WehaveWe have worked.
TheyhaveThey have worked.
HehasHe has worked.
ShehasShe has worked.
IthasIt has worked.

🟢 Positive — Darak gap

Subject + have / has + V3
I have finished my homework.
Men uy vazifamni tugatdim.
She has visited Samarkand.
U Samarqandga tashrif buyurgan.
They have bought a new car.
Ular yangi mashina sotib olishgan.

🔴 Negative — Inkor gap

Subject + have / has + NOT + V3

Have not = haven't

Has not = hasn't

I have not seen this film.
Men bu filmni ko‘rmaganman.
He hasn't finished his work.
U ishini tugatmagan.

🔵 Question — So‘roq gap

Have / Has + Subject + V3?
Have you ever travelled abroad?
Siz hech chet elga sayohat qilganmisiz?
Has she arrived?
U yetib keldimi?

📌 Present Perfect qachon ishlatiladi?

1. Life experiences — Hayotiy tajribalar

We use the Present Perfect to talk about experiences in our lives when the exact time is not important.

Hayotimizdagi tajribalar haqida aniq vaqtni aytmasdan gapirganda ishlatamiz.

I have visited Turkey. — Men Turkiyaga borganman.

2. Recently completed actions — Yaqinda tugagan ishlar

We use the Present Perfect for actions that have recently finished.

Yaqinda tugagan ish-harakatlar haqida gapirganda ishlatamiz.

She has just cleaned the room. — U hozirgina xonani tozaladi.

3. Present result — Hozirgi natija

We use the Present Perfect when a past action has a result in the present.

O‘tmishdagi ishning hozirgi natijasi mavjud bo‘lsa ishlatamiz.

I have lost my keys. — Kalitlarimni yo‘qotib qo‘ydim.

4. Unfinished time — Tugamagan vaqt

We use the Present Perfect with unfinished time periods such as today, this week and this year.

Bugun, shu hafta yoki shu yil kabi hali tugamagan vaqt oralig‘ida ishlatamiz.

I have read two books this month. — Men shu oy ikkita kitob o‘qidim.

⭐ Present Perfect bilan ishlatiladigan so‘zlar

EverHech — Have you ever been there?
NeverHech qachon — I have never tried it.
JustHozirgina — She has just arrived.
AlreadyAllaqachon — I have already eaten.
YetHali — Have you finished yet?
RecentlyYaqinda — I have recently moved.
So farHozirgacha — We have done well so far.
Since...dan beri — since 2020
For...davomida — for three years
Up to nowHozirgacha — Up to now, I have learned a lot.

Since va For farqi

SINCE + boshlanish vaqti

Since 2020, since Monday, since January, since yesterday.

FOR + vaqt davomiyligi

For two days, for three years, for a week, for five hours.

I have lived here since 2020.
Men bu yerda 2020-yildan beri yashayman.
I have lived here for six years.
Men bu yerda olti yildan beri yashayman.

2. GRAMMAR — PAST SIMPLE

Past Simple nima?

Past Simple — o‘tmishda sodir bo‘lgan va tugagan ish-harakatlarni ifodalash uchun ishlatiladigan zamon.

📘 English definition — Daftarga yozing:

We use the Past Simple to talk about completed actions at a definite time in the past.

Biz Past Simple zamonidan o‘tmishda aniq bir vaqtda sodir bo‘lgan va tugagan ish-harakatlar haqida gapirish uchun foydalanamiz.

✅ Past Simple formulasi

🟢 Positive — Darak gap

Subject + V2 + Object
I visited my grandmother yesterday.
Men kecha buvimni ko‘rgani bordim.
He bought a new phone last week.
U o‘tgan hafta yangi telefon sotib oldi.

🔴 Negative — Inkor gap

Subject + DID NOT + V1

Did not = didn't.

Diqqat: did ishlatilganda asosiy fe’l V1 shakliga qaytadi.

I didn't go to school yesterday.
Men kecha maktabga bormadim.
She didn't buy the dress.
U ko‘ylakni sotib olmadi.

🔵 Question — So‘roq gap

DID + Subject + V1?
Did you watch the film?
Siz filmni tomosha qildingizmi?
Did they arrive yesterday?
Ular kecha yetib kelishdimi?

📌 Past Simple qachon ishlatiladi?

1. Completed actions — Tugagan ishlar

We use the Past Simple for actions that started and finished in the past.

O‘tmishda boshlangan va o‘sha vaqtda tugagan ishlar uchun ishlatamiz.

I cleaned my room. — Men xonamni tozaladim.

2. Specific past time — Aniq o‘tgan vaqt

We use the Past Simple when we mention a definite time in the past.

Aniq o‘tgan vaqt ko‘rsatilganda ishlatamiz.

She called me yesterday. — U menga kecha qo‘ng‘iroq qildi.

3. Past habits — O‘tmishdagi odatlar

We use the Past Simple to describe habits or repeated actions in the past.

O‘tmishda takrorlangan odatlar va ishlar haqida gapirganda ishlatamiz.

When I was a child, I played football every day.

Men bolaligimda har kuni futbol o‘ynardim.

3. PRESENT PERFECT VS PAST SIMPLE

Endi ikkala zamonning eng muhim farqini o‘rganamiz.

Asosiy qoida:

Present Perfect — aniq o‘tgan vaqt muhim bo‘lmagan yoki hozirgi vaqt bilan aloqa mavjud bo‘lgan vaziyat.

Past Simple — ish-harakat qachon sodir bo‘lganini aniq aytadigan vaziyat.

Present PerfectPast Simple
I have seen this film.I saw this film yesterday.
She has visited London.She visited London in 2022.
We have finished our work.We finished our work at 5.
He has lost his keys.He lost his keys last night.

6. READING PRACTICE

Reading 1 — An Unforgettable Journey

Last year, I travelled to a beautiful city with my family. We left home early in the morning and arrived there at noon. We visited a famous museum and walked around the city centre. I took many photographs and bought some souvenirs. It was a wonderful day.

I have travelled to several cities in my life, but that journey was special. I have learned that travelling is a great way to discover new places and understand different cultures. Since that trip, I have become more interested in history and geography.

Reading 2 — Learning English

I started learning English several years ago. At first, it was difficult for me to understand new words and grammar rules. I studied every day and tried to practise speaking. Last month, I joined an English learning group. I met new students and talked with them in English.

Since then, I have improved my speaking skills. I have learned many useful expressions and read several short stories. I haven't reached all my goals yet, but I am making progress. I believe that regular practice is the key to success.
