1. What is First Conditional?

First Conditional — kelajakda sodir bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan vaziyatlar va ularning ehtimoliy natijalarini ifodalash uchun ishlatiladigan grammatik struktura.

English explanation

We use the First Conditional to talk about real and possible situations in the future and their likely results.

O‘zbekcha: Kelajakda biror shart bajarilsa, qanday natija yuz berishini aytish uchun ishlatamiz.

If I study hard, I will pass the exam.

Agar qattiq o‘qisam, imtihondan o‘taman.

If she comes early, we will start the meeting.

Agar u erta kelsa, uchrashuvni boshlaymiz.

DAFTARGA YOZING

First Conditional ta'rifini inglizcha va o‘zbekcha yozing.

Yuqoridagi ikkita misol gapni ham tarjimasi bilan ko‘chiring.

2. When do we use First Conditional?

We use the First Conditional when we talk about a possible future situation.

Biz First Conditionalni kelajakda amalga oshishi mumkin bo‘lgan holatlarda ishlatamiz.

Situation 1 — Real possibility

If it is sunny tomorrow, we will go to the park.

Agar ertaga havo quyoshli bo‘lsa, parkka boramiz.

If I have enough money, I will buy a new phone.

Agar yetarli pulim bo‘lsa, yangi telefon sotib olaman.

Situation 2 — Future plans and decisions

If I finish work early, I will visit my friend.

Agar ishimni erta tugatsam, do‘stimni ko‘rgani boraman.

If we have time, we will watch a movie.

Agar vaqtimiz bo‘lsa, kino tomosha qilamiz.

DAFTARGA YOZING

First Conditional ishlatiladigan ikkita asosiy vaziyatni yozing.

To‘rtta misol gapni ko‘chirib, har birining tarjimasini yozing.

3. Main Formula

First Conditionalning asosiy formulasi:

IF + SUBJECT + PRESENT SIMPLE,
SUBJECT + WILL + V1

Formula qismlarini o‘rganamiz

IF — agar degan ma'noni bildiradi.

SUBJECT (S) — ega, ya'ni ish-harakatni bajaruvchi shaxs yoki narsa.

PRESENT SIMPLE — hozirgi oddiy zamon shakli.

WILL — kelajakni ifodalashda yordam beruvchi modal fe'l.

V1 — fe'lning birinchi shakli.

If you work hard, you will succeed.

Agar qattiq ishlasang, muvaffaqiyatga erishasan.

If they invite us, we will come.

Agar ular bizni taklif qilishsa, kelamiz.

DAFTARGA YOZING

Formulani to‘liq yozing.

S, IF, Present Simple, WILL va V1 izohlarini alohida yozing.

Ikki misol gapni tarjimasi bilan yozing.

4. IF Clause — Present Simple

If bilan boshlangan qism shartni bildiradi. First Conditionalda bu qism odatda Present Simple zamonida keladi.

IF + SUBJECT + V1 / V(s/es)

I / You / We / They

If I have time, I will call you.

Agar vaqtim bo‘lsa, senga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.

If they arrive early, we will wait for them.

Agar ular erta yetib kelishsa, biz ularni kutamiz.

He / She / It

He, She, It bilan Present Simple fe'liga odatda -s yoki -es qo‘shiladi.

If he studies, he will pass the exam.

Agar u o‘qisa, imtihondan o‘tadi.

If she works hard, she will get a promotion.

Agar u qattiq ishlasa, lavozimi ko‘tariladi.

DAFTARGA YOZING

I/You/We/They va He/She/It bilan fe'l ishlatilishidagi farqni yozing.

To‘rtta misol gapni ko‘chiring.

5. Main Clause — WILL + V1

Ikkinchi qism shart bajarilganda yuz beradigan natijani ifodalaydi.

SUBJECT + WILL + V1

WILL dan keyin fe'lning birinchi shakli ishlatiladi.

I will help you.

Men senga yordam beraman.

She will learn English.

U ingliz tilini o‘rganadi.

First Conditional examples

If you ask me, I will help you.

Agar mendan so‘rasang, senga yordam beraman.

If he calls, I will answer.

Agar u qo‘ng‘iroq qilsa, javob beraman.

DAFTARGA YOZING

WILL + V1 formulasini yozing.

WILL dan keyin nima uchun V1 kelishini tushuntirib yozing.

Misollarni ko‘chiring.

6. Positive Sentences — Darak gap

IF + S + V1(s/es), S + WILL + V1.

If I wake up early, I will exercise.

Agar erta uyg‘onsam, mashq qilaman.

If she cooks dinner, we will eat together.

Agar u kechki ovqat tayyorlasa, birga ovqatlanamiz.

DAFTARGA YOZING

Darak gap formulasini va ikkita misolni yozing.

7. Negative Sentences — Inkor gap

Natija qismida WILL NOT yoki WON'T ishlatiladi.

IF + S + PRESENT SIMPLE,
S + WILL NOT + V1.

WILL NOT = WON'T

If it rains, we won't go outside.

Agar yomg‘ir yog‘sa, tashqariga chiqmaymiz.

If he doesn't study, he won't pass the exam.

Agar u o‘qimasa, imtihondan o‘ta olmaydi.

Eslatma: If qismidagi inkor shakli Present Simple qoidalari bilan yasaladi: don't / doesn't.

If she doesn't hurry, she will miss the bus.

Agar u shoshilmasa, avtobusga kechikadi.

DAFTARGA YOZING

WILL NOT va WON'T farqini yozing.

Don't va Doesn't ishlatilishini takrorlang.

Uchta misol gapni ko‘chiring.

8. Question Form — Savol shakli

First Conditional savollarida WILL yordamida natija haqida savol berish mumkin.

WHAT / WHERE / WHEN + WILL + S + V1
IF + S + PRESENT SIMPLE?

What will you do if it rains?

Agar yomg‘ir yog‘sa, nima qilasan?

Where will you go if you have a holiday?

Agar ta'tiling bo‘lsa, qayerga borasan?

DAFTARGA YOZING

Savol formulasini yozing.

What va Where bilan ikkita savol tuzing.

9. If Clause at the Beginning

If gapning boshida kelsa, ikki qism orasida vergul ishlatiladi.

If + condition, result.

If I am tired, I will go to bed early.

Agar charchagan bo‘lsam, erta uxlayman.

If we save money, we will travel next year.

Agar pul yig‘sak, kelasi yil sayohat qilamiz.

DAFTARGA YOZING

If boshida kelganda vergul qo‘yilishini yozing.

Misollarni daftarga ko‘chiring.

10. If Clause at the End

If qismi gapning oxirida ham kelishi mumkin.

Result + if + condition.

Bu holatda odatda vergul qo‘yilmaydi.

I will go outside if the weather is nice.

Agar ob-havo yaxshi bo‘lsa, tashqariga chiqaman.

She will call you if she has time.

Agar vaqti bo‘lsa, u senga qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi.

DAFTARGA YOZING

Ikki xil gap tuzilishini yozing va vergul farqini tushuntiring.

11. Other Modal Verbs in First Conditional

Natija qismida faqat WILL emas, boshqa modal fe'llar ham ishlatilishi mumkin.

CAN — qila olmoq

If you finish early, you can go home.

Agar erta tugatsang, uyga ketishing mumkin.

MAY — ehtimol

If it rains, we may stay at home.

Agar yomg‘ir yog‘sa, uyda qolishimiz mumkin.

MUST — shart

If you want to improve, you must practise every day.

Agar rivojlanishni istasang, har kuni mashq qilishing shart.

SHOULD — kerak

If you feel sick, you should rest.

Agar o‘zingni kasal his qilsang, dam olishing kerak.

DAFTARGA YOZING

CAN, MAY, MUST va SHOULD bilan tuzilgan gaplarni yozing.

Har bir modal fe'lning ma'nosini yozing.

12. Common Mistakes

Quyidagi xatolarga e'tibor bering.

❌ If I will have time, I will call you.

✅ If I have time, I will call you.

Agar vaqtim bo‘lsa, senga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.

❌ If she study, she will pass.

✅ If she studies, she will pass.

He/She/It bilan fe'lga -s qo‘shiladi.

❌ If he comes, I will helps him.

✅ If he comes, I will help him.

WILL dan keyin V1 ishlatiladi.

❌ If it will rain, we will stay home.

✅ If it rains, we will stay home.

If qismida odatda kelajak ma'nosi uchun Present Simple ishlatiladi.

DAFTARGA YOZING

To‘rtta noto‘g‘ri va to‘g‘ri gapni yozing.

Har bir xatoning sababini tushuntiring.

13. First Conditional vs Zero Conditional

Bu ikkita mavzuni farqlash muhim.

Zero Conditional

Doimiy haqiqatlar, ilmiy faktlar va takrorlanadigan natijalar uchun.

IF + PRESENT SIMPLE, PRESENT SIMPLE

If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.

Suvni 100°C gacha qizdirsangiz, u qaynaydi.

First Conditional

Kelajakda sodir bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan vaziyatlar uchun.

IF + PRESENT SIMPLE, WILL + V1

If it is hot tomorrow, I will swim.

Agar ertaga havo issiq bo‘lsa, suzaman.

DAFTARGA YOZING

Zero Conditional va First Conditional formulalarini yonma-yon yozing.

Ularning farqini o‘z so‘zlaringiz bilan tushuntiring.
