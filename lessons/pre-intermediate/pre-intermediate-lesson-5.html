1. GRAMMAR — Future Forms

1.1. Future Forms nima?

Ingliz tilida kelajak haqida gapirish uchun bir nechta grammatik shakllar mavjud. Ularning barchasi kelajak bilan bog‘liq bo‘lsa-da, ishlatilish vaziyatlari bir xil emas.

I will call you tomorrow.
Men ertaga sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.
I am going to study tonight.
Men bugun kechqurun o‘qimoqchiman.
I am meeting my friend tomorrow.
Men ertaga do‘stim bilan uchrashyapman.

1.2. WILL — Kelajakdagi qaror va bashorat

Will kelajak haqida gapirishning eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan shakllaridan biridir.

Subject + will + V1

Will quyidagi vaziyatlarda ishlatiladi:

  1. Gapirayotgan paytda qabul qilingan qaror.
  2. Kelajak haqida shaxsiy fikr yoki bashorat.
  3. Va'da berish.
  4. Yordam taklif qilish.

A. Spontaneous decisions — Hozir qabul qilingan qaror

The phone is ringing. I will answer it.
Telefon jiringlayapti. Men javob beraman.
I'm thirsty. I will get some water.
Chanqadim. Biroz suv olaman.

B. Predictions — Bashorat

I think it will rain tomorrow.
Menimcha, ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘adi.
People will use more technology in the future.
Kelajakda odamlar ko‘proq texnologiyadan foydalanishadi.

C. Promises — Va'dalar

I will help you with your homework.
Men sizga uy vazifangizda yordam beraman.
I will never forget your kindness.
Men yaxshiligingizni hech qachon unutmayman.

D. Offers — Yordam taklif qilish

I'll carry your bag.
Sumkangizni ko‘tarib beraman.
I'll open the window.
Derazani ochib beraman.

1.3. WILL — Inkor va so‘roq

Negative: Subject + will not + V1

Will not qisqartmasi — won't.

I won't be late. — Men kech qolmayman.
She won't come tomorrow. — U ertaga kelmaydi.
Question: Will + Subject + V1?
Will you help me? — Menga yordam berasizmi?
Will they arrive on time? — Ular vaqtida yetib kelishadimi?

Qisqa javoblar:

SavolHaYo‘q
Will you come?Yes, I will.No, I won't.
Will she study?Yes, she will.No, she won't.
Will they help?Yes, they will.No, they won't.

1.4. BE GOING TO — Reja va dalilga asoslangan bashorat

Be going to oldindan o‘ylangan reja yoki hozirgi vaziyatdagi dalilga asoslangan bashoratni bildiradi.

Subject + am / is / are + going to + V1

A. Plans — Oldindan tuzilgan reja

I am going to learn English this year.
Men bu yil ingliz tilini o‘rganmoqchiman.
She is going to buy a new laptop.
U yangi noutbuk sotib olmoqchi.
They are going to visit their relatives.
Ular qarindoshlarini ko‘rgani borishmoqchi.

B. Evidence-based predictions — Dalilga asoslangan bashorat

Look at those clouds! It is going to rain.
Ana u bulutlarga qarang! Yomg‘ir yog‘moqchi.
Be careful! You are going to drop the glass.
Ehtiyot bo‘ling! Stakanni tushirib yuborishingiz mumkin.

1.5. BE GOING TO — Inkor va so‘roq

Negative: Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + V1
I am not going to travel. — Men sayohat qilmoqchi emasman.
He isn't going to work tomorrow. — U ertaga ishlamoqchi emas.
They aren't going to sell the house. — Ular uyni sotmoqchi emas.
Question: Am/Is/Are + Subject + going to + V1?
Are you going to study tonight? — Bugun kechqurun o‘qimoqchimisiz?
Is she going to move to another city? — U boshqa shaharga ko‘chmoqchimi?

1.7. PRESENT CONTINUOUS — Kelishilgan kelajak rejasi

Present Continuous faqat hozir sodir bo‘layotgan ishlar uchun emas. Aniq kelishilgan kelajak rejalari uchun ham ishlatiladi.

Subject + am/is/are + V-ing + future time
I'm meeting my manager tomorrow.
Men ertaga menejerim bilan uchrashyapman.
We are flying to Istanbul next Monday.
Biz keyingi dushanba Istanbulga uchamiz.
She is having dinner with her friends tonight.
U bugun kechqurun do‘stlari bilan kechki ovqat qilmoqda.

Bu shakl odatda quyidagilarni bildiradi:

  • Uchrashuv belgilangan.
  • Chipta yoki bron qilingan.
  • Vaqt va joy kelishilgan.
  • Boshqa odamlar bilan reja kelishilgan.

1.8. PRESENT SIMPLE — Jadval va rasmiy vaqtlar

Transport, dars, kino, tadbir va boshqa rasmiy jadvallar haqida gapirganda Present Simple kelajak ma'nosida ishlatiladi.

Subject + V1 / V-s
The train leaves at 8 tomorrow.
Poyezd ertaga soat 8 da jo‘naydi.
The lesson starts at 9 a.m.
Dars ertalab soat 9 da boshlanadi.
Our flight arrives at 6 p.m.
Bizning reysimiz kechki 6 da yetib keladi.

1.11. Future Time Clauses

Kelajak haqida gapirganda when, before, after, as soon as, until kabi so‘zlardan keyin odatda Present Simple ishlatiladi.

Asosiy qoida: When / Before / After / As soon as / Until + Present Simple, asosiy gapda Future Simple.
When I arrive, I will call you.
Yetib borganimda sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.
I'll do my homework before I watch TV.
Televizor ko‘rishdan oldin uy vazifamni qilaman.
As soon as she comes, we will start.
U kelishi bilan boshlaymiz.
Wait here until I return.
Men qaytgunimcha shu yerda kuting.
Xato: When I will arrive, I will call you.
To‘g‘ri: When I arrive, I will call you.
When dan keyingi kelajak ma'noli qismda odatda will ishlatilmaydi.

1.12. Shall I / Shall we?

Shall I ...? yordam taklif qilish uchun, Shall we ...? birgalikda nimadir qilishni taklif etish uchun ishlatilishi mumkin.

Shall I open the door?
Eshikni ochaymi?
Shall we go for a walk?
Sayrga chiqamizmi?

2. LISTENING — Tinglab tushunish

Har bir audioni avval matnsiz tinglang. Asosiy ma'noni tushunishga harakat qiling. Keyin matnni oching va yana tinglang.

🎧 Listening 1 — My Plans for Next Year

Emma kelgusi yil uchun rejalari haqida gapiradi.

Emma: I've got some exciting plans for next year. First, I'm going to improve my English because I want to communicate with people from different countries.

Tom: That sounds great! Are you going to take an English course?

Emma: Yes. I'm starting an online course in January. I've already chosen the school.

Tom: What else are you planning to do?

Emma: I'm going to travel more. I think I'll visit Italy in the summer.

Tom: Will you travel alone?

Emma: No, my sister is coming with me. We've already booked our hotel.

Tom: Wonderful! I hope you have a great year.

Emma: Thank you. I'll work hard to achieve my goals.

🎧 Listening 2 — A Busy Day Tomorrow

James ertangi kun jadvali va rejalari haqida gapiradi.

Tomorrow is going to be a busy day. I have several things to do. The train leaves at seven thirty, so I am getting up at six. I'm meeting my colleague at the station. We are travelling to another city for a business meeting.

The meeting starts at ten and finishes at twelve. After that, we are having lunch with our clients. I think the meeting will be successful. In the evening, I am going to visit my parents. I haven't seen them for two weeks. I'll probably stay with them for a few hours before I return home.

4. READING — My Future Plans

Matnni shoshilmasdan o‘qing. Future Forms shakllarini toping va nima sababdan ishlatilganini aniqlang.

My Future Plans

People often ask me what I am going to do in the future. I have many plans, but I know that I need to work hard to achieve them.

First, I'm going to improve my English. I study a little every day, and I believe that regular practice will help me become more confident. Next year, I'm taking an online course because I want to develop my communication skills.

I'm also planning to learn some new professional skills. I think technology will change many jobs in the future, so people will need to continue learning. I don't know exactly where I will work in five years, but I hope I will find a job that I enjoy.

My family is important to me, too. We are visiting our relatives next month. We have already discussed the dates, and everyone is excited about the trip.

I know that plans sometimes change. If something unexpected happens, I will try to find another solution. I won't give up easily because I believe that small steps can lead to big achievements.

My main goal is to become a better person, learn new things and help the people around me. The future is not completely predictable, but I can prepare for it today.

Task 4 — Future Time Clauses

Qavs ichidagi fe'llarni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing.

  1. When I (arrive), I (call) you.
  2. After she (finish) work, she (go) home.
  3. We (start) when everyone (come).
  4. I'll wait until he (return).
  5. Before you (leave), I (give) you the documents.

6. SPEAKING — Future haqida gapiring

Quyidagi savollarga to‘liq gaplar bilan javob bering. Har bir javobni kamida 2–3 gap bilan kengaytirishga harakat qiling.

  1. What are you going to do tomorrow?
  2. What are your plans for next weekend?
  3. Will you learn a new skill this year?
  4. Where are you going to travel in the future?
  5. What are you doing this evening?
  6. What time does your next lesson start?
  7. Do you think life will be different in ten years?
  8. What will you do if you have more free time?
  9. Are you going to change anything in your daily routine?
  10. What is one important goal you will achieve?
Speaking Challenge: Telefoningizning ovoz yozish funksiyasidan foydalanib, “My Future Plans” mavzusida 1–2 daqiqa gapiring. Keyin yozuvni tinglab, xatolaringizni aniqlang.
Speaking model:

In the future, I am going to improve my English and learn new skills. I will try to practise every day. Next year, I am planning to take a course. I think these steps will help me achieve my goals. I know that success takes time, so I won't give up.
